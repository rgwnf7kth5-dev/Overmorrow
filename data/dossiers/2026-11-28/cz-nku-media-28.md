- **Criterion:** YES if, between 09:00 and 23:59 on Fri 27 Nov 2026 (Europe/Prague), the Chamber adopts the final motion on print 47 (Senate draft constitutional act amending the Constitution, Act 1/1993) with at least 120 "ano" votes, as shown by the psp.cz roll call of that day or reported by ČTK or ČT24 the same day.
