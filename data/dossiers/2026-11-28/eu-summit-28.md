# eu-summit-28
- **Claim:** EU leaders at the special European Council in Brussels, which is due to end on Friday 27 November 2026, agree to provide Ukraine with €45bn of EU-borrowed financing for 2027 (the Commission's proposed loan), whether or not all 27 member states take part.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 10:00 on Thu 26 Nov and 23:59 on Fri 27 Nov 2026 (Europe/Brussels), the European Council agrees to provide Ukraine with financing for 2027 of at least €45bn based on EU borrowing (the Commission's Coordinated Support Facility for Ukraine / top-up of the Ukraine Support Loan, or a changed structure that leaders say is that financing), in any configuration (all 27; some member states not contributing, contributing only to the civilian part, or opting out through enhanced cooperation). Any of these counts: (a) European Council conclusions or another European Council text published on consilium.europa.eu, including a text "firmly supported by" fewer than 27 heads of state or government, with wording such as "agrees to provide", "agrees on" or "endorses the Commission's proposal … and calls for its swift adoption"; (b) António Costa saying at the closing press conference or on X that leaders agreed it; (c) at least two of AFP, Reuters, AP, Bloomberg, Euronews, ČTK or Ukrinform reporting, citing Costa, leaders or officials, that leaders agreed it. NO if, by 23:59 Fri 27 Nov, leaders only "take note", "will revert", hand the file to ministers or the December European Council, agree only on principles, or agree on less than €45bn for 2027; NO also if the agreement is first announced after 23:59 Fri (summit running into Saturday). Reports published later about an agreement announced in the window count.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-26T10:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-27T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Brussels); the decisive session is Friday 27 Nov, when Costa "expect[s] to conclude the proceedings".

- Linkage: Merz conditions the MFF on all 27 sharing Ukraine costs; CZ, SK, HU (and possibly BG) refuse; Fico threatens a veto. If Merz holds the loan hostage to an MFF deal that does not come, NO.
- Form: if Costa only states support "in principle" or tasks ministers, NO. If leaders agree a different amount or split (e.g. civilian part only by all 27), the €45bn total must still be stated.
