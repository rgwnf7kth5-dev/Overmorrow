# cn-reserves-oct-1
- **Claim:** China's foreign exchange reserves stood at … billion dollars at the end of October 2026, according to data published on 7 November. (Interval claim.)
- **Criterion:** China's foreign exchange reserves at end-October 2026 in billions of US dollars, as first published by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange on safe.gov.cn (or the People's Bank of China) on 7 Nov 2026. Later revisions do not count. Void if the figure does not appear by 23:59 Beijing time on 7 Nov 2026.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-07T23:59:00+08:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-05T18:01:09Z

## Schedule
- SAFE publishes on the 7th of each month, weekends included: end-Sep on 2026-10-07 (in Golden Week), end-Aug on 09-07, end-Jul on 08-07, end-Jun on 07-07, end-May on 06-07 (a Sunday), end-Apr on 05-07. Dates from the pages below (read 2026-11-05T17:52:53Z to 17:56Z). No written calendar for November was read.

- End-Jul: 3,418.8bn, +2.5bn — https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2026/0806/27770.html (published 2026-08-07, read 2026-11-05T17:53:54Z) "34188 亿美元".
