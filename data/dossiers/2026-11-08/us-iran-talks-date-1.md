# us-iran-talks-date-1
- **Claim:** Between 06:00 Tehran time on 6 November and 23:59 Tehran time on 7 November 2026, the United States and Iran both publicly confirm the date of a new round of direct or indirect talks.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 06:00 Tehran time on 6 Nov and 23:59 Tehran time on 7 Nov 2026 both of the following state on the record a specific date or named day for a new round of direct or indirect US-Iran talks: (a) the White House (including President Trump or Vice President Vance) or the State Department, and (b) Iran's foreign minister, its foreign ministry spokesman or the secretary of its Supreme National Security Council. As reported by Al Jazeera, BBC, a wire or Iran International. The round need not have taken place. Statements by Qatar or other mediators alone, unnamed sources, or a willingness to talk with no date do not count. NO if both confirmations are not made in the window.
