# us-iran-strike-4
- **Claim:** US forces strike a target on Iranian territory on Saturday 7 November 2026 (Tehran time).
- **Criterion:** YES if the White House (including President Trump), the Department of Defense / Department of War or US Central Command confirms a US military strike (air, missile or drone) on a target on Iranian territory, including Iranian territorial waters and islands, carried out between 00:00 and 23:59 Tehran time on 7 Nov 2026. A confirmation published after the window counts. Iranian claims alone, unnamed officials, strikes only on Iranian proxies outside Iran, on vessels in international waters, or by Israel alone do not count. NO if no such strike took place in the window.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-07T23:59:00+03:30
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-05T17:54Z. Read times as stated.

## Schedule
- No scheduled event. Military plans are reported from unnamed officials only; no date is confirmed. 20:30 UTC on 6 Nov opens the window, 20:29 UTC on 7 Nov closes it.

## Risks
- A strike on 6 Nov alone does not count; one on 7 Nov Tehran time is needed, even if a campaign has already begun.
- Anonymous sourcing alone gives NO even if a strike occurred; WSJ reports debate over whether to acknowledge continuing operations.
- A "defensive" hit on a launcher on Iranian soil counts; one on a boat in international waters does not, and location may be disputed.
- A late confirmation counts, but may arrive after the resolver's first reading; the claim then stays pending.
