# ecb-list-2
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Brussels time on 7 November 2026 the Eurogroup or the Council of the EU publicly names at least three candidates for ECB president.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 on 6 Nov and 23:59 Brussels time on 7 Nov 2026, the Eurogroup (its president or an on-record spokesperson) or the Council of the EU publicly names at least three people put forward for ECB president, as shown on consilium.europa.eu or reported by Reuters, Bloomberg, AFP, dpa or EFE. Unattributed sourcing does not count. NO if no such naming of three or more is made in that time.
