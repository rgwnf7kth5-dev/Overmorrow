# us301-port-2
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Eastern time on 7 November 2026 the US Trade Representative announces a further suspension of the Section 301 port fees on Chinese vessels beyond 10 November.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 Paris time on 6 Nov and 23:59 Eastern time on 7 Nov 2026, USTR announces in a press release, a notice on ustr.gov or a Federal Register document (including public inspection) that the action on China's maritime, logistics and shipbuilding sectors stays suspended beyond 10 Nov 2026. NO if no such announcement is made in that time.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-07T23:59:00-05:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-05T18:01:09Z

## Schedule
- 178 exclusions run "through 11:59 p.m. eastern daylight time on November 9, 2026" per the notice — https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents/2025-21671.json (FR 2025-12-01, read 2026-11-05T17:54Z); USTR's release says "extended until November 10, 2026" — https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2025/november/ustr-extends-exclusions-china-section-301-tariffs-related-forced-technology-transfer-investigation (read 2026-11-05T17:53:33Z). The two wordings differ.
- Port fees under the maritime Section 301 action are scheduled to take effect on 10 Nov 2026; USTR proposes suspension to 9 Jan 2027; comments closed 30 Oct, docket USTR-2026-0694 — https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents/2026-21755.json (FR 2026-10-26, read 2026-11-05T17:54Z) "further suspend that action for two months, through January 9, 2027"; Thompson Hine SmarTrade, https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/10/ (read 2026-11-05T17:55Z) "must be submitted by October 30, 2026".

## Facts
- No extension announced as of 2026-11-05T17:53Z: USTR's November list stops at three items of 2 Nov, none on China — https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/november (read 2026-11-05T17:48:52Z). Federal Register API shows no USTR item on public inspection (read 2026-11-05T17:54:17Z).
- No request for comments on the 178 exclusions was found in the Federal Register in 2026, unlike Sept 2025 (FR 2025-17894). The API search by term covers 8 documents since May 2026; conforming amendments of 2 Sep and 7 Oct 2026 update tariff codes, and their abstracts mention no new end date.
- Section 301 technology actions continued beyond their four-year point — FR 2026-20510 (published 2026-10-07, read 2026-11-05T17:54Z) "will remain in effect, subject to possible further modifications".
- Timing precedents: Wed 26 Nov 2025 for an expiry of 29 Nov, three days, by China Daily "announced on Wednesday", https://www.chinadailyhk.com/hk/article/624449 (published 2025-11-27), FR 1 Dec. Other extensions appeared in the FR on 2 Sep 2025 (expiry 31 Aug), 5 Jun 2025 (expiry 31 May), 30 May 2024 (expiry 31 May), 29 Dec 2023 (expiry 31 Dec), 11 Sep 2023 (expiry 30 Sep); USTR's own announcement days for those were not read (FR API, read 2026-11-05T17:55Z).

SCMP headlines of 5 Nov mention US-China talks and Bessent, and China's offer on farm imports if Board of Trade tariff cuts take effect; articles refuse the reader, no talks date read — https://www.scmp.com/economy/china-economy (read 2026-11-05T17:53:59Z).
