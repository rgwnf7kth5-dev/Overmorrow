- **Criterion:** YES if between 06:00 on 6 Nov and 23:59 Prague time on 7 Nov 2026 the chair or a deputy chair of SPD or Motoristé sobě, or a minister nominated by either party, publicly says whether they agree with, reject or are open to lowering the valorisation trigger for ČT/ČRo funding from 10% to 6%, as reported by ČTK, ČT24, Novinky, Seznam Zprávy, Aktuálně.cz, iROZHLAS or E15. A general remark on abolishing the fees that does not address the trigger does not count. NO otherwise.
