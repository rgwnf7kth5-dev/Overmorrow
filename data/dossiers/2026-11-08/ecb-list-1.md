# ecb-list-1
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Brussels time on 7 November 2026 the Eurogroup or the Council of the EU publicly names the candidates for ECB president, with both Pablo Hernández de Cos and Klaas Knot among them.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 on 6 Nov and 23:59 Brussels time on 7 Nov 2026, the Eurogroup (its president or an on-record spokesperson) or the Council of the EU publicly names the candidates put forward for ECB president and both Pablo Hernández de Cos and Klaas Knot are named, as shown on consilium.europa.eu or reported by Reuters, Bloomberg, AFP, dpa or EFE. Unattributed sourcing does not count. NO if no such naming is made in that time, or if either man is missing from it.

## Research step, 5 Nov evening (after round one)
- Question: Has the Eurogroup said when it publishes the names, and has Spain formally submitted?
- Finding: Neither found. The only Eurogroup statement read is its spokesperson to Euronews: Pierrakakis 'has called on member states to send their nominations by Friday 6 November' and 'the Eurogroup, at its meeting on 9 November, will take stock of the nominations.' Nothing on publishing names, and no hour for the deadline. Handelsblatt says the same. The only precedent is in the dossier: on 9 Jan 2026, deadline day, the Eurogroup announced six vice-president candidates. Spain: no report of a formal submission; a search gave only July-October items on backing him, e.g. titles 'Spain backs Hernandez de Cos in first ever bid' (Euronews, 21 Jul, reader HTTP 406, title only). ARA's 'Spain will submit a candidacy' is from Dec 2025 and concerns the vice-presidency race, so it does not apply. Spanish economic fronts showed no 5 Nov item on it. consilium.europa.eu not read.
