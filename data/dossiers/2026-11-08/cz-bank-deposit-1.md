# cz-bank-deposit-1
- **Claim:** Between 06:00 on 6 November and 23:59 on 7 November 2026, at least one bank operating in the Czech Republic announces a higher interest rate on a savings account or term deposit for retail clients in the Czech Republic.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 06:00 on 6 Nov and 23:59 Prague time on 7 Nov 2026 at least one bank operating in the Czech Republic newly announces an increase in the rate on a retail savings account or term deposit, as shown by the bank's own website or press release or reported by ČTK, E15, Novinky, Seznam Zprávy, Aktuálně.cz or Peníze.cz. mBank's increase announced by 5 Nov does not count. NO otherwise.
- **Deadline / event window:** 2026-11-06T06:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-07T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Prague)
- **Written at:** 2026-11-05T17:58:51Z

## Schedule
- No date. Rate took effect after the 5 Nov decision; banks announce on their own timing. mBank's change takes effect 16 Nov.
