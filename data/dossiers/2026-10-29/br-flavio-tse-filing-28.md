# br-flavio-tse-filing-28

- **Claim:** By 23:59 Brasília time on 28 October 2026, Flávio Bolsonaro, his coalition or the PL has formally filed at the Superior Electoral Court (TSE) a request to annul or invalidate all or part of the 25 October presidential runoff.
- **Criterion:** YES if, at any time from the close of voting on 25 Oct 2026 until 23:59:59 Brasília time (America/Sao_Paulo, UTC−3) on 28 Oct 2026, Flávio Bolsonaro, his campaign coalition or the PL files a petition, representação or action at the TSE. Filings by their lawyers on their behalf count.
  - The filing must seek annulment or invalidation of the presidential runoff, in whole or in part, or a recount or re-vote of it.
  - Evidence: the TSE (tse.jus.br news or the PJe docket), or at least one of Folha, O Globo/g1, Estadão or UOL reporting the filing.
  - Statements of intent, filings at other courts (STF etc.), and audit or clarification requests that do not seek annulment, invalidation, recount or re-vote do not count.
  - NO if no such filing is made at the TSE by then.
- **Deadline (event window):** 2026-10-27T06:00:00-03:00 to 2026-10-28T23:59:59-03:00. A filing from 25 Oct also counts. Resolves by 2026-10-29T05:00:00+01:00.
- **Written at:** 2026-10-26T19:35:00+01:00

## Risks
- **Wrong court:** the challenge may go to the STF against Zanin's extension decision, which does not count.
- **Softer filing:** the PL may prefer an audit request. That is borderline and does not count unless it seeks annulment, invalidation, recount or re-vote.
- **Late filing:** a filing late on 28 Oct may only be reported on 29 Oct. The resolver checks the filing date.
- **Pressure to concede:** allies (governors, Centrão) or the market fall may push Flávio towards conceding.
- **Overlap with the 28 Oct edition:** that edition's br-flavio-tse-filing covers 26 Oct 05:35 to 27 Oct 23:59 BRT. A filing on 26–27 Oct resolves both YES. If a filing is reported before the freeze, replace this claim with "the TSE rules on it by 28 Oct" (see notes).
