# cz-zeman-decorated-28
- **Claim:** On 28 October 2026 President Petr Pavel awards or confers a state decoration (the Order of the White Lion, the Order of Tomáš Garrigue Masaryk or the Medal of Merit) on former president Miloš Zeman.
- **Criterion:** YES if Miloš Zeman (b. 1944, president 2013–2023) appears on the official list of persons on whom President Pavel awarded or conferred state decorations on 28 Oct 2026, as published by the Office of the President (hrad.cz) or reported from that list by ČTK or Czech Television. Inclusion on the list counts whether or not Zeman attends the ceremony in person. NO if he is not on the list, including if he declined the decoration in advance, or if the ceremony is not held on 28 Oct 2026 (between 19:00 and 23:59 Prague time).

- **Written at:** 2026-10-26T19:25:00+01:00

## Risks
- Nomination named a specific order; Pavel could award a lower decoration (Medal of Merit) — counts YES.
- The president is ex officio bearer of the orders during office; whether a former president can be "awarded" the White Lion again is unverified — could affect what is possible (not checked).
- Zeman may decline in advance (he refused to attend in 2023 and excused himself in 2024–2025); then he would not be on the list → NO.
- List published late at night or only on hrad.cz (403 to fetcher): resolver should use ČTK/ČT copy of the list; late publication leaves pending, not NO.
- The list is usually not leaked; a surprise either way is possible.
