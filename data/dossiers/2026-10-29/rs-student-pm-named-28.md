# rs-student-pm-named-28

- **Claim:** By 23:59 Belgrade time on 28 October 2026, Serbia's Student List publicly names one person as its candidate for prime minister.
- **Criterion:** YES if, at any time from the close of polls on 25 Oct 2026 until 23:59:59 Belgrade time (Europe/Belgrade, UTC+1) on 28 Oct 2026, the Student List ("Studentska lista – Studenti pobeđuju") publicly announces one named person as the list's candidate for prime minister (mandatar/premijer). The announcement may come from the list's official spokespeople or leadership, or from the coordinating body of the student movement.
  - Evidence: the list's official channels, or N1, RTS, RFE/RL (Slobodna Evropa), Vreme, Danas, AP or Reuters citing such an announcement.
  - Media speculation, "possible" names, several names, or a statement that the list will decide later do not count.
  - NO if no single named candidate is announced by then.
- **Deadline (event window):** 2026-10-27T06:00:00+01:00 to 2026-10-28T23:59:59+01:00. An announcement made from 25 Oct also counts. Resolves by 2026-10-29T05:00:00+01:00.
- **Written at:** 2026-10-26T19:30:00+01:00

## Schedule
- **No scheduled event.** The list may name a candidate at any time.
- **The formal steps** (secondary sources; constitutional text not re-read):
  - The Republic Electoral Commission (RIK) publishes final results.
  - The National Assembly must convene within 30 days of the final results.
  - The (acting) president proposes a PM candidate after consultations with the lists.
  - A government must be elected within 90 days.
  - Sources: Rio Times https://www.riotimesonline.com/serbia-election-what-happens-now/ (published 2026-10-25, read 2026-10-26T04:00Z, carried over from the 28 Oct dossier); Mezha https://mezha.net/eng/news/mezha-d8d928403b3afa0f05ccae54_serbia-s_student_list (published 2026-10-26 19:06, via the sweep watch_a.json).
- **Acting president:** Speaker Ana Brnabić (SNS) has been acting president since Vučić resigned on 27 Sep (Rio Times).
- **RIK final results** are not expected by 28 Oct. The Guardian's live blog (26 Oct) says official results may be delayed one to two weeks by local repeat votes (sweep world.json; https://www.theguardian.com/world/live/2026/oct/26/serbia-vucic-elections-students-bulgaria-france-urkaine-russia-latest-news-updates; fetch refused tonight).
- **Precedents for slow final results:**
  - Election of 17 Dec 2023: the RIK adopted its final report on 12 Jan 2024 after repeat voting at eight polling stations. — Kosovo Online, https://www.kosovo-online.com/vesti/izbori-u-srbiji/rik-proglasila-konacne-rezultate-parlamentarnih-izbora-u-srbiji-12-1-2024 (published 2024-01-12, read 2026-10-26T18:17Z) "usvojila je na večerašnjoj sednici ukupni izveštaj o konačnim rezultatima izbora održanih 17. decembra"
  - Election of 3 Apr 2022: final results came more than three months later, after repeated voting in Veliki Trnovac. — Danas/BBC News na srpskom, https://www.danas.rs/bbc-news-serbian/srbija-i-politika-rik-saopstio-konacne-rezultate-parlamentarnih-izbora-tri-meseca-posla-glasanja/ (published 2022-07-05 17:10, read 2026-10-26T18:17Z) "Do odlaganja proglašenja konačnih rezultata došlo je zbog ponavljanja izbora"
  - So "RIK final results by 28 Oct" and "constituent session called by 28 Oct" are not open questions. The open next step is the list's choice of PM.

## Base rate
- **No clean class.** Own count of new majorities after a change of power in ex-Yugoslav states where no PM candidate was pre-named:
  - Serbia 2012: PM Dačić agreed about 3 weeks after the vote.
  - Montenegro 2020: Krivokapić put forward about 9 days after the 30 Aug vote.
  - 0 of 2 named within 3 days.
  - Carried over from the 28 Oct dossier rs-student-list-pm; not re-verified tonight.
- **Contrast:** in Serbia 2000, DOS had Đinđić pre-named.
- **This case:** the Student List deliberately campaigned without a declared PM candidate.

## Schedule slip
- **Class:** none. There is no scheduled event, and the list can announce at any time.

## Risks
- **Fast name:** the list may name someone quickly to pre-empt acting president Brnabić's consultations and show it is ready to govern.
- **Ambiguous statements:** for example "Šoškić is our proposal if…" or a "team" announcement. The criterion needs one named candidate, clearly presented as the list's PM candidate.
- **Waiting for formal steps:** the list may wait for RIK final results or the presidential consultations, which push the name past 28 Oct.
- **Overlap with the 28 Oct edition:** that edition's rs-student-list-pm covers 26 Oct 05:35 to 27 Oct 23:59. A naming on 26–27 Oct resolves both YES. If a name is reported before the freeze (27 Oct 05:40 CET), this claim is already settled and should be replaced.
