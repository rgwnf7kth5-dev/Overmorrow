# cz-fee-coalition-deal-28
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Prague time on 28 October 2026, the governing coalition of ANO, SPD and Motoristé publicly announces that the three parties have agreed on a specific change to the bill abolishing the ČT and ČRo licence fees (tisk 231). Examples are voluntary fees, a later effective date, or a different funding amount or formula.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 CET on 27 Oct 2026 and 23:59:59 CET on 28 Oct 2026, one of the following happens, as reported by ČTK, ČT24, iROZHLAS or another major Czech outlet, or by vlada.gov.cz or a coalition party. (a) The coalition council (koaliční rada) announces that ANO, SPD and Motoristé have agreed on a named, concrete change to tisk 231. (b) The leaders of the three parties (Babiš, Okamura, Macinka) say so jointly. (c) One of the three leaders says the three parties have agreed on such a change, and no leader or spokesperson of the other two parties contradicts it by 23:59 on 28 Oct. A concrete change means a stated content: voluntary fees or another form of fee, a new effective date, or a changed funding amount, mechanism or formula. These do not count: one party's proposal or intention (for example a repeat of Babiš's 26 Oct "we will propose voluntary fees"), statements that talks will take place or are under way, statements already made before 06:00 on 27 Oct, and general pledges such as "the law will be changed in second reading". NO if no such announcement is made in the window, including when no coalition meeting takes place.
