# il-yashar-largest-28

- **Claim:** In the latest Central Elections Committee count published by 23:59 Israel time on 28 October 2026, Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar has more votes than Likud (including New Hope) in the 27 October Knesset election.
- **Criterion:** YES if the most recent count published by Israel's Central Elections Committee (CEC) at or before 23:59:59 Israel time (UTC+2) on 28 Oct 2026 for the Knesset election held on 27 Oct 2026 gives Yashar more valid votes than Likud.
  - Votes decide, not projected seats. The share of ballots counted at that moment does not matter.
  - If the CEC site has been updated after 23:59, the last update at or before 23:59 decides. Evidence: the CEC site with its update time, an archived copy, or Times of Israel, Ynet, Haaretz or Kan quoting the CEC figures with their time.
  - NO if that count gives Likud as many votes as Yashar or more.
  - NO if the CEC has published no count by then, including because the vote did not take place on 27 Oct (for example, postponement).
  - The claim stays pending if the count as of 23:59 cannot yet be established from those sources.
- **Deadline (event window):** 2026-10-27T22:00:00+02:00 (polls close) to 2026-10-28T23:59:59+02:00. Evidence is expected the same night. Resolves by 2026-10-29T05:00:00+01:00.

## Schedule slip
- **Class:** none. The deciding event is the CEC's running count, which is published continuously rather than in a fixed slot. A late count still leaves a count published by 23:59.
- **Vote postponement:** this risk is covered in the criterion (NO). Its class is election-held, rate 0.009 (`node scripts/refdata/slip.mjs`, read 2026-10-26T18:20Z).
- **Signs of movement:** none reported as of 26 Oct evening. Earlier there was Iran-escalation talk (Ynet 8 Oct, Israel Hayom 14 Oct; see the 28 Oct dossier).

## Risks
- **Poll miss:** Likud/right underpolling (2022: −4 seats for the bloc) could erase a 2–3 seat Yashar lead.
- **Votes vs seats:** this claim counts votes at a moment in time. The 28 Oct edition's il-yashar-most-seats counts final seats after surplus agreements.
  - The two can differ only if the lists are within a fraction of a seat, or if double envelopes, which lean towards soldiers' votes, move the order after 28 Oct.
  - Yashar ahead in votes on 28 Oct and behind in final seats is possible but not contradictory.
- **Snapshot evidence:** the CEC updates after midnight as double envelopes are added. The resolver must use the last update at or before 23:59 (archive, or timed media liveblogs).
- **Site problems:** the 2026 results URL is unknown. If the CEC site is unreachable, evidence comes from timed media reports of CEC figures.
- **Attack or disruption on election day:** could delay the count (then the claim stays pending, not NO) or cause postponement (NO).
