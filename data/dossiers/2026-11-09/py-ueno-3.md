# py-ueno-3
Claim: By 23:59 Asunción time on 8 November 2026, Paraguay's central bank (BCP) announces guarantee payments to a further group of ueno bank depositors beyond the first group.
Criterion: YES if, between 01:30 Asunción time on 7 Nov 2026 (the freeze) and 23:59 Asunción time on 8 Nov 2026, the Banco Central del Paraguay announces, in a communiqué or on its official channels (bcp.gov.py, @BCP_PY), the start of deposit-guarantee (FGD) payments to a group of ueno bank depositors other than the first group announced on 5 Nov (deposits up to G. 10 million, no debts to ueno, account at another institution), as shown by the BCP or reported by ABC Color, Última Hora, La Nación (Paraguay), Hoy or EFE. NO if no such announcement is made in the window; an announcement made before 01:30 on 7 Nov does not count.
Deadline: 2026-11-08 23:59 America/Asuncion (UTC-3)
Written at (UTC): 2026-11-06T18:24:00Z

## Schedule
The law gives up to five working days from the start (5 Nov) to pay guaranteed deposits (to about Wed 11 Nov by own count: 5, 6, 9, 10, 11 Nov). The BCP said further groups "se anunciará a medida que avance el procedimiento". 7-8 Nov is a weekend. bcp.gov.py refuses the reader (HTTP 403), so the BCP's own pages were not read.
