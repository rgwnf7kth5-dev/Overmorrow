# cz-media-havlicek-model-9

- **Criterion:** YES if in the Nedělní debata broadcast that starts at 12:00 Prague time on 8 Nov 2026, Karel Havlíček himself says that ČT and ČRo are to be funded, or that ANO or the government proposes or is preparing to fund them, by a share of collected tax revenue (for example a share of VAT, or a share of tax revenue set by a coefficient). Naming it as one model under consideration counts; merely not objecting when the moderator or another guest raises it does not. A fixed annual contribution from the state budget, voluntary fees, a new dedicated tax, or a change of the indexation threshold alone do not count. NO if he says nothing of the kind or does not take part.

- Babiš (5 Nov): fixed money for the whole term; model not yet agreed by the coalition; Novinky reported the option of a share of collected taxes by a coefficient — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/babis-ct-a-cro-budou-mit-stabilni-a-predvidatelne-financovani/2898039 (published 2026-11-05 19:25, read 2026-11-06T17:59Z) "Konkrétní model koalice ale ještě neprojednala. ... místo příspěvku ze státního rozpočtu mohla být média placena podílem z vybraných daní podle stanoveného koeficientu."
- Babiš: changes at second reading in February, amending the existing bill, subject to partners; indexation threshold could fall from 10% to 6% — same ČTK report "Změny v návrhu podmínil souhlasem koaličních partnerů" and "by se tato hranice mohla snížit na šest procent".

- Klempíř (culture minister, Motorists): open whether funding is from the budget, an index, the Finnish model (earmarked tax) or taxes; "my zatím zůstáváme na našich pozicích" — ČTK 2898304.
