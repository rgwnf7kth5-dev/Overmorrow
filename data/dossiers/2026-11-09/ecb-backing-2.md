- **Claim:** By 23:59 Brussels time on 8 November 2026 the government of a euro-area country other than Spain and the Netherlands publicly backs Hernández de Cos or Knot for ECB president.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 05:30 on 7 Nov and 23:59 Brussels time on 8 Nov 2026, the head of government, finance minister or an on-record government spokesperson of a euro-area member other than Spain and the Netherlands publicly says the government supports Pablo Hernández de Cos or Klaas Knot for ECB president, as reported by Reuters, Bloomberg, AFP, dpa, EFE, ANP or the government's own site.
