# py-cabinet-1
Claim: By 23:59 Asunción time on 8 November 2026, Pedro Alliana, as President of Paraguay, appoints at least one new cabinet minister.
Criterion: YES if, between 12:00 Asunción time on 6 Nov 2026 and 23:59 Asunción time on 8 Nov 2026, Pedro Alliana, while President of Paraguay, appoints (by decree, oath or a public announcement of the appointment) as head of one of the Executive's ministries (Ministerios, including the Ministry of the Interior and the Ministry of Economy and Finance) a person who was not a minister in Santiago Peña's cabinet at 12:00 on 6 Nov, as reported by ABC Color, Última Hora, La Nación (Paraguay), EFE or AFP or shown by the Presidency (presidencia.gov.py). Secretariats with ministerial rank and the Civil Cabinet do not count; confirming an interim minister who was in office at 12:00 on 6 Nov (such as Carlos Humberto Benítez at Interior) does not count. NO if no such appointment is made in the window, including if Alliana has not become president.
Deadline: 2026-11-08 23:59 America/Asuncion (UTC-3)
Written at (UTC): 2026-11-06T18:20:00Z
Window note: the window opens at 12:00 on 6 Nov, before the freeze, as stated in the claim, because the new president may name ministers on the night of his oath; the morning desk must check whether the outcome is already known.
Premise change: the brief's earlier state (resignation to be accepted, Alliana sworn on 5-6 Nov) did not happen; the Senate refused to accept the resignation and is trying Peña. On 6 Nov at 15:00 local it had admitted the evidence 41-0 and called the closing sessions; the removal vote was expected that evening.

## Schedule
No date for appointments. Sequence reported by Hoy on 6 Nov: a fourth session for closing arguments, then a fifth session to vote on removal. On removal the vice-president succeeds (constitution; previous dossier py-sworn-1.md).

## Base rate
None in reference data. No sourced precedent read for how fast a successor Paraguayan president named ministers (Franco 2012 not verified in this run).

## Schedule slip
slip_class none (no scheduled event; an appointment by a new president). The chain needed: removal vote (expected 6 Nov evening), oath of Alliana, then appointments. Signs: unanimous procedural votes in the Senate (41-0, 39-3), open vacancies at Interior and the Civil Cabinet, Alliana's stated wish to change ministers. Status quo: no (YES needs an act).

## Risks
- Removal vote delayed or failing (needs a two-thirds majority of the Senate; procedural votes so far unanimous), or legal challenge delaying the oath.
- Alliana keeps Peña's ministers for continuity during the bank crisis and fills only the Civil Cabinet or secretariats (do not count), or confirms interim Benítez (does not count).
- Appointments announced but decrees dated 9 Nov; criterion counts a public announcement of the appointment.
- Ministers named before 12:00 on 6 Nov are impossible (Alliana not president then).
