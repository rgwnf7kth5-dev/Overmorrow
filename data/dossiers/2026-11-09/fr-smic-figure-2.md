# fr-smic-figure-2
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Paris time on 8 November 2026 Prime Minister Lecornu or a government minister publicly states the size of a planned increase of the Smic.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 05:30 on 7 Nov and 23:59 Paris time on 8 Nov 2026, Sébastien Lecornu or a member of his government publicly states, on the record, a figure (in euros or per cent) for an increase of the Smic that the government plans or will propose, in an interview, statement or post, as reported by AFP, Le Monde, Le Parisien, La Tribune Dimanche, franceinfo or BFMTV. Repeating the statutory 1 January revaluation without a figure, an aim such as "pass 1,500 euros" already stated before 7 Nov, or a figure attributed to unnamed sources does not count. NO if no such figure is stated in that time.
