# za-iec-decl-2
Claim: By 23:59 SAST on 8 November 2026, the IEC formally declares the results of the 4 November 2026 municipal elections.
Criterion: YES if, between 06:30 SAST on 7 Nov 2026 and 23:59 SAST on 8 Nov 2026, the Electoral Commission of South Africa (IEC) formally declares the final results of the 2026 local government elections nationwide (declaration of results at the National Results Operations Centre), as shown by the IEC (elections.org.za, results.elections.org.za) or reported by SAnews, EWN, News24, SABC or TimesLIVE. Provisional dashboard figures and declarations of single municipalities do not count. NO if the national declaration has not taken place by 23:59 SAST on 8 Nov.
Deadline: 2026-11-08 23:59 SAST
Written at (UTC): 2026-11-06T18:10:00Z
Update of: data/dossiers/2026-11-08/za-iec-decl-1.md (that claim had the deadline Sat 7 Nov; the date reported now is Sunday 8 Nov).

## Schedule
No IEC notice with a date and time was found. Two EWN reports of 6 Nov put the formal declaration on Sunday 8 Nov ("Sunday night" for KZN). The IEC itself said on 5 Nov that collation should be "concluded by the weekend" and that it cannot declare before 17:00 on Fri 6 Nov (objection deadline); it has seven days by law (to Wed 11 Nov). Primary source: IEC results site https://results.elections.org.za/ (no text readable with the shell reader) and the declaration event at the Results Operations Centre, Gallagher Convention Centre, Midrand.

## Base rate
No reference class for IEC declaration timing. Local precedents n=2 (2021, 2024): both declared on the day the IEC had signalled, both inside the statutory seven days.

## Risks
- Unresolved objections or recounts (eThekwini, Emfuleni) delay the national declaration to Monday 9 Nov or later: NO.
- The declaration could happen on Saturday 7 Nov (inside the window, YES) or before 06:30 SAST on Sat 7 Nov (Friday night): then the event falls before the window and the claim resolves NO. On Friday evening Emfuleni and eThekwini were still incomplete, so a Friday-night declaration looks unlikely.
- A late-night ceremony starting before midnight but with the formal declaration after 00:00 on 9 Nov: NO.
