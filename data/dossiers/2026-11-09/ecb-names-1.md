# ecb-names-1
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Brussels time on 8 November 2026 the Eurogroup has publicly named Pablo Hernández de Cos and Klaas Knot as the only candidates for ECB president.
- **Criterion:** YES if, at any time between 06:00 on 6 Nov and 23:59 Brussels time on 8 Nov 2026, the Eurogroup (its president Kyriakos Pierrakakis or an on-record spokesperson) or the Council of the EU publicly names the candidates put forward for ECB president and the names are exactly Pablo Hernández de Cos and Klaas Knot, as shown on consilium.europa.eu or reported by Reuters, Bloomberg, AFP, dpa, EFE or Europa Press. Reports based on national governments or unnamed sources do not count. NO if no such naming is made in that time, or if it names a third candidate or omits either man.
