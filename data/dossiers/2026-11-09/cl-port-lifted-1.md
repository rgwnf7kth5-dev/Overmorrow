# cl-port-lifted-1
- **Claim:** The Agreporsai truckers' blockade of the extra-port container depots at San Antonio, Chile, is lifted by 23:59 Chile time on 8 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if by 23:59 Chile time (UTC−3) on Sunday 8 Nov 2026 the Agrupación de Camioneros Independientes de San Antonio (Agreporsai) has announced the end or suspension of its strike and the blockade of the access roads to San Antonio's extra-port depots has been lifted, as reported by at least one of Emol, BioBioChile, T13, PortalPortuario or MundoMarítimo, and the blockade has not been re-imposed by that time. A lifting that happened before the window opened and still holds at 23:59 on 8 Nov counts. A partial opening (some depots or a humanitarian corridor for cherries only) does not count. NO if the blockade is still in place at 23:59 on 8 Nov. Separate actions by other groups (Fenatrac, the national call for 10 Nov) do not count either way.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-08T23:59:00-03:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-06T18:15Z

## Facts
- Blockade since the night of Wed 28 Oct of the access to extra-port depots; Agreporsai demands higher container haulage rates, citing diesel and tolls — https://portalportuario.cl/duenos-de-camiones-y-operadores-logisticos-de-san-antonio-llaman-a-desbloquear-accesos-a-extraportuarios/ (published 2026-11-06T17:00Z, read 2026-11-06T18:01:12Z) "mantiene bloqueado los accesos a depósitos extraportuarios desde la noche del miércoles 28 de octubre".
- Government proposal: haulers become direct contractors of depot operators at the same per-leg rates, ending intermediaries ("cortacola"); rates indexed to INE's transport cost index; Fogain Transporte credit guarantees. Undersecretary Koehler: "Es hora de destrabar San Antonio" and asked members to accept and end blockades immediately — Emol (above) "llamamos a sus bases a aceptarla y a terminar inmediatamente los bloqueos".
- Koehler described a "marco de acuerdo" reached with depot owners; regional delegate Millones called it a "fórmula robusta" — Emol (above).
- Agreporsai president Manuel Araya: nothing resolved until members analyse and approve; blockade continued on 6 Nov — PortalPortuario (above) "nada será resuelto hasta que la propuesta sea analizada y aprobada por las bases. Aún continúa el bloqueo".
- Gap on 4 Nov between offers and demands CLP 20,000–23,000 per service (Millones); depots near 90% full (Frutas de Chile); two cherry ships diverted to Valparaíso — https://mundomaritimo.cl/noticias/paro-de-camioneros-en-san-antonio-continua-y-obliga-a-desviar-embarques-de-cerezas-a-valparaiso (dated 06/11/2026 on the front page, read 2026-11-06T18:01:33Z) "la distancia entre las ofertas y las demandas de los transportistas fluctúa entre CLP 20.000 y CLP 23.000 por servicio".
- On 31 Oct Agreporsai said the strike continued indefinitely after failing to agree with cargo generators — https://www.t13.cl/noticia/nacional/paro-camioneros-san-antonio-gremio-descarta-deponer-movilizacion-31-10-2026 (published 2026-10-31 16:47, read 2026-11-06T18:01:54Z) "la manifestación continúa de manera indefinida".
- Six truck-owner and logistics firms (about 250 trucks) demanded the blockade end and threatened legal action, calling it a commercial dispute — PortalPortuario (above) "llamado urgente a restablecer la operatividad".
- More than 3,000 containers blocked from entering, 4,500 empties piled at terminals (as of Wed 4 Nov) — https://www.wsws.org/en/articles/2026/11/06/pvfj-n06.html (published 2026-11-06T01:15Z, read 2026-11-06T18:01:53Z) "More than 3,000 containers have been prevented from entering the port".
- Other trucker groups called a national stoppage for Tue 10 Nov; the CNDC confederation refuses to join — https://www.biobiochile.cl/noticias/nacional/chile/2026/11/06/al-borde-de-la-quiebra-gremios-de-transportistas-anuncian-paro-nacional-pero-cndc-descarta-sumarse.shtml (published 2026-11-06T11:22-03:00, read 2026-11-06T18:01:42Z) "convocaron a una movilización nacional para este martes 10 de noviembre".
- Fuel prices: a sixth consecutive rise in petrol and diesel is projected (BioBioChile headline on the same page, read 2026-11-06T18:01:42Z) "Proyectan sexta alza seguida de las bencinas y el diésel".

## Risks
- Members reject the offer (the per-service gap of CLP 20–23k was not closed by 4 Nov) or accept with conditions that need signed contracts first.
- A diesel price rise announced in the window could harden positions; the 10 Nov national call could encourage holding out.
- Lifting may be gradual or partial (cherry corridor), which does not count.
- Police removal of the blockade (demanded by exporters) would count as lifted only if Agreporsai announces the end; a forced clearance without an announcement leaves it NO under the criterion. Note for the resolver.
