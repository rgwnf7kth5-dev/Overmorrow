# kr-samsung-bio-strike-1
- **Claim:** The Samsung Biologics union's partial strike, begun on 6 November, is still in force at the end of Sunday 8 November 2026 (23:59 KST): it has not been called off or suspended and no wage agreement has been reached.
- **Criterion:** YES if, at 23:59 KST on 8 Nov 2026, the partial strike that the Samsung Biologics union (Samsung Biologics Sangsaeng/Coexistence Labor Union) began at 00:00 on 6 Nov has not been ended or suspended by the union, and no wage and collective agreement, tentative or final, between the union and Samsung Biologics has been announced. NO if, before 23:59 KST on 8 Nov, the union announces the end or suspension of the strike, or a tentative or final agreement is announced by either side or reported by Yonhap, The Korea Herald, The Korea Times, MoneyToday or Businesskorea. Scaling the strike up or down while it continues does not count as an end.

## Facts
- Participants work in QC testing, QA, logistics, production support; about 10% of about 4,000 members — Korea Times, above, "About 400 workers, or 10 percent of the union's roughly 4,000 members, are participating".
- Court: the Incheon division of the Seoul High Court granted all nine requests in the company's injunction appeal; nine core processes may not be struck — MoneyToday, above, "granted all nine requests filed by Samsung Biologics in an injunction appeal"; Businesskorea, above, lists "flask and bioreactor culturing, harvesting, media manufacturing and supply, chromatography, virus filtration...".
- Positions: management 6.2% base pay plus one-time payment; union 9.5% and performance pay of 15% of operating profit — Businesskorea, above, "Management has proposed a 6.2% increase in base pay along with a one-time payment, while the union is demanding a 9.5% increase".
- Talks set for 3 Nov did not take place; on 2 Nov the company proposed a two-week postponement — https://news.sbs.co.kr/english/article.do?news_id=N1008826143 (published 2026-11-02T11:17+09:00, read 2026-11-06T18:26:04Z) "it has proposed to the labor union to postpone the labor-management negotiations, which were scheduled to begin on the 3rd, by two weeks." Businesskorea, above: "the two sides were unable even to set up a negotiating table." Two weeks from 3 Nov is about 17 Nov (own arithmetic).
- Company says it would resume talks "at any time" if the union stops "unilateral pressure tactics" — SBS, above.
- Union on 6 Nov: had requested further talks, no response yet; will gradually expand to production processes if the company refuses — MoneyToday, above, "The union said it had requested further negotiations with management but had yet to receive a response."
- Company on 6 Nov: no production disruption so far; contingency staffing within the law — MoneyToday, above, "There have been no production disruptions resulting from the union's strike so far."
- Company on 5 Nov: expanded strikes could make agreement harder — Korea Herald, above, "if the labor union keeps up its hardline responses such as expanding strikes, it might be difficult to conduct constructive talks".
- Since May the union has refused overtime and holiday work — Korea Times, above, "union members have continued their action by refusing overtime and holiday shifts."
- Earlier, the union postponed a planned three-week general strike from 26 Oct after the court ruling — https://news.sbs.co.kr/english/article.do?news_id=N1008819122 (published 2026-10-28T14:29+09:00, read 2026-11-06T18:26:15Z) "postponed the strike immediately following a court ruling that expanded restrictions on industrial action." That was before the start; the present strike has started.
- Union leader Park Jae-seong on 30 Oct: strike if no agreement; strike fund being formed — https://www.mt.co.kr/en/thebio/2026/10/30/2026103010473891079 (published 2026-10-30T10:56+09:00, read 2026-11-06T18:26:05Z) "We are also smoothly proceeding with forming a dispute fund for union members."
- 8 Nov is a Sunday; 7 and 8 Nov are the third and fourth calendar days of the strike.
