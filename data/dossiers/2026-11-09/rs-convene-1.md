# rs-convene-1
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Belgrade time on 8 November 2026 Speaker Ana Brnabić has convened the constitutive session of Serbia's new National Assembly for a set date.
- **Criterion:** YES if, at any time up to 23:59 Belgrade time on 8 Nov 2026, Speaker Ana Brnabić has issued the act convening the constitutive session of the National Assembly elected on 25 Oct 2026, or officially announced its date, as shown on parlament.gov.rs or reported by Tanjug, N1, Danas, RTS or BBC News na srpskom. A statement of intent without a date, or a date from unnamed parliamentary sources, does not count. NO if no date has been set by then.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-08T23:59:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-06T18:09:00Z

## Schedule
- Not scheduled. Legal limit: the new Assembly must be constituted within 30 days of the publication of final results (about 5 Dec): "počeli da teku zakonski rokovi za konstituisanje novog saziva Narodne skupštine, a to je 30 dana od objavljivanja konačnih rezultata" — https://www.tanjug.rs/srbija/politika/295575/rik-objavio-konacne-rezultate-konstitutivna-sednica-skupstine-u-narednih-30-dana/vest (published 2026-11-05T20:51+01:00, read 2026-11-06T18:00:28Z).

## Risks
- Weekend: a convening act is usually issued on a working day; Vučić's "about ten days" points to a session around mid-November, with the act possibly on Mon 9 Nov or later.
- SNS may prefer to slow the handover (Vučević's remarks), within the 30-day limit.
- Evidence: a date reported only from "sources in parliament" does not count.
