# il-letter-eisenkot-1
- **Claim:** On 8 November 2026 President Herzog formally hands Gadi Eisenkot the mandate to form Israel's next government.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 06:30 Israel time on 7 Nov and 23:59 Israel time on 8 Nov 2026 President Herzog formally assigns Gadi Eisenkot the task of forming the next government, by handing him the letter of appointment or at a tasking ceremony, as shown by the President's Residence or reported by Times of Israel, Haaretz, Ynet, Jerusalem Post or a wire agency. An announcement that Eisenkot will receive the mandate at a later date does not count. NO if no such formal assignment takes place in the window, including if the ceremony is set for 9 Nov or later.
- **Deadline:** 23:59 Israel time (Asia/Jerusalem) on 8 Nov 2026; window opens at the freeze, 2026-11-07T05:30+01:00 (06:30 Israel).
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-06T18:10Z. Read times as stated.

## Facts
- Recommendations: "Some 67 lawmakers endorsed Eisenkot, a former military chief, while 46 backed Netanyahu." — Al Jazeera, above.
- Herzog's caveat in televised remarks: "results of the consultations may appear clear", but "mutual vetoes between the blocs and within the blocs themselves" make it hard to say who is best placed to assemble a coalition — Al Jazeera, above.
- Seats: "Likud won the most seats of any single party with 27 ... his wider religious-nationalist coalition garnered only 53"; "Eisenkot's alliance of four Jewish-led parties ... won 48 seats"; "Two parties representing Israel's Palestinian minority won an unprecedented 19 seats." Eisenkot "has ruled out forming a government with the Arab-led parties. Both of those parties nonetheless recommended him" — Al Jazeera, above.
- UTJ "declined to recommend any candidate" — Al Jazeera, above. Liberman dropped his veto on haredi parties; "opposition in talks with UTJ" — https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-914006 (headline in JPost section list, read 2026-11-06T17:59Z)

- Unity push: Herzog asked Yashar and B'Yachad about a unity government with Netanyahu; B'Yachad's Merav Cohen: "You don't do business with scorpions" — JPost 913989, above. Hundreds rallied outside the President's Residence for a Netanyahu-Eisenkot unity government on 5 Nov — https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/2026-11-05/live-updates-913880 (modified 2026-11-06T09:20Z, read 2026-11-06T17:59Z) "Hundreds rally outside President's Residence, demand Netanyahu-Eisenkot unity government"
