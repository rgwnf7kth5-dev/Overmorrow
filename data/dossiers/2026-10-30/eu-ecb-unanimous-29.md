## Base rate
- Reference class: Lagarde's description of the rate decision at 2025–2026 press conferences. Unanimous 12 of 14; not unanimous 2 of 14 (Mar 2025 "consensus" with one abstention; Jun 2025 "almost unanimous" with one dissent). Both non-unanimous cases were rate cuts late in the easing cycle; all 8 holds in the sample were described as unanimous (8 of 8), as were both 2026 hikes. In all 14 she addressed the question (usually asked in the Q&A).
- Pattern: in Apr 2026 and Jul 2026 a hike was debated / favoured by some, yet the hold itself was called unanimous.
