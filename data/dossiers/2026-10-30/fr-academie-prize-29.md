# fr-academie-prize-29

- **Claim:** On 29 October 2026 the Académie française awards its Grand prix du roman 2026 to Yannick Haenel for "La solitude des professeurs est infinie" — the only book on its final shortlist that is also a Goncourt 2026 finalist.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 2026-10-28T00:00:00+01:00 and 2026-10-29T23:59:59+01:00 (Europe/Paris), the Académie française announces that the Grand prix du roman 2026 is awarded to Yannick Haenel (alone or ex aequo). NO if it is awarded to another author (Sébastien Lapaque or Marie Petitcuénot), or if no award is announced in the window.

## Facts
- Académie final shortlist (second and final selection, 3 books): Yannick Haenel, "La solitude des professeurs est infinie" (Gallimard); Sébastien Lapaque, "Le Rappel des oiseaux" (Actes Sud); Marie Petitcuénot, "Heures sauvages" (Héloïse d'Ormesson) — ActuaLitté (above) "la commission chargée du prix a retenu trois romans pour le vote final"; confirmed by the Académie's own page (above).
- First selection had twelve titles (ActuaLitté, above; franceinfo headline "douze auteurs en lice").
- Goncourt 2026 final four (announced 27 Oct): Yannick Haenel, "La solitude des professeurs est infinie" (Gallimard); Philippe Jaenada, "L'inconnue du Quai de Javel" (Flammarion); Clémentine Mélois, "Choses que je croyais perdues" (L'Arbalète/Gallimard); Sylvain Prudhomme, "De l'autre côté du lac" (Minuit); Goncourt awarded 3 Nov — La Libre https://www.lalibre.be/culture/livres-bd/2026/10/27/voici-les-quatre-ecrivains-finalistes-du-prix-goncourt-LC3TMLM7LFG2FPZLDG2RN2UV5Q/ (published 2026-10-27 14:11, read 2026-10-27T18:14Z) "l'ultime sélection du prix Goncourt, qui sera décerné le 3 novembre"
- Overlap: only Haenel appears on both lists. Jaenada, Mélois and Prudhomme are not on the Académie's final shortlist, so the original claim wording ("to one of the four Goncourt finalists") reduces to "to Haenel".
- 2025 Grand prix du roman: Yanick Lahens (ActuaLitté, above) "En 2025, le Grand Prix du roman avait été attribué à l'écrivaine haïtienne Yanick Lahens."
