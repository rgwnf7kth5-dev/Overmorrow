# de-vw-outlook-29
- **Claim:** Volkswagen Group lowers its 2026 full-year outlook for sales revenue or operating return on sales again, below the "around €315 billion" and "up to 1%" it set on 18 September. The cut must come in its nine-month 2026 report or in an ad hoc statement published by 23:59 CET on 29 October 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 CET on 28 Oct and 23:59:59 CET on 29 Oct 2026, Volkswagen AG publishes (in its Q3/9M 2026 interim report, results press release or an ad hoc announcement) a 2026 Group outlook that does either of these:
  - gives a Group sales revenue figure or range whose top is below €315bn;
  - gives an operating return on sales whose upper bound or point value is below 1.0% (for example "up to 0.5%", "around break-even" or a negative figure).
  It is also YES if VW states explicitly that it has lowered or reduced either of these two outlook items. NO if VW confirms these items, narrows them while keeping the upper bound at or above the September levels, raises them, or changes only other KPIs (net cash flow, net liquidity, investment ratio). NO if nothing is published in the window.

## Facts
- Current outlook (ad hoc, 18 Sep 2026):
  - Group sales revenue "around €315bn", the middle of the earlier range of −3% to 0% (2025: €321.9bn).
  - Operating return on sales "up to 1%", cut from 4.0–5.5% (2025: 2.8%; analysts' average expectation then 4.1%). Excluding about €10bn of special items, about 4%.
  - Automotive net cash flow (€3–6bn) and net liquidity (€32–34bn) unchanged.
  - Same ad hoc (above). pressetext copy: https://www.pressetext.com/news/20260918032 (published 2026-09-18 17:15 CEST, read 2026-10-27T18:04Z) "Operating return on sales … 'up to 1 percent'".
- Reasons given on 18 Sep:
  - Weaker market, especially in China.
  - A faster shift of demand to BEVs, hitting Audi and VW Passenger Cars.
  - A non-cash goodwill impairment of about €6bn on Porsche (from Porsche's updated long-term plan).
  - About €2bn more for restructuring (wider early-retirement schemes, the planned sale of Volkswagen Osnabrück GmbH) and write-downs at Chinese companies.
  - Same ad hoc (above).
- Assumptions in the outlook: current tariffs persist. Possible effects of a Middle East escalation and the planned Everllence disposal are excluded — pressetext (above) "The forecast assumes current trade tariffs persist."
- Pre-close call, 13 Oct 2026: about €9bn of the ~€10bn special items fall in H2, including about €8bn in Q3. The outlook "reflects the current tariff situation and includes the third Rivian payment" — https://quartr.com/events/volkswagen-ag-vow-pre-close-call-presentation_3etnNh0n (read 2026-10-27T18:06Z).
- Q3 deliveries: 1.89m, down 14.3% y/y. 9M: 6.01m, down 9%. China Q3: 405,800, down 38.5%. North America: down 5.8%. Western Europe: down 2.8% — RTTNews https://www.rttnews.com/3698112/volkswagen-q3-deliveries-fall-14-3.aspx (published 2026-10-12 06:18 ET, read 2026-10-27T18:06Z) "1.89 million vehicles, down 14.3%".
- H1 2026 (reported 24 Jul):
  - revenue €158.1bn (−0.2%);
  - operating result €5.9bn (−11.6%);
  - margin 3.8% (4.3% before special items);
  - net result €3.1bn (−30.7%).
  - Source: Admiral Markets (ES), https://admiralmarkets.com/es/education/articles/shares/pronostico-acciones-volkswagen (published 2026-10-14, read 2026-10-27T18:07Z). It is a secondary source; VW's own H1 report was not read.
- Implied H2 revenue (own arithmetic): €315bn − €158.1bn ≈ €156.9bn. That is about €2.6bn per half below H1, with Q3 deliveries down 14.3% y/y.
- Tariffs: EU cars face a 15% US tariff under the 2025 framework. In early May 2026 Trump said he planned to raise it to 25% under Section 232; whether this took effect was not confirmed — Admiral Markets (ES), above.
- Consensus (MarketScreener, date of estimates not shown):
  - Q3 2026 revenue €79.8bn, EBIT €3.39bn;
  - FY2026 revenue €320.0bn, EBIT €13.66bn.
  - The FY revenue figure is above VW's "around €315bn", so the estimates may predate the 18 Sep warning. TipRanks and digrin give €77.4bn and €88.4bn for Q3 (agenda note), so the aggregators disagree.

## Base rate
- Reference class: a large European carmaker cutting its full-year guidance again at the next quarterly report, within six weeks after an ad hoc profit warning. No count in pipeline/reference-data/. Stated estimate: second cuts at the next report are uncommon, because under MAR an issuer must publish a material deviation ad hoc as soon as it is known. A further cut would therefore more likely come as a separate ad hoc (possibly on the evening before results) than appear for the first time in the report. No n computed.

## Risks
- If VW publishes an ad hoc cut before 00:00 CET on 28 Oct (for example on the evening of 27 Oct), the claim is NO, even though the story would be about a cut.
- Revenue: Q3 deliveries −14.3% and China −38.5% put pressure on "around €315bn". VW could restate this as "around €310bn" (YES) or keep it.
- Margin: "up to 1%" is an upper bound. Further special items (Osnabrück sale, China write-downs, possible extra Porsche charges, Section 232 tariff increase) could push it to "around 0%" or negative (YES). VW could instead narrow it to "0–1%", which is NO.
- Wording ambiguity: VW may express the outlook "before special items" only. The criterion uses the reported (incl. special items) operating return on sales as in the September ad hoc.
