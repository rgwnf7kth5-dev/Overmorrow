# ua-oleshky-aid-29

- **Claim:** By 23:59 Kyiv time on 29 October 2026, a humanitarian aid convoy or mission delivering aid reaches the Russian-occupied town of Oleshky (Kherson region) under the local ceasefire.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 Kyiv time on 28 Oct and 23:59:59 Kyiv time on 29 Oct 2026 (UTC+2), a convoy or team carrying humanitarian aid (food, water, medicines, medical staff or equipment) enters Oleshky, as confirmed by at least one of the following:
  - a Ukrainian official source: the President or his Office, ombudsman Dmytro Lubinets, the Coordination HQ for POWs, the General Staff or the Kherson regional military administration;
  - a Russian official source: the Kremlin, the Defence Ministry/General Staff, ombudswoman Yana Lantratova or the Russian-installed Kherson administration;
  - the White House or the office of the First Lady (Melania Trump, adviser Mark Beckman);
  - the ICRC or a UN agency;
  - two independent major news outlets (e.g. AP, Reuters, AFP, CNN, BBC, Kyiv Independent, Ukrainska Pravda, Meduza, TASS) reporting the entry as fact.

  Evacuation of civilians out of the town without an aid delivery in does not count; demining of the access route alone does not count. Confirmation may come after the window, but the entry must take place within it. NO if no aid reaches Oleshky in the window, including if the mission is postponed, suspended or called off.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-28T00:00:00+02:00 to 2026-10-29T23:59:59+02:00 (Europe/Kyiv)
- **Deadline:** 2026-10-29T23:59:59+02:00; resolves by 2026-10-31T05:00:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-27T18:30:00Z

## Facts
- **Duration of operation:** Mark Beckman, adviser to Melania Trump: "It will take five to seven days to carry out all the planned activities as part of the humanitarian mission in Oleshky." He gave no guarantee that hostilities would stop for the whole operation — https://news.liga.net/en/war/news/a-ceasefire-has-begun-in-the-oleshky-area-melania-trumps-adviser-said-how-long-the-mission-will-last (published 2026-10-27 09:13 Kyiv, read 2026-10-27T18:01Z) "five to seven days"
- **Coordination:** Lubinets travelled to the area and "will coordinate the process"; Budanov: "We are in constant contact with him." Newsmax to send "a neutral media team" to document the process; Russia assured residents' choice of evacuation destination would be respected — Liga.net (as above).
- **Sequence:** three stages: ceasefire → delivery of humanitarian aid → evacuation of civilians who want to leave; mines to be cleared on routes into the city before aid moves (Lubinets) — ABC/AP (as above) "Mines will be cleared on the routes into the city before aid moves"
- **Russian side:** Gerasimov: Russian forces "will cease fire on the Kherson axis" from 04:00 Moscow time on 27 Oct; Lantratova: anyone wanting to leave "will be guaranteed safe passage"; Peskov: evacuation would likely take "several days" — ABC/AP (as above).
- **Lantratova (27 Oct):** "It is very important that the agreements reached continue to be observed." — CNN via KESQ, https://kesq.com/news/national-world/cnn-world/2026/10/27/melania-trump-brokered-ceasefire-takes-effect-around-russian-occupied-city-of-oleshky/ (published 2026-10-27 08:25, read 2026-10-27T18:03Z)
- **Truce end:** "expected to last until November 1", per MP Davyd Arakhamia, who called it "the most extensive ceasefire to date… along the largest section of the front line in southern Ukraine" — CNN/KESQ (as above).
- **Population (estimates differ):** about 1,700 remain (Ukrainian authorities via CNN/KESQ); up to 2,000 (UN via AP/KY3); about 6,000 in Oleshky and nearby settlements incl. up to 200 children (Lubinets via Liga.net).
- **Operator not named:** none of AP/ABC, CNN/KESQ, Liga.net, Interfax-Ukraine names the ICRC, UN or another body as running the convoy. In Sept 2026 Lubinets said the ICRC was ready to take part in an earlier version of the evacuation (Censor.net, https://censor.net/en/news/4023781/lubinets-russia-has-not-confirmed-the-evacuation-of-people-from-the-occupied-town-of-oleshky, published 2026-09-16 18:38; from the 28 Oct dossier, not re-opened).
- **Earlier failure:** the September 2026 Oleshky evacuation plan collapsed after "the Russian side has for some time now failed to confirm its readiness" (Censor.net, as above).
- **Context:** Melania Trump said Oleshky is "on the brink of disaster" (ABC/AP). Overnight 26–27 Oct Russia struck Kyiv with 16 ballistic missiles and 280+ drones (running.json desk sweep), outside the truce zone.

## Risks
- Demining takes longer, pushing the first convoy to 30–31 Oct (truce runs to 1 Nov, so there is room to slip).
- A truce violation (shelling, drone strike) leads either side to pause the mission.
- Russia stalls on confirmation as in September 2026.
- Ambiguity: an evacuation that starts before aid, or aid handed over at a transfer point outside the town, would not count.
- Reporting lag: Lubinets usually posts on Telegram the same day, so lag risk is low; confirmation after midnight still counts if entry was in the window.
