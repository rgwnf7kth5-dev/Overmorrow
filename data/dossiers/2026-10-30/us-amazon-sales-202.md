# us-amazon-sales-202
- **Claim:** Amazon reports net sales above $202.0 billion, the top of its own guidance range, for the third quarter of 2026 in the results it publishes on 29 October 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if Amazon.com's earnings press release for the quarter ended 30 September 2026, published between 00:00 on 28 Oct and 23:59:59 New York time (UTC−4) on 29 Oct 2026, reports total net sales strictly greater than $202,000 million (the consolidated statements figure in millions; a headline "$202.0 billion" that rounds a figure at or below $202,000 million is NO). Sources: aboutamazon.com / ir.aboutamazon.com or Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 on SEC EDGAR. NO if net sales are $202,000 million or less, or if Amazon does not publish its Q3 results by 23:59:59 New York time on 29 Oct 2026.

## Facts
- Q3 2026 guidance (Q2 release, 30 Jul 2026): net sales $197.0–202.0bn (+9% to +12% y/y), assuming about an 80 bp unfavourable FX impact. Operating income $22.5–26.5bn — https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-earnings-q2-2026-report (published 2026-07-30, read 2026-10-27T17:58Z) "Net sales are expected to be between $197.0 billion and $202.0 billion, or to grow between 9% and 12% compared with third quarter 2025."
- Prime Day timing distorts the year-on-year comparison. Same release: "Excluding the impact of Prime Day in both 2025 and 2026, third quarter 2026 year-over-year growth would be nearly 400 basis points higher." So Prime Day 2026 fell in Q2 and Prime Day 2025 in Q3.
- Year-ago base: Q3 2025 net sales were $180.2bn — https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-earnings-q3-2025-report (read 2026-10-27T17:59Z) "Net sales increased 13% to $180.2 billion in the third quarter". $202.0bn would be +12.1% y/y.
- Prior quarter: Q2 2026 net sales were $200.6bn (+20% y/y), against guidance of $194.0–199.0bn. AWS sales rose 37%, and operating income was $27.5bn — Q2 2026 release (above) "Net sales increased 20% to $200.6 billion in the second quarter".
- Revenue consensus $202.16bn, EPS $1.96 (MarketBeat, 20 Oct). Barchart has EPS $2.02 from 56 analysts (13 Oct). Both are cited by primexbt — https://primexbt.com/for-traders/amazon-earnings-date/ (read 2026-10-27T17:55Z) "Revenue: $202.16bn (MarketBeat, Oct 20, 2026)".
- Pepperstone preview, 23 Oct: revenue consensus about $202bn, "just above the top of Amazon's guidance range". EPS $1.96. AWS growth expected around 35%, after 37% in Q2. Capex plan for 2026 is about $220bn — https://pepperstone.com/en/insights/market-analysis/equities/amazon-q3-2026-earnings-preview (published 2026-10-23, read 2026-10-27T18:07Z).
- Amazon's record of actual net sales against the top of its own guidance, for the last 8 quarters. Guidance is from each prior release; actuals are first-reported figures from the results releases or 8-K Exhibit 99.1 (read 2026-10-27T17:58–18:03Z):
  - Q3 2024: guidance $154.0–158.5bn, actual $158.9bn, **+0.4bn above the top** (SEC 8-K ex. 99.1 of 1 Aug 2024 and 31 Oct 2024)
  - Q4 2024: guidance $181.5–188.5bn, actual $187.8bn, **−0.7bn below the top** (8-K 31 Oct 2024; 8-K 6 Feb 2025)
  - Q1 2025: guidance $151.0–155.5bn, actual $155.7bn, **+0.2bn** (8-K 6 Feb 2025; 8-K 1 May 2025)
  - Q2 2025: guidance $159.0–164.0bn, actual $167.7bn, **+3.7bn** (8-K 1 May 2025; aboutamazon Q2 2025 release)
  - Q3 2025: guidance $174.0–179.5bn, actual $180.2bn, **+0.7bn** (aboutamazon Q2 and Q3 2025 releases)
  - Q4 2025: guidance $206.0–213.0bn, actual $213.4bn, **+0.4bn** (aboutamazon Q3 and Q4 2025 releases)
  - Q1 2026: guidance $173.5–178.5bn, actual $181.5bn, **+3.0bn** (aboutamazon Q4 2025 and Q1 2026 releases)
  - Q2 2026: guidance $194.0–199.0bn, actual $200.6bn, **+1.6bn** (aboutamazon Q1 and Q2 2026 releases)
- Summary (own arithmetic): actual exceeded the top of guidance in 7 of 8 quarters. Excess over the top: mean +$1.16bn, median +$0.55bn, range −$0.7bn to +$3.7bn. Three of the eight margins (+0.2, +0.4, +0.4) were within $0.5bn of the top.
- Q4 2025 net sales of $213.4bn included a $1.5bn favourable FX effect; Q1 2026 had $2.8bn and Q2 2026 $0.1bn of favourable FX — releases above. This matters because the Q3 guidance assumed an 80 bp FX headwind (about $1.6bn on a $200bn base).

## Risks
- The threshold sits exactly at the top of guidance and about $0.16bn below the MarketBeat consensus. A "beat" of the consensus by a small amount still counts. A result between $200bn and $202bn (above the midpoint but below the top) is NO. In Q4 2024 Amazon missed the top by $0.7bn.
- Prime Day shifted into Q2 2026, which makes Q3 2026 an unusual comparison. Guidance already includes this, but the size of the effect is uncertain.
- FX: guidance assumed about an 80 bp headwind. If the dollar was stronger than assumed in Jul–Sep 2026, reported sales would be trimmed.
- Consumer demand amid high oil prices or fuel costs and the US–Iran war; memory-chip cost pressures (mentioned in the guidance caveats); tariffs.
- Rounding: the press-release headline rounds to $0.1bn. The criterion uses the figure in millions.
