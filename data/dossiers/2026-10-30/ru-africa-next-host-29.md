# ru-africa-next-host-29

- **Claim:** By 23:59 Moscow time on 29 October 2026, the host country of the next (fourth) Russia–Africa summit is officially announced.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 Moscow time on 28 Oct and 23:59:59 Moscow time on 29 Oct 2026 (UTC+3), the host country (or host city) of the next Russia–Africa summit is named in at least one of: a statement on record by Putin, Peskov, Ushakov or Lavrov; a kremlin.ru publication; the summit's adopted final declaration or other adopted summit document; or an on-record statement at the summit by the co-chair, AU chair and Burundian President Évariste Ndayishimiye. The statement must say the summit will be held in that country (an agreed decision), not merely that a country has offered or is willing to host. "On the African continent", "in Africa" or a year without a named country does not count. NO if no host country is officially named in the window.

## Facts
- **Attendance:** Ushakov: "48 African countries - out of the 54 recognized by the UN - have confirmed their participation in this third summit"; "Twenty-two countries will be represented by their heads of state"; the previous summit drew 48 countries incl. 17 heads of state — TASS, https://tass.com/politics/2208085 (dated 2026-10-26, read 2026-10-27T18:06Z)
- **Bilaterals:** Ushakov: Putin to hold 24 bilateral meetings (Sputnik, via 29 Oct dossier); 28 Oct: el-Sisi, Abiy Ahmed, Sassou Nguesso; 29 Oct: Madagascar, Zimbabwe, Namibia, Sudan, AU chair (TASS 2208229/2208197/2208119 via 29 Oct dossier).
- **History of the third summit's venue:** in Feb 2024 Vsevolod Tkachenko (MFA Africa Department) said the 2026 summit "will be hosted by an African country" — TASS, https://tass.com/politics/1745605/amp (published 2024-02-13 13:29 MSK, read 2026-10-27T18:04Z). Earlier Partnership Forum communiqués and the 2024 ministerial conference said it would be on the continent (NewsGhana, below). On 8 April 2026 Lavrov said it "is to take place in Moscow at the end of October this year"; the article gives no reason for the move — TASS, https://tass.com/politics/2113697 (published 2026-04-08, read 2026-10-27T18:04Z); NewsGhana, https://www.newsghana.com.gh/third-russia-africa-summit-moved-to-moscow-scheduled-for-late-october/ (undated, ~April 2026, read 2026-10-27T17:58Z) "a shift from earlier indications".
- **Offers to host:** Equatorial Guinea's President Obiang told the State Duma in Sept 2024 that Malabo "is ready to host the Russia-Africa summit" — TASS, https://tass.com/world/1848449/amp (published 2024-09-26 23:43, read 2026-10-27T17:58Z). No other formal bid found in this run.
- **Precedents:** 2019 Sochi (23–24 Oct, co-chaired by Putin and el-Sisi, 43 heads of state or government); the second summit was first scheduled for Oct 2022 in Addis Ababa, then held 26–29 July 2023 in St Petersburg — Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Russia%E2%80%93Africa_Summit (read 2026-10-27T18:05Z). 2023 St Petersburg: 17 heads of state, 49 delegations; ended with a final declaration; Wikipedia names no venue for the next summit — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Russia%E2%80%93Africa_Summit (read 2026-10-27T18:05Z). When the Addis Ababa venue for 2022 was first announced was not established in this run.
- **Cycle:** summits in 2019, 2023, 2026 and an action plan for 2027–2029 (Lavrov, TASS 2113697) imply a next summit around 2029; no official statement on its year was found.

## Risks
- **Toward YES:** the final declaration may state the next summit's venue (African co-hosting would be a natural "return" after the 2026 switch to Moscow); Putin or Ndayishimiye may announce it at the closing press conference on 29 Oct; Equatorial Guinea or another state may be confirmed.
- **Toward NO:** the declaration may say only "in 2029" or "on the African continent" (does not count); an African leader saying his country "offers" to host does not count; the declaration text may be published only on kremlin.ru late on 29 Oct or on 30 Oct (the document must be adopted in the window; publication lag is fine if it is adopted by 23:59 on 29 Oct).
- **Ambiguity:** "the next summit will be held in Africa, possibly in X" — counts only if X is stated as agreed.
