# cz-sfa-chair-named-29
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Prague time on 29 October 2026, the Ministry of Culture or the State Audiovisual Fund (Státní fond audiovize, SFA) publicly names a new chair or an acting chair of the fund's board to replace Petr Dvořák.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 on 28 Oct 2026 and 23:59:59 CET on 29 Oct 2026, the Ministry of Culture, Minister Oto Klempíř, the SFA or its board publicly names a specific person as the new chair of the SFA board (představenstvo) or as its acting or interim chair. This includes the board electing a chair, and a statement that a named vice-chair now leads the board. It must be reported by ČTK, ČT24, iROZHLAS, MediaGuru, Lupa, Médiář or another major Czech outlet, or published on mk.gov.cz or sfa.gov.cz. Naming only a new ministry representative on the board, without saying that person will chair it, does not count. NO if no person is named as chair or acting chair in the window.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-28T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-10-29T23:59:59+01:00
- **Deadline:** 2026-10-29T23:59:59+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-27T18:30:00Z

## Facts
- 27 Oct: the Ministry of Culture removes Dvořák "z čela představenstva". Klempíř gave the reason: "rozdílný pohled na hospodaření fondu a nedostatečná součinnost při hledání úspor". No successor was named. — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/klempir-odvola-exreditele-ct-dvoraka-z-cela-predstavenstva-fondu-audiovize/2893722 (published 2026-10-27 13:06, read 2026-10-27T17:40Z); iROZHLAS/ČTK https://www.irozhlas.cz/zpravy-domov/ministerstvo-kultury-odvola-byvaleho-reditele-ceske-televize-z-predstavenstva_2610271257_adn (published 12:57, updated 13:09, read 2026-10-27T18:03Z) "Připravujeme podrobnosti."
- Mechanism (Médiář): Dvořák sat on the board as the ministry's representative, and the minister may recall that representative without giving a reason. The board elected him chair at its constituent meeting, in the first round, as the only candidate. The fund's leadership learned of the removal from the media. — Médiář (above). The fetched summary gave the constituent meeting as June 2026; ČTK and Lupa give June 2025.
- Board: 15 members, appointed by then-minister Baxa on 10 Jun 2025. They include Badač, Brachtlová (CME), Čech (MPA), Danielis, Dvořák, Kocman (ČAEK), Kolář, Kuchyňka, Režová, Rečka (APK), Švecová Lamperová, Schmarc (FAMU), Singer (Prima/AKTV), Šlajer (APA) and Vyhnisová (MF). — Lupa https://www.lupa.cz/aktuality/v-predstavenstvu-audiovizualniho-fondu-bude-sef-primy-i-byvaly-reditel-ct/ (published 2025-06-10, read 2026-10-27T17:50Z). Who elects or appoints the chair is not stated in the sources read. The statute was not checked.
- Dispute: the cabinet did not approve the fund's 2027 budget last week. Klempíř wants about CZK 400m (51%) less support in 2027, down to 387.5m. All 15 board members refused and kept CZK 787.5m. — MediaGuru https://www.mediaguru.cz/fond-audiovize-odmita-snizeni-podpory-obor-varuje-pred-dopady-skrtu (published 2026-10-23 20:05, read 2026-10-27T17:55Z) "Všech 15 členů rady Státního fondu audiovize odmítlo návrh ministra kultury"
- Industry: 14 organisations led by APA (with UFP, ČFTA, FAMU, Herecká asociace, GDA and others) signed a joint statement against the cuts, the law changes and the personnel restructuring. — MediaGuru (above); Médiář (above)
- Dvořák: "Nejsem jím zaskočen ani překvapen". The minister told him by phone. He called the ministry's budget draft "zbrklý a nediskutovaný". — eXtra.cz https://www.extra.cz/klempir-si-vyrizuje-ucty-s-dvorakem-exreditele-ct-vyrazil-ze-statniho-fondu-audiovize-padala-ostra-slova-76016 (published 2026-10-27 16:59, read 2026-10-27T18:03Z)
- Baxa (ODS) called the removal a "personální čistka". — Médiář (above)
- Klempíř: already allocated incentives "nejsou ohroženy". — eXtra.cz (above)

## Risks
- YES paths: the ministry names a new representative and announces that person as its candidate for chair. Or the fund issues a statement that a vice-chair leads the board in the interim. Or the board meets at short notice, for example before the 2 Nov cabinet budget decision.
- NO paths: the holiday, no board meeting scheduled, and the fund's leadership learned of the removal from the media. The ministry has said nothing about a successor.
- Resolution: a ministry statement naming only a new board member, not a chair, is NO. A mention that a vice-chair "temporarily directs" the board counts if the person is named.
