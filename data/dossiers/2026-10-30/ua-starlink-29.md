# ua-starlink-29

- **Claim:** By 23:59 Kyiv time on 29 October 2026, a Ukrainian official source publicly reports that Starlink or Starshield service for Ukraine's forces has again been restricted, throttled or cut after the restoration of 24 October.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 Kyiv time on 28 Oct and 23:59:59 Kyiv time on 29 Oct 2026 (UTC+2), one of the following publicly states that Starlink or Starshield service used by Ukrainian forces has again been restricted, throttled or cut, in any part of the front, after its restoration on 24 Oct: the President or his Office; the Defence Ministry (minister or deputy ministers); the Ministry of Digital Transformation; the General Staff; a presidential adviser in an official capacity (e.g. Serhii Beskrestnov). Statements about the 14–24 Oct throttling, about the pre-existing speed caps (70–90 km/h for commercial terminals) or whitelists in force since February, or about Russian jamming do not count. NO if no such statement is made in the window.
