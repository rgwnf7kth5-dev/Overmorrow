## Risks
- **Threshold effects:** if lists in the opposition camp (Reservists–NEP, Amcha Yisrael, or an Arab list) fall below 3.25%, wasted votes raise the seats per vote of the others, as in 2022 when Meretz and Balad failing helped the bloc reach 64.
- **Bloc definition:** UTJ is not formally in the coalition since 2025, and Noam is a separate list; the criterion fixes the list set explicitly.
- **Seats vs votes at a moment:** seat allocation from a partial count can shift by 1–2 seats as double envelopes are added (2022: projection 65 at 86%, final 64).
- **Evidence:** CEC 2026 results URL unknown; if the CEC site does not show seat allocations, media reports of seats "based on the CEC count" with a time are needed.
- **Disruption:** an attack or security incident could delay counting (pending, not NO).
