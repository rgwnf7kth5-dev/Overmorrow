# cz-fee-committee-resolution-29
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Prague time on 29 October 2026, the Chamber of Deputies' media committee adopts a resolution recommending that the Chamber approve (with or without amendments) or reject the bill abolishing the ČT and ČRo licence fees (sněmovní tisk 231).
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 on 28 Oct 2026 and 23:59:59 CET on 29 Oct 2026, the Chamber's media committee (chaired by František Talíř, KDU-ČSL) adopts a resolution on tisk 231 that recommends the Chamber approve the bill, approve it with amendments, or reject it. This is the resolution that concludes the committee's deliberation of the bill. Evidence is the resolution published on psp.cz, or a report by ČTK, ČT24, iROZHLAS, Seznam Zprávy or another major Czech outlet. These do not count: a resolution that adjourns or interrupts the deliberation, a resolution that only takes note or calls on the government or minister (for example to withdraw the bill), a resolution that sets up a hearing or a timetable, and a motion that is put to a vote and fails. NO if no such resolution is adopted in the window, including when the session is cancelled or ends without one.
