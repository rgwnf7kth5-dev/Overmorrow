# us-gdp-q3
- **Claim:** The BEA advance estimate of US real GDP for the third quarter of 2026 (29 Oct 2026, 08:30 EDT) puts growth at … per cent, seasonally adjusted annual rate.
- **Criterion:** Value = the headline percent change in real GDP, Q3 2026 vs Q2 2026, seasonally adjusted at annual rates, as printed (one decimal) in the BEA news release "Gross Domestic Product, 3rd Quarter 2026 (Advance Estimate)" published on 29 Oct 2026. The second/third estimates, GDI, real final sales and later revisions do not decide. Void if the release does not take place on 29 Oct 2026 (New York time); pending if published but not yet readable.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-29T00:00:00-04:00 to 2026-10-29T23:59:59-04:00 (release slot 08:30 EDT = 13:30 CET)
- **Deadline:** 2026-10-29T23:59:59-04:00

## Risks
- Net exports and inventories swing the advance estimate (large offsetting contributions are typical); trade and inventory source data for September arrive late.
- The 30 Sep annual update reset levels and seasonal factors; consensus models may lag.
- Headline confusion: media may quote real final sales, GDI or the price index; the criterion uses headline real GDP saar only.
