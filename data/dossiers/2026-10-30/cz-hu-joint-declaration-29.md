# cz-hu-joint-declaration-29
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Prague time on 29 October 2026, Czech PM Andrej Babiš and Hungarian PM Péter Magyar sign a joint declaration, memorandum or other joint document of the two governments at the first Czech–Hungarian intergovernmental consultations in Prague.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 on 28 Oct 2026 and 23:59:59 CET on 29 Oct 2026, Babiš and Magyar both sign, or jointly adopt and publish, at least one written joint document. This can be a joint declaration or statement, a memorandum of understanding or cooperation, or an intergovernmental agreement. It must be reported by vlada.gov.cz, the Hungarian government (kormany.hu), ČTK, ČT24, MTI or another major outlet. Agreements signed only by ministers do not count, nor do oral statements of agreement at the press conference. NO if no such document is signed or adopted by the two PMs in the window, including when the consultations are cancelled or postponed.

- NO paths: the first consultations end with oral statements only, or with ministerial MoUs (for example transport or health) but nothing signed by the PMs. Differences over Ukraine or EU-budget conditionality could keep a joint text from being agreed. Magyar's government is aligning with the EU mainstream, while Babiš is close to Fico.
- YES paths: a "joint declaration" or "roadmap" is the usual deliverable of a first-ever joint government session. Signings are often added to the programme at short notice or held just before the press conference without a separate programme slot.
