# rs-student-pm-named-29

- **Claim:** By 23:59 Belgrade time on 29 October 2026, Serbia's Student List publicly names one person as its candidate for prime minister.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 2026-10-28T00:00:00+01:00 and 2026-10-29T23:59:59+01:00 (Europe/Belgrade), the Student List ("Studentska lista – Studenti pobeđuju") publicly announces one named person as the list's candidate for prime minister (mandatar/premijer). The announcement must come from the list's official spokespeople or leadership, or from the coordinating body of the student movement, and be reported by its official channels or by N1, RTS, RFE/RL, Vreme, Danas, AP or Reuters. Media speculation, "possible" names, several names, or a statement that the list will decide later do not count. NO if no single named candidate is announced in the window.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-28T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-10-29T23:59:59+01:00
- **Deadline / resolves by:** 2026-10-30T09:00:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-27T18:12:00Z

## Schedule
- No scheduled step. The list can name a candidate at any time. The formal sequence is: RIK final results, then the National Assembly convenes within 30 days, then the acting president consults and proposes a PM, then a 90-day window to elect a government (tportal, below; Rio Times carried over in the 29 Oct dossier).
- Srdanović (list head) to RFE/RL describes the order as: RIK proclaims results, then the new assembly and its bodies are formed, then a plan for forming the government. He gives no date.
- Precedents for slow RIK final results: 2023 election final report on 12 Jan 2024; 2022 final results more than three months later (both carried over from data/dossiers/2026-10-29/rs-student-pm-named-28.md).

Own count carried over (not re-verified): new majorities after a change of power in ex-Yugoslav states without a pre-named PM — Serbia 2012 (Dačić agreed ~3 weeks after the vote), Montenegro 2020 (Krivokapić ~9 days after) — 0 of 2 named within 4 days of the vote. 29 Oct is day 4 after the 25 Oct vote.

## Risks
- Toward YES: a 140-seat majority gives an incentive to pre-empt Brnabić's consultations; Šoškić and Srdanović already circulate; one press conference is enough.
- Toward NO: Srdanović explicitly sequences the choice after RIK results and assembly formation; leaderless, collective decision-making; RIK final results possibly delayed by repeat votes.
- Ambiguity: "team" announcements, conditional nominations, or names leaked by unnamed sources do not count.
- Overlap: rs-student-pm-named-28 (29 Oct edition) covers through 28 Oct 23:59. A naming on 28 Oct resolves both YES. If a name is announced before this edition's freeze, replace the claim.
