# de-aigner-nominated-10
- **Claim:** Between 06:00 on 8 November and 23:59 on 9 November 2026 (Berlin time), the CDU leadership formally nominates Ilse Aigner (CSU) as the Union's candidate for federal president.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 on 8 Nov and 23:59 Berlin time on 9 Nov 2026, the CDU Präsidium or the CDU Bundesvorstand decides to propose Ilse Aigner as candidate for the office of Bundespräsident (in a vote or by consensus), or CDU leader Friedrich Merz officially announces on behalf of the CDU (alone or jointly with the CSU) that Aigner is the party's candidate, as published by the CDU or reported by tagesschau.de, ZDF, Spiegel, FAZ, SZ or dpa. Reports citing party circles or unnamed sources do not count. NO if no such formal decision or official announcement is made in the window, including if it is postponed or another person is nominated.

## Risks
- A last-minute hitch (Aigner declining, a CDU rival push such as Prien or Klöckner, SPD/Green talks) could delay the formal decision.
- The announcement could be made jointly by CDU and CSU without a formal Vorstand vote; the criterion counts an official announcement by Merz on behalf of the CDU.
- If Merz announces only "the bodies will decide later this week", NO.
