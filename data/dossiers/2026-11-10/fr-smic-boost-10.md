# fr-smic-boost-10
- **Claim:** Between 06:00 on 8 November and 23:59 on 9 November 2026 (Paris time), Prime Minister Lecornu or a member of his government announces that the Smic will be raised beyond the statutory automatic revaluation, either by a "coup de pouce" or by bringing a revaluation forward to before 1 January 2027.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 on 8 Nov and 23:59 Paris time on 9 Nov 2026, Sébastien Lecornu, Matignon or a member of the government states publicly, on the record, that the government will raise the Smic (or has decided to raise it) in a way that goes beyond the automatic legal revaluation of 1 January 2027: either an increase above the legal formula ("coup de pouce", with or without a figure) or an increase that takes effect before 1 January 2027, as reported by AFP, Le Monde, Le Figaro, Les Echos, franceinfo or BFMTV, or published by Matignon or the labour ministry. Statements that a rise is being considered, is under discussion, or depends on further negotiation do not count; nor does a figure attributed only to unnamed sources. NO if no such announcement is made in the window, including if the conference is postponed.
