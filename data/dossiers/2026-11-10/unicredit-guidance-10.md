# unicredit-guidance-10
- **Claim:** UniCredit raises its 2026 net profit guidance when it publishes its third-quarter results, between 06:00 on 8 November and 23:59 on 9 November 2026 (Milan time).
- **Criterion:** YES if UniCredit's results press release or results presentation for 3Q26/9M26, published between 2026-11-08T06:00 and 2026-11-09T23:59 Europe/Rome, either (a) says its FY2026 net profit guidance or outlook is raised, upgraded, increased or improved, or (b) states a FY2026 net profit figure (point, floor or range) higher than the one given with the 2Q26 results on 23 July 2026 (reported as "well above €11bn"). A higher figure counts whatever its stated reason, including the consolidation of Commerzbank. NO if UniCredit confirms or repeats the July figure, lowers it, gives no FY2026 net profit figure, or does not publish the results in the window. Deciding source: UniCredit's Group Results page and press release (unicreditgroup.eu); Reuters, Bloomberg, ANSA or Il Sole 24 Ore reports quoting the release are acceptable.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-08T06:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-09T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Rome). The board approves the results on Sunday 8 Nov, so publication may come on Sunday evening or at 07:00 Monday.
