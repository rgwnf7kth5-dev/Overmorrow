# cz-media-model-10
- **Claim:** Between 06:00 on 8 November and 23:59 on 9 November 2026, Andrej Babiš, Tomio Okamura or Petr Macinka publicly announces that the coalition of ANO, SPD and Motoristé has agreed a model for funding Czech Television (ČT) and Czech Radio (ČRo) after the licence fees are abolished, and names its funding mechanism.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 on 8 Nov and 23:59 Prague time on 9 Nov 2026, Babiš, Okamura or Macinka says publicly (press conference, statement to media, TV or radio, his own social-media post) that the coalition, the coalition council or the three party leaders have agreed (shodli se, dohodli se) on how ČT and ČRo will be funded once the fees end, AND names at least one funding mechanism of the agreed model. Mechanisms that count include: a fixed contribution from the state budget with a stated indexation rule (for example indexation at 6% cumulative inflation); a share of VAT or of other tax revenue set in law or by a coefficient; a base sum from the budget plus a voluntary fee; an earmarked ("Finnish") tax; or the government bill's existing mechanism (6.8 bn CZK from the budget, indexation at 10%) confirmed as the agreed final model. NO if none of the three says so in the window; if they say talks continue, that the model will be settled later or at the second reading, or that variants will be prepared or worked out by the finance ministry; or if they announce agreement only on principles (stable, predictable or multi-year funding, abolition of the fee) without naming the mechanism. Statements by other people (ministers, MPs, spokespeople) do not count unless they quote one of the three. As reported by ČTK, ČT24, Novinky, Seznam Zprávy, iDNES, Aktuálně.cz, Deník N or iROZHLAS, or as seen in the post or broadcast itself.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-08T06:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-09T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Prague). The decisive occasion is the coalition council on Monday evening, 9 Nov.
- **Written at:** 2026-11-07T18:05Z

## Facts
- Okamura's red lines: abolition of the fees and no tax increase for citizens — ČTK 2898304 (above) "Pro nás nepřipadá v úvahu žádný návrh, kterým by se zvyšovaly daně českým občanům."
- Okamura's own idea: a base sum from the budget plus a voluntary fee at today's level — ČTK 2898304 "Jestli třeba nějaký základ bude z rozpočtu plus to, že kdo bude chtít platit současnou výši koncesionářského poplatku dobrovolně, tak ať si to třeba zaplatí".
- Okamura has no idea yet of the tax-share coefficient — ČTK 2898304 "Okamura na dotaz ČTK uvedl, že zatím nemá představu o výši koeficientu."
- Okamura: any change will be made at the bill's second reading — ČTK 2898304 "Případná změna návrhu bude podle Okamury možná při druhém čtení zákona."
- Culture minister Klempíř (Motoristé) calls the 6.8 bn "fairly decent"; options are budget, index, Finnish model or taxes; "we stay on our positions" — ČTK 2898304 "zda budou média financována z rozpočtu, indexem, finským modelem (účelová daň) nebo z daní. ... my zatím zůstáváme na našich pozicích".
- Macinka (Motoristé leader): the fee debate belongs to the second reading, i.e. next year — ČTK 2898304 "bude debata o poplatcích při druhém čtení zákona o změně financování, tedy až příští rok".
- Babiš (5 Nov): fixed money for the whole term; coalition has not discussed a concrete model; changes at the second reading in February, conditional on partners' consent; indexation threshold could fall from 10% to 6% — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/babis-ct-a-cro-budou-mit-stabilni-a-predvidatelne-financovani/2898039 (published 2026-11-05 19:25 CET, read 2026-11-07T17:54Z) "Konkrétní model koalice ale ještě neprojednala." / "Je otázka, jaký model nakonec najdeme".
- Novinky reported a variant in which ČT and ČRo are paid a share of collected taxes by a coefficient — ČTK 2898039 "místo příspěvku ze státního rozpočtu by média mohla být placena podílem z vybraných daní podle stanoveného koeficientu".
- Babiš at end of October: finance ministry preparing a model where money would not go directly from the budget; fees could stay on a voluntary basis — ČTK 2898039 "Televizní a rozhlasové poplatky by podle něj zůstaly zachovány na dobrovolné bázi."

## Risks
- The leaders have said publicly that changes come at the second reading in February; the council may only agree a direction ("we'll look at a VAT share"), and the finance ministry may be told to draft options. Under the criterion this is NO.
- The council may agree only on a principle (fees go, voluntary fee allowed) without naming the base mechanism. That is NO unless the agreed mechanism is named, e.g. a budget base plus a voluntary fee.
- Wording risk: "we agreed that the finance ministry will prepare a VAT-share model" is a task, not an agreed model; the resolver needs a direct quote.
- The council may be postponed or the item dropped, as on 2 Nov.
- Outcomes may be reported only by anonymous sources ("podle informací…"); that does not count without one of the three named leaders.
