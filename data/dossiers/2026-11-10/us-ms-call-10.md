# us-ms-call-10
- **Claim:** The Associated Press declares a winner of the 2026 US Senate race in Mississippi between 06:00 CET on 8 November and 23:59 Central time on 9 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if the Associated Press declares (calls) a winner of the 3 Nov 2026 Mississippi US Senate election (Scott Colom, Cindy Hyde-Smith or anyone else) between 2026-11-07T23:00-06:00 (06:00 CET on 8 Nov) and 2026-11-09T23:59-06:00, as shown on AP's results page or reported, with the time of the call, by AP News, Mississippi Free Press, WLBT, Clarion Ledger, NBC News or another outlet quoting AP's call. NO if AP has not declared a winner by 23:59 CST on 9 Nov 2026. A call made before 06:00 CET on 8 Nov also resolves NO (the claim is about a call in the window). Calls by other tabulators (Decision Desk HQ, networks) and concessions without an AP call do not count.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-07T23:00-06:00 to 2026-11-09T23:59-06:00 (America/Chicago).
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-07T18:00Z. Read times as stated.

## Schedule
- Statutory receipt deadline: "In Mississippi, absentee ballots must be postmarked by Election Day and received within five business days of the election, or Nov. 10, to be counted" — AP explainer republished by MFP, https://www.mississippifreepress.org/why-its-still-too-early-to-call-the-us-senate-race-in-mississippi/ (published 2026-11-06T23:44Z, read 2026-11-07T17:53Z). The statute is Miss. Code § 23-15-637 ("Timely casting of ballots"; found by search title only, codes.findlaw.com refused the reader and the greeninfo mirror was unreachable). Five business days after Tue 3 Nov: 4, 5, 6, 9, 10 Nov, so Tue 10 Nov.
- ID-cure for affidavit ballots: "only counted if the voter presents their identification to officials within five business days of Election Day" — same AP explainer. Same Tue 10 Nov.
- County certification to the state: "Counties are required to report the results of provisional ballots when they report their certified results to the state 10 days after the election on Nov. 13" — same AP explainer.
- Rankin County: commissioners "intend to work through the weekend"; on weekdays they return "at around 5 p.m."; "They expect to begin scanning in affidavit ballots at 5 p.m. on Tuesday" — MFP liveblog, https://www.mississippifreepress.org/liveblog-mississippis-2026-u-s-senate-election-results/ (modified 2026-11-07T00:33Z, read 2026-11-07T17:53Z)
- No AP call scheduled; AP calls when "the trailing candidate has no path to overtake the leading candidate" — AP explainer above.
