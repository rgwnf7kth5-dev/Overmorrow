# cz-bank-tax-agreed-10
- **Claim:** Between 06:00 on 8 November and 23:59 on 9 November 2026, Andrej Babiš, Alena Schillerová, Karel Havlíček, Tomio Okamura or Petr Macinka publicly announces that the Czech government or the ANO–SPD–Motoristé coalition has agreed to impose a special tax or levy on banks.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 on 8 Nov and 23:59 Prague time on 9 Nov 2026, Babiš, Schillerová, Havlíček, Okamura or Macinka says publicly (press conference, post-cabinet briefing, statement to media, TV or radio, own social-media post), or a vlada.gov.cz release states, that the government, the cabinet or the coalition (council) has agreed or decided that banks will be subject to a sector-specific tax, levy, tax surcharge or mandatory payment, in any form — including extending the refinery windfall-tax bill to banks by an amendment, or a sector tax on a list of sectors that names banks. The rate, yield and form need not be settled. NO if no such statement is made in the window; if they say only that a bank tax is being considered, analysed or prepared as one variant, that the finance ministry will draw up options, or that a decision comes later; if the agreement is a voluntary arrangement with banks (a fund, a contribution from dividends agreed with the banks, lower mortgage rates); if it is a general rise in corporate income tax; or if the coalition agrees sector taxes that exclude banks. As reported by ČTK, ČT24, Novinky, Seznam Zprávy, E15, Hospodářské noviny, Deník N, Echo24 or iROZHLAS, or as seen in the post, broadcast or release.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-08T06:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-09T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Prague). Decisive occasions: the cabinet meeting and post-cabinet press conference on Monday 9 Nov and the coalition council on Monday evening.

## Risks
- The leaders may say only that sector taxes were "discussed" or that Schillerová will bring variants (NO).
- Motoristé (Turek, Pikora) oppose; the coalition may agree a different sector first (energy, defence, retail chains) or a non-tax bank arrangement (voluntary fund, dividend contribution), which is NO.
- Babiš may present it as a threat to extract mortgage-rate cuts from banks rather than a decision.
- Post-cabinet press conferences sometimes avoid unfinished topics; the council's outcome may surface only on Tuesday morning (an announcement after 23:59 on 9 Nov is NO).
- Wording: "we agreed to prepare an amendment taxing banks" counts (decision that banks will be taxed); "we agreed to analyse a possible bank tax" does not. The resolver needs the quote.
