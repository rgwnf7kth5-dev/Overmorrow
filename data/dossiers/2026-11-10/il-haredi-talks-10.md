# il-haredi-talks-10
- **Claim:** After President Herzog tasks a Knesset member with forming Israel's next government on 8 November 2026, that member, or the negotiating team he has named, meets in person with the leadership or negotiating team of United Torah Judaism or Shas by 23:59 Israel time on 9 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if (1) President Herzog formally tasks a Knesset member with forming the government on 8 Nov 2026 (Israel time), and (2) between that tasking and 23:59 Israel time on 9 Nov 2026 the tasked member, or a negotiating team acting for him or his party, holds an in-person meeting with any of: the chairman of United Torah Judaism (Yakov Asher), the leader of either of UTJ's constituent factions (Degel HaTorah, Agudat Yisrael), the chairman of Shas (Aryeh Deri), or an official negotiating team or delegation of UTJ or Shas; as reported by Times of Israel, Jerusalem Post, Ynet/Ynetnews or Haaretz (a report from those outlets citing party statements or unnamed party sources that the meeting took place counts; reports of phone calls, of meetings only planned, or of contacts between unnamed "associates" do not). NO if no such meeting takes place in the window, or if no one is tasked on 8 Nov 2026.
- **Event window:** from the tasking on 8 Nov 2026 to 2026-11-09T23:59+02:00 (Asia/Jerusalem). Window cannot open before 2026-11-08T06:00+02:00.
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-07T17:58Z. Read times as stated.

## Risks
- Herzog postpones the tasking beyond Sunday (unity push, "mandate not guaranteed" remarks reported by WION) — then NO.
- Eisenkot's first steps may be with his own bloc (Liberman, Bennett, Golan) and the Knesset speaker fight before Tuesday's swearing-in; haredi contacts may stay indirect, by phone or through unnamed intermediaries (do not count).
- UTJ says any offer goes to its rabbis first; it may avoid a public meeting until a draft proposal exists.
- Secret meetings reported only later, or with vague sourcing ("contacts"), make resolution harder; criterion needs a report that a meeting took place.
- If Netanyahu were tasked, a Likud–Shas meeting would be routine; the claim covers that case too.
