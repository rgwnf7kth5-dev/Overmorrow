# us-iran-response-30
- **Claim:** By 23:59 US Eastern time on Friday 30 October 2026, Iran gives a formal answer to the Trump-backed proposal that Pakistan's army chief Asim Munir carried to Tehran, or the US or Iranian government announces a date for US–Iran talks.
- **Criterion:** The window runs from 2026-10-29T00:00:00-04:00 to 2026-10-30T23:59:59-04:00. YES if, within it, any of the following is made on the record:
  - **(a)** A named Iranian official states that Iran has sent or delivered its response to the proposal Munir brought to Tehran on 26–27 October, or states that Iran accepts or rejects that proposal. Officials who count: the Supreme Leader or his office, President Pezeshkian, Foreign Minister Araghchi, Foreign Ministry spokesman Baghaei, the Supreme National Security Council or its secretary, and Parliament Speaker Ghalibaf. A statement that Iran has responded to "the latest US proposal" or to "the American message conveyed by Pakistan" also counts.
  - **(b)** The US government (President Trump, the White House, the State Department, envoy Witkoff, Vice President Vance) or the Pakistani government (Prime Minister Sharif, Foreign Minister Dar, the Foreign Office, ISPR) states that Iran's response to that proposal has been received.
  - **(c)** The US or Iranian government officially announces a calendar date for a round of US–Iran negotiations, direct or indirect, with both delegations in the same venue.

  NOT counted: statements that Iran is "reviewing" or "will respond"; reports that cite unnamed officials or sources; general restatements of Iran's conditions (for example the Hormuz reopening plan) that are not presented as the answer to this proposal; and "talks soon" without a date. NO if none of (a)–(c) happens in the window. Statements may be reported after the window if they were made inside it.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-29T00:00:00-04:00 to 2026-10-30T23:59:59-04:00 (America/New_York; 07:30 Tehran 29 Oct to 07:29 Tehran 31 Oct)

## Facts
- **Delivery date conflicts between sources.**
  - The 27 Oct House of Saud article: Munir delivered the proposal on 26 October in Tehran, at the first joint meeting of Iran, Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Pakistan — https://houseofsaud.com/pakistan-munir-tehran-iran-proposal/ (published 2026-10-27 21:51Z, read 2026-10-28T18:05Z)
  - The 28 Oct tracker: Munir attended the talks on 27 October. Its own teaser says 26 Oct. — https://houseofsaud.com/iran-hormuz-ceasefire-conditions-oct28/ (published 2026-10-28 07:45Z, read 2026-10-28T17:58Z)
- **What the proposal contains.** Its terms are not public. A senior Pakistani security official (unnamed) described the aim as "a regional mechanism to prevent military attacks between countries", with Mecca defence pact members and Qatar as possible guarantors — House of Saud, 27 Oct (as above).
- **First Iranian reaction.** On 26 Oct, Supreme National Security Council secretary Mohsen Rezaei called the talks "very good" and briefed Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei — House of Saud, 27 Oct (as above).
- **Iran's Hormuz conditions.** On 28 Oct, Iranian state media said the strait could reopen within four to five days if the US lifts its naval blockade and meets Tehran's demands. CBS described Iran's seven-day ceasefire conditions as "still on the table" — House of Saud tracker, 28 Oct (as above).
- **Message-passing.** On 25 Oct, an Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman said Tehran was still passing messages to Washington through intermediaries — House of Saud, 27 Oct (as above). On 26 Oct, Baghaei said indirect negotiations continue — carried over from us-iran-talks-announced-29 (newscord, published 2026-10-26).
- **Earlier refusal.** On 20 Oct, during Munir's earlier bilateral visit, Iranian officials reportedly told him there would be no peace talks without sanctions removal — The National, via House of Saud 27 Oct; headline "US peace talks off without sanctions removal, Iran tells Pakistan", https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/gulf/2026/10/20/pakistan-army-chief-arrives-in-tehran-as-iran-seeks-leverage-in-us-talks/ (search result only, not opened).
- **Precedent for a public answer.** Iran has announced its answers to US proposals before:
  - On 4 Oct, Araghchi confirmed that Tehran "responded to the latest US proposal" and said "there are additional points that still need to be communicated to the American side" — Daily Beirut https://dailybeirut.com/en/world/iran-announces-response-latest-us-proposal/ (published 2026-10-04 11:28, read 2026-10-28T18:07Z).

  - Four-country mediation (Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, Turkey, Qatar) is pressing for a reply.
  - Rezaei called the talks "very good".
  - A blunt rejection by Araghchi or Baghaei would also count as YES under (a).
- **Toward NO:**
  - Diplomacy is reported to be paused until after the 3 Nov midterms.
  - Tehran tends to pass answers quietly through Pakistan, so a reply may be reported only by unnamed sources, which do not count.
  - Friday is Iran's weekend.
  - Iran has escalated at sea (closure of Hormuz, blockade extended to the Gulf of Oman).
- **Ambiguity:**
  - Iranian officials may say "we have conveyed our views" without naming this proposal. A resolver must judge whether that refers to the Munir proposal.

  - US claims that Iran "wants a deal" do not count.
