# un-colombia-30
- **Claim:** By 23:59 New York time on Friday 30 October 2026, the UN Security Council adopts a resolution renewing the UN Verification Mission in Colombia with all 15 members voting in favour.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 2026-10-29T00:00:00-04:00 and 2026-10-30T23:59:59-04:00, the UN Security Council adopts a resolution extending the mandate of the UN Verification Mission in Colombia, with 15 votes in favour, none against and no abstentions. Adoption "without a vote" or by consensus with no recorded abstention also counts as YES. NO if a resolution is adopted in the window with any abstention or vote against, if a member does not participate, or if no renewal resolution is adopted in the window (including adoption on 31 October or later, or only a short technical rollover adopted with fewer than 15 votes in favour). A short technical rollover adopted 15-0-0 in the window counts as YES.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-29T00:00:00-04:00 to 2026-10-30T23:59:59-04:00 (America/New_York)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-28T18:20:00Z

## Base rate
- Reference class: annual renewals of the UN Verification Mission in Colombia, 2017–2025 (res. 2366, 2435, 2487, 2545, 2603, 2655, 2694, 2754, 2798). Unanimous in 8 of 9; the exception is 2025 (13-0-2, US and Russia abstaining). 2024 and 2025 votes verified tonight; 2017–2023 unanimity from general knowledge, consistent with Moncloa's "without major internal splits". Rate 0.89, n = 9.
- Conditioning point: the only non-unanimous renewal occurred when the penholder text did not match the views of the US (2025); this year the US publicly supports the host government's proposed narrowing.

## Schedule slip
- Slip class un-security-council: 0.120 [estimate] (`node scripts/refdata/slip.mjs un-security-council`).
- Specific risk: the mandate runs to Saturday 31 Oct; the Council can meet on a Saturday, and in 2025 the vote happened on the expiry day after being expected the day before. A vote on 31 Oct falls outside the window (NO). A vote on Thursday 29 Oct is inside the window.

## Risks
- Russia (and possibly China) abstaining again, e.g. objecting to narrowing the mandate (rural reform removal) or to US-driven cost cuts, would make the outcome NO.
- Members favouring the full 2016 Agreement mandate (Denmark publicly opposed dropping rural reform) could abstain if the rural reform is removed; conversely, the US could abstain if the text keeps rural reform against the host government's wish.
- Vote slipping to Saturday 31 Oct or a short technical rollover adopted with abstentions.
- Vote date not confirmed from a UN source tonight.
