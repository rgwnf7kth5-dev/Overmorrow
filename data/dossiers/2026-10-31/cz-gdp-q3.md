## Risks
- Inventories: RB expects a stock drawdown to cut growth; the Q2 estimate already showed a negative stock contribution. Inventory swings drive most ČSÚ preliminary surprises.
- Q2 base: if ČSÚ revises Q2 (now 0.3) again, the Q3 q/q moves the other way.
- Energy shock: oil and gas import bill rising (August trade), fuel retail falling; October confidence weakest since April 2025 (Q4, but signals Q3-end weakness).
- Upside: industry and retail were firm through August; net exports helped in Q2.
- The release gives q/q and y/y; media may headline y/y (RB 1.6%). The criterion uses q/q only.
