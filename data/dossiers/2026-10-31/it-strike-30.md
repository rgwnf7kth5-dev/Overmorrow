# it-strike-30
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Rome time on Friday 30 October 2026, Transport Ministry ordinance no. 204 T of 27 October, which defers USB's 30 October strike in the transport sectors, is suspended in whole or in part by the TAR Lazio (or the Council of State), or is revoked or amended by the ministry so that the transport strike may lawfully take place on 30 October.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 on 29 Oct and 23:59:59 on 30 Oct 2026 (Europe/Rome), a TAR Lazio (or Council of State) decree or order suspending ordinance 204 T in whole or in part is issued, or the MIT revokes or amends it to allow the strike to go ahead on 30 Oct (in full or in reduced form), as reported by the court, the MIT, USB or major Italian media (ANSA, Corriere, Repubblica, Il Sole 24 Ore). NO if the ordinance remains in force unchanged through the window. Workers striking in defiance of the ordinance does not count.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-29T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-10-30T23:59:59+01:00 (Europe/Rome)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-28T18:25Z

## Facts
- Strike called by USB, backed by ADL-Cobas, CLAP, Unicobas; against cost of living, war and rearmament; demands include wage indexation and price controls — wantedinrome (as above)
- Ministry's basis: art. 8 of law 146/1990; sanctions under art. 9; companies must report non-complying staff within 30 days; appeals lie to the TAR Lazio — qualitytravel (as above)
- Ground: the franchise (franchigia) period around the commemoration of the dead (2 Nov) — https://www.soldionline.it/notizie/economia-politica/sciopero-trasporti-30-ottobre-salvini-precetta-lo-stop-cosa-succede-ora (published 2026-10-27 21:10, read 2026-10-28T18:00Z) "franchigia del 2 novembre"
- Commissione di garanzia: 13 Oct "indicazione immediata" asking USB to exclude franchise-covered sectors; 22 Oct deliberation 26/320 warning the minister of "fondato pericolo di pregiudizio grave ed imminente" — qualitytravel (as above)
- MIT convened unions 26 Oct; unions said revocation or postponement not possible — qualitytravel (as above)
- USB: "un ordine sbagliato e illegittimo, al quale disobbediamo"; confirms full-day strike in the covered sectors, accepting sanctions while contesting them — https://www.affaritaliani.it/politica/sciopero-dei-trasporti-di-venerdi-30-ottobre-salvini-precetta-e-lusb-risponde-cosi-disobbediamo.html (published 2026-10-28 10:15, read 2026-10-28T18:00Z)
- No TAR appeal against ordinance 204 T reported as of 28 Oct afternoon — https://www.quotidianocontribuenti.com/sciopero-del-30-ottobre-scontro-tra-governo-e-sindacati-salvini-firma-la-precettazione-lusb-non-arretra/ (published 2026-10-28 09:04 UTC, read 2026-10-28T18:02Z) "a legal or administrative challenge could follow" (paraphrase of model summary); varesenews (below) "no new appeal"
- Trenord: strike "è stato differito per il trasporto ferroviario a seguito della precettazione del ministero"; trains on normal timetable — https://www.varesenews.it/2026/10/sciopero-del-30-ottobre-il-mit-precetta-treni-regolari-anche-a-varese-ma-usb-non-si-ferma/2721961/ (published 2026-10-28 15:17 UTC, read 2026-10-28T18:01Z)
- Precedents: TAR Lazio in Dec 2024 suspended a similar Salvini ordinance limiting a strike to four hours; in March 2024 it upheld a USB appeal against a 2023 ordinance — varesenews (as above)
- Ferrovie Appulo Lucane runs normal timetable per the ordinance; TPL FVG still warns of possible disruption in Trieste and Pordenone — qualitytravel; https://www.diariofvg.it/2026/10/28/sciopero-30-ottobre-governo-impone-rinvio-usb-conferma-protesta-cosa-succede-fvg (published 2026-10-28 15:00 UTC, read 2026-10-28T18:01Z)

- Non-transport sectors (schools, public offices, health non-urgent) are not covered by the ordinance — wantedinrome / qualitytravel sector lists

## Base rate
- Stated reference class: Salvini transport-strike ordinances (precettazioni) 2023–2025 suspended by the TAR before the strike day: about 1 of 4 (Dec 2024 suspended; Nov 2023, and others not suspended before the strike). Rate ≈0.2, conditional on a union appeal being filed; no appeal reported for 204 T. Source: news record (varesenews summary of precedents); estimate pending a full count.

## Risks
- USB (or another union, e.g. CUB, ADL-Cobas) files an urgent appeal on 29 Oct and a single-judge TAR decree suspends the ordinance, as in Dec 2024.
- Ministry softens the order (e.g. converts deferral into a reduced strike) after talks — counts YES.
- USB's chosen line is disobedience rather than litigation, which lowers the chance of any court ruling in time.
