# ua-oleshky-evac-30
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Kyiv time on Friday 30 October 2026, a first organised group of civilians leaves Russian-occupied Oleshky under the truce's evacuation arrangements, as confirmed by Ukrainian or Russian officials.
- **Criterion:** YES if a Ukrainian official (Lubinets, the Ukrainian government, Kherson regional administration) or a Russian official (Lantratova, Russian MoD, occupation authorities) states that civilians have departed Oleshky in an organised evacuation under the Oleshky truce agreement (any number, any destination), with the departure taking place between 00:00 on 29 Oct and 23:59:59 on 30 Oct 2026 (Europe/Kyiv); a confirmation issued later still counts if it places the departure in that window. NO if no organised departure from Oleshky happens in the window. Evacuations from the Ukrainian-held right bank (Kherson city and nearby communities) and individual self-organised departures do not count.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-29T00:00:00+02:00 to 2026-10-30T23:59:59+02:00 (Europe/Kyiv)
