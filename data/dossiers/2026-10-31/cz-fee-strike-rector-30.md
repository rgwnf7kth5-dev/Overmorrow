# cz-fee-strike-rector-30
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Prague time on Friday 30 October 2026, the rector of at least one Czech public university, or the Czech Rectors' Conference (ČKR), publicly backs the 3 November strike against the bill abolishing the ČT and ČRo licence fees, or announces that teaching at the university is suspended or student absence excused on 3 November.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 on 29 Oct 2026 and 23:59:59 CET on 30 Oct 2026, either of the following is made public. (a) The rector of a Czech public university (veřejná vysoká škola), acting as rector, or the Czech Rectors' Conference as a body issues a statement that expressly supports the 3 Nov 2026 strike or protest against the bill on licence fees and ČT/ČRo funding (sněmovní tisk 231), or that says the university joins it. (b) The rector announces a rector's day (rektorský den), a suspension of teaching, or excused absence for students university-wide on 3 Nov in connection with the strike. Evidence is the university's or ČKR's website or social media, or a report by ČTK, ČT24, iROZHLAS, Seznam Zprávy, Novinky, Deník N, Aktuálně.cz or another major Czech outlet. These do not count: statements by deans, faculties, academic senates, students or unions; general criticism of the bill or support for public-service media that does not mention the strike or protest action; and statements made public before 29 Oct. NO if nothing qualifying is made public in the window.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-29T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-10-30T23:59:59+01:00 (Europe/Prague)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-28T18:25:00Z

## Schedule
- No rector or ČKR step is announced. This is an unscheduled step in a running mobilisation. The strike date is fixed: Tue 3 Nov 2026. Výbor solidarity studentů called it on 28 Oct and asked other universities and schools to join — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/studenti-vyhlasili-kvuli-ruseni-poplatku-pro-ct-a-cro-na-3-listopad-stavku/2894141 (published 2026-10-28 12:25, updated 17:24, read 2026-10-28T18:11Z) "Vyzýváme, aby se k vyhlášení stávky připojily i další univerzity, školy a iniciativy"
- 29 and 30 Oct are working days. 28 Oct was a state holiday.

## Facts
- Výbor solidarity studentů, a platform linking schools across the country, called a warning strike for Tue 3 Nov at a Brno rally (náměstí Svobody). The strike is a series of activities to inform the public about public-service media and independent culture — ČTK 2894141 (above) "Výstražná stávka bude sestávat ze série různých aktivit"
- The Brno rally drew about 2,000 people, mostly students (estimate by Brno-střed secretary Petr Štika) — ČT24 https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/domaci/studenti-vyhlasili-stavku-kvuli-ruseni-poplatku-pro-ct-a-cro-379086 (published 2026-10-28 14:24, read 2026-10-28T18:00Z)
- Strike readiness had already been declared by students of Masaryk University (Média nedáme MUNI), the DAMU academic community, students of FSV UK and FAMU, and the AMU unions — ČTK 2894141 "Stávkovou pohotovost v minulých dnech vyhlásili například studenti brněnské Masarykovy univerzity…"
- Olomouc on 3 Nov: a march at 15:00 from the ČT regional office (RCO) to ČRo Olomouc, by students of several Palacký University faculties, then a rally at 16:00 on Horní náměstí — ČTK 2894141 "průvod bude vycházet v 15:00"
- ČTK says the government plan "faces criticism also from university rectors, churches, theatres and other cultural institutions". The article names no rector who backs the strike — ČTK 2894141 "čelí kritice také od rektorů vysokých škol"
- ČKR on 16 Jun 2026 (letter to PM Babiš, signed by ČKR chair Prof. Milan Pospíšil): ČKR backs independent and predictable funding of public media and asked the government to reconsider. It also said it is not the rectors' place to enter the "thoroughly political debate" on licence fees — https://www.crc.muni.cz/media/4176660/ckr-a-babis-1662026_signed.pdf (dated 2026-06-16, read 2026-10-28T18:15Z) "nám nepřísluší vstupovat do veskrze politické debaty o koncesionářských poplatcích"
- The Veřejnoprávně initiative (ČT and ČRo staff) called a full-day strike for 3 Nov on Fri 23 Oct. Its spokesman Daniel Stach said viewers would notice non-standard programming, within the law. The Syndikát novinářů ČR supports it — MediaGuru https://www.mediaguru.cz/ceska-televize-a-cesky-rozhlas-vstoupi-v-utery-3-listopadu-do-stavky (published 2026-10-23 15:20, read 2026-10-28T18:02Z)
- National Theatre: the drama unions declared strike readiness after a Hamlet performance (27 Oct). They demand Culture Minister Klempíř's resignation and withdrawal of the funding transfer. The Herecká asociace (president Ondřej Kepka) backs them. Klempíř cited CZK 1.03bn for ND operations this year. The article does not say whether the drama ensemble strikes on 3 Nov, and gives no ND management position — ČT24/ČTK https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/kultura/odbory-cinohry-narodniho-divadla-vyhlasily-stavkovou-pohotovost-379078 (published 2026-10-27, read 2026-10-28T18:05Z) "Odbory činohry Národního divadla (ND) vyhlásily stávkovou pohotovost"
- The bill: tisk 231 passed first reading overnight into Sat 24 Oct (04:36) with coalition votes. ČT would get CZK 5.74bn from the budget next year (CZK 1bn less than planned fee income) and ČRo CZK 2.065bn (about CZK 400m less). The coalition did not shorten the 60-day committee period — ČT24 379086 and ČTK 2894141 (above)
- Babiš (26 Oct): the coalition will not withdraw the bill, but its form will still be negotiated — ČTK 2894141 "koalice návrh nestáhne, o jeho podobě se ale ještě bude jednat"
- Media committee, 29 Oct (meeting called for 14:00): it has not happened at the time of writing, so the outcome is unknown. Yesterday's dossier (data/dossiers/2026-10-30/cz-fee-committee-resolution-29.md) covers it. The articles read today (ČT24 379086, ČTK 2894141) do not mention it. No coalition amendments or second-reading date have been reported; the 60-day committee period puts second reading after about 24 Dec.
- Previous precedent: on 22 Jun 2026, ČT and ČRo staff held a 24-hour warning strike over the same plan — ČTK 2894141 "Čtyřiadvacetihodinovou výstražnou stávku … uspořádali pracovníci ČT a ČRo už 22. června"

## Risks
- YES paths: AMU's rector (AMU's faculties DAMU and FAMU and its unions are already in strike readiness), the rector of Masaryk University (where the Brno rally was held), or Charles University's rector (FSV UK students) declares support or excuses absence. ČKR issues a statement.
- NO paths: rectors keep to general support for public media without endorsing a strike (ČKR's June line: "nám nepřísluší vstupovat do veskrze politické debaty"). Decisions are left to deans, who do not count. A rector's backing was made public before 29 Oct (then only a new statement in the window counts) — not found in sources read, but not ruled out.
- Resolution ambiguity: "supports students' right to protest" versus "supports the strike". The criterion needs express support for the strike or protest action, or that the university joins it. A rector allowing students to take part without academic penalty counts under (b) only if it applies university-wide.
