## Risks
- Q4 is usually burdened by banking levies and year-end costs, so analysts may expect Q3 above the H2 average; that would make Q3 > Q2 easier.
- Higher Polish risk costs (guided 25–30bp vs 20bp in Q2) or integration costs bunching in Q3 could pull profit below Q2.
- Rate cuts in CEE (Czech, Hungarian, Polish central banks) or FX movements could weigh on NII.
- One-offs (sales, provisions, Hungarian/Polish legal or tax items) can move the quarter by ±100m.
