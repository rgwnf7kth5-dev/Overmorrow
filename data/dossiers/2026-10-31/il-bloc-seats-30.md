# il-bloc-seats-30

- **Claim:** In the Central Elections Committee (CEC) count as it stands at 23:59 Israel time on Friday 30 October 2026, the lists of Netanyahu's bloc (Likud, Shas, United Torah Judaism, Otzma Yehudit, Religious Zionist Party–Zehut and Noam) together hold 54 or more of the 120 Knesset seats.
- **Criterion:** YES if the seat allocation based on the latest CEC count published at or before 23:59:59 IST (UTC+2) on 30 Oct 2026, including whatever double-envelope ballots have been counted by then, gives those six lists 54 or more seats combined. NO if it gives them 53 or fewer. Pending (not NO) if no seat allocation from a CEC count can be established for that moment. Evidence: the CEC results site with its update time or an archived copy; otherwise Times of Israel, Ynet, Haaretz, Kan, JNS or Israel National News reporting seats "based on the CEC count" with a time stamp. Seat allocation includes surplus-vote agreements as the CEC (or media citing it) computes them.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-29T00:00:00+02:00 to 2026-10-30T23:59:59+02:00 (Asia/Jerusalem). The state of the count at the end of the window decides.
- **Written at:** 2026-10-28T18:20:00Z

## Risks
- **Starting point is disputed (53 vs 54).** If the true end-of-regular-count figure is 53, YES needs a gain from double envelopes; if 54, YES needs no loss.
- **Last-seat effects:** a single remainder seat swings between blocs with ~0.1 pp of votes; surplus agreements (Likud–RZP–Zehut; Shas–UTJ) can flip one seat.
- **Threshold:** if Reservists–Economic or Amcha Yisrael gained enough double-envelope votes to pass 3.25% (very large gap), all other lists lose seats.
- **Evidence:** CEC site URL unknown; media may report rounded "nearly final" figures without time stamps. The resolver needs a figure valid at 23:59 IST 30 Oct; a figure published on Sat 31 Oct describing the count as finished on Friday counts if it states that.
- **Shabbat:** CEC work and Israeli media reporting slow down from Friday afternoon; the count at 23:59 is likely the count at Friday midday.
