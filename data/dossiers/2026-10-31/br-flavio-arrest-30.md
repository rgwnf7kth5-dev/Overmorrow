## Risks
- Toward YES: open breach of an STF order on record; PGR sources say preventive arrest is now possible; Moraes acted on the passport request within hours on 25 Oct; precedent of Zambelli (sitting deputy) in 2025; an arrest order is the prerequisite for an Interpol red notice and an extradition request.
- Toward NO: senator's immunity (arrest only in flagrante for a non-bailable crime, then a Senate vote within 24 hours); PF says the existing order did not authorise arrest and it is still drafting its report; PGR doubts about the underlying evidence; lawyers expect the STF may wait until the mandate ends; an order is futile while he is in the US.
- Timing: an order could come on 28 Oct (outside the window, moot) or after 30 Oct, after the PF report.
- Secrecy: the inquiry may be under seal; a decision could be signed in the window but only reported later — the decision date governs, so resolution may need to wait for disclosure.
