# g20-wangyi-30
(The id was assigned for a Lavrov claim. The claim was switched to China's Wang Yi because Lavrov's attendance was confirmed by Moscow on 28 Oct; see Schedule.)

- **Claim:** China's Foreign Minister Wang Yi attends the G20 foreign ministers' meeting in Atlanta in person on Friday 30 October 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if Wang Yi is physically in Atlanta on 30 Oct 2026 (US Eastern time) and takes part in person in at least one of the meeting's 30 Oct events: a session, a working lunch or dinner, the family photo, or a bilateral meeting held on the meeting's margins in Atlanta. This must be shown by any of the following:
  - the Chinese Foreign Ministry (fmprc.gov.cn / mfa.gov.cn, e.g. "Wang Yi Attends the G20 Foreign Ministers' Meeting" or a readout of a bilateral in Atlanta dated 30 Oct);
  - a State Department readout, transcript or photo;
  - a major news outlet's report.

  NO if China is represented by someone else on 30 Oct, if Wang Yi joins only by video, if he arrives in Atlanta only on 31 Oct, or if he does not attend.
