# rs-pm-candidate-30

- **Claim:** By 23:59 Belgrade time on Friday 30 October 2026, Serbia's Student List publicly names one person as its candidate for prime minister.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 2026-10-29T00:00:00+01:00 and 2026-10-30T23:59:59+01:00 (Europe/Belgrade), the Student List ("Studentska lista – Studenti pobeđuju") publicly announces one named person as the list's candidate for prime minister (mandatar/premijer). The announcement must come from the list's official spokespeople or leadership, or from the coordinating body of the student movement, and be reported by its official channels or by N1, RTS, RFE/RL (Slobodna Evropa), Vreme, Danas, Beta, FoNet, AP or Reuters. Media speculation, "possible" names, several names, a negotiating team, or a statement that the list will decide later do not count. NO if no single named candidate is announced in the window. (A name announced before 29 Oct 00:00 would make this claim moot; the editor replaces it.)
- **Event window:** 2026-10-29T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-10-30T23:59:59+01:00 (Europe/Belgrade)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-28T18:30:00Z
