# un-minurso-30
- **Claim:** By 23:59 New York time on Friday 30 October 2026, the UN Security Council adopts a resolution extending the UN mission in Western Sahara (MINURSO, possibly renamed) with at least three members abstaining.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 2026-10-29T00:00:00-04:00 and 2026-10-30T23:59:59-04:00, the UN Security Council adopts a resolution that extends the mandate of MINURSO — under that name or a new one (e.g. MINUSO), including a resolution that converts it into or continues it as a special political mission — and three or more Council members abstain in the vote. NO if the resolution is adopted in the window with two or fewer abstentions, if a draft is vetoed or fails, or if no such resolution is adopted in the window (including a vote on 31 October or later). Votes against and non-participation do not count as abstentions.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-29T00:00:00-04:00 to 2026-10-30T23:59:59-04:00 (America/New_York)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-28T18:25:00Z

## Facts
- 2025 vote on res. 2797 (31 Oct 2025): 11 in favour, 0 against, 3 abstentions (China, Pakistan, Russia), Algeria did not vote — UN Digital Library https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4093660 / voting record https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4093666 (read 2026-10-28T17:48Z) "Abstain (3): China, Pakistan, Russian Federation; Non-voting (1): Algeria"; same count in Hespress (published 2026-09-30 20:00, read 2026-10-28T17:49Z) https://en.hespress.com/146429-moroccan-sahara-what-changed-since-the-last-u-n-security-council-vote.html "11-0-3".
- Res. 2797 said genuine autonomy under Moroccan sovereignty could constitute "a most feasible solution" — Hespress (as above).
- Algeria's Council term ended on 31 Dec 2025; 2026 elected members are Denmark, Greece, Pakistan, Panama, Somalia (2025–26) and Bahrain, Colombia, DR Congo, Latvia, Liberia (2026–27) (general knowledge, not re-verified tonight). Morocco World News (published 2026-10-02, read 2026-10-28T17:42Z) lists current members' positions: Bahrain, DRC and Liberia support Moroccan sovereignty and have consulates in the territory; Latvia recognises neither claim; France calls the autonomy plan the "only basis", the UK the "most credible, viable and pragmatic basis", Denmark "a good basis", Greece "serious and credible"; "Pakistan abstained on the 2025 resolution"; US is penholder — https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/10/340497/un-security-council-to-meet-on-western-sahara-october-7-8-ahead-of-minurso-vote/
- US "zero draft" (dated 20 Oct, circulated 22 Oct per Polisario, unverified): renames MINURSO to "UN Mission for Western Sahara" (MINUSO), drops the referendum; peacekeeping mandate to end 30 Apr 2027, then special political mission 1 May–31 Oct 2027; uniformed personnel cut by at least half by 31 Jan 2027; "genuine autonomy under Moroccan sovereignty is the only feasible solution" — Morocco World News 2026-10-22 (as above).
- Yabiladi (published 2026-10-25 10:00, read 2026-10-28T17:47Z): the US draft of 20 Oct would end MINURSO's peacekeeping role, convert it into a special political mission and reduce the military component — https://en.yabiladi.com/articles/details/205651/minurso-from-referendum-supporting-implementation.html
- Revised draft: "continues to support the renaming of MINURSO to MINUSO"; describes the autonomy plan as "the most feasible political solution" and "sole basis for negotiations" instead of "only basis"; per Le360 the penholder reintroduced UN terminology "to secure consensus"; includes self-determination wording and a six-month Tindouf camp registration request — Morocco World News 2026-10-27 (as above).
- Russia and China "vuelven a ocupar un lugar destacado en las negociaciones"; amendments and veto speculation are not decisions taken — No te olvides del Sáhara Occidental (as above). No stated 2026 position of Russia, China or Pakistan found.
- 2024–2025 precedent: China and Russia amended the 2025 US draft before abstaining — Yabiladi headline "Sahara: China and Russia amend U.S. draft resolution without challenging Morocco's interests" https://en.yabiladi.com/articles/details/205951/sahara-china-russia-amend-draft.html (search result only, not opened).

## Base rate
- Reference class: MINURSO renewals 2018–2025 with three or more abstentions. From general knowledge (not re-verified tonight except 2025): Apr 2018 res. 2414 12-0-3 (Russia, China, Ethiopia); Oct 2018 res. 2440 12-0-3 (Russia, Ethiopia, Bolivia); 2019 2494 13-0-2; 2020 2548 13-0-2; 2021 2602 13-0-2; 2022 2654 13-0-2; 2023 2703 13-0-2; 2024 2756 12-0-2 with Algeria not voting; 2025 2797 11-0-3 (verified). Three or more abstentions in 3 of 9 renewals; rate 0.33, n = 9 (abstainer names before 2025 from memory). Russia abstained in every one of these votes; China abstained in Apr 2018 and 2025.
- The three 2025 abstainers (Russia, China, Pakistan) all remain on the Council in 2026; Algeria (non-participant in 2024–25) has left.

## Risks
- The revised draft's softer wording ("to secure consensus") could bring China and/or Pakistan to vote in favour, leaving fewer than three abstentions.
- A more radical text (ending peacekeeping, dropping the referendum, troop cuts) could instead draw a Russian or Chinese veto (no adoption → NO) or votes against from non-permanent members (not counted as abstentions).
- Additional abstentions possible from members not aligned with the autonomy plan (none publicly indicated).
- Vote on Saturday 31 Oct (outside window) or a short technical rollover.
- Vote date not confirmed from a UN source.
