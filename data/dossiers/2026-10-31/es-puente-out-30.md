# es-puente-out-30
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Madrid time on Friday 30 October 2026, Óscar Puente announces his resignation as Spain's Minister of Transport and Sustainable Mobility, or his removal (cese) is announced by La Moncloa or published in the BOE.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 on 29 Oct and 23:59:59 on 30 Oct 2026 (Europe/Madrid), Puente publicly announces that he is resigning or has resigned as minister, or La Moncloa/the Prime Minister announces his removal, or a Royal Decree of his cese is published in the BOE. A cese decree published in the BOE on 31 Oct still counts if the resignation or removal was announced inside the window. NO if no such announcement or publication occurs in the window. A statement that he will "consider" resigning, or that he will leave after the 29 Nov election, does not count.
