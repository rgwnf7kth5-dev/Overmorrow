## Risks
- The hawks may judge that a hike only six weeks earlier is enough and wait for December, as the "no need to rush" view suggests; in September Takata and Tamura did not dissent on the rate.
- Two members (Asada, Sato) voted against the September hike; a hawk may express views only through the Outlook Report price-outlook dissent, which does not count.
- A dissent proposing a different policy (e.g. faster JGB taper) does not count.
- A surprise hike with no one proposing an even higher rate resolves NO.
