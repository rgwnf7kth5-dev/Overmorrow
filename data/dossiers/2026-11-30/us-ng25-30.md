## Risks

- Instantaneous window: any weather violation (cumulus, anvil, winds), technical hold or late abort at T-0 pushes the launch to 30 Nov or later.
- Florida late-November weather: cold fronts bring cumulus/thick cloud and upper-level winds; no 45 WS figure available.
- Recovery/landing-zone weather: booster returns to land, so no droneship sea-state constraint (orbitalradar: "lands back at the launch site").
- A launch on the date in UTC but not ET is impossible here (T-0 15:47 UTC = 10:47 EST); a late-day recycle within 29 Nov is unlikely given the single instantaneous opportunity.
- Resolution: NASA ISS blog and SpaceX posts are reliable; Next Spaceflight / Wikipedia give the liftoff timestamp as backup.
