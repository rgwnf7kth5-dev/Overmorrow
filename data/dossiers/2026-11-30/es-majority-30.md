# Dossier es-majority-30

Claim (MAIN): The People's Party and Vox together win at least 176 of the 350 seats in the Congress of Deputies in Spain's general election on Sunday 29 November 2026.

Criterion (draft): YES if the Interior Ministry's provisional results (resultados.29neleccionesgenerales.es), with 100% of polling-station returns processed (or the highest share processed, at least 99%, when read at 05:00 CET on 30 Nov), allocate at least 176 Congress seats in total to the PP's candidacies and to Vox. PP candidacies are all lists of the Partido Popular in every constituency (including Partit Popular / Partido Popular lists in Catalonia, the Valencian Community and the Balearic Islands) and the joint list "Unión del Pueblo Navarro en coalición con el Partido Popular" (UPN–PP) in Navarre. NO if the total is 175 or fewer, or if the election is not held on 29 Nov 2026 (Europe/Madrid). The general (definitive) count by the provincial electoral boards on 4–7 Dec, which adds the votes of Spaniards abroad (CERA), and any later court challenge do not change the resolution. If the ministry's site cannot be read, the seat totals of the ministry's provisional count reported by RTVE or EFE decide.

Deadline: tz Europe/Madrid; event window 2026-11-29T09:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-29T23:59:00+01:00 (polls 09:00–20:00 local; Canary Islands close 20:00 local = 21:00 CET); provisional results from 21:00 CET; evidence_at 2026-11-30T01:00:00+01:00; resolves_by 2026-11-30T05:00:00+01:00. Slip class election-held. Case: es-election-2026. Event: es-general-election-2026-11-29.

Kind of claim: a seat total under proportional representation (D'Hondt in 52 constituencies); no single-seat (winner) poll model applies. Below every final poll's seat projection for PP, Vox and PP+Vox is recorded instead.

## Risks

- Repeat of 2023: polls overstated PP and missed late left mobilisation (Catalonia, PSOE useful vote). In 2026 the final-week trend already runs against the right (PSOE up, Vox down, PP+Vox seats shrinking in most trackers); four days of fieldwork (24–28 Nov) are unpublished in Spain.
- Seat efficiency: 35 seats in dispute per ElectoPanel; fewer than 10,000 votes decide the last seat in 23 provinces (Sigma Dos). A 2–3 point swing can move 15–20 seats.
- Vox decline: Vox seat projections fall from 80 (Hamalgama) to 50 (SW Demoscopia); 40dB/ElectoPanel at 55–57 are the lowest of the mainstream panels.
- Turnout assumptions vary 69.5–76.9%; record turnout (>76%) is flagged by Demócrata as a different scenario.
- Wording: the UPN–PP list counts as PP; the Interior Ministry site may list it under UPN. A reader must add it. The provisional count excludes votes from abroad; the criterion fixes the provisional count at ~100%.
