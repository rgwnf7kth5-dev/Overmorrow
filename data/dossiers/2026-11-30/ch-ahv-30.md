# Dossier ch-ahv-30

Claim: Swiss voters reject the federal decree raising VAT to finance the 13th AHV pension.

Criterion: YES if the official provisional result of the federal popular vote of 29 November 2026 on the federal decree of 19 June 2026 on additional funding for the AHV/OASI through an increase in VAT (mandatory referendum, double majority required) shows that the decree fails to obtain both a popular majority and a majority of cantons, i.e. yes votes do not exceed no votes nationally, or cantonal votes in favour do not exceed 11.5 of 23 (either failure is enough), as published on the Federal Statistical Office results dashboard (abstimmungen.admin.ch / VoteInfo) or by the Federal Chancellery (admin.ch). NO if the decree is accepted by both people and cantons, or if the vote is not held on 29 Nov 2026 (Europe/Zurich). Later recounts, appeals or the final validation do not change the resolution. If the official pages cannot be read, SRF/Keystone-SDA reporting of the official provisional result decides.

## Facts

### What is on the ballot
- Background: voters accepted the 13th AHV pension initiative in March 2024; first paid December 2026 to about 2.6 million pensioners; costs over CHF 4 bn rising to over 5 bn a year; the Confederation pays about a fifth, "Der Rest ist zurzeit nicht finanziert" — booklet (URL above).
- The decree raises VAT by 0.4 percentage points (not on certain everyday goods such as food and medicines), about CHF 1.4 bn a year, covering with the federal contribution roughly half of the extra cost; earliest entry into force start of 2028 — booklet "mit einer Erhöhung der Mehrwertsteuer (MWST) um 0,4 Prozentpunkte zu finanzieren".
- Federal Council and Parliament recommend Yes; a parliamentary minority opposes (wants structural measures such as a higher retirement age). National Council 108–85 (6 abst.), Council of States 28–13 (3 abst.) — booklet "108 Ja … 85 Nein".
- Supporters: left, Centre, GLP; opposed: FDP and SVP supporters — Le Temps (URL below) "The proposal is backed by the left, the Centre party and the Green Liberals. It is strongly opposed by supporters of the FDP… and the Swiss People's Party".

## Risks

- Government-backed items tended to finish above the SRG wave-2 Yes in 2026 (three cases); SRG's 5-point No lead is within that range plus the ±2.8 margin.
- High turnout among older voters (who back the decree) and the left (SP/Greens rising Yes) could narrow the gap; gfs.bern sees intended turnout 52%.
- Double-majority asymmetry: even a narrow popular Yes would likely fail the cantons (YouGov finds no canton clearly Yes; Central Switzerland and Ticino lowest), which makes YES on this claim reachable by either route.
- Wording: "reject" = fails either majority. A popular Yes with a cantonal No resolves YES.
