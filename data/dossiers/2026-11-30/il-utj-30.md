# Dossier il-utj-30

Claim: The rabbinic leadership of United Torah Judaism authorises the party to open formal coalition negotiations with Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar by 23:59 Israel time on Sunday 29 November 2026.

Criterion (draft): YES if, between 00:00 on Saturday 28 November and 23:59 on Sunday 29 November 2026 (Asia/Jerusalem), UTJ's rabbinic leadership (the Degel HaTorah leaders Rabbis Dov Lando and Moshe Hillel Hirsch, or a Council of Torah Sages of UTJ or of either of its factions) authorises UTJ, or its Degel HaTorah faction, to enter formal coalition negotiations with Eisenkot / Yashar, or UTJ and Yashar formally open coalition negotiations with that approval, as reported by at least two of Times of Israel, Jerusalem Post (incl. ## Facts

### State of UTJ–Yashar contacts
- KAN (Thu 26 Nov): Lando and Hirsch "came to an agreement with Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar to negotiate the party's inclusion in the next government, including concessions on the IDF draft"; "the two sides generally agreed to exempt yeshiva students from the draft, while increasing oversight of those who do not actually learn"; "The approval from the Degel Hatorah's Lithuanian haredi leaders would allow formal coalition negotiations to proceed, although a final decision on joining the government has yet to be made." — JPost 916291 above.
- JPost live-blog headline 26 Nov: "Degel HaTorah approves open talks with Yashar as Abbas says Ra'am, UTJ will join Eisenkot coalition"; entry 9:51 PM: "United Torah Judaism to meet with legal adviser ahead of formal coalition talks with Eisenkot" — JPost live 916169 above.
- Maariv (Fri 27 Nov) contradicts a completed approval: "Formal authorization to begin negotiations has yet to be granted, with further progress dependent on the rabbis' decision and political developments over the weekend"; rabbis "reportedly inclined to authorize" — JPost 916321 above.
- Kikar HaShabbat (Fri): a source says the Lando–Hirsch meeting (about half an hour, Thursday evening) "hardly dealt with politics", contrary to reports — Kikar URL above "שני המנהיגים כמעט שלא עסקו בפוליטיקה"
- Draft text: "the sides have already formulated an agreed-in-principle text of a draft law, but are awaiting professional approvals from the IDF and the legal advisers" — Kikar URL above "הצדדים כבר גיבשו נוסח מוסכם עקרונית ל חוק גיוס , אך ממתינים לאישורים מקצועיים מצה"ל ומהייעוץ המשפטי"
- Lawyers drafting: Raz Nizri (Yashar) and Boaz Ben-Tzur (UTJ); UTJ "expected to receive the Construction and Housing Ministry and the chairmanship of the Knesset Finance Committee"; "significant disagreements remain unresolved, particularly regarding the legal status of yeshiva students, sanctions and arrests related to military conscription, and government funding for haredi educational institutions. No final coalition agreement has been reached." — JPost 916321 above.
- UTJ's official line (this week, via Kikar): the party is not dealing with portfolios at all; its only agenda items are the status of yeshiva students, independence of haredi education, the status quo and the Jewish character of the state — Kikar URL above.
- Senior UTJ official attacks Shas's "incomprehensible loyalty to Netanyahu" — JPost 916321 above.
