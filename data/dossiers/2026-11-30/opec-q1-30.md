# opec-q1-30

> Desk note (27 Nov, 19:10 CET): this dossier was first drafted for the horizon claim, now the secondary claim opec-2027-30. The story's main claim is opec-q1-30: "At their meetings on Sunday 29 November 2026, OPEC+ ministers keep oil production targets for January–March 2027 unchanged from the levels in force for December 2026." The facts below serve both.
- **Claim (proposed main):** At their meetings on Sunday 29 November 2026, OPEC+ ministers set oil production levels for a period that runs beyond the first quarter of 2027.
- **Criterion (short):** YES if an OPEC press release on opec.org dated 29 Nov 2026, from any of Sunday's meetings, states production levels, required production or targets (unchanged or new) for OPEC+ countries, group-wide or for the seven, that apply to a month after March 2027 (e.g. "until 31 December 2027", "for 2027", a table for April 2027 or later). NO if what is decided runs only to 31 March 2027 or earlier, or is open-ended with no stated period after March, or if no production decision is announced by 23:59 Vienna time (UTC+1) on 29 Nov. Full text in the spec. Alternatives (Q1 unchanged; new 2027 baselines adopted) are set out under **Wording options** below and in the spec notes.

- **What "unchanged" means — the seven:** the seven (Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria, Oman) "decided to maintain September 2026 required production for December 2026" — OPEC 1 Nov release (above). Same formula for October (OPEC 6 Sep, https://www.opec.org/pr-detail/1835613-6-september-2026.html, read 18:00Z) and November (OPEC 4 Oct, https://opec.org/pr-detail/1891616-4-october-2026.html, read 18:00Z). So for the seven, "unchanged" = January (or Q1) 2027 required production equal to the September–December 2026 table.
- **What "unchanged" means — group-wide:** September 2026's hike "completes the unwinding of a layer of voluntary output cuts" (the 1.65 mb/d of 2023); "Those roughly 2-million bpd in cuts date back to 2022 and are due to remain in place until the end of this year. Opec+ is carrying out a review of its members' oil production capacity that will be used for the 2027 output baselines from which quotas are set." — Reuters via BusinessDay, 2 Aug 2026, https://www.businessday.co.za/markets/2026-08-02-opec-completes-rollback-of-voluntary-oil-output-cuts/ (read 17:57:57Z). Reuters, 30 Nov 2025: the 2 mb/d cut "is in place until the end of 2026"; OPEC+ "approved a mechanism to assess members' maximum production capacity to be used for setting output baselines from 2027" — https://finance.yahoo.com/news/opec-set-hold-oil-output-102114025.html (published 2025-11-30T17:16:55Z, read 17:58:38Z). So the group-wide levels currently run out on 31 Dec 2026; a Q1 rollover would extend the 2026 levels (with the seven's 2023 cuts fully unwound) to 31 March 2027.

## Wording options (for the desk; facts behind each)
- **A. Q1 targets unchanged** (seven's Jan–Mar required production = Dec 2026 table; group-wide 2026 levels extended through 31 Mar 2027): three Reuters sources say "agreement in principle"; hold expectations in this series matched every time (base rate below). Near-certain on the reporting; the open risk is only a surprise or no decision.
- **B. New 2027 baselines/targets from the capacity review adopted on Sunday:** Bloomberg said figures were meant as baselines from January, but Reuters (26/27 Nov) says disputes complicate it and Novak says the review completes "by the end of the year", ministers will "see what stage the review was at". Reporting leans against adoption on Sunday.
- **C. (proposed main) Levels set beyond Q1:** Reuters 26 Nov "for at least the first quarter"; 27 Nov "first-quarter 2027 output targets unchanged". Nothing found that says whether the group-wide 2026 levels would be rolled to end-2027 or only to March. Open on the reporting.
- **D. The seven decide all three Q1 months at once vs January only:** they have met monthly since 2023; on 2 Nov 2025 the then eight decided a Jan–Mar pause in one statement. Reuters' "Q1" wording suggests a block decision, not confirmed.
