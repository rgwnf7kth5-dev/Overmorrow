### Turnout thresholds
- First round needed one quarter of registered voters; a People's Assembly seat (Baurci No. 12) was declared invalid at 24.6% — CEC release (above) "au fost declarate nevalabile întrucât la votare au participat mai puțin de ¼ din numărul alegătorilor înscriși"; Interfax (above) "в селе Баурчи выборы не состоялись, там проголосовали 24,6% избирателей".
- Runoff threshold reported as 20% — The Insider https://theins.ru/news/299618 (published 2026-11-16T06:57Z, read 2026-11-27T17:56Z) "явка составила 37,6% при необходимом пороге в 25% для признания первого тура состоявшимся. Порог явки для второго тура составляет 20%." Not confirmed in a CEC text tonight. Interfax says repeat elections (Baurci) have no minimum ("На повторных выборах минимальной явки нет"). The criterion treats any official declaration of invalidity as NO.
- 2023 comparison: Bashkan runoff turnout 57.99% (first round 61.70%) — earlier dossier data/dossiers/2026-11-16/md-gagauzia-16.md citing Wikipedia 2023 Gagauz gubernatorial election.

### Candidates and endorsements
- Stoianoglo: independent; ex-Prosecutor General (Nov 2019–Oct 2021); 2024 presidential runoff 45.09% nationally (51.19% inside Moldova); MP since 2025 for the "Alternativa" bloc; holds Romanian citizenship and backs EU integration — Interfax (above) "заявляет о поддержке курса Молдавии на европейскую интеграцию".
- Gagauz: 68, PSRM candidate; ex-head of Gagauz police (1999–2001); MP 2014–2025; holds Russian and Bulgarian citizenship; favours closer ties with Russia, opposes EU integration — Interfax (above) "Выступает за более тесное сотрудничество с Россией, и против евроинтеграции Молдавии."
- Endorsements by eliminated candidates (Dudnic, Koksal, Anastasov, Garizan, Vlah, Cîlcic) for the runoff: none found in the pages read or in one targeted search (Russian, "Дудник второй тур ... поддержал") — not established either way.
- Regional assembly: PSRM won only 3 of 23 contested seats outright in round one, 10 more in runoffs — SOVA (above) "Из 23 представителей партии ... лишь трое победили в первом туре. Еще десять кандидатов прошли во второй тур".
- IMAS pre-election poll (published 12 Nov, all respondents): Stoianoglo 26.5, Gagauz 17.5, Koksal 9.6, >27% undecided — earlier dossier md-gagauzia-16 citing Logos Press 13 Nov. No runoff poll found.
- Promo-LEX preliminarily recorded 41 incidents in round one (secrecy of vote, attempts to vote for others, possible vote buying) — The Insider (above) "Предварительно организация зафиксировала 41 подобный инцидент."

- Invalidity: if turnout falls below the legal minimum (20% per The Insider), the round is invalid → NO.
