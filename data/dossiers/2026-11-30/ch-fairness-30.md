# Dossier ch-fairness-30

Claim: Swiss voters reject the 'Fairness' initiative on federal taxation of married couples.

Criterion: YES if the official provisional result of the federal popular vote of 29 November 2026 on the popular initiative "Yes to fair federal taxes for married couples – finally ending discrimination against marriage!" (Fairness Initiative; double majority required) shows that it fails to obtain both a popular majority and a majority of cantons, i.e. yes votes do not exceed no votes nationally, or cantonal votes in favour do not exceed 11.5 of 23 (either failure is enough), as published on the Federal Statistical Office results dashboard (abstimmungen.admin.ch / VoteInfo) or by the Federal Chancellery (admin.ch). NO if the initiative is accepted by both people and cantons, or if the vote is not held on 29 Nov 2026 (Europe/Zurich). Later recounts, appeals or the final validation do not change the resolution. If the official pages cannot be read, SRF/Keystone-SDA reporting of the official provisional result decides.

Deadline: tz Europe/Zurich; event window 2026-11-29T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-29T23:59:00+01:00; evidence_at 2026-11-29T18:00:00+01:00; resolves_by 2026-11-30T05:00:00+01:00. Slip class election-held. Case: ch-votes-2026-11 (new). Story "ch-fairness" (main claim; topic economy).

## Facts

### What is on the ballot
- Context: voters accepted individual taxation on 8 March 2026 (law, 54.26% Yes; cantons 10 for, 13 against — not needed for a law); from 2032 everyone is taxed individually at federal and cantonal level — booklet "Mit der vom Stimmvolk am 8. März 2026 angenommenen Individualbesteuerung entfällt diese unterschiedliche Behandlung"; result https://swissvotes.ch/page/dataset/swissvotes_dataset.csv (read 2026-11-27T17:59Z).
- The Centre party's initiative would keep joint assessment of married couples for direct federal tax and bar disadvantaging them, in the constitution; fallback "alternative Steuerberechnung" if no law by 2030; relief of an estimated CHF 680 m to 3 bn a year at federal level — booklet "um schätzungsweise 680 Millionen bis 3 Milliarden Franken pro Jahr entlastet".
- Federal Council and Parliament recommend No. National Council 102–97 (0 abst.), Council of States 24–20 — booklet "102 Nein … 97 Ja".

## Risks

- Polls disagree: YouGov still had Yes 45 vs No 42 overall (though No ahead at the end of fieldwork); 13% undecided.
- Mobilisation of married, older, rural, Centre and SVP voters; turnout below average among Centre sympathisers (gfs.bern) cuts the other way.
- A cantonal majority alone does not make the initiative pass; both majorities are required, so only a popular Yes with a cantonal Yes resolves NO.
