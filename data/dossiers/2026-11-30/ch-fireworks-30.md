## Facts

### What is on the ballot
- The initiative would ban the sale and use of noise-producing fireworks; low-noise items (Bengal fires, fountains) to remain per the initiators; cantons may grant exceptions for events of supra-regional importance (e.g. 1 August), but private individuals may not set off noisy fireworks even there — booklet (URL above) "will den Verkauf und die Verwendung von Feuerwerkskörpern, die Lärm erzeugen, verbieten".
- Federal Council and Parliament recommend No ("geht die Initiative zu weit"); no counter-proposal on the ballot. Parliament: National Council 131 No, 50 Yes, 18 abstentions; Council of States 40 No, 3 Yes, 1 abstention — booklet "131 Nein … 50 Ja".

## Risks

- The usual initiative erosion: −6 points between SRG waves; a further fall of similar size from wave 2 to the result would still leave a majority, but the Bargeld case (61 → 45.6) shows a fall of 15 points is possible.
- Cantonal majority: YouGov sees Central Switzerland and Ticino close; small conservative cantons could flip together while the national Yes holds (Konzernverantwortung 2020 precedent).
- FDP/SVP voters moved to No in November in all three pollsters; the "no need for new bans" argument gained weight (YouGov).
- Interaction with fireworks traditions (1 August) in rural cantons.
