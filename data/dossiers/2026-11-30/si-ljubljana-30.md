# Dossier si-ljubljana-30

Claim: Zoran Janković is re-elected mayor of Ljubljana in the runoff on Sunday 29 November 2026.

Criterion (draft): YES if the State Election Commission's (DVK, volitve.dvk-rs.si) preliminary unofficial results for the second round of the Ljubljana mayoral election held on 29 Nov 2026, with all or nearly all (≥ 95%) votes counted, show Zoran Janković with more votes than Luka Mesec; the official result (including postal votes) takes precedence if published before the resolver reads. ## Facts

### First round (15 Nov 2026)
- Partial results, 99.23% counted: Janković 34.97%, Mesec 26.16% — rtvslo 800084 (above) "Ljubljana : po 99,23 % preštetih glasov: Zoran Janković (34,97 %), Luka Mesec (26,16 %)".
- Full field (earlier count): Janković 34.94, Mesec 26.14, Aleš Vrhovec (SDS) 8.80, Aleš Primc (Glas za otroke in družine) 8.31, Anton Rop (Ljubljana smo mi vsi) 6.18, Klemen Boštjančič (Svoboda) 5.05, Aleksandar Repić 4.26, Jasmin Feratović (Pirati) 2.47, Mojca Sojar (NSi) 1.25, others < 1 each — rtvslo 800217 (above) "Aleš Vrhovec (SDS) je dobil 8,80 % glasov, Aleš Primc ... 8,31 % glasov, Anton Rop ... 6,18 % glasov, Klemen Boštjančič (Gibanje Svoboda) 5,05 %".
- Arithmetic: first-round lead 8.81 points (99.23% count). Right-of-centre candidates (Vrhovec + Primc + Sojar) ≈ 18.4%; centre-left others (Rop + Boštjančič + Feratović + SD 0.37 + Vesna 0.77) ≈ 14.8%.
- Votes: Janković "dobrih 39 tisoč", Mesec "nekaj več kot 29 tisoč", > 44,000 for third candidates; Janković's lowest ever (previously 46–82 thousand) — N1 https://n1info.si/novice/slovenija/lahko-mesca-ze-odpisemo-v-ljubljani-bi-se-moral-zgoditi-cudez/ (published 2026-11-16T20:44+01:00, read 2026-11-27T17:56Z). (A second N1 piece gives 27,819 votes for Janković — https://n1info.si/novice/slovenija/kdo-so-zmagovalci-in-kdo-porazenci-volitev-v-ljubljani/ (published 2026-11-16T13:35+01:00, read 2026-11-27T17:56Z) — inconsistent with the 34.97% share and the 39k figure; treated as an error.)
- Ljubljana turnout ≈ 48%, highest since 2006, ~8 points above 2022 — N1 "kdo-so-zmagovalci" (above) "Ta je bila po neuradnih podatkih skoraj 48-odstotna, kar je največ po letu 2006." Janković's list won 13 of 45 council seats (same source).
- Janković won every Ljubljana mayoral election since 2006 in the first round (2018: 60.45%; 2022 Ninamedia had him at 68% of decided) — rtvslo 800217 "Zadnjih dvajset let so občani Ljubljane župana vedno izbrali v prvem krogu"; https://sl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lokalne_volitve_v_Sloveniji_2018 (read 2026-11-27T18:00Z) "Ljubljana ( Zoran Janković , 60,45 %)".

### Poll (only runoff poll found)
- Ninamedia for Dnevnik, 12–14 Oct 2026, n = 691, phone+online, all respondents: hypothetical runoff Janković 35.2, Mesec 33.4, neither 23.6, don't know 7.1 — N1 https://n1info.si/novice/slovenija/anketa-vox-populi-v-ljubljani-se-obeta-drugi-krog-zupanskih-volitev/ (published 2026-10-17T19:17+02:00, read 2026-11-27T17:56Z) "Jankovića bi podprlo 35,2 odstotka vprašanih, Mesca pa 33,4 odstotka vprašanih. 23,6 odstotka anketiranih je navedlo, da ne bi izbralo nobenega izmed njiju". Decided two-way: Janković 51.3, Mesec 48.7 (lead **2.6**); all-respondent lead 1.8.
- Same poll, first round: Janković 34.6, Mesec 21.0, Rop 8.2, Primc 6.2, Boštjančič 5.2, Vrhovec 4.6 (all respondents) — same URL. Result vs poll: Janković +0.4, Mesec +5.2, Vrhovec +4.2, Primc +2.1, Rop −2.0 (poll base all respondents incl. 6.7% don't know, so not a strict like-for-like).
- Same poll: right-of-centre voters were over-represented among those who would pick neither in all three runoff pairings — same URL "izstopajo volilci desnosredinskih strank".
- Runoff polls after 15 Nov: none found (one targeted search and reads of N1/RTV).

### Endorsements
- Gibanje Svoboda (PM-level national opposition party, Golob) executive unanimously backs Janković in the Ljubljana runoff; Boštjančič (5.05%) agrees — N1 https://n1info.si/novice/slovenija/svoboda-v-drugem-krogu-zupanskih-volitev-podprla-jankovica-mescu-ocitajo-neoperativnost/ (published 2026-11-19T17:31+01:00, read 2026-11-27T17:56Z) "Največja opozicijska stranka Gibanje Svoboda bo v drugem krogu županskih volitev v Ljubljani podprla sedanjega župana Zorana Jankovića".
- Svoboda MEP Marjan Šarec publicly backs Mesec and visited his HQ on election night — same N1 URL "To je v nedeljo že storil strankin evroposlanec Marjan Šarec , ki je Mesca tudi javno podprl"; rtvslo 800217 "V štabu Luke Mesca je bil tudi aktualni evropski poslanec Gibanja Svoboda Marjan Šarec."
- SDS: no endorsement found; ex-SDS city chair Aleš Hojs expects right-wing voters to stay home — N1 "lahko-mesca-ze-odpisemo" (above) "Mislim, da desni volilci ne prenašajo ne enega ne drugega."
- Rop: said results show change is coming; no runoff endorsement found — rtvslo 800217 "izidi kažejo na to, da bo v Ljubljani prišlo do spremembe".
- Political scientist Igor Lukšič expects most moderate right voters to choose Janković over Mesec; Svoboda voters to Janković; Rop voters split — N1 "lahko-mesca-ze-odpisemo" (above) "bi se po mojem mnenju moral zgoditi čudež, da bi zmagal Luka Mesec". (An opinion, not a measurement.)

## Risks

- Mesec outperformed the October poll by ~5 points in round one; the only runoff measurement was close (+2.6 two-way, pre-round-one).
- Right-of-centre voters (~18% in round one) may abstain (expected by Hojs; poll shows them over-represented among "neither") or vote against the incumbent; anti-incumbent mood (Rop "change", Janković's lowest vote ever).
- Svoboda split: party backs Janković, Šarec backs Mesec.
- Postal votes are added after election night; in a very close result the preliminary leader could change (2018 Koper runoff was decided by postal votes, 7-vote margin — sl.wikipedia 2018 page).
