# gulf-ukmto-30
- **Claim:** UKMTO or JMIC reports at least one attack, explosion, mine strike, projectile or drone hit, armed hostile approach, boarding or seizure involving a merchant vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, the Persian Gulf, the Gulf of Oman or the Arabian Sea that took place on Sunday 29 November 2026 (00:00–23:59 Gulf time).
- **Criterion:** as gulf-ukmto-29, shifted to Sunday 29 Nov. YES if UKMTO or JMIC reports a qualifying incident involving a merchant vessel that occurred between 00:00 and 23:59 Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4) on 29 Nov 2026.
  - Qualifying: attack, explosion, mine strike, projectile or drone hit, hostile approach (armed craft firing on or attempting to board), boarding, hijacking or seizure. A projectile or explosion UKMTO/JMIC reports close to a merchant vessel counts even if filed as "suspicious activity" with no damage.
  - Area: Strait of Hormuz, Persian (Arabian) Gulf, Gulf of Oman, Arabian Sea (incl. waters around Socotra).
  - Evidence: ukmto.org, UKMTO's X account, or a wire or established outlet quoting UKMTO or JMIC. Late reports count if the incident falls in the window; a date-only report dated 29 Nov counts.
  - UTC conversion: 20:00–23:59 UTC on 28 Nov falls on 29 Nov; after 20:00 UTC on 29 Nov falls on 30 Nov.
  - Not counted: suspicious approaches without fire or boarding attempt; GPS interference alone; bare "incident"/"suspicious activity"/"tanker and military forces" notices stating no attack, explosion, hit, boarding or seizure; Gulf of Aden, Bab el-Mandeb, Red Sea; Iranian, Houthi or other claims not reported by UKMTO/JMIC; US or other military action against a merchant ship unless UKMTO/JMIC itself reports it.
