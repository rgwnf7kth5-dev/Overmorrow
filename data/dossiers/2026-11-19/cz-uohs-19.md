## Facts
- Incumbent Petr Mlsna will apply. ČTK, 16 Nov: "Přihlášku do výběrového řízení podám, chci se o funkci předsedy úřadu ucházet". His term ends in early December (2 Dec per Seznam Zprávy/Ekonomický deník).
- The NBÚ withdrew Mlsna's "Tajné" clearance at the start of November. He will file a rozklad and a criminal complaint over the leak. Under the selection terms a clearance is not a condition. — ČTK 2902732; https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/2900467 (published 2026-11-11T13:29, read 2026-11-16T17:54Z).
- Deník N (cited by Seznam Zprávy and ČTK) said Mlsna's chances "fakticky padly". — Seznam 320270.
- President Pavel and the NGOs Transparency International, Datlab Institut, Česko, funguj! and Lobbio had pressed for an open selection. — ČTK 2902732. Pavel and Babiš discussed the selection on 11 Nov. — ČTK 2900467.
- No other applicant has been named in reports up to 2026-11-16T17:55Z (searches below).
- Precedent from 2020, the same PM Babiš and the first open selection for the post:
  - Announced 22 Oct, deadline Fri 6 Nov 12:00. — https://www.denik.cz/cesko/uohs-predseda-vyberove-rizeni/ (ČTK, published 2020-10-22).
  - The Office of the Government received 11 applications. Its spokesman gave the number on the deadline day: "Ve stanovené lhůtě do pátečních 12 hodin obdržel Úřad vlády 11 přihlášek" — https://www.novinky.cz/clanek/ekonomika-o-post-sefa-uohs-se-po-rafajovi-uchazi-11-lidi-40341537 (published 2020-11-06T13:31Z, about 2.5 h after the deadline). The names were reported the same day (Mlsna, Neruda, Brom, Petr, Sixta, Kremlík and others).
  - The 2020 application window was 15 days; the 2026 window is 8 days (10–18 Nov).
- 2020 requirements: magisterské vzdělání in law or economics; 5 years in a managerial post in the last 15 years; 5 years of practice in competition or public procurement; a one-year and a three-year plan for the office (denik.cz 2020). The 2026 terms were not read.
