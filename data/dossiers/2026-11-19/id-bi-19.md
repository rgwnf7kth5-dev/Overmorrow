- Path in 2026: +100 bp in three moves May–June; holds since the July RDG — NST above "raised rates by 100 bps in three moves between May and June to defend the rupiah ... but it has since kept rates unchanged"; Bloomberg Technoz https://www.bloombergtechnoz.com/detail-news/127775/alasan-bi-masih-akan-tahan-suku-bunga-pekan-ini (published 2026-11-16T07:01+07:00, read 2026-11-16T17:53Z) "menahan suku bunga acuan BI Rate sejak pertemuan Rapat Dewan Gubernur (RDG) Juli".
- Survey for November: Bloomberg survey median of 22 economists = hold at 5.75% — Gotrade above "BI diproyeksi menahan BI Rate di 5,75% pada pengumuman RDG Rabu (18/11), sesuai median 22 ekonom dan analis yang disurvei Bloomberg". Split of the survey not reported. No Reuters poll for November found.
- Bloomberg Economics expects BI to prefer FX intervention and hedging incentives over another hike (Gotrade citing Bloomberg Technoz).
- October Reuters poll (13–19 Oct): 15 of 29 hold, 13 +25 bp, 1 +50 bp; BI held — NST above; KuCoin summary https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/bank-indonesia-expected-to-hold-interest-rates-amid-market-volatility (published 2026-10-19T10:16Z, read 2026-11-16T17:52Z): "15 of 22 economists expected the benchmark rate to rise from 5.75% to 6.00% or more before year-end" (view predating the MSCI warning).
- Rupiah: record low Rp18,270/US$ intraday on 16 Nov (−0.41%); −1.45% since the last BI decision; −8.64% year to date — Bloomberg Technoz 127850 above "rupiah melemah 0,41% ke posisi terlemah baru Rp18.270/US$". BNP Paribas and Bank Permata see Rp18,500 by year-end (same).
- Inflation 3.23% y/y, within BI's 2.5±1% target; real policy rate 2.52%, highest among major ASEAN economies — Bloomberg Technoz 127775 above.
- US backdrop: Fed widely expected to hike again in December; US Treasury yield 5.14% on 16 Nov (Gotrade above).
- In July BI said it considered another hike but opted for incentives: "These incentives are more effective to attract foreign investment and control the exchange rate without impacting domestic interest rates. This is what we chose" (Governor Perry Warjiyo, via Jakarta Globe/Reuters) — https://business-indonesia.org/news/bank-indonesia-holds-benchmark-rate-at-5-75-while-expanding-measures-to-support-rupiah-stability-and-economic-growth (22 Jul 2026, read 2026-11-16T17:52Z).
