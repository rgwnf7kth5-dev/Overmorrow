## Risks
- Timing: pricing on Thursday (Friday debut) makes the claim not held.
- Size vs price trade-off: company said to care more about cash raised than valuation (FT via MLQ) — upsizing the share count instead of raising the price would keep the price at/near $78.
- Supply overhang: $64–90bn of paper in one night, tech selloff, 10-year >5%; Bloomberg's 15 Nov "$1.4 trillion" target suggests top of range rather than above.
- Allocation to existing backers and the $26bn placement fixed in dollars do not constrain the price.
- Valuation bases differ (fully diluted vs outstanding); report wording on "valuation" must not be confused with price per share.
