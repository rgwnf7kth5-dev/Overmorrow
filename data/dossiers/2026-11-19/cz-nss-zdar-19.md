- Court: seven-member election senate chaired by Vojtěch Šimíček (members Pospíšil, Bejčková, Malík, Mikeš, Molek, Podhrázký). — 11 Nov release.
- Legal options: the court rules on petitions for invalidity of the vote, of the election, or of a candidate's election (§ 90 s.ř.s., § 87(2)–(5) of Act 247/1995). The test is a breach of the election law "způsobem, který hrubě ovlivnil výsledky". The court rules "usnesením bez nutnosti nařizovat ústní jednání, a to do dvaceti dnů". — https://www.nssoud.cz/aktualne/tiskove-zpravy/detail/pravomoc-nejvyssiho-spravniho-soudu-v-prezkumu-voleb-do-senatu-4 (published 2026-10-09, read 2026-11-16T17:52Z). Possible outcomes: rejection (odmítnutí, inadmissible), dismissal (zamítnutí), or a declaration of invalidity. Precedent: after an annulment the president calls a new election within 90 days (Praha 11 2004 and Most 2016, below).
- Parallel case: on 3 Nov the Ostrava regional court ordered a recount of one precinct in the Karviná municipal election, which gave SPD 13 more votes and 2 seats. — https://www.seznamzpravy.cz/clanek/319540 (ČTK, published 2026-11-03T15:19Z, read 2026-11-16T17:53Z).

## Risks
- A one-vote margin: any net recount shift of ≥1 vote toward Černý in the 26 precincts produces a tie or reversal of 2nd/3rd place. Recounts often find small errors (Karviná 2026: +13 votes in one precinct; Šojdrová 2024; Blansko 2021).
- The court chose to recount 26 precincts rather than reject the complaint. This shows it found the grounds specific enough to investigate. Courts usually reject vague complaints outright.
- Even if errors are found, the court may hold, as in Prostějov 2018, that they did not "hrubě ovlivnit" the result. That holding is only possible if the corrected count still leaves Klement second.
- If the order flips, the remedy is unclear: annulment of the election and a new vote, or invalidity of Klement's election. Either is NO.
- Whether a tie would be resolved by lot was not verified.
- Decision issued on 19 Nov (deadline day) → NO.
- Complaint withdrawn by Černý: the criterion says "otherwise ends the case without declaring invalid" → YES, if the termination is issued on 18 Nov.
