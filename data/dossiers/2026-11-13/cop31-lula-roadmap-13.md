# cop31-lula-roadmap-13
- **Claim:** In his national statement to the COP31 Leaders' Summit in Antalya on 11 or 12 November 2026, Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva explicitly calls on countries to adopt or follow a roadmap or national plans for transitioning away from fossil fuels.
- **Criterion:** YES if Lula delivers a statement in person at the Antalya Leaders' Summit between 2026-11-11T08:00+03:00 and 2026-11-12T23:59+03:00 (Europe/Istanbul) and that statement, per the text published by the Brazilian presidency (gov.br/planalto), the UNFCCC webcast, or quotes in Agência Brasil, a major agency or outlet, explicitly calls for countries (all countries, the world, or the COP) to adopt, implement, follow or endorse a roadmap ("mapa do caminho"/"roteiro") or national plans for transitioning away from / phasing out / ending dependence on fossil fuels. NO if he speaks and makes no such call (praising the COP30 roadmap without calling on others to adopt or follow it does not count), or if he does not deliver a statement in person at the summit in the window (statement read by a minister or delivered only at a side event = NO).
- **Event window:** 2026-11-11T08:00+03:00 to 2026-11-12T23:59+03:00 (Europe/Istanbul)

## Facts
- Brazil's COP30 presidency released on 3 Nov a voluntary roadmap for transitioning away from fossil fuels: "transitioning away from fossil fuels is urgent, inevitable and yet possible"; three phases 2028–2033 — Japan Times/AFP above.
- 10 Nov: COP30 President Corrêa do Lago presented the TAFF roadmap in Antalya; India, Nigeria, Germany welcomed it; Germany, Netherlands, UK, Australia back it publicly; the roadmap "invites countries to draw up national plans to build clean energy systems by 2028"; "Brazil is also working on" its own national roadmap — Climate Home https://www.climatechangenews.com/2026/11/10/big-fossil-fuel-countries-back-brazil-led-transition-roadmap/ (published 2026-11-10T17:26Z, read 2026-11-10T17:56Z).
- Guterres on 10 Nov said all countries should start progress on cutting fossil fuels by 2028 — same article.
- Lula was re-elected (surprise re-election) in late October — Climate Home briefing https://www.climatechangenews.com/2026/11/05/cop31-briefing-what-to-expect-from-the-antalya-climate-summit/ (published 2026-11-05T14:09Z, read 2026-11-10T17:55Z).
- At COP30 the roadmap idea was a flashpoint with "only around half of countries in favour" — Climate Home briefing above.
