# cz-prague-scheinherr-13
- **Claim:** Between 06:00 on 11 November and 23:59 on 12 November 2026 (Prague time), the STAN–Spolu–Praha sobě coalition publicly names Adam Scheinherr (Praha sobě) as its nominee for the Prague council post responsible for transport.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 2026-11-11T06:00+01:00 and 2026-11-12T23:59+01:00, the coalition or Praha sobě publicly states that Adam Scheinherr will be its nominee for the Prague city council member (deputy mayor or councillor) in charge of transport, e.g. at the 11 Nov 10:00 press conference or in a party statement, as reported by ČTK (ceskenoviny.cz), ČT24, praha.eu or the parties' own channels. NO if someone else is named for transport, if the transport nominee is not named in the window, or if Praha sobě does not join the coalition. Anonymous-source reports do not count.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-11T06:00+01:00 to 2026-11-12T23:59+01:00; evidence by 2026-11-13T05:00+01:00
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-10T18:12Z. Read times as stated.
