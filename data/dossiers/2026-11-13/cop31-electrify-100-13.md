# cop31-electrify-100-13
- **Claim:** By the end of Thursday 12 November 2026 (Antalya time), the COP31 presidency announces that at least 100 countries have endorsed Türkiye's "35 by 35" Global Electrification Pledge.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 2026-11-11T08:00+03:00 and 2026-11-12T23:59+03:00 (Europe/Istanbul), the COP31 presidency (Türkiye or Australia: cop31 website, presidency press release, President Kurum or a minister speaking on the record, or the UNFCCC) announces a count of at least 100 countries (national governments; the EU as a bloc counts as one, its member states individually) that have endorsed/signed/joined the 35 by 35 Global Electrification Pledge, as published by the presidency or quoted by a major news agency or outlet (Anadolu, AFP, Reuters, AP, Climate Home News, Carbon Brief). Cumulative endorsements count, including any made before the window. NO if the highest count announced by the presidency in the window is below 100, or if the presidency announces no count in the window. Counts from NGOs or reporters' own tallies do not count; "countries and organisations" totals count only if the country number is given separately.
