# ua-talks-swap-13
- **Claim:** Russia and Ukraine carry out a prisoner exchange between the morning of Wednesday 11 November and the end of Thursday 12 November 2026 (Kyiv time).
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 07:00 Kyiv time (UTC+2) on 11 Nov 2026 (= 06:00 Prague) and 23:59 Kyiv time on 12 Nov 2026, Russia and Ukraine carry out an exchange of prisoners (prisoners of war, and civilians if released in the same swap) in which both sides hand over people, as confirmed by Zelensky, the Ukrainian Presidential Office, the Coordination Headquarters for the Treatment of POWs or the Russian Ministry of Defence, or reported as fact by at least two major outlets (e.g. Reuters, AP, AFP, Kyiv Independent, Ukrinform, Ukrainska Pravda, Moscow Times). The exchange need not be linked to the Abu Dhabi talks. An exchange that starts in the window counts even if further stages follow later. Does NOT count: an announced agreement or date for a future exchange; exchanges of bodies only; one-sided releases or handovers; the return of children or civilians outside a two-way prisoner swap. NO if no such exchange takes place in the window. A report published after the window counts if it makes clear the exchange took place in the window.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-11T07:00+02:00 to 2026-11-12T23:59+02:00; evidence: same-day posts by Zelensky / Coordination HQ (swaps are usually announced on the day, often around midday); resolver reads 2026-11-13T05:00+01:00.

- Body repatriations (frequent) do not count; only two-way prisoner handovers.

- Russian strikes or a collapse of day two could delay humanitarian steps.
