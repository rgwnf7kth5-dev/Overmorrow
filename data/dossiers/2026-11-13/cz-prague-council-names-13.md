# cz-prague-council-names-13
- **Claim:** Between 06:00 on 11 November and 23:59 on 12 November 2026 (Prague time), the STAN–Spolu–Praha sobě coalition publicly names all 11 people it will nominate to the Prague city council.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 2026-11-11T06:00+01:00 and 2026-11-12T23:59+01:00, the coalition or its three groupings publicly name, by person, all 11 nominees for the Prague city council (rada: mayor, deputy mayors and councillors), e.g. at the 11 Nov press conference or in party statements, as reported by ČTK (ceskenoviny.cz), ČT24, praha.eu or the parties' own channels. Names may come from different announcements in the window, but each must be attributed to the coalition or the nominating party, not to anonymous sources. Announcing only the split of seats or portfolios, or fewer than 11 names, is NO.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-11T06:00+01:00 to 2026-11-12T23:59+01:00; evidence by 2026-11-13T05:00+01:00
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-10T18:00Z. Read times as stated.
