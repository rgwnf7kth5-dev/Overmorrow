# nl-belastingplan-85-13
- **Claim:** On Thursday 12 November 2026 the Dutch Tweede Kamer adopts the Belastingplan 2027 bill (Kamerstuk 37022) in its final vote with at least 85 of the 150 MPs voting in favour.
- **Criterion:** YES if the official result on tweedekamer.nl (stemmingsuitslagen / 37022 bill page; NOS/ANP reporting it as backup) shows bill 37022 adopted in a final vote held on 12 Nov 2026 between 00:00 and 23:59 Amsterdam time with 85 or more votes in favour (in a show-of-hands vote: the summed seats of the groups and members recorded as voting for; in a roll-call vote: the number of yes votes). NO if fewer than 85 vote in favour, if the bill is rejected, or if the final vote is not held in that window.
- **Deadline:** window 2026-11-12T00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-12T23:59+01:00; evidence by 2026-11-13T05:00+01:00
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-10T18:05Z. Shares all facts with data/dossiers/2026-11-13/nl-belastingplan-13.md (read that file for schedule, sources and seat table).

## Seat arithmetic (seats counted from tweedekamer.nl group pages, read 2026-11-10T17:57Z; see main dossier)
- Coalition D66 26 + VVD 22 + CDA 18 = 66.
- Route A (declared willing, NOS 5 Nov): + CU 3 + 50PLUS 2 + Markuszower 7 = 78 → below 85.
- Route A + SGP 3 = 81; + BBB 3 = 84; + JA21 9 = 87 or more.
- Route B (right bloc): coalition + JA21 9 + BBB 3 + SGP 3 = 81; + route A parties = 93.
- Route C (left): coalition + Pro 20 + Volt 1 = 87; + route A parties = 99. If VVD then votes against: 44 + 21 + 12 = 77.
- PVV (19 by subtraction) would push any route above 85; NOS 9 Nov: PVV would not say how it votes on Thursday (said on box 3).
- So 85+ requires at least one of JA21, Pro, PVV voting for, or the whole right bloc plus route A minus at most one small party. The bare route-A majority (78) gives NO.
