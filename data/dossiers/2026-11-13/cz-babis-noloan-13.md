# cz-babis-noloan-13
- **Claim:** On Thursday 12 November 2026, Andrej Babiš publicly states that the Czech Republic will not contribute to (fund or guarantee) a new EU loan or other additional EU financing for Ukraine's 2027 budget gap.
- **Criterion:** YES if ČTK (ceskenoviny.cz), ČT24, Seznam Zprávy, Novinky or vlada.gov.cz report a statement Babiš made on 12 Nov 2026 (in Dublin, in Prague, in an interview or on his social media) in which he says unambiguously that Czechia will not take part in, pay into or guarantee a new EU loan or further EU financing for Ukraine's budget needs in 2027. Rejecting only the link between Ukraine funding and the EU long-term budget, or evasive/conditional answers ("we will see", "we will discuss it"), do not count. NO if no such statement is made on 12 Nov (00:00–23:59 Prague time).
