# de-stromsteuer-13
- **Claim:** Between 06:00 on Wednesday 11 November and 23:59 on Thursday 12 November 2026 (Berlin time), the CDU/CSU–SPD coalition announces, or is reported to have agreed, as part of the final changes to the 2027 federal budget, a cut in the electricity tax for all consumers, including private households, taking effect in 2027.
- **Criterion:** YES if, in that window, coalition leaders, the Chancellor, the finance ministry or the coalition's budget spokespeople announce such an agreement, or Tagesschau, Spiegel, Handelsblatt, FAZ, dpa or bundestag.de (hib) report, with a timestamp inside the window, that the coalition has agreed to it (e.g. in its amendments for the Bereinigungssitzung). The cut must lower the standard rate (now 2.05 ct/kWh) for private households and take effect on a date in 2027. NO if no such agreement is announced or reported by 23:59 on 12 Nov (a report after midnight does not count, even about the night session), or if what is agreed is only a test, a promise for a later year, or a cut for businesses only.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-11T06:00+01:00 to 2026-11-12T23:59+01:00 (Europe/Berlin)
- **Written at:** 2026-11-10T18:05Z
