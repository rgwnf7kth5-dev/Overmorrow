# cop31-fossil-23
- **Claim:** The cover decision adopted at COP31 by 23:59 Antalya time on Sunday 22 November 2026 contains the words "fossil fuel" or "fossil fuels".
- **Criterion:** YES if the COP31 cover decision gavelled through between 07:00 on Sat 21 Nov and 23:59 on Sun 22 Nov 2026, Europe/Istanbul time (the decision that counts under cop31-adopt-23), contains anywhere in its adopted text (preamble, operative paragraphs, footnotes, or the title of an initiative or roadmap it names) the string "fossil fuel" or "fossil fuels" (also hyphenated "fossil-fuel"), checked against the adopted text on unfccc.int or a verbatim quote by Reuters, AFP, AP, Carbon Brief, ENB (IISD) or Climate Home News. Mentions of "coal", "oil", "gas" or "transitional fuels" without the words do not count, nor does a reference to "paragraph 28 of decision 1/CMA.5" without them. NO if no cover decision is adopted in that window, or if the adopted cover decision does not contain the words.
