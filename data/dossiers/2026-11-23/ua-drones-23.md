# ua-drones-23
- **Claim (interval):** The number of drones that the Ukrainian Air Force's morning summary says Russia launched at Ukraine in the night of 21–22 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** The UAV total in the Air Force's first summary of the overnight attack published on Sun 22 Nov 2026 (Kyiv time), covering the attack that normally counts from 18:00 Kyiv time on 21 Nov. "Drones" means the strike UAVs as the summary counts them: Shahed-type and jet-powered Shaheds, Gerbera, Parodiya and other decoys, and any jet munitions such as Banderol/Dan-T that the summary includes in its UAV total. Missiles listed separately are not counted. Source: the Air Force Telegram channel (t.me/kpszsu) or Facebook page, or Ukrinform, Ukrainska Pravda, Kyiv Independent or Interfax-Ukraine repeating it. A summary issued late in the morning or around noon (as after the large attacks on 17 and 20 Nov) still counts as the first summary. Separate daytime reports and later corrections do not count. If the Air Force reports no overnight attack, the value is 0.
- **Event window:** attack 2026-11-21T18:00:00+02:00 to 2026-11-22T12:00:00+02:00 (Europe/Kyiv, EET since 25 Oct). The summary normally comes 08:00–10:00 Kyiv; after very large attacks up to about 12:00 (17 Nov ~11:57; 20 Nov "as of 10:30").

## Risks
- **Skew:** a single mass strike (≥280) on the claim's night would land far above the median. Such nights came every 1–8 nights in the last month, and the last one was on the night to 20 Nov.
- **Back-to-back pattern:** 3 and 4 Nov were consecutive big nights. Big strikes are not always followed by quiet nights.
- **Retaliation cycle:** Putin's vow (19 Nov) was followed by the 20 Nov strike. Further "retaliation" waves are possible, as are Ukrainian strikes on the Crimean Bridge and Russian ballistic launchers.
- **Daytime waves** (19 Nov, evening of 20 Nov) may move drones out of the night count.
- **Diplomacy:** Trump–Putin–Xi "declaration of peace" (19 Nov), Coalition of the Willing meeting in Berlin, and preparations for a POW exchange. No pause in strikes has been announced.
- **Summary format:** if the attack runs into the day, the first summary may be "as of 12:00" with a partial count. The criterion takes the first summary as published.
