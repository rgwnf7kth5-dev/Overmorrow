# it-malpensa-23
- **Claim:** The strike by ENAV air-navigation staff at Milan Malpensa airport called by RSA FAST-CONFSAL-AV takes place on Sunday 22 November 2026: it is not revoked, postponed or barred by a government order.
- **Criterion:** YES if the strike of ENAV staff at Milano Malpensa called by RSA FAST-CONFSAL-AV for Sun 22 Nov 2026 (registered with the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport, MIT) is still in force at the start of its registered hours on 22 Nov, so that a stoppage of any length takes place that day. That means it has not been revoked by the union, postponed to another date, or suspended or prohibited in full by an order of the Minister of Infrastructure and Transport or a prefect (precettazione), or after an intervention of the Commissione di garanzia. A further shortening of the hours counts as YES while some stoppage remains on 22 Nov. NO if, before the registered start on 22 Nov, the MIT strike register shows it as revoked, postponed (differito) or suspended, or an order bars the whole stoppage, or ENAV, ENAC, SEA, ANSA or major Italian outlets report that it has been called off. Turnout and the number of cancelled flights do not count. Missing evidence leaves the claim pending, not NO.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-21T05:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-22T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Rome)
- **Evidence expected:** MIT register status on 22 Nov (scioperi.mit.gov.it, mirrored by scioperiitalia.it); ENAV and ENAC notices; reports by ANSA, MilanoToday, Varesenews, Il Giorno or Corriere on Sun 22 Nov.

So the mirror may lag on revocations.
- ENAC protected bands for air-transport strikes are 07:00–10:00 and 18:00–21:00 (DeepArrival, ENAC page, as above). The 13:00–17:00 stoppage lies outside both. Viaggi-USA adds that intercontinental departures must be protected for at least half of each carrier's flights outside the bands. As of 18 Nov, ENAC had not posted a 22 Nov guaranteed-flight list (Viaggi-USA).

## Why a binary "held" claim and not a cancellations figure
- The strike now lasts 4 hours on a Sunday afternoon, outside the protected bands. No source found publishes a stable count of strike cancellations at Malpensa: SEA's site rejects scripted reading, and ENAC posts guaranteed-flight lists, not cancellation totals. Any count would come from scattered press reports that might never appear. So a figure claim fails the "deciding source will publish" check. The held/revoked status is recorded in the MIT register and by ENAV and the press.
