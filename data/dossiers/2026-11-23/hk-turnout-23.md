# hk-turnout-23
- **Claim:** The overall voter turnout in the 31 contested subsectors of Hong Kong's 2026 Election Committee Subsector Ordinary Elections on Sunday 22 November 2026, as first announced officially at the close of poll, is … %. (Interval claim.)
- **Criterion:** The overall cumulative turnout rate (%) for all contested subsectors at the close of poll (18:00 HKT) on Sun 22 Nov 2026, as first published by the Registration and Electoral Office (REO) / Electoral Affairs Commission (EAC) — the "Overall (Contested Subsectors)" figure on https://www.elections.gov.hk/ecss2026/eng/turnout.html or the REO/EAC press release on info.gov.hk. The first official close-of-poll figure counts; later revisions do not. If polling does not take place on 22 Nov the claim has no value (postponement); if no official figure is published by the resolver's deadline the claim stays pending.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-22T09:00:00+08:00 to 2026-11-22T23:59:00+08:00 (Asia/Hong_Kong); polling 09:00–18:00 HKT = 02:00–11:00 CET.
- **Evidence expected:** REO hourly cumulative turnout on the election website through polling day; the 18:00 HKT figure (11:00 CET) is the close-of-poll figure (in 2021 the 18:00 row gave the final 89.77%). EAC press release on completion of counting overnight.
- **Deadline (resolves by):** 2026-11-24T05:00:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-20T18:20:00Z

## Consensus and scale
- No consensus or official forecast exists. **Centre: 92.4%** = 2021 subsector turnouts re-weighted to the 2026 contested electorate (own arithmetic above). It lies between 2021's 89.77% (which included Education at 83.94%) and the 2025 by-elections' 97.33%.
- Scale: no series (`node scripts/refdata/series.mjs scale hk-ec-subsector-turnout` → basis "published or pooled", n 0, pooled_factor 1.353). Used **pooled**: sample sd of the two overall EC-subsector turnouts under the new system (89.77, 97.33) = 5.35 pp × 1.353 = **7.2 pp**. n=2 is thin; there is no surprise series.
- `node scripts/refdata/models.mjs quantiles 92.4 7.2 1.6` → {"q10":77.6365,"q50":92.4,"q90":107.1635}. Turnout cannot exceed 100%; the upper tail must be truncated, and forecasters should skew (both new-system results were ≥ 89.8%).

## Risks
- Lower: the contested electorate is much larger and more individual-heavy than in 2021 (2,784 Area-Committee members); a 09:00–18:00 single day; large ballots slow voting; electronic poll register (EPR) problems (the 2023 DC turnout release counts votes "as at midnight", i.e. polling ran beyond the normal close; the cause was not checked in a source for this dossier). The 2021 count took 14 hours (Wikipedia), which affects results, not turnout.
- Higher: the government and pro-Beijing groups mobilise for a symbolic high turnout (2025 by-elections 97.33%; Commercial (Third) 100% in two hours); corporate voters vote through authorised representatives.
- Definitional: the REO labels hourly figures "for reference only"; the claim takes the first official close-of-poll overall figure. A separate figure for dedicated polling stations could be added later.
