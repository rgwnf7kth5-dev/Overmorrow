# gulf-ukmto-23
- **Claim:** UKMTO or JMIC reports at least one new attack or security incident involving a merchant vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, the Persian Gulf, the Gulf of Oman or the Arabian Sea that took place between 08:00 Gulf time on Saturday 21 November and 23:59 Gulf time on Sunday 22 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if the UK Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) or the Joint Maritime Information Center (JMIC) reports a qualifying incident involving a merchant vessel that occurred between 08:00 Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4) on 21 Nov 2026 and 23:59 GST on 22 Nov 2026.
  - Qualifying incidents: an attack, explosion, mine strike, projectile or drone hit, hostile approach (armed craft firing on or attempting to board the vessel), boarding, hijacking or seizure.
  - Area: the Strait of Hormuz, the Persian (Arabian) Gulf, the Gulf of Oman or the Arabian Sea (including the waters around Socotra).
  - Accepted evidence: ukmto.org, UKMTO's X account, or a wire, gCaptain, Lloyd's List, Khaleej Times, Gulf News, Arab News, Iran International, Ship & Bunker, Al Jazeera, Newsquawk or another established news outlet quoting UKMTO or JMIC.
  - Timing: when UKMTO/JMIC give only a date and no time, an incident dated 22 Nov counts. An incident dated 21 Nov without a time also counts, unless the report places it before 08:00 GST. A report published after the window (including a "time-late" report) counts if the incident occurred in the window.
  - Military action: a merchant vessel disabled, boarded or seized by US or other military forces counts only if UKMTO or JMIC itself reports that incident.
  - Not counted: suspicious approaches, hailings or sightings without weapons fire or a boarding attempt; GPS or navigation-aid interference alone; notices of an "incident" or "suspicious activity" that state no attack, explosion, hit, boarding or seizure; incidents in the Gulf of Aden, the Bab el-Mandeb or the Red Sea; claims by Iran (including IRGC statements), the Houthis or other parties that neither UKMTO nor JMIC reports.
  - NO if no such incident occurred in the window.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-21T08:00:00+04:00 to 2026-11-22T23:59:00+04:00 (Asia/Dubai) = 21 Nov 05:00 CET to 22 Nov 20:59 CET. **Note:** the task asked for 00:00 Sat. 00:00 GST on 21 Nov is 20 Nov 21:00 CET, before the morning freeze (21 Nov 05:00 CET), so the window opens at the freeze, 08:00 GST.
- **Evidence expected:** UKMTO Warning/advisory (ukmto.org, X @UK_MTO), relayed by gCaptain, Arab News, Gulf News, Newsquawk, Iran International and Anadolu. JMIC advisory notes. UKMTO weekly overview (Fridays). evidence_at 2026-11-23T12:00:00+04:00.

## Risks
- **Late reporting:** UKMTO dated strikes to 13 Nov only on 17 Nov, and the 17 and 18 Nov strikes came as time-late/delayed reports. An incident reported after 24 Nov 05:00 CET would be missed by the resolver.
- **Under-reporting:** UKMTO says its figures "do not capture every incident".
- **Quiet days do happen** (12, 14, 19 Nov; 8–9 Nov as a pair).
- **De-escalation:** an Iran–Oman route agreement or US–Iran talks. No pause in attacks has been announced.
- **Escalation:** an Iranian tanker convoy out of Kharg, US interdictions (count only if UKMTO/JMIC reports them), and US strikes on Qeshm.
- **Geography:** Gulf of Aden and Red Sea incidents (frequent this week) do not count. Far-offshore piracy east of Socotra counts only with firing or a boarding attempt.
- **Vague notices** and IRGC/Houthi claims alone do not count.
