# ua-pow-23
- **Claim:** Russia and Ukraine carry out a prisoner-of-war exchange (any size) between 06:00 Kyiv time on Saturday 21 November and 23:59 Kyiv time on Sunday 22 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if Zelensky, Ukraine's Coordination Headquarters for the Treatment of Prisoners of War or the Russian Defence Ministry states that an exchange of prisoners of war between Russia and Ukraine took place between 06:00 Kyiv time on 21 Nov and 23:59 Kyiv time on 22 Nov 2026, with prisoners returned to both sides. Any size counts, including the first stage of a multi-stage exchange. Returns of bodies, one-way releases, and returns of civilians only (with no POWs) do not count. A statement published after 23:59 on 22 Nov counts if it says the exchange took place in the window. NO if no such exchange took place in the window. If no statement can be found by the deadline, the claim stays pending.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-21T06:00:00+02:00 to 2026-11-22T23:59:00+02:00 (Europe/Kyiv) = 21 Nov 05:00 CET to 22 Nov 22:59 CET.

## Risks
- **Timing words point later:** "next week" said on 19 Nov points to 23–29 Nov, and "coming weeks" is vaguer still. Large exchanges in the past have come 1–9 days after "next week" statements.
- **Lists:** Russia had not handed over its lists as of 19 Nov (per Zelensky). List verification by the intelligence services is ongoing (Kyiv Post headline).
- **Escalation:** the 20 Nov mass strike, the Poland incident and Crimean Bridge strikes could delay or harden positions. Exchanges have often gone ahead despite strikes.
- **Weekend timing is possible:** past large swaps were held on Fridays–Sundays (e.g. May 2025).
- **Mixed format:** if the swap includes civilians, it still counts as long as POWs are returned to both sides.
- **Staged exchanges:** a first stage in the window counts.
