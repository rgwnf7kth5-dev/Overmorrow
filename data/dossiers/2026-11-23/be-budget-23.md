# be-budget-23
- **Claim:** The five parties of Bart De Wever's coalition (N-VA, MR, Les Engagés, Vooruit, CD&V) announce an agreement on the federal budget by 23:59 Brussels time on Sunday 22 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 05:00 on Sat 21 Nov 2026 and 23:59 on Sun 22 Nov 2026 (Europe/Brussels), Prime Minister Bart De Wever or his office, or the five coalition parties (N-VA, MR, Les Engagés, Vooruit, CD&V), announce that all five have reached an agreement on the federal budget (the 2027 budget or the budget framework under negotiation), as reported by Belga, VRT NWS, Le Soir, De Standaard or RTBF. An agreement that excludes Vooruit or any other of the five parties does not count; an agreement announced after 23:59 on Sun 22 Nov does not count. NO if no such agreement is announced in that window. A report published after 23:59 about an announcement made within the window counts.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-21T05:00+01:00 – 2026-11-22T23:59+01:00 (Europe/Brussels)
- **Evidence expected:** night-time announcement by the PM or party presidents (X posts, press statement), reported live by VRT NWS / Belga; evidence_at 2026-11-23T08:00+01:00.
- **Deadline (resolves by):** 2026-11-24T05:00:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-20T18:15Z

- Timeline: De Wever tendered his resignation; the King put it on hold — Eunews 2 Nov: "The Palace has announced that the King 'is keeping his decision on hold'" — https://www.eunews.it/en/2026/11/02/belgian-government-in-turmoil-over-budget-king-puts-prime-minister-de-wevers-resignation-on-hold/ (published 2026-11-02T12:20+01:00, read 2026-11-20T18:07Z). Note discrepancy: Brussels Times dates the resignation to 6 Nov ("De Wever handing in his resignation on 6 November") — https://www.brusselstimes.com/2412834/vooruit-not-invited-to-new-round-of-one-on-one-budget-meetings-with-pm-de-wever-tbtb (date not shown, read 18:07Z). VRT 16 Nov: "The king had asked him to return after the Belgian leader tendered his resignation two weeks ago." The 16 Nov audience was a second report, at which the King gave him another week (not a fresh refusal of a resignation).
- Talks collapsed on Sunday night 15 Nov; four of five parties accepted De Wever's framework, Vooruit refused — VRT NWS 16 Nov https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/en/2026/11/16/empty-handed-pm-bart-de-wever-heads-to-king/ (published 2026-11-16T08:32+01:00, read 18:06Z) "Four out of the five parties are said to have given the green light to De Wever's new proposal, but it was Flemish socialist Vooruit ... which continues to stand in the way". Same article: "This was certainly not a final deal yet, but rather a sort of framework within which the measures still had to be worked out in detail."
- Size of the package: the government agreed to find €10 billion extra by 2029; De Wever then narrowed the exercise to the 2027 budget — VRT NWS FR (Belga) 15 Oct https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/fr/2026/10/15/budget-federal-de-wever-abandonne-la-reforme-de-la-tva-et-se-c/ (published 2026-10-15T13:46+02:00, read 18:07Z) "Le gouvernement fédéral a convenu de trouver 10 milliards d'euros supplémentaires d'ici 2029". Eunews 2 Nov: "a 10-billion-euro recovery plan to be implemented by 2029"; deficit "5 per cent of GDP" last year. Elements of the 15 Nov framework: healthcare cuts reduced "from over 3.6 billion euros to roughly 2.5 billion euros"; index saving "currently estimated at around one billion" (VRT 16 Nov, above); Vooruit says the index saving rose "van 800 miljoen naar 1,2 miljard euro" (VRT liveblog). No total 2027 figure for the framework found in today's reporting.
- Vooruit sidelined: De Wever held one-on-ones with Van Peteghem (CD&V) and Prévot (Les Engagés) on Tuesday and Clarinval (MR) on Wednesday, not with Vandenbroucke — Brussels Times (above) "De Wever has planned one-on-one meetings with the Vice PMs of each party, except Health Minister Frank Vandenbroucke". Confirmed by VRT NWS 19 Nov https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/en/2026/11/19/pm-bart-de-wever-talking-with-all-coalition-partners-bar-vooruit/ (published 2026-11-19T08:41+01:00).
- Vooruit's position (Fri 20 Nov): Rousseau has had contact with N-VA chair Valerie Van Peel but not with De Wever since the collapse; waits for an invitation — VRT liveblog (above): "Rousseau zegt dat hij nu wacht op een uitnodiging van de premier. - Main sticking point: a contribution from millionaires; De Wever's possible offer is lowering the securities-account tax threshold from €1m to €750,000, which Vooruit "voorlopig nog afketsen" — VRT liveblog (above).

- No sign of an exit: Rousseau says he wants to keep negotiating and to avoid the government falling and new elections (VRT liveblog, 20 Nov). Vandenbroucke (16–17 Nov): "Denkt u dat ik zin heb om in een kiescampagne te stappen?" (VRT liveblog).
- Pressure points: Bouteca: "Als Vooruit zich als een kat in het nauw gedreven voelt, kan die er wel eens de stekker uit trekken" (VRT liveblog). Vooruit excluded from one-on-ones since 17 Nov; Anders available as partial replacement (RTBF, 76/150 seats).
- A Vooruit exit would most plausibly come at or after Monday's audience, not before; no party meeting or congress of Vooruit announced for the weekend (none found).

## Risks
- A deal struck in a Sunday-night session but announced after 23:59 (or early Monday before the Palace) → NO as worded.
- A four-party deal without Vooruit, or with Anders → does not count.
- Ambiguity of "agreement": a "framework" agreement (like the one four parties accepted on 15 Nov) announced by the PM as an accord of all five would count; partial agreements or "progress" statements would not.
- De Wever may go to the King on Monday with a report of progress rather than a deal, or offer his resignation again; neither is a YES.
