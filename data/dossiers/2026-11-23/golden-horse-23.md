## Risks
- Spread: Arrested Memory's nominations are mostly craft categories where Girl (cinematography, editing, art, makeup, sound) and Kung Fu (art, makeup, action, sound, VFX) compete directly; a split of craft awards can leave it with 2–3 wins and a tie.
- A top-category sweep by Double Happiness (feature, director, screenplay, actor, actress, supporting actor) or We Are Born Good (actress, supporting actress, new director, screenplay) could give either the lead with 4–5 awards.
- Jury taste (president Sylvia Chang) is unknown; Golden Horse juries often spread awards.
- Counting: the criterion counts competitive categories only and treats a tie as NO; a category with two co-winners or a jury special award (none listed among the categories) is not counted.
