# cn-trilateral-20
- **Claim:** Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin hold their three-way meeting in Shenzhen on Thursday 19 November 2026 and, by 23:59 Shenzhen time, an agreement of the three countries to hold further talks on nuclear arms control (or another meeting in the three-way format) is announced in a joint statement or by at least two of the three governments.
- **Criterion:** YES if (a) Trump, Xi and Putin meet together in person in Shenzhen on 19 Nov 2026 (all three in the same meeting, as reported by the White House, the Kremlin or China's foreign ministry/Xinhua, or by two major outlets) AND (b) between the start of that meeting and 23:59 Asia/Shanghai on 19 Nov an 'agreed follow-up' is announced. An agreed follow-up is an agreement of all three countries to start or hold further trilateral talks, negotiations, consultations or a working/expert group on nuclear arms control, nuclear arms reduction or strategic stability, or to hold another meeting in the US–China–Russia format at any level, announced in a joint three-way statement or by at least two of the three governments (US: Trump himself, a White House statement/readout/fact sheet, the Secretary of State; Russia: Putin, Peskov, Ushakov, the Kremlin or the MFA; China: Xi as reported by Xinhua, the MFA). Vague wording ('stay in touch', 'maintain communication', 'continue dialogue') without naming further talks/consultations or another meeting does not count; a US–Russia-only follow-up does not count. NO if no qualifying follow-up is announced by 23:59 Shenzhen time on 19 Nov, or if the three-way meeting does not take place on 19 Nov (postponed, cancelled, or only separate bilaterals). ## Risks
- Trump announces on his own (Truth Social, press gaggle) that "we agreed to start talks" — counts only if a second government (most likely Russia, via Ushakov) also states it. Russia has an interest in announcing a continuation of the format (status), which could make a US+Russia announcement plausible even if China is silent.
- Ushakov habitually briefs about "agreed to continue contacts"; the criterion excludes vague wording unless further talks/consultations or another meeting in the three-way format are named. Borderline wording is the main resolution risk.
- A joint three-way statement mentioning "strategic stability dialogue" would count (a qualifying follow-up → claim YES under the positive wording).
- Meeting format changes (e.g. Kim joins): still counts if the three meet in the same meeting.
- Meeting cancelled or moved to 18/20 Nov → claim NO.
