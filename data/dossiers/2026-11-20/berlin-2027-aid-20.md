# berlin-2027-aid-20
- **Claim:** The Coalition of the Willing summit in Berlin on Thursday 19 November 2026 ends with a joint commitment to military support for Ukraine in 2027 that is stated as a monetary amount.
- **Criterion:** YES if, on 19 Nov 2026 (by 23:59 Berlin time), the co-chairs' or leaders' statement of the Berlin meeting, or Chancellor Merz, President Macron or Prime Minister Burnham at the post-summit press conference, states a monetary amount (in euros, dollars or pounds) for military support to Ukraine in 2027 that is collective — a total pledged or agreed by the participants, or a joint target they commit to (e.g. "partners commit to provide €X billion of military aid in 2027").
