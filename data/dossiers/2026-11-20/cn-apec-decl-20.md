# cn-apec-decl-20
- **Claim:** APEC leaders meeting in Shenzhen adopt a joint Leaders' Declaration by the close of the summit on Thursday 19 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, by 23:59 Asia/Shanghai on 19 Nov 2026, the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting adopts or issues a Leaders' Declaration (a document issued in the name of the APEC economic leaders collectively, under whatever name, e.g. "Shenzhen Declaration"), as published on apec.org, by Xinhua/China's MFA, or reported by two major news agencies. A Chair's Statement or host-economy statement alone does NOT count; topical joint statements (e.g. on AI) do not count unless a Leaders' Declaration is also issued. A declaration accompanied by a Chair's Statement (as in Lima 2024) counts. Publication on apec.org after midnight still counts if the adoption at the meeting on 18–19 Nov is reported. NO if no leaders' declaration is adopted by 23:59 Shenzhen time on 19 Nov, or if the meeting is postponed.

## Facts
- APEC's official list of leaders' documents (read 2026-11-17T17:54Z, https://www.apec.org/meeting-papers/listings/leaders-declarations): 2025 Gyeongju Declaration, 2024 Machu Picchu Declaration, 2023, 2022, 2021, 2020 Leaders' Declarations; "2019 Host Economy Leader's Statement" (Chile cancelled the meeting); "2018 Chair's Statement" (Port Moresby, no declaration); 2017, 2016, 2015, 2014 Leaders' Declarations.

## Base rate
- APEC leaders' meetings held 2014–2025 (2019 cancelled): 11 held, 10 ended with a Leaders' Declaration (or equivalent collective leaders' text: 2021 Leaders' Declaration), 1 with only a Chair's Statement (2018, US–China dispute over trade wording). Source: apec.org listing above. Class rate of a declaration: 10/11.
- The 2018 failure came during an open US–China trade war, with the US and China as the two disputants — the same dyad as now, though relations are now under a truce.
