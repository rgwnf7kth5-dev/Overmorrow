- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 and 23:59 Berlin time on 19 Nov 2026, the US government (White House, State Department or envoys Kushner/Witkoff), the Kremlin (Peskov, Ushakov), the Ukrainian presidency, or a co-chair of the Berlin Coalition of the Willing meeting (Merz, Macron, Burnham) publicly announces a specific calendar date (day, or a start date of a multi-day round) for a meeting of negotiators that includes the joint European team together with Russian, US and Ukrainian officials. ## Facts
- Ushakov (16 Nov): "The date has not yet been agreed and the expansion of the format ... has not been agreed with us"; there "may be necessary to hold another round, or perhaps more than one round, of trilateral talks" first — Euronews (same URL).
- Kushner, after speaking with Ushakov: preparations under way for a multilateral meeting "in the near future" — Euronews (same URL).
- European team composition: France, Germany, UK, Italy, Poland, EU Council and Commission, NATO; Commission spokesperson: "We are absolutely ready to engage at the multilateral level"; European officials privately say expectations "should remain low" — Euronews (same URL).
- Zelensky (17 Nov): US envoys, Ukrainian officials and European allies meet in Berlin this week; Kushner earlier said Moscow was open to European participation — https://kyivindependent.com/zelensky-announces-berlin-talks-with-us-european-allies-to-shape-next-phase-of-russia-negotiations/ (published 2026-11-17T14:27Z, read 2026-11-17T17:56Z).
- Stubb (FT interview, 17 Nov) urges European leaders to talk to Putin directly; "there will be no deal without Putin's agreement" — https://kyivindependent.com/finlands-stubb-urges-europe-to-take-russia-talks-into-its-own-hands/ (published 2026-11-17T09:39Z, read 2026-11-17T17:57Z).
- Last trilateral round: Abu Dhabi, ended without a ceasefire breakthrough; only concrete outcome a planned prisoner exchange — Kyiv Independent (17 Nov, above).
