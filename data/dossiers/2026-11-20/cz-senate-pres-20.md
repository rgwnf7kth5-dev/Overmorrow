# cz-senate-pres-20
- **Claim:** On Thursday 19 November 2026, at the constituent (1st) session of its 16th term, the Czech Senate re-elects Miloš Vystrčil (ODS) as its President with at least 62 votes (his 2024 total).
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 and 23:59 Prague time on 19 Nov 2026, the Senate elects Miloš Vystrčil President of the Senate and the officially announced result (senat.cz voting record / ČTK, ČT24 report of the secret ballot) gives him 62 or more votes in the round in which he is elected. NO if he receives 61 or fewer votes in that round, if he is not elected, if someone else is elected, or if the election does not take place on 19 Nov (postponement = NO).

- **Evidence expected:** ČTK/ČT24 report and senat.cz record on 19 Nov, around midday (in 2024 the result was out by about 11:00–12:00); evidence_at 2026-11-19T14:00+01:00

## Risks
- ANO's 14 senators are now a government party facing a Senate its leader attacks; they may vote against or abstain in larger numbers than in 2024 → fewer votes.
- Absences (illness, the two-site Grolich hejtman role, a senator not sworn in) cut the number of ballots; each absent supporter lowers the count by one.
- A "rally" effect: STAN, KDU-ČSL and the non-attached senators (ex-Pirates, SEN 21) gained after ANO's zero-seat result and may give a fuller count than 2024 → more votes.
- The threshold sits exactly on his 2024 total: a one-vote difference decides.
- The criterion counts votes in the winning round; if a second round were needed (no sign of this), the second-round figure counts.
