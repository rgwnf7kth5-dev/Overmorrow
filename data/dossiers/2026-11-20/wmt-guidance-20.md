## Risks
- Management guided Q3 profit low (adjusted operating income +2–4%) because of price investment funded by tariff refunds; it may hold the full-year EPS range and only nudge sales guidance (NO).
- Narrowing the range to e.g. $2.82–2.85 keeps the midpoint at $2.835 → NO; a narrowing to $2.83–2.87 (midpoint $2.85) → YES. Criterion uses the midpoint only.
- More than $2bn of extra fuel-related costs and Vibe integration costs.
- Currency: guidance in USD includes FX effects; a stronger dollar can trim the EPS range.
- If Walmart moves to a single-point guide ("about $2.85"), the single figure is compared with $2.835.
