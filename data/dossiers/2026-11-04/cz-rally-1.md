# cz-rally-1
- **Claim:** The rally in support of ČT and ČRo on lower Wenceslas Square in Prague on 3 November 2026 draws at least 10,000 people, by an estimate of the organisers or the police.
- **Criterion:** YES if, by 23:59 Prague time on 3 Nov 2026, ČTK or ČT24 reports an estimate by the organisers (the Veřejnoprávně initiative or co-organisers named by ČTK) or by the Police of the Czech Republic that the Prague rally of 3 Nov on Václavské náměstí (Můstek) was attended by at least 10,000 people. A figure of 10,000 or more, or wording "desítky tisíc" (tens of thousands) or "přes deset tisíc", attributed to the organisers or the police, counts. "Tisíce", "tisícovky" or a figure below 10,000, estimates by journalists or third parties only, no estimate at all, or a rally that does not take place on 3 Nov resolve NO. If organisers and police give different figures, the higher one counts.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-03T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-03T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Prague)
- **Written at:** 2026-11-01T18:20Z. Read times between 17:45Z and 18:10Z on 1 Nov.
