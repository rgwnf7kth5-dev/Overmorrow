# cz-energy-1
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Prague time on 3 November 2026, E.ON Energie, innogy Energie or Pražská energetika (PRE) publicly announces its 2027 electricity or gas prices for households on open-ended tariffs.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 06:00 on 2 Nov and 23:59 Prague time on 3 Nov 2026 at least one of E.ON Energie, innogy Energie or Pražská energetika publicly announces (press release, new price list on its website, or statement to media reported by ČTK or another major Czech outlet) the household price of electricity or gas on open-ended (non-fixed) products that will apply from 1 January 2027, either as new prices, as a stated percentage or CZK/MWh change, or as an explicit statement that these prices stay unchanged from 1 January 2027. Changes to fixed-price products only, and statements without a figure or a clear "unchanged" (e.g. "sledujeme vývoj", "budeme zdražovat"), do not count. NO otherwise.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-02T06:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-03T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Prague)
- **Written at:** 2026-11-01T18:25Z. Read times are 2026-11-01 between 17:55Z and 18:15Z unless stated.

## Schedule slip
- Slip class: none (`node scripts/refdata/slip.mjs none` returns "unknown class none", run 2026-11-01T18:10Z). No scheduled event.

## Risks
- YES paths: an E.ON press release in the first week of November, as on 6 Nov 2025; a ČTK or E15 round-up where a spokesperson gives a figure or says "beze změny"; customer letters reported in media; a PRE notice to its open-ended customers.
- NO paths: the 30-day rule leaves until about 1 Dec; suppliers may wait for ERÚ's final regulated prices (end-November), since the regulated part is in the total price; answers like "sledujeme trh".
- Resolution traps: fixed products do not count; a change effective in December 2026 is not "from 1 January 2027"; statements by innogy's or E.ON's parent firms abroad do not count; a statement only about business customers does not count.
