# us-house-dem-1
- **Claim:** The Democratic Party wins a majority (at least 218 of 435 seats) in the US House of Representatives in the elections of 3 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if AP race calls (or, for any seat AP has not called, the state's certified result) show Democratic candidates winning at least 218 of the 435 House seats in the general election held on 3 Nov 2026. Seats are counted by the party label under which the winner was elected; independents are not counted as Democrats. NO if Republicans and others win at least 218 seats, or if the general election is not held on 3 Nov 2026. If neither side has reached 218 when the resolver reads the evidence, the claim stays pending (late counting is not a NO).
- **Event window:** 2026-11-02T19:00:00-10:00 (00:00 ET on 3 Nov) to 2026-11-03T23:59:00-10:00 (Pacific/Honolulu; the last polls, in Hawaii and the Aleutian Islands, close on 3 Nov in this zone). Voting by 3 Nov decides the outcome; AP calls and certification come later.

## Risks
- Redistricting: about 6 net seats moved toward Republicans on paper, so a national popular-vote win does not guarantee 218.
- A generic-ballot miss like 2014 (polls overstated Democrats) or concentrated Democratic votes in safe seats.
- Litigation or federal interference over counting (DOJ California suit) could delay the 218th call past the resolver's date (pending, not NO).
- Vacancies and independents: winners elected as independents (none prominent in House races) do not count toward the 218.
