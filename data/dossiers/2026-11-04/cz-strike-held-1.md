# cz-strike-held-1
- **Claim:** Employees of Česká televize or Český rozhlas hold a strike on 3 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if on 3 Nov 2026 employees of ČT or ČRo stop work in a strike or warning strike against the bill abolishing the licence fees, as reported by ČTK, ČT24, iROZHLAS or another major Czech outlet. A symbolic action without a work stoppage (a statement only, a broadcast continuing as normal with a symbol on screen, or a rally outside working hours) does not count. NO otherwise, including if the strike is called off or postponed. (Same as board question cz-media-strike-3-nov.)
- **Event window:** 2026-11-03T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-03T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Prague)
- **Written at:** 2026-11-01T18:20Z. Read times are approximate, between 17:45Z and 18:10Z on 1 Nov unless stated.

## Facts

- **Broadcast impact planned:** ČT24 cuts live output and reruns older programmes; Události without prompters; news hourly instead of every half hour; at ČRo most staff take part and some departments strike in full — Refresher (above) "vysílání bude velmi omezené".
- **ČRo management:** spokeswoman Lorencová: "Stávka se ve vysílání projeví a posluchači budou o důvodech programových změn informováni." — EnergoZrouti https://energozrouti.cz/clanek/ceska-televize-kvuli-stavce-omezi-vysilani-reprizy-a-hlavni-zpravy (published 2026-11-01 15:50, from sweep notes, not re-opened).

## Risks
- Called off on 2 Nov after the coalition council (no sign of this as of 1 Nov).
- A format so reduced that outlets describe it as symbolic; the criterion requires a work stoppage, and the planned cuts to ČT24 and ČRo output imply one.
- An emergency on 3 Nov interrupting the strike after it starts would still leave a work stoppage that day (YES if it began).
