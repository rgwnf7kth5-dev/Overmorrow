# cz-dukovany-2
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Prague time on 3 November 2026, KHNP names a new construction partner for Dukovany II to replace the Daewoo E&C–Doosan Enerbility consortium, or announces that it will carry out the construction works itself.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 06:00 on 2 Nov and 23:59 Prague time on 3 Nov 2026 KHNP, Elektrárna Dukovany II or the Czech Ministry of Industry and Trade publicly states, as reported by ČTK, Yonhap or another major outlet, either that KHNP has selected a named company or consortium (other than Daewoo E&C–Doosan Enerbility) for the Dukovany II construction (civil) works, or that KHNP will perform those works itself instead of through an external main construction contractor. Lists of candidates, talks with named firms, "preferred negotiating partner" reports, the launch of a tender, and statements that KHNP is considering doing the work itself do not count. NO otherwise.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-02T06:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-03T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Prague). Seoul is 8 hours ahead, so a Korean announcement during KST working hours on 3 Nov and 4 Nov morning up to 07:59 KST falls inside the window.
- **Written at:** 2026-11-01T18:30Z. Read times are 2026-11-01 between 17:50Z and 18:15Z unless stated.

## Why this next step
- The 3 Nov edition's claim cz-dukovany-1 (events of 1–2 Nov) covers formal termination of the cooperation agreement or a new tender by 2 Nov. Naming a replacement (or self-performance) is the next distinct, checkable step. Other options were weaker: a Czech government "formal comment" is hard to define; no Babiš/Havlíček meeting with the Korean side is scheduled or reported; the Korean ministry has already spoken (1 Nov).

## Schedule
- Nothing is scheduled. No KHNP, EDU II or MPO announcement is dated 2–3 Nov.

## Facts
- ČTK, 1 Nov: Korea's trade, industry and energy minister Kim Čong-kwan wrote on X that Korea will not back away from completing the Dukovany expansion and criticised KHNP over the failed talks (must show more responsibility and leadership, listen to partner companies). KHNP "will consider its next steps"; the article has no Czech government reaction — https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/korea-podle-ministra-neustupuje-od-zavazku-dokoncit-rozsireni-dukovan/2894657 (published 2026-11-01 15:57, read 17:52Z) "rozdílné postoje k zásadním podmínkám".
- Analyst Petr Zámečník (Electree), same ČTK report: "Pokud se KHNP nepodaří rychle získat náhradu, bude to vytvářet tlak na časový harmonogram". Petr Lajsek (Purple Trading): KHNP withdrawal "by pro ni znamenalo obrovskou reputační i finanční ránu".
- KHNP statement to ČT, 30 Oct: the reason is "rozdílné postoje stran k zásadním smluvním podmínkám"; the schedule, "jehož dodržení pro nás nadále zůstává nejvyšší prioritou"; KHNP wants to choose a new construction contractor and keep key project work going — ČT24 https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/ekonomika/korejci-hledaji-nove-stavebniky-pro-dukovany-cesi-jim-pripominaji-cenu-a-sankce-379156 (published 2026-10-30, read 17:58Z). No date for a new contractor is given.
- ČTK/ČT24, 29 Oct: KHNP ended talks with the consortium; it is weighing ending the cooperation agreement, plans to prepare a new tender, and will secure continuity of key work partly by doing it directly — https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/ekonomika/khnp-se-nedohodla-na-smlouve-na-stavebni-prace-v-dukovanech-379114 (published 2026-10-29, read 2026-10-31T18:04Z, per cz-dukovany-1).
- EDU II CEO Petr Závodský: "za neplnění podmínek obsahuje případné sankce"; signed contracts, including Doosan Škoda Power turbines, stay valid. ČEZ's Daniel Beneš: "Rozhodně se to nemůže zlehčovat, je to zásadní zpráva" and it may be "jen cenové vyjednávání přes média" — ČT24 30 Oct (above).
- Havlíček (29 Oct, paraphrase by ČTK/ČT24): the schedule is not endangered according to his Korean counterpart; KHNP is ready to talk to other contractors and enough large firms are interested (per cz-dukovany-1).
- KHNP's procurement: more than 60 Czech firms passed KHNP supplier qualification; KHNP planned its first tender (auxiliary equipment and materials, not civil works) for November 2026; building-permit documentation due April 2027; unit 1 completion 2036 — oEnergetice https://oenergetice.cz/en/nuclear-power/more-than-60-czech-firms-meet-requirements-for-dukovany-khnp-to-launch-first-tender-in-november (published 2026-09-16 12:26, read 18:08Z).
- Timetable: construction of unit 1 to start in 2029; total CZK 407bn for two units at last year's prices (ČTK 1 Nov, above).
