# us-iran-ship-15
- **Claim:** UKMTO or JMIC reports at least one new attack or incident involving a merchant vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, the Gulf of Oman or the Persian Gulf that occurs on Saturday 14 November 2026 (Gulf time).
- **Criterion:** YES if the UK Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) or the Joint Maritime Information Center (JMIC) reports an attack, explosion, mine strike, projectile hit, hostile approach (armed craft firing on or attempting to board the vessel), boarding or seizure involving a merchant vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, the Gulf of Oman or the Persian Gulf that occurred between 00:00 and 23:59 Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4) on 14 Nov 2026, as shown on ukmto.org or by a wire, gCaptain, Lloyd's List, Khaleej Times, Gulf News, Arab News, Iran International, Ship & Bunker, Al Jazeera or another established news outlet quoting UKMTO or JMIC. When UKMTO/JMIC give only a date and no time, that date decides. A merchant vessel disabled, boarded or seized by US or other military forces counts only if UKMTO or JMIC itself reports that incident. A report published after the window counts if the incident occurred in it. Suspicious approaches, hailings or sightings without weapons fire or a boarding attempt, GPS or navigation-aid interference alone, notices of an "incident" or "suspicious activity" that state no attack, explosion, hit, boarding or seizure, and claims by Iran (including IRGC statements) or other parties that neither UKMTO nor JMIC reports do not count. NO if no such incident occurred on that day.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-14T00:00:00+04:00 to 2026-11-14T23:59:00+04:00 (= 13 Nov 21:00 CET to 14 Nov 20:59 CET); evidence by 2026-11-15T00:00+01:00 usually; resolver reads by 2026-11-16T05:00+01:00 because UKMTO classifies some incidents a day or more late (Warnings 203-26 and 204-26 for 10-11 Nov incidents were posted on 12 Nov).
