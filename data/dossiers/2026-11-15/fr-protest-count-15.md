# fr-protest-count-15
- **Claim:** The French Interior Ministry counts at least 50,000 demonstrators nationwide on Saturday 14 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if the nationwide number of demonstrators in France on Saturday 14 Nov 2026 (all marches and rallies that day, Paris included), as stated by the Interior Ministry and published by it or reported by AFP, Le Monde, franceinfo, BFMTV or Le Figaro citing the ministry or "les autorités" as a national figure, is 50,000 or more. If several figures appear, the last one given by 23:59 on 15 Nov counts. Union, organiser, Occurrence or media counts do not count. NO if that figure is below 50,000, or if no demonstrations take place on 14 Nov. Void if the ministry gives no national figure for 14 Nov by 23:59 on 15 Nov 2026 (Paris time).
