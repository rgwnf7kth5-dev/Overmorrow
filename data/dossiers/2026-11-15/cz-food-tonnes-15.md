# cz-food-tonnes-15
- **Claim:** In the autumn Sbírka potravin held in shops on Saturday 14 November 2026, the first official total announced by the organisers is at least 780 tonnes of food and toiletries.
- **Criterion:** YES if the first official total for the 14 Nov 2026 in-store collection announced by the Česká federace potravinových bank and/or Svaz obchodu a cestovního ruchu ČR (press release, or as reported by ČTK/ČT24 citing them) is 780 tonnes or more of goods (food plus drugstore/hygiene items combined). If that first announcement gives both an in-store Saturday figure and a combined figure including online/company donations, the in-store Saturday figure counts.
