# my-malacca-ph-15
- **Claim:** In the Melaka (Malacca) state election on Saturday 14 November 2026, Pakatan Harapan wins at least 5 of the 28 state seats (at least its 2021 tally).
- **Criterion:** YES if the official results of the 16th Melaka state election published by the Election Commission of Malaysia (SPR; dashboard.spr.gov.my / spr.gov.my/prudunmelaka16) for polling held on 14 Nov 2026 show that candidates standing for Pakatan Harapan (PKR, DAP, Amanah) won 5 or more of the 28 seats. Seats won by Bersama, Bersatu or independents do not count, even if they later align with PH. NO if PH wins 4 or fewer, or if polling is not held on 14 Nov 2026 (if polling is postponed in some seats, count PH seats declared for 14 Nov polling). Official results published overnight count. If SPR's site is unreachable, results announced by the EC and reported by Bernama, The Star, NST or Malaysiakini count.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-14T08:00+08:00 to 2026-11-14T23:59+08:00 (Asia/Kuala_Lumpur); evidence: SPR official results, Saturday evening to early Sunday local time; resolver reads by 2026-11-15T12:00+01:00.
- **Written at:** 2026-11-12T18:08Z
- Companion of my-malacca-bn-15 (same election, same sources). The two claims are strongly but not perfectly correlated: seats may also go to Bersatu, Bersama or independents.

## Facts
- 2021: PH won 5 seats (35.80%); PKR lost all its seats; the five were Bukit Katil (Amanah), Ayer Keroh, Bandar Hilir, Kesidang, Kota Laksamana (DAP) — Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Malacca_state_election (read 2026-11-12T17:54Z) "Pakatan Harapan (PH) suffered a major defeat, winning just 5 seats, with the People's Justice Party (PKR) losing all its contested seats"; seat list from The Vibes https://www.thevibes.com/articles/news/129314/melaka-state-election-battle-hinges-on-urban-strongholds (published 2026-11-09T08:02+08:00, read 2026-11-12T17:53Z).
- PH contests 24 seats; Adly Zahari: "We have 18 focus seats. We must defend the seats we currently hold"; BN targets the four DAP seats — The Vibes (9 Nov), URL above.
- Adly: PH needs about 30% of Malay votes; "if we fail to even reach 20%, we risk losing some of the seats we won previously"; Bukit Katil 65% Malay, 29.7% Chinese, 4.26% Indian; majority 1,057 in 2021 (3,159 in 2018) — The Vibes https://www.thevibes.com/articles/news/128779/ph-risks-losing-melaka-seats-with-modest-election-target-warns-analyst (published 2026-10-26T09:58+08:00, read 2026-11-12T17:54Z).
- In 2021 Bukit Katil was won by Adly "comfortably" — The Vibes live blog https://www.thevibes.com/articles/news/47727/EC-confirms-BN-clinches-melaka (read 2026-11-12T17:54Z); in 2021 BN and PN ran separately, now they run as a pact.
- Narrow 2021 BN wins over PH (potential PH pickups): Pengkalan Batu (131 votes), Serkam (84) — The Vibes live blog 2021, URL above. In both, PN also stood in 2021 (e.g. Pengkalan Batu PN 2,608), so the pact now adds PN's vote to BN.
- Ilham Centre (NST, 9 Nov): about 13% undecided, mostly Gen Z; BN–PN cooperation "may pose a threat to PH in constituencies that have seen an increase in Malay voters"; PH's campaign marketing "not particularly appealing" — NST https://www.nst.com.my/amp/news/regional/2026/11/1573660/melaka-election-final-push-win-over-voters (published 2026-11-09T07:05+08:00, read 2026-11-12T17:53Z). No numeric survey published.
- BN–PN aim to "spring surprise" in Kesidang — NST https://www.nst.com.my/amp/news/regional/2026/11/1573230/melaka-polls-bn-pn-aim-spring-surprise-kesidang (published 2026-11-08T13:01+08:00, read 2026-11-12T17:55Z).
- Bersama (Rafizi Ramli's party) fields 16 candidates; Bersatu 7 — The Star (10 Nov), URL above.
- Precedent Negeri Sembilan (1 Aug 2026): PH fell from 17 to 11 of 36 seats against a BN–PN pact; DAP's Anthony Loke lost Chennah to MCA — Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Negeri_Sembilan_state_election (read 2026-11-12T17:55Z). Ilham Centre estimated PH's Malay support there at about 18% and Chinese support "no longer automatic" — The Star https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/07/31/negri-polls-bn-pn-on-track-to-win-majority-says-ilham-centre (read 2026-11-12T17:56Z).
- GE15 (2022): PH won 3 of Melaka's 6 parliamentary seats (Alor Gajah, Hang Tuah Jaya, Kota Laksamana) — NST (9 Nov), URL above.
- PH left the state government in July 2026 (DAP on 14 Jul; coalition reaffirmed 16 Jul) — Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Malacca_state_election (read 2026-11-12T17:54Z).

## Base rate
- Own count, n=1: PH defending seats against a BN–PN pact in 2026 (Negeri Sembilan): PH kept 11 of 17 (65%), i.e. did not hold its previous tally: 0 of 1.
- Seat-level: of PH's 5 seats, 3 Chinese-majority DAP urban seats (Kesidang, Kota Laksamana, Bandar Hilir) are its core; Ayer Keroh is mixed; Bukit Katil is Malay-majority with a 1,057 majority in a three-cornered 2021 race. YES requires holding all five or offsetting losses with gains.

## Risks
- Vote splitting by Bersama in urban/mixed seats could cost PH narrow seats; Bersatu splitting the Malay vote could help PH in Bukit Katil.
- Chinese turnout and the Negeri Sembilan pattern (MCA win in a DAP seat) are the main downside risks; any BN gain in Ayer Keroh or Kesidang makes YES unlikely unless PH picks up a seat elsewhere.
- PH's 18 focus seats include possible gains; a hung or close result would push PH above 5.
