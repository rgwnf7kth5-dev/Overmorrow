# cop31-sb-close-15
- **Claim:** At the COP31 climate conference in Antalya, both subsidiary bodies (SBSTA 65 and SBI 65) close their sessions by 23:59 Antalya time on Saturday 14 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if the Earth Negotiations Bulletin (ENB) daily report for 14 November 2026 (or the UNFCCC record / SB reports) shows that both the SBSTA 65 Chair and the SBI 65 Chair declared their sessions closed at or before 23:59 Europe/Istanbul time on 14 Nov 2026 (closure on an earlier day also counts). NO if either body is declared closed at 00:00 on 15 Nov or later (e.g. the ENB reports a closing time such as "12:23 am"), or if either session is suspended and not closed by 23:59 on 14 Nov. The time given by ENB (local time) is decisive; if ENB gives no time, a UNFCCC or Carbon Brief / Climate Home News report of the closing time is used.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-12T00:00+03:00 to 2026-11-14T23:59+03:00 (Europe/Istanbul)
- **Written at:** 2026-11-12T18:00Z

## Risks
- Joint vs separate plenaries: if SBSTA closes before midnight but SBI after, the claim is NO.
- A plenary may be suspended before midnight and resumed after; ENB gives the time of the final closure, which decides.
- ENB does not normally publish on Sunday; the 14 Nov report likely appears Mon 16 Nov (COP30's Saturday report was dated Monday 17 Nov 2025). Carbon Brief / Climate Home News usually report the time earlier.
- The SBs could close early on Saturday afternoon (as at COP30), which counts YES.
