# us-trump-depart-15

- Claim: Donald Trump departs the United States mainland (contiguous 48 states) on Air Force One for his Asia trip (Manila ASEAN–US Special Summit, APEC Shenzhen) by 23:59 ET on Saturday 14 November 2026.
- Criterion: YES if, by 23:59 ET (America/New_York) on Sat 14 Nov 2026, Trump has taken off from Joint Base Andrews (or any other airport in the contiguous US) on the outbound leg of the Manila/Shenzhen trip, as shown by the White House schedule/pool reports or two major outlets. NO if his outbound departure happens later or the trip is cancelled. A stop in Alaska or Hawaii after a departure by the deadline still counts as YES.
- Event window: 2026-11-12T00:00-05:00 to 2026-11-14T23:59-05:00
- Written at: 2026-11-12T18:00Z
- Recommendation: DROP as a 14-Nov main (most likely resolves NO; see arithmetic below).

## Schedule
- Manila arrival per ASEAN organisers' media advisory: 17:30 PHT Tuesday 17 Nov (= 04:30 ET Tue 17 Nov; PHT is UTC+8, EST UTC−5, 13 h difference). Not confirmed by the White House. — Philstar, 12 Nov.
- ASEAN–US Special Summit is the last engagement of 17 Nov; Trump–Marcos bilateral confirmed by the chairship spokesperson. APEC leaders' meeting Shenzhen 18–19 Nov.
- No White House announcement of the departure time/date or of any stopover found as of 12 Nov 17:55 UTC.
- Arithmetic: Washington–Manila needs a refuelling stop for Air Force One (2017: Andrews–Hawaii 10-hour leg; route via Hawaii/Anchorage/Guam), total roughly 20–24 h. Arrival 04:30 ET Tue ⇒ departure about Mon 16 Nov 04:30–08:30 ET, i.e. likely Sunday night or Monday morning ET. A Saturday departure requires an unannounced stopover of 1.5+ days (e.g. Hawaii, Guam, Japan).
- Earlier reports (White House to NY Post, 12 Oct) said Trump would be in Manila on 16 Nov; the 11–12 Nov Philippine schedule moved his participation to 17 Nov.

## Base rate
Own count, n=2 (Trump's ASEAN-season Asia trips 2017 and 2025): departure from Washington preceded the first Asian arrival by about 1.5 calendar days (2025: Fri→Sun; 2017: Fri→Sun via Hawaii). Applied to a Tue 17 Nov 17:30 PHT arrival, both precedents point to a Sunday 15 Nov (ET) departure, not Saturday. Rate of departure ≥2.5 days before first arrival: 0/2.

## Schedule slip
Class summit-or-visit, rate 0.012 (slip.mjs, estimate, 0/18 own). Signs of movement: the Manila date already moved from 16 to 17 Nov between the October announcements and the 11–12 Nov Philippine advisories; no White House confirmation of arrival time; an added stop (Hawaii/Guam/Japan/Alaska) would shift departure earlier.

## Risks
- An unannounced stopover (e.g. Hawaii, Guam, Japan) could bring departure forward to Saturday — that is the main YES path.
- Domestic politics after the 3 Nov midterms (Democrats won both chambers, per streamlinefeed) could keep him in Washington longer, pushing departure later.
- Resolution needs exact take-off time: White House pool reports/schedule give it; ET vs UTC confusion near midnight.
