# my-malacca-bn-15
- **Claim:** In the Melaka (Malacca) state election on Saturday 14 November 2026, candidates of the Barisan Nasional–Perikatan Nasional pact (BN's 23 nominees plus PN's 5) win at least 24 of the 28 state seats.
- **Criterion:** YES if the official results of the 16th Melaka state election published by the Election Commission of Malaysia (SPR; dashboard.spr.gov.my / spr.gov.my/prudunmelaka16) for polling held on 14 Nov 2026 show that candidates standing for BN (UMNO, MCA, MIC) and for PN under the BN–PN seat-sharing pact together won 24 or more of the 28 seats. Bersatu's 7 candidates, who stand separately from the pact, and any independents do not count, even if they later align with the pact. Post-poll defections or coalition deals do not count. NO if the pact's candidates win 23 or fewer seats, or if polling for the general election is not held on 14 Nov 2026 (a postponement of polling in one or two seats only: count the seats declared on 14 Nov night; if fewer than 24 are declared for the pact, NO). Official results published overnight (after 23:59 local) count if they concern polling held on 14 Nov. If SPR's site is unreachable, results announced by the EC and reported by Bernama, The Star, NST or Malaysiakini count.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-14T08:00+08:00 to 2026-11-14T23:59+08:00 (Asia/Kuala_Lumpur); evidence: SPR official results, Saturday evening to early Sunday local time; resolver reads by 2026-11-15T12:00+01:00.

## Base rate
- Own count, n=1: BN–PN pact in a 2026 state election (Negeri Sembilan, 1 Aug): the pact's "last election" tally on the Wikipedia infobox is 17 seats (Bersatu's 2 listed separately) and it won 25 (+8) — the pact improved on its components' previous tally: 1 of 1. Too small to weigh heavily.
- Structural: pact components held 23 of 28 seats in 2021 (BN 21 + PN 2), but both PN seats were Bersatu's, and Bersatu now stands against the pact. Reaching 24 needs a net gain of at least one seat over 2021, from PH seats (Bukit Katil most exposed) or from holding the former Bersatu seats.

## Risks
- Bersatu (7 seats) and Bersama (16 seats) split votes in multi-cornered fights; Bersatu defends its 2021 base (Sungai Udang, Bemban).
- Chinese-majority DAP seats (Kesidang, Kota Laksamana, Bandar Hilir) likely stay PH even in a wave; Ayer Keroh is mixed.
- Low turnout among PH supporters (analyst Azmi Hassan) would help the pact; the 13% undecided (Ilham) could break either way.
- Counting of pact seats: SPR lists results by party/coalition; PN candidates may appear under PN or party labels (PAS etc.) — the criterion counts the 5 PN pact nominees whatever label is shown; Bersatu does not count.
- Claimed targets (25–26) are party statements, not surveys.
