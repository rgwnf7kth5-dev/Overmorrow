# starship-booster-catch-15

- Claim: The Super Heavy booster (Booster 22) of Starship Flight 15 is caught by the Starbase launch tower's arms between 05:30 CET on Fri 13 Nov 2026 and 23:59 CST on Sat 14 Nov 2026.
- Criterion: YES if Flight 15 lifts off in the window 2026-11-13T05:30+01:00 to 2026-11-14T23:59-06:00 and Booster 22 is caught and held by the launch tower's chopstick arms at Starbase, as shown by SpaceX's webcast/@SpaceX or reported by Space.com, SpaceNews or Spaceflight Now. NO if there is no liftoff in the window, if the booster is diverted to the Gulf, splashes down, is lost, or hits the tower without being held.
- Event window: 2026-11-13T05:30:00+01:00 (= 2026-11-12T22:30-06:00) to 2026-11-14T23:59:00-06:00
- Written at: 2026-11-12T18:15Z

## Schedule
- SpaceX (mission page and @SpaceX, 7 Nov): Flight 15 targets Fri 13 Nov 2026, 75-minute window 4:45–6:00 p.m. CST (22:45–24:00 UTC), "pending regulatory approval". Still the target on 12 Nov per Space.com onsite live blog.
- FAA licence: not issued as of 10–11 Nov (SpaceX "pending regulatory approval"; NSF "awaits FAA green light"; X reply 11 Nov "NOTAM, not the launch license"). Not verified on 12 Nov.
- Booster catch planned at ~T+6:35 (Fri ~16:52 CST if on time). Ship catch planned at ~T+22:32:34 (Sat ~15:17 CST if Friday launch at window open; Sunday if launch slips a day).
- Schedule status: reported/official target, licence pending → "official" date but conditional.

## Base rate
Own count from the Wikipedia list (sources in Facts): booster tower-catch attempts Flights 5–8: 3 caught of 4 attempts (Flight 6 waved off to the Gulf because of tower damage at liftoff) = 0.75, n=4 (all Block 1/Block 2 boosters, Pad 1).
V3 (Block 3) booster return record, Flights 12–14: landing burn completed with controlled splashdown 1 of 3 (B19 and B20 lost after engine relight failures; B21 controlled despite several engine shutdowns during descent). No V3 booster has yet attempted a catch; this is the first catch at Pad 2.
The claim also needs the launch in window (launch base rate 3/5 above), so the unconditional rate is lower than either figure alone.

## Schedule slip
Class `launch`, rate 0.300 (slip.mjs, prior 0.30, own 0/0, estimate) — P that the launch slips (the catch follows ~6.5 minutes after liftoff) out of its day; our window spans Fri and Sat CST, so a single 24-h slip still resolves YES.
Signs it may move: FAA licence not issued as of 11 Nov (SpaceX still says "pending regulatory approval"); the date already moved in FAA planning slides (8 → 12 → 13 Nov) before SpaceX fixed 13 Nov; first booster RTLS of a V3 booster and first ship RTLS over populated Mexico/Texas raise licence and range complexity; South Texas weather (not checked). Signs it holds: SpaceX and Space.com (onsite, 12 Nov) still on 13 Nov; Booster 22 on the pad 10 Nov; FAA TFRs/NOTAMs for Fri launch and Sat ship catch issued 11 Nov (per unofficial report).

## Risks
- FAA licence: if not issued by Fri afternoon, launch moves; licence for a ship RTLS over land is new.
- Scrubs: Flights 10, 12, 13 each had at least one scrub (ground systems, weather, T−0 engine abort).
- Timezone: window end is 23:59 CST Sat 14 Nov = 05:59 UTC Sun 15 Nov. Friday window 22:45–00:00 UTC crosses midnight UTC.
- V3 booster engine relight problems on Flights 12–14 (boostback/landing burn); a go/no-go poll before the return can divert the booster to the Gulf.
- Pad 2 tower has never caught a booster.
