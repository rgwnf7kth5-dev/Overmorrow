# cz-babis-israel-18
- **Claim:** On Tuesday 17 November 2026, during his visit to Israel, PM Andrej Babiš publicly states that the Czech Republic will move its embassy in Israel from Tel Aviv to Jerusalem, or that his government has decided or will decide to move it, or names a date or time frame for the move.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 Israel time (23:00 Prague time on 16 Nov) and 23:59 Prague time on 17 Nov 2026, Andrej Babiš says publicly (press statement, press conference, remarks to journalists, interview, his own social-media post or video), or the Czech Government Office (vlada.gov.cz / its official accounts) states, that (a) the Czech Republic will move / is going to move its embassy to Jerusalem, or (b) the government has decided, or will decide, to move it, or (c) the move will happen by a stated date, year or period (e.g. "next year", "by the end of this government's term"), as reported by ČTK, ČT24, iROZHLAS, Seznam Zprávy, Novinky, Aktuálně.cz, Times of Israel, Jerusalem Post or the Israeli President's/PM's office, or visible in his own recording. A statement that he personally supports the move counts only if it also says the Czech Republic will move it or the government will decide to do so. Does NOT count: that the issue will be discussed, examined or "is on the table"; that it is a government decision without saying which way; that it depends on the EU, on peace talks or on another unmet condition; upgrading or expanding the existing Jerusalem office of the Tel Aviv embassy (in place since 2021) or a Czech House/cultural centre; statements only by Macinka or other ministers. Statements made on 16 or 18 Nov do not count (a repetition on 17 Nov of an earlier announcement does count). NO otherwise, including if Babiš rules the move out or if the 17 Nov programme does not take place.
