# rs-speaker-18

- **Criterion:** YES if, by 23:59 Belgrade time on 17 Nov 2026, the National Assembly votes to elect as its Speaker a person proposed by the "Studentska lista – Studenti pobeđuju" list (or its parliamentary group), as shown on parlament.gov.rs or reported by N1, Vreme, Beta, Tanjug, RTS or B92. ## Baseline
status_quo: yes — the Speaker election is item 2 on the convened agenda, the Student List holds 140/250 and says it will proceed immediately after verification; if things go as announced the vote happens on 17 Nov.

## Risks
- Duration: mandate verification (verification committee report, debate) plus Speaker debate with 103 SNS-list MPs able to take the floor may push the vote past midnight; under Serbian practice a constituent session can continue on following days.
- Student List internal process (plenums) delays naming a candidate or a Deputy Speaker deal.
- Procedural disputes with the SNS-appointed acting chair Radenović over agenda/order.
- Speaker vote held but the candidate is not a Student List nominee (very unlikely given 140 seats).
