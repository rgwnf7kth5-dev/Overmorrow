# cz-babis-fees-18
- **Claim:** On Tuesday 17 November 2026 PM Andrej Babiš publicly states that the compulsory ČT and ČRo licence fees will be abolished or that the government will not withdraw its bill abolishing them.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 and 23:59 Prague time on 17 Nov 2026, Andrej Babiš says publicly (to journalists in Israel, at the Havel bench in Tel Aviv, in an interview, or in his own social-media post or video) either (a) that the government/coalition will not withdraw ("nestáhne") the bill abolishing the ČT/ČRo licence fees, or (b) that the compulsory licence fees will be abolished / that the government is sticking to abolishing them ("poplatky zrušíme", "na zrušení poplatků trváme", including "poplatky budou dobrovolné" or that they may end earlier than 2028), as reported by ČTK, ČT24, iROZHLAS, Seznam Zprávy, Novinky or Aktuálně, or visible in his own recording. A statement that the bill will be changed or that a new financing model will come in January counts as YES only if accompanied by (a) or (b). Statements by other ministers (e.g. Havlíček, Schillerová at Národní) or ANO officials do not count. Statements made on 16 or 18 Nov do not count. NO if he makes no such statement on 17 Nov (including if he does not speak about the issue or only says "we will discuss it" / "we respect the protesters" / "I disagree with the reasons for the protest"), or if he announces that the bill will be withdrawn, frozen or suspended, that the fees will be kept, or that abolition depends on a referendum or other condition not yet met.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-17T00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-17T23:59+01:00 (Europe/Prague)
- **Evidence expected:** remarks to Czech journalists accompanying him after the Herzog/Eisenkot meetings or at the Havel bench; any 17 Nov video on his Facebook/X; ČTK/ČT24 reports same day. evidence_at 2026-11-17T20:00+01:00
