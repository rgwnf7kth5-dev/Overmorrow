# fr-strike-18
- **Claim:** The French Interior Ministry counts at least 300,000 demonstrators nationwide on the strike and protest day of Tuesday 17 November 2026. (Converted by the desk at 2026-11-15T18:00Z from an interval claim to a binary claim, because the edition already has three interval claims; facts below unchanged.)
- **Criterion:** YES if the nationwide number of demonstrators in France on Tue 17 Nov 2026 (all marches and rallies that day, Paris included) given by the Interior Ministry ("ministère de l'Intérieur", "Beauvau" or "les autorités" as a national figure), as published by the ministry or reported by AFP, Le Monde, franceinfo, BFMTV or Le Figaro, is 300,000 or more. If several ministry figures appear, the last one given by 12:00 Paris time on 18 Nov counts. Union (CGT), organiser, Occurrence or media counts do not count. NO if the figure is below 300,000, or if no strike/protest day takes place on 17 Nov. If the protest day takes place but the ministry gives no national figure by 12:00 on 18 Nov 2026, the claim stays unresolved (not NO).
