# cz-korzo-18
- **Claim:** The organisers of Korzo Národní report that at least 110,000 people came to Prague's Národní třída on 17 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if the first attendance figure for Korzo Národní / Národní třída on 17 Nov 2026 published by the organisers (spolek Díky, že můžem / Korzo Národní, typically based on T-Mobile mobile data), as reported by ČTK (ceskenoviny.cz) or posted on the organisers' website or official social-media accounts, is 110,000 or more. A figure stated as "more than / over 110,000" counts as YES; a range counts by its midpoint; "about 110,000" counts as YES. If the organisers later revise the figure, the first figure decides. Police or other third-party estimates do not count. NO if the first organiser figure is below 110,000, or if Korzo Národní does not take place on 17 Nov. If the figure is published after 23:59 on 17 Nov, it still decides the claim (first figure published by 20 Nov 2026 23:59).
