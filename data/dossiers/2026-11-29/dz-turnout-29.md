# Dossier dz-turnout-29

Claim (draft): Turnout in Algeria's municipal (APC) elections on Saturday 28 November 2026, as announced by the electoral authority ANIE, exceeds the 36.58% recorded in 2021.

Criterion (draft): YES if the national turnout for the municipal people's assemblies (APC) that ANIE's president announces for the elections of 28 Nov 2026 (first announcement of the final national turnout at the close of polls, normally the evening of 28 Nov or the next day) is above 36.58%. NO if it is 36.58% or lower, or if the elections are not held on 28 Nov 2026 (Africa/Algiers). Hourly interim figures do not count. If ANIE gives only a combined APC/APW figure, that figure counts.

Threshold problem (see claims JSON, criterion_fix): 36.58% is the 2021 APC turnout from ANIE's PROVISIONAL RESULTS (announced Tue 30 Nov 2021). The 2021 figure announced at the close of polls (the same kind of figure the draft criterion resolves on) was 35.97% (APC) / 34.39% (APW). The draft compares a 2026 election-night figure with a 2021 revised figure. In both 2021 (locals, +0.61 pp) and July 2026 (legislative, +0.45 pp) the provisional-results figure came out higher than the election-night figure. Proposed: threshold 35.97% (like-for-like election-night figures).

## Risks

- Threshold mismatch: with 36.58% (a revised figure) vs a 2026 election-night figure, a 2026 result between 35.97% and 36.58% would be "equal to 2021 like-for-like" but resolve NO.
- Who announces: organisation moved to the Interior Ministry under the amended electoral law; ANIE supervises. If the Interior Ministry rather than ANIE announces the national turnout, the draft's "ANIE's president announces" fails literally. In July 2026 ANIE's interim president still announced the night figure.
- Timing: in 2021 the night figure came at the last press conference late on polling day (published by algerie360 08:45 UTC next morning); in 2024 it came after 23:00 with delays from floods. Polls may be extended by one hour (done in July 2026).
- Figure type: ANIE may label it "taux moyen" (2024) or give APC and APW separately (2021); the APC figure is normally slightly higher than APW.
- Downward pressure: July 2026 legislative record low (20.79%); parties struggled to fill lists. Upward pressure: new territorial division, more registered voters, locals historically higher than legislatives.
- Small change in electorate (24.24 m vs 23.72 m in 2021) has no direct effect on the rate.
