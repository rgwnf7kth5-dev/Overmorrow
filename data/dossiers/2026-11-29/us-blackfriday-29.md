## Risks

- Intraday partial: Adobe/media publish "so far" figures on Friday evening (2025: $8.6bn by 6:30 pm ET). The criterion should exclude these; only the full-day figure counts.
- Upside surprise: season to date running +9.1% vs +6.7% season forecast; Adobe's single-day forecasts were beaten in all six 2024–25 cases. A first figure of $13.0–13.2bn is plausible.
- Downside: consumer weakness/tariff-driven price effects (Salesforce 2025 saw volumes down, prices up); a demand pull-forward into the record pre-Thanksgiving days ($4bn+ days already seen).
- Format: Adobe may give "$12.9 billion" vs "$12.94 billion"; resolver should take the number as stated (2022 used two decimals, $9.12bn).
- Resolves_by (23:00 ET) is one hour before the criterion's 23:59 ET cut-off.
