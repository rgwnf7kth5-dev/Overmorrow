# gulf-ukmto-29
- **Claim (text_fix):** UKMTO or JMIC reports at least one attack, explosion, mine strike, projectile or drone hit, armed hostile approach, boarding or seizure involving a merchant vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, the Persian Gulf, the Gulf of Oman or the Arabian Sea that took place on Saturday 28 November 2026 (00:00–23:59 Gulf time).
- **Criterion (criterion_fix):** as gulf-ukmto-28, shifted to Saturday 28 Nov. YES if UKMTO or JMIC reports a qualifying incident involving a merchant vessel that occurred between 00:00 and 23:59 Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4) on 28 Nov 2026.
  - Qualifying: attack, explosion, mine strike, projectile or drone hit, hostile approach (armed craft firing on or attempting to board), boarding, hijacking or seizure. Proposed addition: an explosion of a projectile reported by UKMTO/JMIC close to a merchant vessel counts even if UKMTO files it as "suspicious activity" and reports no damage (case 222-26, below).
  - Area: Strait of Hormuz, Persian (Arabian) Gulf, Gulf of Oman, Arabian Sea (incl. waters around Socotra).
  - Evidence: ukmto.org, UKMTO's X account, or a wire or established outlet quoting UKMTO or JMIC.
  - Date only: an incident dated 28 Nov counts. Late reports count if the incident falls in the window. UTC conversion: 20:00–23:59 UTC on 27 Nov falls on 28 Nov; after 20:00 UTC on 28 Nov falls on 29 Nov.
  - Not counted: suspicious approaches without weapons fire or boarding attempt; GPS interference alone; bare "incident"/"suspicious activity"/"tanker and military forces" notices stating no attack, explosion, hit, boarding or seizure; Gulf of Aden, Bab el-Mandeb, Red Sea; Iranian, Houthi or other claims not reported by UKMTO/JMIC; military action against a merchant ship unless UKMTO/JMIC itself reports it.
