## Risks
- Polls are split on direction; the average is a Shen lead of ~3 pp but the two independent media pollsters (TVBS, ETtoday) both have Chiang ahead (+2, +6.1). Either side can win; this is not an "obvious outcome".
- Turnout gap: several polls show Shen ahead among "certain to vote" (FTNN 49.6–43.8; Hsinmin 54.4–35.6; TIA reports DPP supporters 89.9% certain vs KMT 79.4%, TPP 65.5%).
- TPP voter loyalty to Chiang (75–94% in polls) and Vivian Huang's non-endorsement.
- Ten-day blackout: no published data since 17 Nov; late shifts are invisible.
- Resolution nuance: a recount (difference under 0.3%) or court challenge does not change the result per the criterion; CEC count on election night is final for us.
