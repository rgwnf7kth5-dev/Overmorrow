# cz-babis-congratulates-vucic

**Claim:** By 12:00 on 26 October 2026, Andrej Babiš publicly congratulates Aleksandar Vučić or his list on the result of Serbia's 25 October parliamentary election.
**Criterion:** YES if, between the close of polls (20:00 CET, 25 Oct 2026) and 12:00 CET on 26 Oct 2026, Babiš congratulates Vučić, the "Aleksandar Vučić – United Serbia" list or SNS on the election result, in a post on his own social media (X, Facebook, Instagram) or in a statement reported by ČTK or the Government Office. Comments that do not congratulate (e.g. "we respect the result") do not count; congratulating another list does not count. Otherwise NO.
**Deadline:** 2026-10-26T12:00:00+01:00
**Written at:** 2026-10-23T19:20:00+02:00

## Schedule
- Serbian election Sunday 25 Oct 2026; polls close 20:00 CET; CeSID/Ipsos and CRTA projections on election night; RIK preliminary results possibly only Monday evening (see rs-sns-45.md, Rio Times 23 Oct https://www.riotimesonline.com/serbia-final-polls-split-before-sunday-vote/).
- Babiš flies to Algeria on Monday morning 26 Oct (ČTK/Respekt 22 Oct https://www.respekt.cz/informacni-servis/babis-s-havlickem-miri-na-sever-afriky-v-alzirsku-budou-dva-dny-2026-10-22-15-53).
- Schedule: the election is official; the congratulation is speculative.

## Facts
- Babiš visited Belgrade on an economic mission in July 2026, opening with a working dinner with Vučić and PM Đuro Macut; he said he has known Vučić for a long time, that Serbia belongs in Schengen and the EU, and that Kosovo and Serbia–Croatia relations should be handled by EU leaders (N1, 22 Jul 2026). https://www.n1info.rs/vesti/babis-kosovo-hrvatska-lideri-eu
- Government Office (English) page on the Belgrade talks: "Prime Minister Babiš held talks in Belgrade on boosting trade, expressing his support for Serbia's EU membership" (search result; date not seen). https://uv.gov.cz/en/media-centrum/aktualne/prime-minister-babis-held-talks-in-belgrade-on-boosting-trade--expressing-his-support-for-serbia_s-eu-membership-228307/
- Precedent: after Hungary's April 2026 election, Babiš congratulated winner Péter Magyar on X the next morning (Epoch Times CZ, 13 Apr 2026 07:53), even though the loser Orbán was his ally ("Čelit tak silnému soupeři, jakým je Viktor Orbán, nebylo nikdy snadné"; he will work with whoever voters elect). https://www.epochtimes.cz/ceska-republika/babis-pogratulovat-magyarovi-k-vitezstvi-tesi-se-na-spolupraci-a266102.html
- Polls before the vote are split: Ipsos (pub. 22 Oct, n=3,624, commissioned by ruling list) SNS 47.4 / Students 40.0; NSPM (25 Sep–5 Oct, n=1,050) Students 44.5 / United Serbia 42.7; Rio Times average SNS 44.3, Students 42.5 (Rio Times 23 Oct; see rs-sns-45.md).
- A congratulation is conditional on Vučić's list being declared or claiming the win by Monday morning; Vučić claimed victory on election night in 2023 (see rs dossiers).
- Not found within budget: whether Babiš congratulated Vučić after the Dec 2023 election (Babiš was then in opposition), or after other 2026 foreign elections apart from Hungary.
