# br-lula-share

- **Claim:** Lula's share of valid votes in the 25 Oct 2026 runoff, per TSE.
- **Criterion:** Lula's percentage of valid votes (votos válidos) in the TSE presidential runoff results at 100% totalised, or the latest TSE figure at 26 Oct 2026 12:00 CET.
- **Deadline:** 2026-10-26T12:00:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-23T19:05:00+02:00

## Consensus and usual error (interval claim)
- Consensus: 49.3% = mean of Lula's valid-vote share in the three polls with fieldwork 17–22 Oct (Datafolha 50, Quaest 49, PoderData/Aya 49). Including AtlasIntel 3–8 Oct (47.2): 48.8%. To be updated with final Datafolha/Quaest of Sat 24 Oct if they move it.
- Usual error: rms Datafolha runoff lead error 1.345 pp (2014, 2018, 2022; pipeline/reference-data/poll-error.json, pooled.brazil_datafolha_runoffs); per-candidate share error ≈ half the lead error → scale 0.7 pp. Observed per-candidate errors: +0.36, −0.13, +1.1 (mean abs 0.53). Caveat: n=3; the all-elections pooled sd of party error is 2.61 pp (n=29, mostly Czech multiparty), so 0.7 is the narrow end.
