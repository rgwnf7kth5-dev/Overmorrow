# br-lula-share

- **Claim:** Lula's share of valid votes in the 25 Oct 2026 runoff, per TSE.
- **Criterion:** Lula's percentage of valid votes (votos válidos) in the TSE presidential runoff results at 100% totalised, or the latest TSE figure at 26 Oct 2026 12:00 CET.
- **Deadline:** 2026-10-26T12:00:00+01:00
