# br-lula-wins

- **Claim:** Lula receives more valid votes than Flávio Bolsonaro in the 25 Oct 2026 runoff, per TSE.
- **Criterion:** YES if the TSE results (resultados.tse.jus.br / tse.jus.br), as published by 26 Oct 2026 12:00 CET, show Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva with more valid votes than Flávio Bolsonaro in the presidential runoff (100% totalised, or latest TSE figure at the deadline if totalisation is incomplete but the result is mathematically decided). NO if Flávio Bolsonaro has more, or if the runoff is postponed or TSE publishes no result by the deadline.
- **Deadline:** 2026-10-26T12:00:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-23T19:05:00+02:00

## Risks
- Final Datafolha/Quaest (Sat 24 Oct) can move the average either way; part of Datafolha fieldwork follows the Globo debate.
- Polls in 2022 overstated Lula (Datafolha lead +2.2 pp vs result); a repeat would cut against YES. n=3 makes the error scale fragile; AtlasIntel had Flávio +5.6 (3–8 Oct).
- Turnout differences (abstention record in 1st round) and Northeast vs South/Southeast mobilisation.
- Count timing: very close race could leave result undecided until totalisation; 2022 finished 04:18 CET, well before deadline. Legal challenges do not change the TSE published count.
