# cz-pardubice-coalition-signed

**Claim:** By 12:00 on 26 October 2026, the five groupings negotiating to run Pardubice city hall (ODS, STAN, Progresivní Pardubice, Naše Pardubice, Noví lidovci) have signed a coalition agreement.
**Criterion:** YES if ČTK, iRozhlas, Deník or another major Czech outlet reports, in a report time-stamped no later than 12:00 CET on 26 Oct 2026, that representatives of these five groupings (or of a coalition containing at least ODS and STAN that excludes ANO) have signed a coalition agreement for the Pardubice city council for 2026–2030. A memorandum, "agreement in principle" or announcement of a future signing date does not count. Signing at any time after the 9–10 Oct 2026 election counts, including before this claim was written. Otherwise (no signature, deal collapses, signing set for later) NO.

## Facts
- Council has 39 seats; majority 20. Seats: ANO 10, ODS 7, Žijeme Pardubice 5, STAN 5, Progresivní Pardubice 4, Naše Pardubice 4, SPD 2, Noví lidovci 2. Five-party coalition = 22 seats. (ČTK, 14 Oct 2026) https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/petikoalice-v-pardubicich-ladi-program-subjekty-deklaruji-ochotu-ke-kompromisu-/2886580
- 10 Oct (Sat evening): ANO, STAN and Žijeme Pardubice signed a memorandum on a 20-seat coalition; on 11 Oct STAN councillor and MP Anežka Nedomová disowned it ("Absolutně s tím nesouhlasím…"), removing the majority. (iEuro/ČTK, 21 Oct) https://ieuro.cz/lidr-stan-dohodu-podepsal-jeho-zastupitelka-ji-zablokovala-pardubice-muze-vest-pet-stran-s-rozdilnymi-programy/
- 14 Oct: ČTK said the coalition and programme agreements "could be completed" the following week (week of 19 Oct). (ČTK, 14 Oct)
- 21 Oct: target moved to "this week" / "start of next week" (iRozhlas, 21 Oct). The target has moved twice.
- ANO (mayor Jan Nadrchal) has run Pardubice since 2014 and would go into opposition.
- Comparable cities in 2026: Olomouc five-party coalition signed on 19 Oct (iRozhlas 19 Oct https://www.irozhlas.cz/volby/zastupci-peti-stran-podepsali-v-olomouci-koalicni-smlouvu-primatorem-bude-lidr_2610191713_ntu); Plzeň ANO–STAN–Pro Plzeň signed by 22 Oct (iRozhlas 22 Oct https://irozhlas.cz/volby/koalice-v-plzni-se-dohodla-na-slozeni-rady-mesta-primatorem-zustane-zarzycky_2610221252_elv); Brno signed 23 Oct; Hradec Králové split portfolios but set signing for 2 Nov (Deník https://hradecky.denik.cz/komunalni-volby/koalice-v-hradci-kralove-si-rozdelila-kompetence-podpis-dohody-2-listopadu/).
