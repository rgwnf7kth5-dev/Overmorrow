# bg-iotova-45 — Iotova above 45% in round one

- **Claim:** Iliana Iotova receives more than 45.0% of valid votes in the first round of Bulgaria's presidential election on 25 Oct 2026, per CIK preliminary results.
- **Criterion:** YES if the latest CIK (results.cik.bg / cik.bg) preliminary or final first-round result published by 26 Oct 2026 12:00 CET shows the Iotova–Valchev ticket at more than 45.00% of valid votes, valid votes being as CIK counts them (all candidate pairs plus the "I do not support anyone" option, as in 2016 and 2021). If CIK shows a percentage, that figure decides; otherwise Iotova's votes divided by that valid-vote total. NO if 45.00% or less, if the vote is postponed, or if CIK has published no result by the deadline.
- **Deadline:** 2026-10-26T12:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-23T19:10+02:00

## Schedule
- First round of the presidential (and vice-presidential) election on Sunday 25 Oct 2026; runoff between the top two on 1 Nov 2026 unless a candidate wins outright (official). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bulgarian_presidential_election ; https://sofiaglobe.com/2026/10/05/bulgarias-2026-presidential-elections-yotova-44-5-gyurov-26-1-alpha-research-poll/
- Outright first-round win requires a majority AND turnout of at least 50%; this has never happened. 27 candidate pairs on the ballot. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Bulgarian_presidential_election
- Polls close 20:00 EET = 19:00 CET (Europe is on CET, UTC+1, from 03:00 on 25 Oct; Bulgaria EET = UTC+2). Exit polls and CIK preliminary results (results.cik.bg, by share of processed polling-station protocols) follow overnight — customary cadence, not confirmed for 2026 (sweep note). Presidential-only ballot this time (no concurrent parliamentary vote or referendum found), so counting should be simpler than in 2021.
- How CIK percentages work (2021 and 2016 arithmetic): each candidate's percentage = votes / all valid votes, where valid votes INCLUDE the "I do not support anyone" (none of the above) option. 2021: Radev–Iotova 1,322,385 / 2,675,935 valid = 49.42%; NOTA 60,786 = 2.27%. 2016: NOTA 5.59%, included in the 3,827,650 valid. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Bulgarian_general_election ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Bulgarian_presidential_election. Machine-voting and paper ballots are both counted into the same totals; no source opened describes a separate machine-vote base.

## Risks
- The freshest poll (Gallup) has her below 45; the Trend figure is three weeks old.
- Low turnout composition: Progressive Bulgaria's April 2026 vote (43.9%) mobilised a wider electorate; a presidential-only vote with 37–40% turnout changes the mix in ways polls of "likely voters" may miss in either direction.
- NOTA and the 27-ticket field: minor candidates plus NOTA together took ~12% (2021 others + NOTA, 6.31 + 2.27 + Panov 3.68 ≈ 12.3) and can shave the leader's share.
- Partial count: if CIK is below 100% processed at the deadline, the latest figure decides; urban (Sofia) and abroad protocols tend to arrive late and differ in profile.
- CIK site outage / no publication by 12:00 → NO.
