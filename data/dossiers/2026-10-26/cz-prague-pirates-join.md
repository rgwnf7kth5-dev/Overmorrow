Negotiating teams of STAN, Spolu and Praha sobě meet on Monday 26 Oct, after which they are expected to announce portfolio split. Three-party plan: STAN 5, Spolu 4, Praha sobě 2 council seats, transport to Praha sobě; without a third partner STAN 6, Spolu 5. Pirates wanted 2 seats, STAN offered 1; Hlaváček did not rule out the Pirates changing position. Target: coalition at November constituent session (19 Nov per sweep). https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/regiony/hlavni-mesto-praha/v-hlavnim-meste-asi-vznikne-koalice-stan-spolu-a-prahy-sobe-naznacil-hlavacek-378900
