# rs-sns-45: SNS-led list at 45% or more

- **Claim:** In Serbia's 25 October 2026 parliamentary election, the "Aleksandar Vučić – United Serbia" list gets at least 45.00% in the Republic Electoral Commission's (RIK) results.
- **Criterion:** YES if the vote share that RIK itself prints for list "Aleksandar Vučić – United Serbia" is 45.00% or more, in the most recent RIK preliminary or final results published on rik.parlament.gov.rs (or announced at a RIK session) by 12:00 CET on 27 Oct 2026. The share is RIK's printed percentage. In past RIK releases this is taken over all voters who voted, with invalid ballots included; see Facts. If RIK publishes no results by the deadline, or the election is postponed, the claim resolves NO.
- **Deadline:** 2026-10-27T12:00:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-23T19:10:00+02:00
- **Replaces:** rs-sns-most-votes. Edition 2026-10-25 already carries "rs-sns-first" (SNS list wins most votes). A 45% threshold sits close to the middle of the market distribution and of the polls. A seat-count claim (126 or more seats) was rejected: the 2023 RIK preliminary announcement gave no seat allocation (N1, 18 Dec 2023), so seats may not be published in time.

## Schedule
- The election is on Sunday 25 Oct 2026. RIK set 6,450,523 registered voters when the roll closed (Novosti/Tanjug, 10 Oct 2026: https://www.novosti.rs/drustvo/vesti/1658233/rik-ukupan-broja-biraca-srbiji-parlamentarne-izbore-6-450-523). There are 250 seats, 16 lists and a 3% threshold, with minority lists exempt (Vreme: https://vreme.com/en/izbori-2026/izbori-2026-izborne-liste-rik/).
- Polls close at 20:00 CET (Rio Times, 23 Oct: https://www.riotimesonline.com/serbia-final-polls-split-before-sunday-vote/). Projections from CeSID/Ipsos and CRTA are expected on election night (Wikipedia 2023 page).
- **When RIK publishes (key risk):**
  - **2023:** election on Sun 17 Dec. RIK announced preliminary results at a session that began shortly after 21:00 on Mon 18 Dec, from 96.68% of polling stations (N1, 18 Dec 2023 21:31: https://n1info.rs/vesti/izbori-2023/preliminarni-rezultati-rik-a/).
  - **2022:** RIK said preliminary results would come on Monday 4 Apr after 20:00, with no press conference on election night (biznis.rs: https://biznis.rs/vesti/ipsos-cesid-objavio-prve-preliminarne-rezultate-izbora-rik-se-oglasava-tek-sutra-posle-20-casova/).
  - **2016:** RIK gave figures on election night at about 00:40, from 97.46% of polling stations, including seats (Insajder: https://www.insajder.net/vesti/rik-with-9746-ballots-counted-seven-tickets-in-parliament).
  - **Consequence:** RIK's first figures may come only on the evening of Mon 26 Oct. The deadline is therefore 27 Oct 12:00 CET, not 26 Oct 12:00.
- **Publishes:** RIK session and rik.parlament.gov.rs. Partial figures may come overnight 25/26 Oct, but in 2022 and 2023 the preliminary results came on Monday evening (~20:00–21:00 CET, 26 Oct).

## Facts
- **Polls:**
  - **Ipsos**, published 22 Oct 2026, n=3,624, commissioned by the ruling list; fieldwork dates not stated: SNS 47.4, Students 40.0, SPS 3.2 (Rio Times, 23 Oct).
  - **NSPM**, published 20 Oct, fieldwork 25 Sep–5 Oct, n=1,050, telephone, decided voters: Students 44.5, United Serbia 42.7, 18.3% undecided (Rio Times, 23 Oct). ANSA (22 Oct) gives NSPM, "late September", as 41.7 / 44.4: https://ansa.it/nuova_europa/en/news/sections/news/2026/10/22/latest-polls-in-serbia-indicate-an-uncertain-outcome-for-students-and-vucic_707d7628-9b19-431c-bb4e-08b44d7d252b.html
  - **Faktor Plus** (described by ANSA as government-affiliated): SNS 48.6, Students 37.1. ANSA gives no date; the sweep gives 21 Sep, with SPS 4.9 and European list 3.6 (unverified).
  - **CRTA**, 15 Jul: Students 44.9, SNS 35.7 (Rio Times tracker: https://www.riotimesonline.com/serbia-election-poll-tracker/).
  - **Birodi** (model): Students 48.5 (45.9–51.1), SNS 38.6 (36.3–40.9) (ANSA; N1: https://n1info.rs/english/news/birodi-model-puts-serbias-student-list-at-485-percent-ahead-of-vucics-sns/).
  - **Averages:** the Rio Times tracker (23 Oct) has SNS 44.3, Students 42.5, SPS 3.4. It rests on two pollsters and excludes party-commissioned polls. The PolitPro aggregate (23 Oct) has SNS 45.0, Students 42.5 (Rio Times).
  - **Wikipedia** has no poll after 26 Jun 2026 (Faktor Plus, n=1,200: SNS 47.1, Students 30.7): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_polling_for_the_next_Serbian_parliamentary_election
- **Vučić (N1, 19 Oct):** expects about 4.1 million voters. He says turnout above 63% would give his list significantly more votes, and that his list would get over 50%: https://n1info.rs/english/news/vucic-expects-turnout-of-more-than-4-million-accuses-opponents-of-planning-election-fraud
- **Percentage basis (vote arithmetic):**
  - In 2023, RIK's preliminary figure for the SNS list was 46.71% (1,721,572 votes) (N1, 18 Dec 2023).
  - Wikipedia gives the official result as 48.06% (129 seats), as a share of valid votes.
  - The ratio 46.71/48.06 = 0.972 fits RIK dividing by all voters who voted, invalid ballots included (about 2.8% invalid). This is an inference from arithmetic, not a stated RIK rule.
  - The Rio Times tracker says the threshold is "3% of votes cast".
  - Consequence: RIK's printed share runs about 1–1.5 points below a share of valid votes. Polls of decided voters are closer to the valid-vote basis.
- **Last comparables (SNS list):**
  - 2023: 46.71% (RIK preliminary), 48.06% (official, valid votes) (Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Serbian_parliamentary_election).
  - 2022: Ipsos/CeSID projection 43.6% at 62.5% of the sample (biznis.rs).
  - 2016: 48.25% (RIK, 97.46% of stations) (Insajder).

## Base rate
- **Class:** SNS-led list at or above 45% in RIK or official figures, parliamentary elections with a sourced figure here: 2016 yes (48.25), 2022 no (Ipsos projection 43.6), 2023 yes (46.71 in RIK terms). That is 2 of 3, rate 0.67, n=3. Sources: Insajder, biznis.rs, N1.
- This is the first election with a single united opposition (student) list, so the class is a poor fit.

## Schedule slip
- **Class:** election-held, rate 0.010 (slip.mjs, estimate). No sign of postponement.
- Police arrested six people on 21 Oct over an alleged election-day violence plot (Rio Times, per sweep). Partial reruns at single polling stations happened in 2023; they would delay final results but not preliminary ones.

## For number claims
- **Polls:** non-commissioned average 44.3 (Rio Times), PolitPro 45.0. The range across pollsters is 35.7–48.6.
- **Usual error:** no compiled Serbian poll-error data. pipeline/reference-data/poll-error.json lists Serbia 2020/2022/2023 as "not compiled". Pooled party-level sd of poll minus result there is 2.61 pp (all) and 2.92 pp (Czech parliamentary). Serbian pollsters here differ by up to 13 points on the SNS share, so the spread between pollsters is larger than that.

## Risks
- RIK publishes later than 27 Oct 12:00 CET (resolves NO).
- RIK's all-ballots basis lowers the printed share by about 1–1.5 points against poll-style shares.
- Pollsters split along political lines; the commissioned Ipsos poll is the most favourable to SNS.
- High turnout: Vučić says it favours him, while the opposition claims mobilisation.
- Irregularities or annulled polling stations change the figures between preliminary and final results; the claim takes the latest figure by the deadline.
