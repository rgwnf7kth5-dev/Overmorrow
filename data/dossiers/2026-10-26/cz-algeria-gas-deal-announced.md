# cz-algeria-gas-deal-announced

**Claim:** By 12:00 on 26 Oct 2026, the Czech government, Babiš or ČEZ publicly announces that a gas agreement with Algeria has been or will be signed during the visit.
**Criterion:** YES if, 23 Oct – 12:00 CET 26 Oct, Government Office, a minister, Babiš or ČEZ states that a gas-supply/gas-cooperation document between Czech and Algerian parties has been or will be signed during the 26–27 Oct visit (vlada.gov.cz, cez.cz, ČTK). "Gas will be discussed" does not count. Otherwise NO.
**Deadline:** 2026-10-26T12:00+01:00
**Written at:** 2026-10-23T19:10:00+02:00

## Schedule
- Visit 26–27 Oct 2026 (government statement quoted by Neovlivní 23 Oct). Topics: trade and investment, energy cooperation, defence industry (ČTK via Respekt 22 Oct). Neither source mentions any agreement to be signed. https://www.respekt.cz/informacni-servis/babis-s-havlickem-miri-na-sever-afriky-v-alzirsku-budou-dva-dny-2026-10-22-15-53 ; https://neovlivni.cz/premier-jede-do-alzirska-pro-plyn-potrebuje-ho-i-agrofert/

## Facts
- Babiš (Sept 2026 speech, quoted by Neovlivní): "Obrovský problém bude s plynem. Blíží se zima, nevypadá to vůbec dobře."
- Neovlivní argues cheaper gas matters to Agrofert's chemical division (author's view).
- ČEZ already has a contract with Algeria and extended it (Seznam Zprávy headline "ČEZ prodloužil smlouvu na dodávky plynu s Alžírskem"; date not verified). https://www.seznamzpravy.cz/clanek/ekonomika-finance-cez-prodlouzil-smlouvu-na-dodavky-plynu-s-alzirskem-289326?noredirect=1
- First Algerian gas: ~2 % of Czech annual consumption (e15, Ekonomický deník headlines). https://ekonomickydenik.cz/cez-zacal-odebirat-plyn-z-alzirska-objem-je-skromny-jde-o-2-procenta-rocni-spotreby-ceska/
- Precedent: Babiš's Azerbaijan trip — government release "jednal ... o možnostech zajištění dodávek plynu" (talks, framing as possibilities). https://vlada.gov.cz/cz/media-centrum/aktualne/premier-andrej-babis-jednal-v-azerbajdzanu-o-moznostech-zajisteni-dodavek-plynu-226645/

- Documents usually signed and announced on the day of talks (Monday afternoon) — after deadline → NO even if signed.
- Deal could be between companies other than ČEZ (e.g. MND, Agrofert units) — counts if Czech government/ČEZ announce it; an announcement only by another company does not count under criterion.
