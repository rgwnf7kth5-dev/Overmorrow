# br-margin-under-4

- **Claim:** The runoff winner leads the runner-up by less than 4.0 percentage points of valid votes, per TSE.
- **Criterion:** YES if, in the TSE presidential runoff results (resultados.tse.jus.br / tse.jus.br) at 100% totalised, or the latest TSE figure at 26 Oct 2026 12:00 CET, the winner's share of valid votes minus the runner-up's share is less than 4.00 percentage points. NO if the gap is 4.00 pp or more, or if the runoff is postponed or TSE publishes no result by the deadline.
- **Deadline:** 2026-10-26T12:00:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-23T19:05:00+02:00

## Base rate
- Class: poll-lead model. Lead ~N(−1.3, 1.345) (three-poll average; rms Datafolha runoff lead error, n=3, pipeline/reference-data/poll-error.json) → share of mass with |lead| < 4: 0.976 (python NormalDist). With the all-elections rms lead error 2.734 (n=6, pooled.all) the mass is smaller.
- Last comparable outcomes: runoff margins 2014 3.28 pp, 2018 10.26 pp, 2022 1.80 pp (poll-error.json); 2026 first-round gap 1.87 pp. In 2014 and 2022 Datafolha's final poll showed a 4-pp lead and the result came in under 4.
- Threshold note: the proposed 4.0 pp is kept. Polls point to a margin of 0–3 pp, while the market prices a sizeable 4–8 pp Flávio bucket (0.305) — the threshold sits where polls and market disagree. A 3-pp threshold (Manifold 0.574) is the alternative if a sharper claim is needed.
