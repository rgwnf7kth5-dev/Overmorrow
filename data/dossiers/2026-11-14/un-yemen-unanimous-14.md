# un-yemen-unanimous-14
- **Claim:** By 23:59 New York time on Friday 13 November 2026, the UN Security Council adopts a resolution renewing the Yemen (2140) sanctions measures with all 15 members voting in favour.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 on 12 Nov and 23:59 on 13 Nov 2026 New York time, the Security Council adopts a resolution that renews or extends the 2140 Yemen asset-freeze and travel-ban measures (a full renewal or a short technical rollover) with 15 votes in favour, none against and no abstentions, as recorded by UN Meetings Coverage (press.un.org), the UN Digital Library or Security Council Report. ## Risks
- A text that adds dual-use, maritime interdiction or Houthi asset-freeze language (as the US and UK want) would likely draw China/Russia abstentions again, as in 2025.
- A straightforward or technical rollover (as in 2024) would likely pass 15-0.
- An Iran-war-related clash (US framing Yemen within its confrontation with Iran) could harden Russian/Chinese positions; other elected members could also abstain.
