## Facts
- Budget committee backed the bill on 11 Nov with no amendments — oEnergetice/ČTK https://oenergetice.cz/energetika-v-cr/stat-chce-zdanit-rafinerie-vybor-dal-vladnimu-navrhu-zelenou (published 2026-11-11 10:55, read 2026-11-11T17:55Z) "Sněmovní rozpočtový výbor dnes podpořil vládní návrh ... Žádné pozměňovací návrhy k němu nepřijal."
- Committee rejected STAN's proposals (tax on importers of foreign refinery output; limit on the tax exemption for sales of investment flats) — same source "Poslanci STAN neprosadili úpravu, podle níž by dani podléhali i zahraniční výrobci".
- Announced floor amendments: TOP 09 (Jakob) cut the rate from 50% to 39.5% and remove deductibility; Pirates (Svobodová) 25% and extension to coal power producers — same source "Piráti budou podle poslankyně Venduly Svobodové prosazovat snížení mimořádné daně na 25 procent a její rozšíření i na výrobce energií z uhlí."
- Opposition asked whether the coalition plans sector levies via amendments; no clear answer; Papajanovský's (STAN) resolution against doing so failed; Havlíček says the government is not considering sector taxes now — same source "Jasná odpověď nezazněla."
- Babiš on 11 Nov: sector taxes not discussed by the government; "Neočekávám ani, že by to proběhlo ve Sněmovně tento týden" — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/babis-na-moznych-sektorovych-danich-pracuje-se-schillerovou/2900336, as cited in work/agenda_cz.json (11 Nov); not re-read tonight.
- Bill content: applies to firms with total income ≥ CZK 2bn and refining income ≥ CZK 50m; 2026 and 2027 periods vs 2025; about CZK 5.5bn for 2026; in practice Orlen Unipetrol's Kralupy and Litvínov refineries — same oEnergetice 11 Nov source.
- First reading on 13 Oct was completed the same day: the bill survived opposition motions to reject and to return it to the government — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/snemovna-bude-moci-opet-jednat-az-do-noci-prosadila-to-koalice/2887012 (published 2026-10-13 20:08, read 2026-11-11T17:56Z) "Zákon odolal návrhům na zamítnutí i na vrácení vládě, které vznesla opozice." ODS's Benda also failed to get the bill sent to the foreign affairs committee — ČT24 https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/domaci/poslanci-by-meli-zahajit-jednani-o-zavedeni-mimoradne-dane-na-rafinerie-378520 (updated 2026-10-13 21:06, read 2026-11-11T17:56Z).
- Positions (ČT24, 13 Oct, same URL): ODS considers the bill disputable; STAN does not criticise the tax itself; KDU-ČSL's Činčila called it "obrovskou ránu pro předvídatelnost podnikatelského prostředí". No source opened tonight reports any opposition plan to obstruct the second reading.
- Babiš is in Dublin on 13 Nov for the informal EU leaders' meeting (work/agenda_cz.json, Irish presidency page https://irish-presidency.consilium.europa.eu/en/events/informal-meeting-of-eu-heads-of-state-or-government/); the bill is presented by Finance Minister Schillerová.
- Second reading consists of general and detailed debate in which amendments are tabled; no final vote on the bill takes place in it. No source mentions a third reading on 13 Nov; the sitting was called for the second reading only (single agenda item).
