## Facts
- Nvidia's board launched an independent external investigation on Sunday 8 Nov after a New York Times report that Huang had a close relationship with Alice Huang (no relation), whom US intelligence officials believed was a spy for Beijing; the board earlier said his interactions with her were "not unusual or unexpected" — BBC https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ckgel338xql3o (modified 2026-11-12T04:01Z, read 04:14Z).
- Huang admitted earlier in the week to having been "unfaithful" to his wife and apologised — same BBC page.
- On Wednesday 11 Nov the NYT reported a second extramarital relationship, with an Nvidia executive who reports directly to him; the shares fell sharply; the board was reportedly meeting on Wednesday evening (PT) to decide whether he should remain — same BBC page; Al Jazeera https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/12/nvidia-shares-plunge-amid-reports-board-to-discuss-ceo-huangs-future
- Nvidia has 11 directors including Huang; the CEO can be removed with or without cause by a majority of directors (Reuters, via BBC). Possible interim CEOs named by analysts: CFO Colette Kress; co-founder Chris Malachowsky (BBC).
- Overnight desk (12 Nov ~05:15 CET): BBC sources say his future "doesn't look good"; some investors urge the board to wait for the investigation; DW https://www.dw.com/en/nvidia-board-reportedly-weighs-ousting-huang-amid-alleged-affairs-with-exec-and-suspected-chinese-spy/a-79925002 . No Nvidia statement or 8-K on a decision as of 05:15 CET 12 Nov.
- Relevant reference: boards that open an external investigation into a CEO's conduct often wait for its findings (weeks), but a relationship with a direct report breaches most codes of conduct on its own (e.g. McDonald's 2019, Intel 2018: CEOs left within days of the board learning of a consensual relationship with an employee).
