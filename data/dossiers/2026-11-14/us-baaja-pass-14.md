- Under Senate Rule XXII a cloture motion filed on Tuesday ripens one hour after the Senate convenes on the second day of session, i.e. Thursday 12 Nov (procedural rule, background; no source page opened tonight). If cloture on the substitute is invoked, up to 30 hours of post-cloture time follow, then the cloture vote on the bill and up to 30 more hours, unless time is yielded back by unanimous consent. Without consent, final passage would fall after Friday.
