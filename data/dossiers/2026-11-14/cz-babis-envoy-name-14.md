## Facts
- 10 Nov: Babiš said EU leaders should already nominate their representatives for peace talks: "Není možné, aby za nás vyjednávali Američané." He backed Meloni's request that the EU decide by end-December who negotiates and that the European Council give that person "jasný a silný politický mandát" — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/babis-a-fico-se-ohradili-vuci-vyzve-merze-k-financni-podpore-ukrajiny/2899954 (published 2026-11-10T11:42+02:00 as given, read 2026-11-11T17:55Z).
- Same ČTK report: Fico proposes Commissioner Maroš Šefčovič and, asked by journalists, could imagine Czech President Petr Pavel — "Za vyjednavače EU o míru na Ukrajině slovenský premiér navrhuje člena Evropské komise, Slováka Maroše Šefčoviče. Na novinářský dotaz pak řekl, že by si v uvedené roli dokázal představit také českého prezidenta Petra Pavla." No name from Babiš is reported.
- EU source: names to be left until the December summit — EUobserver 248409 above; EUobserver https://euobserver.com/247519/trump-vows-beautiful-russia-deal-as-eu-agonises-over-which-envoy-to-send-to-ukraine-peace-talks/ (read 2026-11-11T17:55Z) "leaving till later proposals on individual names".
