# rs-student-list-pm
- **Claim:** The Student List publicly names its candidate for prime minister of Serbia by 23:59 Belgrade time on Tuesday 27 October 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 05:35 CET on 26 Oct and 23:59 Belgrade time (Europe/Belgrade) on 27 Oct 2026, the Student List ("Studentska lista – Studenti pobeđuju") or its official spokespeople/leadership publicly announce one named person as the list's candidate for prime minister (mandatar/premijer), as shown on the list's official channels or reported by N1, RTS, RFE/RL or Reuters/AP citing such an announcement. Media speculation, "possible" names or lists of several options do not count. NO if no single named PM candidate is announced in the window.
- **Deadline:** 2026-10-27T23:59:59+01:00 (window 2026-10-26T05:35:00+01:00 to 2026-10-27T23:59:59+01:00; resolves by 2026-10-28T12:00:00+01:00)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-26T05:08:00+01:00
## Schedule
- No scheduled event. RIK final results expected within about four days (~29 Oct); the new Assembly must meet within 30 days of final results; the formal PM nomination comes from the (acting) president after consultations. A party may name its candidate any time.

## Base rate
- No clean reference class. Partial: new Serbian/ex-Yugoslav majorities after a change of power — 2000 (DOS: Đinđić known as PM candidate before the December vote, n=1, pre-named); 2012 (SNS-led coalition: PM Dačić agreed ~3 weeks after the May vote, n=1); Montenegro 2020 (opposition won 30 Aug; Krivokapić named as PM-designate candidate on ~8 Sep, ~9 days). Movements without a pre-designated leader typically take days to weeks to agree a name. 0 of the ~3 cases without a pre-named candidate named one within 2 days. The Student List deliberately campaigned without a declared PM candidate (per ABC/Rio Times).

## Risks
- The list may name a candidate quickly to pre-empt acting president Brnabić and show readiness — a fast announcement on 26–27 Oct is plausible.
- Ambiguous statements ("Srdanović will lead the talks", "our candidate is X if...") — criterion needs one named PM candidate.
- The list may wait for RIK final results (~29 Oct), pushing any name past the window.
