# cz-babis-fico-statue
- **Claim:** Czech PM Andrej Babiš and Slovak PM Robert Fico both attend in person the unveiling of the Czech–Slovak friendship statue in the garden of the Czech Government Office (Strakova akademie, Prague) on 27 October 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 and 23:59:59 Prague time (CET, UTC+1) on 27 Oct 2026, the Czech–Slovak friendship statue is unveiled in the garden of the Czech Government Office in Prague and both Andrej Babiš and Robert Fico are physically present at the unveiling, as shown by the Czech Government Office (vlada.gov.cz), the Slovak Government Office (vlada.gov.sk), ČTK or TASR reports or photos. NO if the unveiling does not take place on 27 Oct (including if it is postponed or cancelled), or if either prime minister is absent or takes part only remotely or via a representative.
- **Deadline (event window end):** 2026-10-27T23:59:59+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-25T19:05:00+01:00
