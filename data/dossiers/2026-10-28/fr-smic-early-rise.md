# fr-smic-early-rise
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Paris time on 27 October 2026, the French government announces a decision to raise the minimum wage (SMIC) beyond the regular legal revaluation of 1 January 2027 — either an increase taking effect before 1 January 2027, or a discretionary increase ("coup de pouce") above legal indexation.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 26 Oct 2026 06:00 CET and 27 Oct 2026 23:59:59 CET, the President, the Prime Minister (Sébastien Lecornu), the Labour Minister (Jean-Pierre Farandou) or the government's spokesperson, or an official government communiqué or decree, announces as decided (not "under study"/"envisaged") an increase of the SMIC that (a) takes effect on a date before 1 Jan 2027, or (b) exceeds the legally required indexation. An announcement concerning only the regular 1 Jan 2027 legal revaluation, or of bonuses/allowances (e.g. prime d'activité) without a SMIC rise, does not count. NO if no such announcement is made in the window, including if a decision is announced only on 28 Oct or later.
- **Deadline (event window end):** 2026-10-27T23:59:59+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-25T19:20:00+01:00

## Risks
- Government may decide on 28–30 Oct (Council of Ministers is usually Wednesday — 28 Oct, outside window), or wait to bundle measures before the 5 Nov strike.
- Government may choose bonuses (prime d'activité, chèque énergie) instead of a SMIC rise — not counted.
- Budget constraint: ministers stress rising interest rates; PLFSS rejected 23 Oct with deficit near 24 bn €.
- Ambiguity: a minister saying "nous allons anticiper" without date/amount — counts only if the decision itself is stated as taken.
