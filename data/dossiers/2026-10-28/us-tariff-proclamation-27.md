# us-tariff-proclamation-27
- **Claim:** By 23:59 ET on 27 October 2026, President Trump signs a new proclamation or executive order that imposes, raises, lowers, suspends or changes the product scope of US import tariffs on any country.
- **Criterion:** YES if a presidential proclamation or executive order signed (dated) 26 or 27 October 2026 — as shown on whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions or in the Federal Register — imposes, increases, reduces, suspends, terminates or modifies the product coverage of tariffs/additional import duties on goods from any country (any authority: Section 232, 338, 301, 122, or implementation of a trade agreement).
