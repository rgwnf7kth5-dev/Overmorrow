# cz-pavel-diplomats-media

- **Claim:** In his address to the diplomatic corps at Prague Castle on 27 October 2026, President Petr Pavel explicitly mentions public-service media.
- **Criterion:** YES if the text of the President's address to the diplomatic corps delivered on 27 Oct 2026 (Prague Castle, from 15:00 CET), as published on hrad.cz, explicitly mentions public-service media: the words "média veřejné služby"/"veřejnoprávní média" (or "public service media"/"public broadcasters"), Czech Television or Czech Radio by name, or the TV/radio licence fees or their abolition. General references to free media, press freedom, journalists or disinformation do not count. If hrad.cz publishes no text, a full official video recording of the address may be used instead. NO if the address delivered in the window contains no such mention, including if the reception is cancelled or postponed beyond 27 Oct or no address is given.
