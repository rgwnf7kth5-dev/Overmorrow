# rs-students-reject-result
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Belgrade time on 27 October 2026, the Student List or the Students in Blockade movement publicly states that it does not recognise the result of Serbia's 25 October parliamentary election, or demands its annulment or a nationwide re-run, or calls a protest against the election result.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 26 Oct 2026 00:00 and 27 Oct 2026 23:59:59 Belgrade time (CET, +01:00), the Student List (its list leader Ilija Srdanović, its campaign HQ/štab, or its official channels) or the Students in Blockade movement (official social-media accounts or spokespeople) publicly (a) states that it does not recognise / rejects the election result (preliminary or official), or (b) demands annulment of the election or a nationwide repeat vote, or (c) calls a protest or rally against the election result or alleged electoral fraud; as reported by N1, AP, Al Jazeera, Reuters (via other outlets), ČTK/ČT24 or another major outlet. Statements made only before 26 Oct 00:00 do not count, but a repetition inside the window does. Allegations of irregularities, objections (prigovori) to the RIK about specific polling stations, or calls for repeat voting at specific polling stations do NOT count on their own. NO if no such statement or call is made in the window, including if the Student List wins and accepts the result.
- **Deadline (event window end):** 2026-10-27T23:59:59+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-25T21:40:00+01:00

## Base rate
- Class (counted myself): main opposition in Serbian parliamentary elections since 2014 publicly rejecting the result / demanding annulment within ~2 days of the vote. Only the 2023 case is sourced tonight (AP: "the 2023 vote was followed by opposition fraud protests" — in Dec 2023 Serbia Against Violence rejected the results and began protests the day after the vote, general knowledge). 2014, 2016, 2020 (largely boycotted) and 2022 not re-sourced. No reliable rate; base_rate: null.
- The claim is essentially conditional on the outcome: if the SNS list is projected first or with a majority, a rejection/protest call within two days follows the 2023 pattern and the movement's two-year protest record; if the Student List is projected first, a YES requires a rejection of RIK figures that diverge from the students' own count (possible if RIK and independent projections differ).

## Risks
- Students win or results remain ambiguous for days: the movement may wait for RIK's count and say "we will defend every vote" without rejecting the result (NO).
- Rejection made only tonight (25 Oct, before window) and not repeated — unlikely to go unrepeated if real.
- Borderline wording: "we do not recognise RIK's preliminary figures until all records are checked" counts as (a); "we will file objections" does not. Editor should apply strictly.
- Statements by other opposition lists (Evropska Srbija / Đilas etc.) do not count; only Student List / Students in Blockade.
