# ua-oleshky-ceasefire-starts

- **Claim:** By 23:59 Kyiv time on 27 October 2026, officials of both Ukraine and Russia have publicly said that the local ceasefire around Russian-occupied Oleshky (Kherson region) has taken effect or is in force.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 Kyiv time on 26 Oct and 23:59:59 Kyiv time on 27 Oct 2026, both of the following happen:
  - At least one Ukrainian official source says the Oleshky ceasefire ("silence regime") has begun or is in force. Ukrainian official sources are: the President or his Office, ombudsman Dmytro Lubinets, the General Staff, the Defence Ministry, the Coordination HQ for POWs, or the Kherson regional military administration.
  - At least one Russian official source says the same. Russian official sources are: the Kremlin, the Defence Ministry, the General Staff, negotiator Yana Lantratova, or the Russian-installed Kherson administration.

  Statements that the ceasefire is in force but has been violated still count. Statements made only before 03:00 Kyiv time on 27 Oct (the scheduled start) that it "will" start do not count. Media reports quoting such an official statement are enough. NO if the window ends without both statements, including if the ceasefire is postponed or cancelled, or if either side says before the end of the window that it has not begun.

## Schedule
- **Scheduled start: 03:00 Kyiv time (04:00 Moscow) on Tuesday 27 Oct.** Source: Kyiv Post, https://www.kyivpost.com/post/88088 (published 2026-10-25 08:58, read 2026-10-25T17:45Z): "the localized silence regime will take effect across the designated sector on Tuesday, Oct. 27, at 3:00 a.m. Kyiv time". The time was announced by Lubinets on Telegram. The head of Russia's negotiating team, Yana Lantratova, confirmed it to him: she "informed me of the same".
- **Russian side:** General Staff chief Valery Gerasimov said Russian forces "would cease fire on the Kherson front starting at 4 a.m. on October 27". The arrangements include "safe passage for a humanitarian convoy into Alyoshki". Source: Meduza citing Interfax, https://meduza.io/en/news/2026/10/23/russia-announces-october-27-ceasefire-on-southern-ukraine-s-kherson-front-to-evacuate-residents-of-occupied-oleshky (published 2026-10-23 17:23, read 2026-10-25T17:45Z).
- **Ukrainian General Staff:** said the ceasefire starts at 03:00 Kyiv time on 27 Oct and that Ukrainian forces will carry out the decision. Russia's Gerasimov separately confirmed. "Neither side has yet reported that the operation has begun." Source: Hromadske, https://hromadske.ua/en/amp/war/273075-ukraine-russia-agree-to-ceasefire-in-occupied-oleshky-for-humanitarian-mission-starting-oct-27 (published 2026-10-24 16:15, read 2026-10-25T17:46Z).
- **Humanitarian operation on 29 Oct:** Al Jazeera says both sides give 27 Oct as the start and that a humanitarian operation is planned for 29 Oct. Source: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/24/ukraine-says-starlink-restored-as-attacks-continue-in-russia-and-ukraine (published 2026-10-24, read 2026-10-25T17:40Z).
- **Discrepancy:** Meduza notes that "The White House had said the ceasefire would begin October 29". The 27 Oct date, confirmed by both militaries, is the later and more specific statement.

## Facts
- **Announcement:** the agreement was announced on Friday 23 Oct. It followed "secret consultations between Melania Trump and civilian negotiators from Russia and Ukraine". The purpose is a "local ceasefire to allow aid next week" into the town. Source: AP via News4JAX, https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/10/24/ukraine-and-russia-eye-new-peace-talks-and-melania-trumps-mission-but-steep-obstacles-remain/ (published 2026-10-24 05:29, read 2026-10-25T17:40Z).
- **Purpose:** the ceasefire is to allow demining of the route to Oleshky, delivery of food, water, medicines and hospital equipment, and the evacuation of civilians. Lubinets: "The operation will proceed gradually and is extremely challenging to implement." Source: 112.ua citing Espreso, https://112.ua/en/rezim-tisi-z-27-zovtna-dla-rozminuvanna-olesok-ta-dostavki-gumanitarnoi-dopomogi-193628 (published 2026-10-24 21:48, read 2026-10-25T17:46Z).
- **Phases:** the framework is a "silence regime", then emergency food and medical supplies, then "phased evacuation of non-combatants". It "will be executed in gradual phases". No end time is published. Source: Kyiv Post 88088 (as above).
- **Area:** "the designated sector" or "the Kherson front" around Oleshky, on the Dnipro left bank. No width or coordinates have been published (Kyiv Post 88088; Meduza).
- **Monitoring:** no monitoring body (ICRC, UN) is named in the 23–25 Oct reports. Melania Trump's team "facilitated technical consultations". Sources: Kyiv Post 88088; Hromadske. In September, Lubinets said the ICRC was ready to take part in an earlier version of the evacuation (Censor.net, below).
- **Population:** about 6,000 civilians, including roughly 200 children, may remain in Oleshky. The town has been largely cut off from food supplies since late May 2026 (Hromadske).
- **Earlier failed attempt:** on 16 Sep 2026, Lubinets said an evacuation list and a ceasefire for the mission had been agreed, but "the Russian side has for some time now failed to confirm its readiness". Source: Censor.net, https://censor.net/en/news/4023781/lubinets-russia-has-not-confirmed-the-evacuation-of-people-from-the-occupied-town-of-oleshky (published 2026-09-16 18:38, read 2026-10-25T17:50Z).
- **Melania Trump's role:** Trump credited his wife with securing the local ceasefire (Al Jazeera, above).
- **Wider talks:** the Kremlin says a next round of US–Ukraine–Russia talks is likely in Abu Dhabi around mid-November (AP, above).

## Risks
- **Postponement:** Russia may postpone or stop confirming, as in September.
- **Russian silence:** Russian official sources may not say publicly on 27 Oct that it is in force, because their communication runs via Lantratova and Lubinets. A Lubinets report that "Lantratova confirmed" would count only if the criterion reads it as a Russian official statement. It counts if it quotes her as saying the regime is in force.
- **Phasing:** the ceasefire may in practice begin with demining, and "start" may be described ambiguously.
- **Shelling:** shelling right after 03:00 could prompt one side to say the truce "did not start".
