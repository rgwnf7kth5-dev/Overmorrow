- **Criterion:** YES if, between 26 Oct 2026 07:00 and 27 Oct 2026 23:59:59 Sofia time (EET, +02:00), Boyko Borissov or an official GERB/GERB-SDS body (executive committee, national leadership, official party statement) publicly states that GERB supports Andrey Gyurov in the runoff or calls on GERB voters/supporters to vote for him, as reported by BTA, BNR, the Sofia Globe or another major outlet.
