# br-flavio-tse-filing
- **Claim:** Flávio Bolsonaro, his campaign coalition or the PL formally files a request at the Superior Electoral Court (TSE) to annul or invalidate the 25 October 2026 presidential runoff by 23:59 Brasília time on Tuesday 27 October 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 05:35 CET on 26 Oct and 23:59 Brasília time (America/Sao_Paulo) on 27 Oct 2026, Flávio Bolsonaro, his coalition, or the PL (or their lawyers on their behalf) files (protocola) at the TSE any petition, representação or action seeking annulment, invalidation or a recount/re-vote of all or part of the presidential runoff, as shown by the TSE (tse.jus.br news / PJe docket) or reported by at least one major Brazilian outlet (Folha, O Globo/g1, Estadão, UOL) citing the filing. Announcements of intent, statements or filings at other courts (STF etc.) do not count. NO if no such filing is made at the TSE in the window.
- **Deadline:** 2026-10-27T23:59:59-03:00 (window 2026-10-26T05:35:00+01:00 to 2026-10-27T23:59:59-03:00; resolves by 2026-10-28T12:00:00+01:00)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-26T05:06:00+01:00

## Base rate
- Reference class: Brazilian presidential runoff losers who contested the result at the TSE since 2014 (n=2): 2014 PSDB (Aécio) filed an audit request at the TSE on 30 Oct, 4 days after the 26 Oct runoff; 2022 PL (Bolsonaro) filed its "representação" to invalidate votes from older machines on 22 Nov, 23 days after the 30 Oct runoff. Neither filed within ~2 days. Both took days to weeks to prepare technical filings (2022 relied on a commissioned audit report).
- Wider class (losers of Latin American presidential runoffs who publicly refused to concede and announced legal action, e.g. Keiko Fujimori 2021 — filed nullity requests at the JEE within ~2–3 days, n small): filings within 48 h do happen when there is a specific procedural grievance and deadlines are short.
- Here the grievance is a specific, already-known decision (the voting extension), which could be filed quickly; but 0 of 2 Brazilian cases filed within 2 days.

## Risks
- Filing could go to the STF (against Zanin's decision) rather than the TSE — does not count.
- PL leadership (Valdemar) may prefer delay or a softer "audit" request; an audit/clarification request that does not seek annulment/recount would be borderline — criterion requires seeking annulment, invalidation, recount or re-vote.
- Filing could be made late on 27 Oct and only reported on 28 Oct; resolver should check the filing date, not the report date.
- Pressure from allies (governors, Centrão) to concede could stop the filing.
