# ua-starlink-restricted-again

- **Claim:** By 23:59 Kyiv time on 27 October 2026, a Ukrainian official source publicly reports that Starlink or Starshield service for Ukraine's forces has again been restricted or throttled after the restoration of 24 October.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 Kyiv time on 26 Oct and 23:59:59 Kyiv time on 27 Oct 2026, one of these publicly states that Starlink or Starshield service used by Ukrainian forces has again been restricted, throttled or cut, in any part of the front, after its restoration on 24 Oct:
  - the President or his Office;
  - the Defence Ministry, including the minister or deputy ministers;
  - the Ministry of Digital Transformation;
  - the General Staff;
  - a presidential adviser in an official capacity, such as Serhii Beskrestnov.

  Statements about the past throttling of 14–24 Oct do not count. NO if no such statement is made in the window.
- **Deadline (event window end):** 2026-10-27T23:59:59+02:00 (resolves by 2026-10-28T05:00:00+01:00)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-25T19:10:00+01:00

## Schedule
- Unscheduled event; nothing is announced. This claim tests whether the 24 Oct restoration holds over the next 48 hours amid Musk's accusation.

## Facts
- **Start of throttling:** overnight on Wed 14 Oct, without warning, service was limited for terminals moving faster than 100 mph. Musk then said the US military, "and ultimately the president", decide speed or usage limits. The Pentagon did not respond. Source: Al Jazeera, https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/14/ukraine-accuses-elon-musks-spacex-of-throttling-starlink-terminals (published 2026-10-14, read 2026-10-25T17:55Z).
- **Restoration:** restoration began overnight into Sat 24 Oct. Zelenskyy on X: "The speeds are now where they need to be." Defence Minister Yevhenii Khmara credited "direct dialogue" with Washington. Source: AP via News4JAX, https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/10/24/ukraine-says-starlink-speed-limits-lifted-after-a-slowdown-that-hobbled-its-drones/ (published 2026-10-24 06:45, updated 16:16, read 2026-10-25T17:40Z).
- **Khmara on stability:** Starlink and Starshield connections were stable on equipment moving above 400 km/h. Source: Al Jazeera, https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/24/ukraine-says-starlink-restored-as-attacks-continue-in-russia-and-ukraine (read 2026-10-25T17:40Z).
- **Musk's admission:** on X on 24 Oct, Musk said "Ukraine military violated Starlink terms of service and used it for offensive drone strikes to kill people" and "That loophole was closed last week". He also said the US government controls changes to Starshield. Source: AP, above.
- **US administration:**
  - Defence Secretary Hegseth and Secretary of State Rubio denied administration involvement.
  - Vice President Vance hinted at White House coordination with Musk.
  - Trump said he did not make the call.

  Source: AP, above. No formal Pentagon statement was found as of 25 Oct.
- **Trump's earlier claim:** on Thu 22 Oct, Trump said Musk had restored access. On Fri 23 Oct, Kyiv still reported limits. Source: ČT24, https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/svet/rusko-uvedlo-ze-drony-zasahlo-ukrajinsky-pristav-cornomorsk-a-nakladni-lod-378974 (published 2026-10-24, read 2026-10-25T17:41Z).
- **Kyiv's rebuttal (25 Oct):**
  - Beskrestnov: speed cutoffs of 70–90 km/h have applied since early 2026, and commercial service is geofenced inside Russia, so "we are physically unable to use commercial Starlink for deep strikes".
  - Sternenko: whitelists introduced in February allow mobile use only via Starshield.

  Source: Kyiv Post, https://www.kyivpost.com/post/88082 (published 2026-10-25 08:31, read 2026-10-25T17:56Z).
- **Poland:** Poland pays for Ukraine's Starlink subscriptions, about $50m a year. Foreign Minister Sikorski said on 23 Oct that mixing business with politics "usually ends badly, especially when we talk about Elon Musk". No reaction from Digital Minister Gawkowski was found. Sources: IBTimes AU, https://www.ibtimes.com.au/starlink-restrictions-ukraine-satellite-network-alternatives-1877367 (published 2026-10-26 03:15 AEDT, read 2026-10-25T17:57Z); Al Jazeera 14 Oct (above).
- **No new terms:** no formal SpaceX statement on new terms of service had been found as of 25 Oct (Kyiv Post 88082: "does not describe any new rules going forward").

  - Musk says SpaceX closed an "offensive drone" loophole. If Ukraine keeps using terminals on strike drones, SpaceX could tighten again.
  - Partial or local outages may be reported by front-line units or volunteers, but those are not official sources and do not count.
