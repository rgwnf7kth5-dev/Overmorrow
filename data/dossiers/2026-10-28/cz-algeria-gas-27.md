# cz-algeria-gas-27
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Algiers time on 27 October 2026, a Czech party (the Czech state or a ministry, or a Czech company such as ČEZ, EPH, Pražská plynárenská, MND or Agrofert) and Sonatrach or the Algerian state sign a contract, memorandum or agreement on supplies of natural gas or LNG to the Czech Republic during PM Andrej Babiš's visit to Algeria (signing on 26 or 27 October counts).
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 on 26 Oct 2026 and 23:59:59 on 27 Oct 2026 (Algiers time, UTC+1, same as Prague CET), a contract, memorandum (MoU), letter of intent or intergovernmental agreement is signed by a Czech party (Czech government, a ministry or a Czech company) and an Algerian party (Sonatrach, another Algerian state company, the Algerian government or a ministry) whose subject includes the supply or sale of natural gas or LNG to the Czech Republic or to that Czech company, as announced by the Czech Government Office (vlada.gov.cz), the Ministry of Industry and Trade, the Czech company, Sonatrach, APS or ČTK. A signing at any point during the visit counts, including on 26 Oct (so the 26 Oct claim cz-algeria-gas-agreement and this claim can both resolve YES from the same signing). A broad energy or economic document counts only if the official report says it covers gas supplies. Statements that gas "was discussed", "talks continue" or that a deal will be signed later do not count. NO if no such document is signed in the window, including if the visit is cancelled, cut short or postponed.
- **Deadline (event window end):** 2026-10-27T23:59:59+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-25T19:05:00+01:00
