# il-turnout-above-2022

- **Claim:** Final national turnout in Israel's Knesset election on 27 October 2026, as published by the Central Elections Committee (CEC), is above 70.63%, the 2022 turnout.
- **Criterion:** YES if the turnout in the CEC's official results for the 27 Oct 2026 election is greater than 70.63%. If the CEC prints no percentage, votes cast ÷ eligible voters from those results is used. The CEC's preliminary election-night figure does not decide.
  - NO if turnout is 70.63% or lower.
  - NO if the vote does not take place in the window, including if the election is postponed.
  - If no official figure has appeared yet, the claim stays pending.
- **Deadline (event window end):** 2026-10-27T23:59:59+02:00. Resolves by 2026-11-05T12:00:00+01:00.
- **Written at:** 2026-10-25T19:20:00+01:00

- **Official results:** about 4 Nov (Wikipedia 2026 page).

## Facts
- **Source dossier:** derived from il-turnout; all facts and sources are there.
- **The 2022 benchmark:** 70.63%, from 4,794,593 votes cast out of 6,788,804 eligible (Wikipedia 2022 page, read 17:56Z). The CEC's election-night figure in 2022 was 71.3% (ToI, 1 Nov 2022).
- **History, 2013–2022:** turnout rose over the previous election in 5 of 7 elections. In 2019–2022 it exceeded 70.63 only in 2020 (71.5) (Wikipedia, Elections in Israel).
- **Drivers:** a close race in the final polls and the first vote since 7 Oct 2023 could raise turnout. A security threat on election day and Arab-sector participation are the risks.

## Base rate
- **Own count:** 1 of the last 5 elections (2019–2022) exceeded 70.63 (2020, 71.5). 2 of the last 8 (2009–2022) did, adding 2015 at 72.4.

- **Class:** election-held, rate 0.010 (slip.mjs, estimate); no postponement reported as of 25 Oct.

## Risks
- Same as il-turnout. An election-day attack would cut turnout. The election-night CEC figure may read about 0.7 pp higher than the official one.
