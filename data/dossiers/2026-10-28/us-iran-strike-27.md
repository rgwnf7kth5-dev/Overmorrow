# us-iran-strike-27

- **Claim:** By 23:59 Tehran time on 27 October 2026, the US government confirms that the US military has struck a target on Iranian territory, with the strike carried out between 26 Oct 06:00 CET and 27 Oct 23:59 Tehran time.
- **Criterion:** YES if the strike happens between 2026-10-26T08:30+03:30 (06:00 CET) and 2026-10-27T23:59:59+03:30. It must be a strike by US forces (missiles, aircraft, armed drones or naval fire) on a target on Iranian territory: the mainland, Iranian islands (e.g. Qeshm, Larak, Kish, Abu Musa) or Iranian ports and coastal installations. It must also be confirmed by the US government: President Trump, the White House, the Pentagon/Department of Defense (or a named Pentagon official), or CENTCOM. The confirmation can be published up to 2026-10-28T05:00+01:00. A joint US–Israeli strike counts if the US confirms that US forces took part. These do not count: Iranian claims alone; Israeli-only strikes; US interceptions of Iranian missiles, drones or boats; strikes on vessels at sea outside Iranian ports; US surveillance flights, including drones Iran says it shot down; anonymous "US official" briefings with no on-record confirmation. NO if no such US-confirmed strike happens in the window, including if a planned strike is postponed.

## Risks
- **Toward YES:**
  - An Iranian attack on US forces or bases, or a mine/tanker incident, could trigger a quick "self-defense" strike, as on 9 Jun, 30 Jul and 30 Aug. The IRGC threat of 22 Oct and the drone shoot-down of 25 Oct raise this risk.
  - Trump's "finish the job" talk and the qualifier "today" leave Monday and Tuesday open.
  - The deal talk may collapse after the Qatari visit.
- **Toward NO:**
  - The midterms are on 3 Nov, and the reported plan targets after the election.
  - Qatari mediation is under way.
  - The IDF chief sees no need to strike before the election.
- **Ambiguity:**
  - Covert or deniable strikes, Israeli-only strikes, or US confirmation only via anonymous officials do not count.
  - Strikes on Iranian boats near the coast count only if they hit a port or coastal installation.
