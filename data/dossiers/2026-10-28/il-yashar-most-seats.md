# il-yashar-most-seats

- **Claim:** Yashar, Gadi Eisenkot's list, wins strictly more seats than Likud in Israel's Knesset election on 27 October 2026, by the Central Elections Committee (CEC) seat allocation.
- **Criterion:** YES if the CEC seat allocation for the election held on 27 Oct 2026 gives Yashar more seats than Likud. Likud includes New Hope, which merged into it. The allocation is taken after surplus-vote agreements. The deciding figure is the CEC's near-final count, which is the first CEC publication with all regular and double-envelope ballots counted. If the CEC's official results, expected about 4 Nov, differ from that count, the official results win.
  - NO if Likud has more seats or the two lists tie.
  - NO if the vote does not take place in the window, including if the election is postponed.
  - If the CEC has published nothing yet, the claim stays pending.
- **Deadline (event window end):** 2026-10-27T23:59:59+02:00. Voting runs 07:00–22:00, and voters still queuing at closing may vote. Evidence is expected 28–29 Oct; resolves by 2026-11-05T12:00:00+01:00.
- **Written at:** 2026-10-25T19:20:00+01:00

## Risks
- **Polling miss:** in 2022 the polls understated the right by about 4 seats. A Likud swing of +2 to +3 seats closes the gap.
- **Threshold failures:** the Reservists (2.4–3.0% in two polls) and People of Israel (2.5–3.1%) may fall below 3.25%. The share of wasted votes would then reshuffle seats, and surplus agreements can move one seat.
- **Tie:** a tie gives NO.
- **Security incident on election day:** an Iranian or proxy attack could cut turnout unevenly.
- **Media vs CEC:** media "largest party" calls from exit polls at 22:00 do not decide this claim; only the CEC count does.
