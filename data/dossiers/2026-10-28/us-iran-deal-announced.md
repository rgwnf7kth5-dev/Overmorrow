# us-iran-deal-announced

- **Claim:** By 23:59 Tehran time on 27 October 2026, the United States and Iran (or Qatar as mediator) announce that an agreement, framework or ceasefire between the US and Iran has been reached.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 2026-10-26T08:30+03:30 (06:00 CET) and 2026-10-27T23:59:59+03:30, two conditions are both met:
  1. President Trump or the White House publicly states that the US and Iran have reached an agreement. Any of these counts: a ceasefire or truce, a memorandum, a framework or interim deal, or a Strait of Hormuz arrangement.
  2. The Iranian government or the government of Qatar, as mediator, also publicly confirms that this agreement has been reached. Iranian government means the president, the foreign minister or the foreign ministry spokesman, or the Supreme National Security Council.

  These do not count: statements that a deal is close, possible or being weighed; one-sided proposals or offers; announcements of talks or meetings; a unilateral US pause in strikes. NO if no such jointly confirmed announcement is made in the window, including if an announcement is postponed.

## Risks
- **Toward YES:**
  - Trump explicitly framed 25 Oct as "deal or strike".
  - The Qatari mission is US-coordinated.
  - Before the midterms Trump may want a Hormuz or oil-price win.
  - Trump announced the 8 April ceasefire suddenly, under similar threat-of-strike conditions.
- **Toward NO:**
  - Iran says there will be no talks until its seven conditions are met (20 Oct).
  - Trump said "We are not negotiating" (22 Oct).
  - Gaps remain on sequencing (nuclear vs Hormuz).
- **Ambiguity:**
  - Trump may claim a deal that Iran denies. YES needs confirmation by Iran or Qatar.
  - An "agreement to hold talks" is not an agreement under this criterion.
