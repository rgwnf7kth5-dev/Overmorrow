# cz-okamura-recall-signatures
- **Claim:** By 23:59 CET on 27 October 2026, opposition MPs submit to the Chamber of Deputies (handing it to the Speaker or the Chamber's office) a written proposal to recall Tomio Okamura as Speaker that at least 80 MPs have signed.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 26 Oct 06:00 CET and 27 Oct 23:59 CET, a written proposal to recall Okamura as Speaker, signed by at least 80 MPs (two-fifths of all deputies), is submitted. This must be confirmed by ČTK, ČT24, iROZHLAS or another major Czech outlet, by an official statement of an opposition club, or by psp.cz. Announcements, signature collection still in progress, or a submission with fewer than 80 signatures do not count. NO if no such submission happens in the window, including if the opposition waits to submit it at the resumed sitting on 3 Nov.
- **Deadline (event window end):** 2026-10-27T23:59:59+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-25T19:10:00+01:00

## Facts
- A recall requires a written proposal from "dvou pětin všech poslanců", i.e. 80 MPs. The opposition has 92 MPs: Pirates 18, KDU-ČSL 16, TOP 09 9, STAN 22 (the remaining 27 are ODS) — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/pirati-navrhnou-odvolani-okamury-z-cela-snemovny-podpori-je-dalsi-strany/2892523 (read 2026-10-25T17:40Z)
- Party positions per the same ČTK report. TOP 09 chair Matěj Ondřej Havel signalled support. KDU-ČSL spokesperson Iva Petrusová said the party will support the recall but called it mainly a political gesture. STAN chair Vít Rakušan said the club will discuss it first. ČTK quotes no ODS position. — ČTK, as above
- On 25 Oct (03:32), Around Prague reported that the Pirates are counting on KDU-ČSL and TOP 09 while STAN "will still consult". It gave no timeline. — https://aroundprague.cz/en/news/politics/okamura-responds-to-pirates-proposal-to-remove-him-as-speaker (read 2026-10-25T18:02Z)
- Okamura told ČTK he will not react to the Pirates' accusations — ČTK, as above
- Precedent: on 13 Jan 2026 all five opposition clubs (ODS, STAN, Pirates, KDU, TOP 09) handed the Speaker 92 signatures for an agenda item on recalling Okamura. That was during a running sitting, and they said they would propose it again at the start of later sittings — https://www.pirati.cz/jak-pirati-pracuji/pirati-pokracuji-ve-snaze-odvolat-tomia-okamuru-z-cela-snemovny-opozicni-strany-predaly-sesbirane-podpisy-predsedovi-dolni-komory/ (published 2026-01-13 13:02, read 2026-10-25T17:52Z). ČTK (24 Oct) says that earlier attempt failed.
- Trigger: the overnight 23–24 Oct sitting on tisk 231 (abolition of ČT/ČRo licence fees). The Pirates argue Rule 56 of the rules of procedure required a public debate — ČTK, as above
- Tisk 231 passed first reading early on 24 Oct. The motion to reject or return it got 81 of 177 MPs present. The bill went to the výbor pro média a kulturu, which has 30–60 days. The opposition can block a shorter deadline. — Seznam Zprávy https://www.seznamzpravy.cz/clanek/domaci-zivot-v-cesku-otazky-a-odpovedi-o-poplatky-se-svedou-dalsi-bitvy-povinnost-platit-nekonci-318677 (published 2026-10-24 ~07:00, read 2026-10-25T17:35Z); iROZHLAS https://www.irozhlas.cz/zpravy-domov/otazky-a-odpovedi-co-znamena-schvaleni-navrhu-o-koncesionarskych-poplatcich-a-co_2610241447_pik (published 2026-10-24 14:47, read 2026-10-25T17:35Z) "výbor pro média a kulturu … 60 dní"
- The výbor pro média a kulturu meets (meeting no. 13) on Thu 29 Oct at 14:00. Its invitation lists tisk 231 ("Novela z. o České televizi a o Českém rozhlasu") as a related print — https://www.psp.cz/sqw/text/text2.sqw?idd=291858 (read 2026-10-25T17:50Z)

## Base rate
- No class in pipeline/reference-data applies. I counted the class myself: opposition recall initiatives against Okamura in the 10th term (n=1, January 2026). There, signatures were handed over while a sitting was running, not in a week without a plenary. The class is too small for a rate.

## Schedule slip
- No fixed slot; the slip class is not applicable (none). One sign the event may move: the Pirates themselves tie the proposal to "the next sitting" (3 Nov, 14:00), which is outside the window.

## Risks
- YES path: the opposition may collect and deliver signatures early (the Speaker's office is open on 26–27 Oct) to get the item ready for 3 Nov, or as a media gesture before the 28 Oct holiday.
- NO path: STAN has not decided and ODS has not spoken, but the 80-signature threshold needs at least 15 MPs beyond Pirates, KDU and TOP 09 (43 MPs together), which means STAN and ODS. The submission is naturally made at the sitting on 3 Nov.
- Resolution risk: a "handed over" signature list may be reported only vaguely. The outlet must state both the submission and the count of at least 80.
