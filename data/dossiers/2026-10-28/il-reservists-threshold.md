# il-reservists-threshold

- **Claim:** The joint list "The Reservists – New Economic Party" passes the 3.25% threshold in Israel's Knesset election on 27 October 2026. The list is led by Yoaz Hendel and Yaron Zelekha, and media also call it "Reservists–Economic Party". Passing means winning at least one seat in the Central Elections Committee (CEC) count.
- **Criterion:**
  - **YES:** the CEC seat allocation for the 27 Oct 2026 election gives this list one or more seats.
  - **Deciding count:** the CEC's near-final count, meaning the first CEC publication with all regular and double-envelope ballots counted. If the official results (about 4 Nov) differ, the official results win.
  - **NO:** the list gets 0 seats because it is below 3.25% of valid votes. Also NO if the vote does not take place in the window, including if the election is postponed.
  - **Pending:** if the CEC has published no count, the claim stays pending.
- **Deadline (event window end):** 2026-10-27T23:59:59+02:00. Evidence is due 28–29 Oct; the claim resolves by 2026-11-05T12:00:00+01:00.
- **Written at:** 2026-10-25T19:45:00+01:00

## Risks
- **Small-list polling:** Israeli polls have repeatedly misread small lists by 1 pp or more, as with Meretz in 2022 and New Right and Zehut in 2019.
- **Wasted-vote appeals:** late tactical voting away from lists near the threshold can push a borderline list down, as bloc voters fear a wasted vote. Calls to back it as a kingmaker can push it up.
- **Recount and double envelopes:** in 2022 the Meretz gap was about 0.09 pp, so soldiers' and other double-envelope votes counted last can decide. Use only the near-final or official CEC count.
