# eu-uk-sps-21
- **Claim:** At the EU–UK summit in Brussels on Friday 20 November 2026, the EU and the UK announce that negotiations on both the agrifood (sanitary and phytosanitary, SPS) agreement and the agreement linking their emissions trading systems (ETS) are concluded — or sign or initial those agreements.
- **Criterion:** YES if, by 23:59 Brussels time (CET) on 20 Nov 2026, the joint statement of the summit or an official statement of the European Council, the European Commission or the UK government (gov.uk) says, for BOTH the SPS agreement AND the ETS-linking agreement, that negotiations have been concluded/finalised, or that the agreement has been reached/agreed, initialled or signed. NO if for either agreement the text speaks only of progress, of being "close", or of a commitment to conclude it later, or if the summit does not take place on 20 Nov. Formal signature, Council decisions and ratification are not required. Statements published on 20 Nov but read later still count; if no official text appears, reporting by two of Reuters, AFP, BBC, Guardian, Euronews, Politico of what the leaders announced decides.

## Risks
- Wording: the statement may "welcome the agreement in principle" or "the finalisation of the text" — the criterion counts agreed/finalised/concluded; "progress", "close to" or "aim to conclude" are NO. Formal signature looks unlikely because the EU side needs a Council decision; no reporting says one has been taken.
- ETS: Euronews says "expected to confirm"; less is reported on ETS than on SPS. If ETS is left to a later date, NO.
- Package linkage: the EU ties deals together ("choreography"); a breakdown over youth tuition fees, Made in Europe access or a budget contribution could hold back the whole package (Burnham: "down to the wire").
- Burnham's "three tests" give him a public reason to walk away if terms are poor.
