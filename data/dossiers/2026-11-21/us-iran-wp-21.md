# us-iran-wp-21
- **Claim:** The US Senate votes on H.Con.Res.93 (Iran war-powers concurrent resolution) between 05:00 CET on 19 Nov and 23:59 ET on 20 Nov 2026, and at least 49 senators vote yes.
- **Criterion:** YES if the Senate holds the vote on H.Con.Res.93 (adoption of the concurrent resolution, under the 9 Nov unanimous-consent agreement) between 2026-11-18T23:00 ET (= 05:00 CET 19 Nov) and 2026-11-20T23:59 ET, and the official tally shows 49 or more Yeas, as recorded on senate.gov (roll-call vote page) or, until that is posted, the Senate Daily Press / Senate Democrats floor wrap-up. NO if the vote takes place in the window with 48 or fewer Yeas, or if no vote on H.Con.Res.93 takes place in the window (postponement, vitiated agreement, vote before 23:00 ET on 18 Nov). A voice vote counts as YES only if the resolution is declared adopted (which requires a majority of those voting, i.e. well over 49 in a full chamber); a voice vote declared rejected counts as NO. Late publication of the roll call does not change the outcome.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-19T05:00+01:00 – 2026-11-20T23:59-05:00 (America/New_York)
- **Evidence expected:** Senate Daily Press live log at the vote; senate.gov roll-call page within hours; evidence_at 2026-11-21T06:00+01:00
- **Resolves by:** 2026-11-22T05:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-18T18:05Z
- **Why N = 49:** the last vote on this kind of measure (H.Con.Res.89, roll call 244, 24 Sep 2026) had 49 yeas; per the brief, N = last roll call's yes count.

There will be 6 hours for debate only, with the time equally divided ... Following the use or yielding back of that time, the Senate will vote on the concurrent resolution." — https://www.democrats.senate.gov/2026/11/09/wrap-up-for-monday-november-9-2026 (published 2026-11-09, read 2026-11-18T17:53Z); https://www.democrats.senate.gov/2026/11/17/wrap-up-for-tuesday-november-17-2026 (published 2026-11-17, read 2026-11-18T17:53Z).
- The agreement fixes the latest day for discharge/consideration (Fri 20 Nov), not the vote time; no day or hour has been announced. The threshold is not stated in the UC: a concurrent resolution is adopted by a simple majority of those voting (roll call 244 page: "Required For Majority: 1/2"). The Vice President may break a tie.
- Wed 18 Nov schedule lists only H.R.2347 votes and judicial cloture motions; no Iran item — https://www.democrats.senate.gov/2026/11/17/schedule-for-wednesday-november-18-2026 (read 2026-11-18T17:53Z). Floor on 18 Nov occupied by H.R.2347 (permitting reform) votes — https://www.dailypress.senate.gov/wednesday-november-18-2026/ (modified 2026-11-18T17:45Z, read 2026-11-18T17:58Z).
- Recess: "Nov 23 - Nov 27 State Work Period Thanksgiving" — https://www.senate.gov/legislative/2026_schedule.htm (read 2026-11-17T17:55Z, see us-permit-20). The vote is therefore expected Thu 19 or Fri 20 Nov. It could in principle be held on Wed 18 Nov evening (before our window opens) — nothing on the 18 Nov schedule points to that.
