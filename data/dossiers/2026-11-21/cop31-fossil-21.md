# cop31-fossil-21
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Antalya time on Friday 20 November 2026, COP31 adopts a cover decision whose text speaks of transitioning away from, or phasing out, fossil fuels.
- **Criterion:** YES if a cover decision that counts under cop31-close-21 (gavelled through between 00:00 on 19 Nov and 23:59 on 20 Nov 2026, Europe/Istanbul) contains, anywhere in its adopted text (preamble, operative paragraphs, footnotes or the title of a roadmap/initiative it names), at least one of: "transition away from fossil fuels", "transitioning away from fossil fuels" (with or without "in energy systems"), "phase out fossil fuels", "phase-out of fossil fuels", "phase out of fossil fuels", "phasing out fossil fuels" (also with "coal, oil and gas" in place of "fossil fuels"), checked against the adopted text on unfccc.int or a verbatim quote by Carbon Brief, Climate Home News, ENB (IISD), Reuters or AFP. Not enough on their own: "fossil fuel subsidies", "phasedown of unabated coal", "transitional fuels", "energy transition", an acronym ("TAFF") without the words, or a reference to "paragraph 28 of decision 1/CMA.5" without the words. NO if no cover decision is adopted in the window, or if the adopted cover decision has none of the listed wordings.

## Facts
- The 16 Nov draft "contained two sets of phrasing around fossil fuels ... One reiterated wording agreed in 2023 around 'transitioning away' from their use, and another more ambitious clause talked about their 'phase out'. Both could be omitted from a final deal." — Reuters via Al-Monitor https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/first-draft-cop31-deal-signals-tough-negotiations-come (published 2026-11-16T20:35Z, read 2026-11-18T17:53Z).
- Draft wording quoted: it "calls for accelerated action to phase out fossil fuels, including through the immediate transition away from fossil fuels in energy systems." — AFP via Daily Sabah https://www.dailysabah.com/business/energy/cop31-draft-calls-for-accelerated-action-to-phase-out-fossil-fuels (published 2026-11-16T14:13Z, read 2026-11-18T17:54Z); same quote in Euronews https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/18/cop31-is-once-again-shying-away-from-a-fossil-fuel-exit-but-one-european-country-is-taking (modified 2026-11-18T13:35Z, read 2026-11-18T17:53Z).
- Bowen: "Will it look exactly like it does in the draft? No. Is it appropriate that there's a conversation about fossil fuels in the draft? Absolutely." — Reuters via Al-Monitor (above).
- The draft "only 'welcomes'" Brazil's COP30 voluntary global fossil-fuel roadmap, listing it in paragraph 140 among voluntary initiatives — Climate Home Bulletin Day 8 https://www.climatechangenews.com/2026/11/17/cop31-bulletin-day-8-arab-group-russia-reject-cover-text-over-fossil-fuels-and-electrification-goal/ (read 2026-11-18T17:56Z). Whether that paragraph names the roadmap with the words "transitioning away from fossil fuels" verbatim was not verified (the presidency's 13 Nov note called it "roadmaps on transitioning away from fossil fuels (TAFF)" — data/dossiers/2026-11-17/cop31-fossil-17.md).
- LMDC (China, India, Saudi Arabia among them) said the draft crosses red lines including "the roadmap on transitioning away from fossil fuels"; Arab Group and Russia rejected the whole draft — Climate Home Day 8 (above).
- Arab Group at the weekend plenary: would not accept "global targets imposed from above or targets directed at particular sectors or energy sources" (per ENB) — Hürriyet Daily News https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/cop31-turns-to-ministers-for-deal-on-finance-energy-229443 (read 2026-11-18T17:53Z).
- Germany (State Secretary Heike Henn, 16 Nov): TAFF in the cover decision "is not for us the ultimate benchmark for success" — Euronews (above). Spain and Vanuatu want the cover decision to refer to Brazil's roadmap — Climate Home Day 8.
- Eight EU countries have published or announced national TAFF roadmaps (Finland, France, Germany, Netherlands; Denmark, Portugal, Luxembourg, Spain on 17 Nov) — Euronews (above).
- COP30 precedent: "the final text gavelled through during the closing plenary did not include references to ... transitioning away from fossil fuels" — Carbon Brief https://www.carbonbrief.org/cop30-key-outcomes-agreed-at-the-un-climate-talks-in-belem/ (read 2026-11-18T17:55Z).
- COP29 precedent: the presidency "failed to adopt a deal relating to the 'global stocktake' from 2023, which had called for 'transitioning away' from fossil fuels" — Carbon Brief text tracker https://www.carbonbrief.org/interactive-tracking-negotiating-texts-at-the-cop31-climate-summit (read 2026-11-18T17:53Z).
- COP28 GST decision (para 28) contains "transitioning away from fossil fuels in energy systems" — standard language; COP26 cover text ended with "phasedown of unabated coal power and phase-out of inefficient fossil fuel subsidies" (would not count) — https://www.carbonbrief.org/cop26-key-outcomes-agreed-at-the-un-climate-talks-in-glasgow/ (read 2026-11-18T17:55Z).
