# cop31-close-21
- **Claim:** The closing plenary of COP31 in Antalya adopts the summit's main cover decision by 23:59 Antalya time on Friday 20 November 2026, the scheduled last day of the conference.
- **Criterion:** YES if the COP31 cover decision (the overarching presidency decision drafted by Türkiye and Australia, under whatever title it is finally given, adopted by the COP and/or the CMA) is gavelled through in plenary between 00:00 on 19 Nov and 23:59 on 20 Nov 2026, Europe/Istanbul time (UTC+3), as shown by the UNFCCC record of the plenary or by the gavel time reported by Carbon Brief, Climate Home News, ENB (IISD), Reuters or AFP. NO if it is adopted later (from 00:00 on 21 Nov Antalya time), if the plenary is suspended past midnight before adopting it, or if no cover decision is adopted. Adoption of other agenda items alone does not count. A delay in publishing the record does not make it NO: the time of the gavel decides.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-19T00:00+03:00 – 2026-11-20T23:59+03:00 (Europe/Istanbul) = 2026-11-20T21:59+01:00 Paris
- **Evidence expected:** time-stamped live reporting of the closing plenary (Carbon Brief, Climate Home, Reuters/AFP); ENB summary issue ~1–2 days later; unfccc.int decisions page. evidence_at 2026-11-21T08:00+01:00 (whether a gavel fell before midnight Antalya is known overnight).

## Facts
- First 18-page draft cover decision published Monday 16 Nov ("early on Monday afternoon"), "containing a wide range of demands that emerged from consultations last week" — Climate Home Bulletin Day 7 https://www.climatechangenews.com/2026/11/16/cop31-bulletin-day-7-sharp-divisions-lower-expectations-for-cop-outcome/ (published 2026-11-16, read 2026-11-18T17:56Z) "A first 18-page draft of the cover decision was published early on Monday afternoon".
- Bowen (Australia, President of Negotiations): the draft contains clauses some nations already reject; "Frankly, if we only talk about what we all agree on ... at this point so early in the process it would be pretty short document" — Reuters via Al-Monitor (above).
- Monday evening heads-of-delegation meeting (per ENB): LMDC (incl. China, India, Saudi Arabia) said the draft crosses their red lines (35x35 electrification target, TAFF roadmap, methane); "The Arab Group and Russia went further, rejecting the draft text entirely"; Azerbaijan also critical — Climate Home Bulletin Day 8 https://www.climatechangenews.com/2026/11/17/cop31-bulletin-day-8-arab-group-russia-reject-cover-text-over-fossil-fuels-and-electrification-goal/ (published 2026-11-17, read 2026-11-18T17:56Z).
- 18 Nov: "the catch-all initial draft of the cover decision - which the Arab Group and Russia say they reject - has yet to be slimmed down"; presidency convened a ministerial "Divan" at 17:00 local, chaired by COP31 President Murat Kurum; "It is unclear whether the next version of the text will come out before or after the 'Divan' meeting. The presidency has said only that it will be published as soon as possible." — Climate Home Day 9 (above).
- Other open files: Climate Finance Work Programme (LDCs want it continued with a goal to triple adaptation finance; EU and Switzerland want no text) — Climate Home Day 8 (above); Adaptation Fund pledging session raised $50m against a $300m annual target — Climate Home Day 9.
- The cover decision is meant as "the cherry on the cake of 'a balanced package' including negotiated agenda items" — Climate Home Day 9 (above); talks cover 160 agenda items and 60 mandates — Hürriyet Daily News https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/cop31-turns-to-ministers-for-deal-on-finance-energy-229443 (published 2026-11-16T02:59Z, read 2026-11-18T17:53Z).
- Bowen on consensus: "We'll be working pretty hard to get consensus and then you'll see how that plays out in the [closing] plenary." — Climate Home Day 7 (above).
- Experts (E3G's Alden Meyer) said the sharp divisions would be hard to reconcile in time to land an ambitious cover decision — Climate Home Day 7 (above).
- No presidency statement found that it aims to finish early or exactly on time (searched Climate Home, Hürriyet, Al-Monitor, Daily Sabah).

## Risks (to the obvious NO)
- A presidency that strips the text to the lowest common denominator (dropping fossil-fuel, electrification and finance targets) could gavel quickly on Friday night; COP28 showed the cover/GST text can be adopted "at lightning speed" once agreed.
- Edge: plenary opened before midnight but gavel after 00:00 Antalya → NO. Time zone: Antalya is UTC+3 (no DST), 22:00 Paris.
- Edge: the presidency may split the outcome (e.g. "Antalya package" of several decisions); only the overarching cover decision counts.
