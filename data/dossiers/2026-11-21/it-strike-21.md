# it-strike-21
- **Claim:** On Friday 20 November 2026, the last figure the Italian government's Department of Public Administration (Dipartimento della Funzione pubblica) or Public Administration Minister Paolo Zangrillo gives that day for public employees' participation in the CGIL general strike is below 10%.
- **Criterion:** YES if the last public-sector participation rate (adesione dei dipendenti pubblici, % of staff, national, all administrations reporting) published or stated on 20 Nov 2026 by 23:59 CET by the Dipartimento della Funzione pubblica (GEPAS data, note or website) or by Minister Zangrillo is below 10.0%, as reported by ANSA, Adnkronos, LaPresse, Il Sole 24 Ore, Corriere della Sera or la Repubblica (or on funzionepubblica.gov.it). Partial figures count (they are always partial on the day); the latest one given by 23:59 decides. Figures for a single sector (schools from the Education Ministry, health from the Health Ministry) do not count. NO if that last figure is 10.0% or more, or if neither the Department nor the minister gives any national public-sector figure on 20 Nov 2026 (including if the strike is called off).
- **Event window:** 2026-11-20T00:00+01:00 – 2026-11-20T23:59+01:00 (Europe/Rome)
- **Evidence expected:** minister's note or Department figure in the afternoon/evening of 20 Nov (2023: data to 14:00; 2025: notes ~13:00, ~15:40 and 18:30), reported by ANSA/LaPresse; evidence_at 2026-11-20T21:00+01:00.
- **Resolves by:** 2026-11-22T05:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-18T18:15Z

## Usual surprise size

## Risks
- Only an early partial figure is given (as the "20%" at 13:00 on 12 Dec 2025) and no later update → could be ≥10%.
- No national figure given on the day → NO under the criterion.
- Ambiguity: a figure "on 50% of workers" vs "on all"; the claim takes whatever national PA rate is stated last.
- A sector figure (schools, health) quoted as "adesione nel pubblico" by media; criterion excludes single-sector figures.
