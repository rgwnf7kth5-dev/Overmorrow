# cop31-draft-21
- **Claim:** On Friday 20 November 2026, the scheduled last day of COP31, the presidency publishes a new version of the draft cover decision, and the last version published that day (by 23:59 Antalya time) still speaks of transitioning away from, or phasing out, fossil fuels.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 00:00 and 23:59 on 20 Nov 2026, Europe/Istanbul time (UTC+3), the COP31 presidency (Türkiye/Australia) publishes on unfccc.int (COP31 draft texts / presidency pages) or circulates to Parties a new version of the draft cover decision — any document presented as a revised/new draft of the overarching decision, including a "Proposal by the President" (L-document) for that decision; drafts of other agenda items do not count — AND the last such version published in that window contains, anywhere in its text (preamble, operative paragraphs, options, brackets, footnotes or the title of a roadmap or initiative it names), at least one of: "transition away from fossil fuels", "transitioning away from fossil fuels" (with or without "in energy systems"), "phase out fossil fuels", "phase-out of fossil fuels", "phase out of fossil fuels", "phasing out fossil fuels" (also with "coal, oil and gas" in place of "fossil fuels"). Checked against the document on unfccc.int or a verbatim quote by Carbon Brief, Climate Home News, ENB (IISD), Reuters or AFP; publication time from the document page or the first time-stamped report. Not enough on their own: "fossil fuel subsidies", "phasedown of unabated coal", "transitional fuels", "energy transition", "TAFF" without the words, or a reference to paragraph 28 of decision 1/CMA.5 without the words. NO if no new version of the draft cover decision is published in the window (a version published on 19 Nov or after 23:59 on 20 Nov does not count), or if the last version published in the window has none of the listed wordings. Adoption of the decision is not required.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-20T00:00+03:00 – 2026-11-20T23:59+03:00 (Europe/Istanbul) = 2026-11-19T22:00 – 2026-11-20T21:59 Paris

## Facts
- First draft: 18 pages, published around midday Monday 16 Nov — Climate Home Bulletin Day 7 https://www.climatechangenews.com/2026/11/16/cop31-bulletin-day-7-sharp-divisions-lower-expectations-for-cop-outcome/ (read 2026-11-18T17:56Z) "On Monday lunchtime, the COP Presidency published an 18-page draft cover decision"; it tried to include all options from 45 submissions representing more than 160 governments (same source).
- Fossil-fuel wording in the 16 Nov draft: "calls for accelerated action to phase out fossil fuels, including through the immediate transition away from fossil fuels in energy systems" — AFP via Daily Sabah https://www.dailysabah.com/business/energy/cop31-draft-calls-for-accelerated-action-to-phase-out-fossil-fuels (published 2026-11-16T14:13Z, read 2026-11-18T17:54Z); same quote NewsBytes https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/what-does-cop31-s-draft-deal-say/story (published 2026-11-16T14:23Z, read 2026-11-18T18:21Z).
- Two options: "One reiterated wording agreed in 2023 around 'transitioning away' from their use, and another more ambitious clause talked about their 'phase out'. Both could be omitted from a final deal." — Reuters via Al-Monitor (above).
- Brazil's COP30 voluntary fossil-fuel roadmap is only "welcomed", in paragraph 140, among voluntary initiatives — Climate Home Bulletin Day 8 https://www.climatechangenews.com/2026/11/17/cop31-bulletin-day-8-arab-group-russia-reject-cover-text-over-fossil-fuels-and-electrification-goal/ (read 2026-11-18T17:56Z). 350.org "welcomed the roadmap for transitioning away from fossil fuels but criticized the absence of a process to advance it" — NewsBytes (above). Exact wording of para 140 not verified.
- Opposition: at the heads-of-delegation meeting on 16 Nov evening, LMDC (incl. China, India, Saudi Arabia) said the draft crosses red lines incl. "the roadmap on transitioning away from fossil fuels"; "The Arab Group and Russia went further, rejecting the draft text entirely"; Azerbaijan critical — Climate Home Day 8 (above). Arab Group: no "global targets imposed from above or targets directed at particular sectors or energy sources" — Hürriyet Daily News https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/cop31-turns-to-ministers-for-deal-on-finance-energy-229443 (read 2026-11-18T17:53Z).
- Presidency: Bowen — "Will it look exactly like it does in the draft? No. Is it appropriate that there's a conversation about fossil fuels in the draft? Absolutely." and "What we'll be concentrating on in the coming days are those issues where people can find common ground" (Reuters via Al-Monitor; AFP via Daily Sabah, above). Before the draft he declined to say whether it would include TAFF language (Hürriyet, above).
- Support: EU wanted the text to reflect GST implementation "including transitioning away from fossil fuels" (ENB 14 Nov, see data/dossiers/2026-11-17/cop31-fossil-17.md); Spain and Vanuatu want a reference to Brazil's roadmap (Climate Home Day 8); Germany says TAFF in the cover decision is "not for us the ultimate benchmark" — Euronews https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/18/cop31-is-once-again-shying-away-from-a-fossil-fuel-exit-but-one-european-country-is-taking (read 2026-11-18T17:53Z). The COP30 final text dropped the subject "debido a la oposición de países productores, entre ellos Arabia Saudita" — La Nación (EFE) https://www.nacion.com/el-mundo/cop31-propone-acelerar-el-abandono-del-petroleo-el/OSCOTMMECNA57L7XLSAEPI3MVE/story/ (published 2026-11-16T14:21Z, read 2026-11-18T18:21Z).
- 18 Nov: draft "has yet to be slimmed down"; ministerial "Divan" at 17:00 chaired by Kurum — Climate Home Day 9 (above).
