# cz-algeria-gas-agreement

- **Claim:** During PM Andrej Babiš's official visit to Algeria, by 23:59 Algiers time on 26 October 2026, a Czech party (the government, a ministry, ČEZ or another Czech company) and an Algerian party (the government, a ministry or Sonatrach) sign an agreement, contract or memorandum on natural-gas supply or natural-gas cooperation.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 on 25 Oct 2026 and 23:59 on 26 Oct 2026 (Algiers time = CET, UTC+1), a document whose subject is natural-gas supply or natural-gas cooperation (pipeline gas or LNG; a broader energy document counts only if the official report says it covers natural gas) is signed by a Czech party (government, ministry, ČEZ or another Czech company) and an Algerian party (government, ministry, Sonatrach or another Algerian state company), as stated by the Czech Government Office (vlada.gov.cz), the Ministry of Industry and Trade (mpo.gov.cz), ČEZ, Sonatrach, APS or ČTK. Documents signed on 27 Oct or later, statements that gas "was discussed", and announcements of a future signing do not count. NO if no such document is signed in the window (including if the visit is cancelled or postponed).
