# cz-pardubice-coalition-signed-26

- **Claim:** The five groupings negotiating to run Pardubice city hall (ODS, STAN, Progresivní Pardubice, Naše Pardubice, Noví lidovci) sign their coalition agreement between 06:00 on 25 October and 23:59 on 26 October 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 CET on 25 Oct 2026 and 23:59 CET on 26 Oct 2026, representatives of ODS, STAN, Progresivní Pardubice, Naše Pardubice and Noví lidovci (or of a coalition containing at least ODS and STAN that excludes ANO) sign a coalition agreement for the Pardubice city council for 2026–2030, as reported by ČTK, iRozhlas, Deník, ČT24 or the parties' official channels. A memorandum, "agreement in principle", agreed programme or announced future signing date does not count. NO if no such signature takes place in the window, including if it was signed before 06:00 on 25 Oct or is signed after 26 Oct.
- **Deadline (event window end):** 2026-10-26T23:59:59+01:00
