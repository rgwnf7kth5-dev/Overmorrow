# cz-prague-coalition-agreement

- **Claim:** By 23:59 on 26 October 2026, STAN, Spolu and Praha sobě publicly announce that the three of them have agreed how the Prague City Council (rada) portfolios are divided among them, or sign a three-party coalition agreement.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 CET on 25 Oct 2026 and 23:59 CET on 26 Oct 2026, the three groupings jointly, or a lead negotiator of one of them (e.g. Petr Hlaváček, Tomáš Portlík, Adam Scheinherr) without contradiction from another of the three by 23:59 on 26 Oct, publicly state that STAN, Spolu and Praha sobě have agreed the allocation of council portfolios (gesce) among the three, as reported by ČTK, ČT24 or another major Czech outlet. Signing a three-party coalition agreement in the window also counts. Statements that talks continue, agreement "in principle" on a coalition without the portfolio split, a seat count without portfolios, or a STAN–Spolu split without Praha sobě do not count. NO if no such announcement is made in the window.
- **Deadline (event window end):** 2026-10-26T23:59:59+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-24T19:05:00+02:00

## Facts
- Results (65 seats, majority 33): STAN 17, Spolu 17, Praha sobě 13, ANO 11, Pirates 7 — https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/praha-sobe-nabidla-stan-vetsinu-v-rade-a-post-primatora-v-koalici-s-piraty/2890525 (ČTK, 20 Oct; carried over).
- Planned split: STAN 5, Spolu 4, Praha sobě 2 council seats; one Praha sobě seat with transport; without a third partner STAN 6, Spolu 5 — ČT24 URL above "Jedním z nich by byla gesce dopravy."
- Deadline logic: if no three-party coalition by the November session, STAN and Spolu (34 seats) govern alone — Aktuálně headline "Pokud nebude trojkoalice do listopadu, bude Prahu řídit koalice STAN a Spolu".
- STAN and Spolu negotiate as one team; their programme statement is ready (Portlík) — Aktuálně URL above "STAN a Spolu už budou v jednáních vystupovat jako jeden tým."
- Pirates rejected STAN's offer (wanted 2 seats, offered 1) — ČT24 URL above "Piráti ale jejich nabídku odmítli."
- Praha sobě (20 Oct) preferred STAN–Praha sobě–Pirates and wanted guarantees on transport and housing; on 22 Oct said it wants to keep negotiating (ČTK/ČT24, carried over). Novinky 19 Oct: "Praha sobě wants a coalition without Spolu" (paraphrase) — https://www.novinky.cz/clanek/volby-komunalni-prazska-koalice-se-rysuje-vitezne-stan-rozdava-nabidky-40603616 (published 2026-10-19 18:39, read 2026-10-24T16:49Z).
- Hlaváček: "preferujeme trojkoalici" (Novinky URL above); rejects talks extending into January (ČTK via Forum24, carried over).
