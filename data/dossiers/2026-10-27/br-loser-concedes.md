# br-loser-concedes

- **Claim:** The candidate who loses Brazil's 25 October 2026 presidential runoff (Lula or Flávio Bolsonaro) publicly acknowledges defeat or congratulates the winner by 23:59 Brasília time on 26 October 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 BRT on 25 Oct 2026 and 23:59 BRT on 26 Oct 2026, the losing candidate himself, in a speech, interview, video, phone call he or the winner makes public, or a post on his own verified social-media account, either (a) recognises that his opponent won the election (e.g. "reconheço a vitória", "perdemos", "o povo escolheu X", calling the opponent "presidente eleito") or (b) congratulates the winner or wishes him success as president. Statements by aides, party leaders, ministers, relatives or campaign staff do not count; neither do statements that only thank voters, promise to keep fighting, call for calm or say he will "respect the Constitution" without recognising the outcome. NO if no such statement is made by the losing candidate in the window (including if the runoff is not held on 25 Oct). "Losing candidate" = the one with fewer valid votes in the TSE count.
- **Deadline (event window end):** 2026-10-26T23:59:59-03:00
- **Written at:** 2026-10-24T19:05:00+02:00

## Schedule
- Runoff Sunday 25 Oct 2026, voting 08:00–17:00 BRT; TSE releases results from 17:00 BRT nationwide. Facts carried over from data/dossiers/2026-10-26/br-flavio-wins.md (sources: jornalopcao.com.br, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Brazilian_general_election, tse.jus.br).
- 2022 precedent for timing: totalisation of all sections finished 00:18 BRT on Mon 31 Oct 2022 — https://www.tse.jus.br/comunicacao/noticias/2022/Outubro/100-das-secoes-totalizadas-confira-como-ficou-o-quadro-eleitoral-apos-o-2o-turno (carried over). The window therefore gives the loser about 31 hours after polls close.
- Concession itself is not a scheduled event; no announced time or venue for either candidate's election-night speech found.

## Base rate
- Class: losing candidate in a Brazilian presidential runoff acknowledges defeat or congratulates the winner by the end of the day after the vote. Cases (acessa.com, agazeta, poder360 above): 1989 no (about two days, paper count), 2002 yes, 2006 yes, 2010 yes, 2014 yes, 2018 yes (via next-day tweet; election-night speech alone would be borderline under this criterion), 2022 no. Rate 5/7; electronic-voting era (2002–2022) 5/6. No class in pipeline/reference-data/.
- Note: the only "no" in the electronic era is the Bolsonaro family's own 2022 case.

## Risks
- Very close result (polls within 2 pp) → loser may wait for totalisation, audits or a party "auditoria especial" request (PSDB 2014 precedent) and stay silent past 26 Oct.
- If Flávio loses: family precedent (2022 silence, ~45 h), pressure from the base, his own earlier doubts about the machines; on the other hand his August pledge and a TSE led by Bolsonaro appointees.
- If Lula loses: sitting president; his 1998 statement shows acknowledgement mixed with questioning legitimacy — a hedged statement may be hard to classify.
- Ambiguity: statements like Haddad's 2018 election-night speech or Bolsonaro's 2022 "respect the Constitution" line do not count under the criterion; a resolver must check the exact words.
- Statement through an aide (e.g. a chief of staff announcing the transition, as Ciro Nogueira did in 2022 — general knowledge, not checked this session) does not count.
