# ca-toronto-chow-reelected

- **Claim:** Olivia Chow wins the Toronto mayoral election on 26 October 2026 (most votes in the City of Toronto's unofficial results).
- **Criterion:** YES if the City of Toronto's unofficial election-night results for Mayor (toronto.ca/election-results), with all voting places reported, show Olivia Chow with more votes than any other candidate. NO if another candidate has the most votes, or if the vote does not take place on 26 Oct 2026. Later recounts or the official declaration do not change the resolution.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-26T10:00:00-04:00 to 2026-10-26T23:59:59-04:00 (voting day)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-24T19:10:00+02:00

## Schedule
- Election day Monday 26 Oct 2026, 10 a.m.–8 p.m.; unofficial results posted at toronto.ca/election-results after voting places close; DS200 tabulators at every voting place, mail-in ballots on a DS950 central-count tabulator; 53 certified mayoral candidates — https://www.toronto.ca/news/fact-sheet-2026-toronto-municipal-election/ (published 2026-09-22, read 2026-10-24T16:52Z) "Monday, October 26, 2026, 10 a.m. to 8 p.m." Status: official.

- Single-round, first-past-the-post (no runoff).
