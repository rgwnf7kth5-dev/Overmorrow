**Criterion:** YES if the Council of the EU (press release, meeting page or presidency) states that ministers reached political agreement on the Council regulation fixing 2027 fishing opportunities in the Baltic Sea, and the agreement was reached at or before 23:59 CET on 26 Oct 2026 — shown by a Council press release dated 26 Oct 2026, or by a presidency/minister statement or agency report time-stamped no later than 23:59 CET on 26 Oct 2026 saying the deal is done.
