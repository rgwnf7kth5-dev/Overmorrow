# br-flavio-tse-31

- **Claim:** By 23:59 Brasília time on Saturday 31 October 2026, Flávio Bolsonaro, his coalition or the PL files a new action at the Superior Electoral Court (TSE) seeking to annul the 25 October presidential run-off or to strip the elected Lula–Alckmin ticket of its registration or diploma (cassação).
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 2026-10-30T00:00:00-03:00 and 2026-10-31T23:59:59-03:00 (America/Sao_Paulo), Flávio Bolsonaro, his campaign coalition or the PL files a petition, representação or action at the TSE. Filings by their lawyers on their behalf count. Any such filing counts, including an AIJE or AIME. The filing must seek one of: (i) annulment or invalidation of all or part of the presidential run-off; (ii) a recount or re-vote; or (iii) cassação of the registration or diploma of the Lula–Alckmin ticket. Evidence: the TSE (tse.jus.br news or the PJe docket), or at least one of Folha, O Globo/g1, Estadão, UOL or Agência Brasil reporting the filing and its date. The following do not count: actions filed before 30 Oct, including the AIJEs filed during the campaign; statements of intent; filings at other courts (STF etc.); and audit or clarification requests that seek none of (i)–(iii). NO if no such filing is made at the TSE in the window.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-30T00:00:00-03:00 to 2026-10-31T23:59:59-03:00 (America/Sao_Paulo)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-29T18:20:00Z

Why the claim was reworded: the suggested claim was "file an annulment or review request". Our 28 Oct claim (br-flavio-tse-filing-28) resolved NO: no annulment or recount filing was made through 27 Oct. Since 26 Oct the reported plan has been an AIJE seeking cassação of Lula's ticket, not an annulment petition. The claim therefore covers both routes. The fallback ("TSE rejects it by 31 Oct") does not apply, because nothing has been filed.

## Schedule
- **No scheduled step.**
- **Deadlines.** None falls in the window. An AIJE can be filed up to the diplomação. AIME is the 15-day window after diplomação. Both are own knowledge of Brazilian electoral law and were not re-verified tonight. The 2026 diplomação date was not found tonight; in 2022 it was 12 Dec.
- **How filing works.** TSE filings go through PJe electronically, so a Saturday filing is possible.
- **Flávio's public plans.** He said on 28 Oct he would "address the public regularly in the coming days and present a strategy to mobilise the opposition" — Estado de Minas https://www.em.com.br/politica/2026/10/7530105-o-recado-duro-da-casa-branca-para-flavio-bolsonaro.html (published 2026-10-29 11:01 BRT, read 2026-10-29T18:05Z).
- **Press conference.** A press conference in Miami was announced for Thu 29 Oct. A planned conference was cancelled on 28 Oct and replaced by a letter (same source).

## Facts
- **Announced annulment.** On election night (25 Oct), Flávio said: "É impossível reconhecer o resultado das eleições no contexto como essas coisas aconteceram". His grounds:
  - Justice Zanin's extension of voting to 19h;
  - TSE guidance reaching polling stations at 17h49, after machines had closed;
  - alleged removal of PL observers from the sala-cofre.
  As of 26 Oct morning, it was not confirmed that the PL had formally filed — Correio da Manhã https://www.correiodamanha.com.br/politica/2026/10/329920-flavio-prepara-pedido-de-anulacao-da-eleicao-e-acusa-stf-de-quebra-de-isonomia.html (published 2026-10-26 08:55 BRT, read 2026-10-29T17:58Z).
- **Retreat from annulment.** Less than 24 hours after announcing it, Flávio dropped the plan to seek annulment in court, under pressure from allies, senators, governors (incl. Tarcísio de Freitas) and lawyers. Allies said "página virada". The account is based on Folha's Painel column — Brasil 247 https://brasil247.com/brasil/apos-pressao-de-aliados-flavio-bolsonaro-recua-da-ideia-de-pedir-anulacao-da-eleicao (published 2026-10-26 17:10 BRT, updated 19:25, read 2026-10-29T18:03Z) "desistiu de pedir na Justiça anulação da eleição"
- **Planned AIJE.** His campaign is preparing an AIJE against Lula's ticket for alleged abuse of economic power, targeting government measures announced during the campaign. It could seek cassação of the ticket. No filing date was given — Bahia Notícias https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/noticia/322995-flavio-bolsonaro-desiste-de-pedir-anulacao-da-eleicao-e-prepara-acao-contra-lula (published 2026-10-26 17:10 BRT, read 2026-10-29T17:55Z) "deve apresentar … Ação de Investigação Judicial Eleitoral (Aije) por suposto abuso de poder econômico"
- **Our previous resolution, 28 Oct.** O Povo (28 Oct, citing Folha) and Times Brasil (26 Oct) report that Flávio dropped the annulment route and only announced an intended cassação action. The PL recognised the result. No annulment or recount filing was made through 27 Oct — data/resolutions/2026-10-28.json, evidence https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/mundo/2026/10/28/flavio-altera-caminho-para-buscar-reversao-da-derrota-eleitoral.html (not opened tonight; permission blocked).
- **Existing AIJEs.** At least 22 AIJEs involve the two tickets: Lula is a defendant in 15 and Flávio in 7, per the TSE public database. Those admitted continue after the vote. Flávio's side had already filed actions against Lula, e.g. over the Acadêmicos de Niterói parade. No new post-election AIJE by Flávio or the PL was reported. Flávio said his campaign "acionou o setor jurídico" — CNN Brasil https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/eleicoes/disputa-entre-lula-e-flavio-continua-no-tse-mesmo-apos-o-fim-das-eleicoes/ (published 2026-10-27 03:30 BRT, updated 12:56, read 2026-10-29T18:00Z).
- **28 Oct letter.** On 28 Oct Flávio sent a three-page letter to Brazilian and foreign media from the US. Its first version called him "o verdadeiro presidente eleito do Brasil"; minutes later this was corrected to "o líder do Brasil". The article mentions no TSE filing — Estado de Minas (above).
- **PL reaction to the letter.** PL members said they "não foram consultados sobre o conteúdo do documento". Rogério Marinho (PL) said there are no elements to allege fraud — Rede Hoje https://redehoje.com.br/2026/10/29/reacoes-viagem-e-carta-sobre-eleicao-trazem-reveses-para-flavio-bolsonaro-com-aliados-e-autoridades-americanas-e-italianas/ (published 2026-10-29 10:29Z, read 2026-10-29T18:04Z).
- **TSE position.** TSE president Kassio Nunes Marques: "todos os questionamentos apresentados dentro do devido processo legal serão analisados e respondidos" — Rede Hoje (above). Interlocutors of Nunes Marques say there is "não há disposição para rever o resultado". He said Zanin's decision "não comprometeu a apuração" — A Crítica https://acritica.net/politica/flavio-contesta-eleicao-tse-deve-manter-vitoria-lula/ (published 2026-10-26 11:05 BRT, read 2026-10-29T17:59Z).
- **Result.** Lula 64,256,232 votes (51.79%), Flávio 59,826,041 (48.21%), TSE result confirmed 25 Oct — data/dossiers/2026-10-31/br-flavio-arrest-30.md (Poder360).
- **Flávio's situation.** He is in Miami since 28 Oct. His Brazilian passports are cancelled. He is under an STF inquiry (Moraes) for suspected money laundering — same dossier.

- **Historical precedent (own recall, not re-verified tonight).**
  - After the 30 Oct 2022 run-off, the PL (Valdemar Costa Neto) filed a "representação para verificação extraordinária" at the TSE on 22 Nov 2022, 23 days after the vote. It sought to invalidate votes from older voting machines.
  - Moraes rejected it within about a day and fined the PL about R$22.9 m for bad-faith litigation.
  - So the Bolsonaro camp has filed a post-election contest before, but weeks later, not within days.

## Risks
- **Toward YES.**
  - An AIJE prepared since 26 Oct could be filed at any time.
  - Flávio keeps contesting the result publicly (letter of 28 Oct).
  - A filing would be a way to show action after losing allies' support.
  - Electronic filing is possible on any day.
- **Toward NO.**
  - Allies, PL senators and governors oppose contesting the result; the PL reportedly recognised it.
  - The 2022 precedent shows a lag of weeks.
  - There is no deadline before December.
  - Flávio is abroad, and his legal focus may shift to the STF inquiry and US status.
  - A filing by another party (e.g. a PL deputy individually, Novo) or by voters would not count unless made for the coalition or the PL.
- **Resolution risk.**
  - A filing may surface only days later. The filing date on the docket governs.
  - An AIJE about campaign-period conduct may be reported without the word cassação. The resolver must check the petition's request.
