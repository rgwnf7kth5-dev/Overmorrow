# es-puente-out-31
(The id was assigned as `es-ave-resumed-31`. The claim was switched because a reopening of the Madrid–Andalusia high-speed line by 31 Oct is practically ruled out: Renfe has blocked high-speed ticket sales on the corridor until 13 Dec, Ouigo has cancelled its services until at least 2 Nov, and the Orgaz court has restricted about 2 km of the line. The suggested fallback, a summons of Puente to Congress, has already happened: on 29 Oct his appearance was set for 3 Nov, outside the window. See Schedule.)

- **Claim:** By 23:59 Madrid time on Saturday 31 October 2026, Óscar Puente announces his resignation as Spain's Minister of Transport, or his removal is announced, or the president of Adif resigns or is removed.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 on 30 Oct and 23:59:59 on 31 Oct 2026 (Europe/Madrid), any of the following is announced:
  - Puente publicly announces that he is resigning or has resigned as minister;
  - La Moncloa or the Prime Minister announces Puente's removal (cese);
  - the president of Adif announces his resignation;
  - the Ministry of Transport, the Government or Adif announces the president of Adif's removal or replacement.

  A BOE decree published later still counts if the announcement was made inside the window. NO if no such announcement is made in the window. None of the following counts:
  - statements that someone will "consider" resigning;
  - a departure scheduled for after the 29 Nov election;
  - the dismissal or suspension of lower-ranking Adif staff, such as the control-centre operator or directors below the president.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-30T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-10-31T23:59:59+01:00 (Europe/Madrid)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-29T18:10Z

## Facts
- **Death toll.** At least 69 dead. The CIAF report raised the toll to 74 at 17:28, then rectified it at 18:27, returning the official toll to 69 — Libertad Digital (as above).
  - Okdiario cites 74 deaths from Moncloa sources. Figures differ between sources.
- **Puente's stance.** Puente again rejected resigning: "En este momento en lo que estoy pensando en esclarecer esto, no estoy pensando en mí." — https://okdiario.com/espana/moncloa-filtra-que-sanchez-respalda-puente-que-no-destituira-pese-dos-megatragedias-solo-9-meses-20661923 (published 2026-10-29 18:34 CET, upd. 18:46, read 2026-10-29T17:58Z).
- **Moncloa's backing.** Moncloa sources (to Efe) say Sánchez keeps his confidence in Puente. The government attributes the crash to human error at the Atocha control centre and separates it from Puente's management — okdiario (as above).
- **Sánchez.** "no es asumible para un país como España sufrir dos accidentes ferroviarios de alta velocidad en un mismo año"; he promised "todas las medidas que se consideren oportunas" — okdiario (as above).
- **Demands for his removal.**
  - CCOO has requested the "cese de Óscar Puente" — andaluciainformacion (as above).
  - CSIF (Pedro Poves) demanded Puente's resignation: "en lugar de salvar a sus marineros, los echa por la borda" — Libertad Digital, entries 12:39 and 14:40.
- **Parliamentary requests.** Podemos requested an urgent appearance before the Diputación Permanente (Libertad Digital, 11:04). So did Frente Amplio, ERC and Podemos (campogibraltar24h).
- **Feijóo (PP).** "el Gobierno vino a echar la culpa a alguien" — Libertad Digital, 13:23.
- **Protests.** Hundreds of protesters gathered outside the Transport Ministry demanding his resignation — Libertad Digital, 06:39.
- **Cause and investigation.**
  - Leaked Adif recordings: the AVE driver was told to proceed in normal running rather than "marcha a la vista" — Libertad Digital, 07:21.
  - The Adif worker who authorised the movement has been identified and is part of the investigation. The judge has had both trains' black boxes seized — campogibraltar24h (as above).
- **Puente on what is known.** "Lo que ha pasado en los 17 minutos" between the departure order and the collision "no lo sabemos con precisión" — andaluciainformacion (as above).
- **Adif leadership.** None of the sources read mentions any move against Adif's president after Urda.
  - A Jan 2026 photo caption names Pedro Marco de la Peña as Adif president — https://www.eldebate.com/espana/cataluna/20260126/presidente-adif-no-concreta-cuando-volvera-normalidad-rodalies-seria-imprudente-dar-fecha_378367.html (search result only). Whether he is still in post was not verified.
  - Precedent: in April 2026, after Adamuz, the Andalusian government demanded the heads of the Renfe and Adif presidents or Puente's own. Esdiario, "El Gobierno de Moreno exige a Óscar Puente 'la cabeza' de los presidentes de Renfe y Adif o la suya", dated 2026-04-29 — https://www.esdiario.com/andalucia/260429/186394/gobierno-moreno-pide-oscar-puente-dimision-presidentes-renfe-adif-suya.html (search result title only).
  - Puente dismissed Adif president Ángel Contreras in Sept 2024. Elindependiente, "Óscar Puente destituye al presidente de Adif Ángel Contreras", dated 2024-09-13 — https://www.elindependiente.com/economia/2024/09/13/oscar-puente-destituye-al-presidente-de-adif-angel-contreras/amp/ (search result title only).
- **Adamuz precedent.** After the Jan 2026 Adamuz crash (46 dead), Puente did not resign despite calls from PP, Vox and ERC and a Senate reprobation — dossier es-puente-out-30 (eldebate 2026-02-04, title).

## Risks
- **Toward YES:**
  - Puente blames Adif's control centre, so an Adif-level scalp (the president) is a plausible way to show accountability before 3 Nov.
  - New revelations from the recordings or the CIAF could shift blame to ministry or Adif management.
  - Election-campaign pressure (29 Nov) could push Sánchez to act.
- **Resolution ambiguity:**
  - Removal of an Adif director-general or the head of the control centre does not count.
  - A resignation announced on 1 Nov or later does not count.
