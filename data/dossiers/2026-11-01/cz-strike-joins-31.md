# cz-strike-joins-31
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Prague time on Saturday 31 October 2026, the National Theatre (Národní divadlo, its management, one of its ensembles or a trade union there), the Czech Philharmonic or the trade-union confederation ČMKOS announces that it will join the 3 November strike against the bill abolishing the ČT and ČRo licence fees.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 on 30 Oct 2026 and 23:59:59 CET on 31 Oct 2026, one of the following publicly announces that it will take part in a strike or strike action on Tue 3 Nov 2026 (for example stopping work, cancelling or altering a performance, or a timed work stoppage) in connection with the 3 Nov strike or protest against the bill on licence fees and ČT/ČRo funding (sněmovní tisk 231): (a) Národní divadlo, meaning its management, any of its ensembles (Činohra, Opera/Státní opera, Balet, Laterna magika) or a trade-union organisation at ND; (b) the Czech Philharmonic (Česká filharmonie), meaning its management, the orchestra or its trade union; (c) the Českomoravská konfederace odborových svazů (ČMKOS) as a confederation, by declaring a strike or strike action, or by formally calling its member unions to take part in the 3 Nov strike. Evidence is the institution's website or social media, or a report by ČTK, ČT24, iROZHLAS, Seznam Zprávy, Novinky, Deník N or Aktuálně.cz. These do not count: statements of support or solidarity without taking part in a strike or strike action; strike readiness (stávková pohotovost) alone; individual artists or employees; and announcements made public before 30 Oct. NO if nothing qualifying is announced in the window.

## Risks
- YES paths: the ND drama unions turn strike readiness into a stoppage on 3 Nov (e.g. a cancelled or delayed evening performance). The ND management announces a symbolic action. ČMKOS calls members to join. Friday (30 Oct) is the most likely day.
- NO paths: the ND unions keep to readiness over their own dispute (Klempíř, the ND funding transfer), or announce only on Mon 2 Nov. A Babiš concession tonight cools the mobilisation. The Czech Philharmonic, a state-funded orchestra on tour schedules, stays out.
- Resolution ambiguity: "joins with a symbolic action" (e.g. a statement read before a performance, black clothing) versus a strike action. The criterion needs a strike or strike action such as a work stoppage or a cancelled or altered performance. A statement read on stage alone does not count. The desk should apply this strictly.
- Announced before 30 Oct: if the ND unions announced their 3 Nov participation on 29 Oct (not found in the sources read), only a new announcement in the window counts.
