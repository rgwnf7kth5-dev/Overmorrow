State of California et al., No. 2:26-cv-12025-DOC-ADS (C.D. Cal.). A panel, motions panel or en banc court counts. The order must do one of the following:
  - grant or deny, in whole or in part, a United States motion for an injunction pending appeal, a stay, an administrative injunction or other emergency relief;
  - dismiss the appeal;
  - decide it on the merits.

  The order date on the Ninth Circuit docket governs. It can be confirmed by the docket (PACER/CourtListener) or by a report in AP, Reuters, CNN, NBC, CBS, NPR, The New York Times, Politico or Democracy Docket.

  The following do not count: orders setting briefing or argument schedules, orders calling for a response, and rulings by the district court or the Supreme Court.

  NO if no such order is entered in the window. That includes a ruling entered before 30 Oct 00:00 PT, which makes this claim moot (the editor replaces it).

## Facts
- **The 26 Oct order.** The Ninth Circuit (No. 26-7070, DktEntry 63.1, filed 26 Oct 2026, "FOR PUBLICATION", per curiam) "grants the County's motion to dismiss the appeal for lack of appellate jurisdiction". It denied the US emergency motion as moot. Judges Bennett and Sanchez concurred; Judge Bumatay dissented.
  - Caption: "UNITED STATES OF AMERICA, Plaintiff-Appellant, v. STATE OF CALIFORNIA", with Shirley Weber (Secretary of State) and Dean Logan (LA County Registrar) among the defendants-appellees. D.C. No. 2:26-cv-12025-DOC-ADS.
  - Source: SAN court-docs PDF https://san.com/wp-content/uploads/securepdfs/2026/10/CaliforniaBallotCourtDocs.pdf (read 2026-10-29T18:13Z).
- **The majority on the emergency.** "The United States's claimed emergency, at least in part, is a self-constructed one." Bennett and Sanchez wrote separately that they would also deny on the merits — SAN https://san.com/cc/appeals-court-rejects-trump-administration-push-to-set-aside-20000-california-mail-ballots/ (published 2026-10-27 07:00 CDT, read 2026-10-29T18:06Z); Democracy Docket (below).
- **The 25 Oct argument.**
  - Bennett: "I don't see how we have jurisdiction."
  - Sanchez questioned the reliability of the list.
  - Bumatay: "I think the government is also concerned about not having the illegal vote count."
  - The panel said it "would try to move as fast as possible."
  - Source: Democracy Docket https://www.democracydocket.com/news-alerts/appeals-court-appears-skeptical-of-dojs-emergency-bid-to-block-20000-ballots-in-california/ (published 2026-10-25 22:54Z, read 2026-10-29T18:06Z).
- **Carter's 28 Oct ruling.** Carter held the request ran afoul of "longstanding constitutional principles mandating that states have control over their elections". He wrote: "Never before in American history has the federal government, on the eve of an election, sought to coerce a state" and "These threats must stop" — Al Jazeera and 2paragraphs (above). He also said the relief "would wreak havoc and disrupt the election" — Just the News https://justthenews.com/politics-policy/elections/judge-rejects-doj-request-stop-california-counting-ballots-alleged (published 2026-10-29 09:18, read 2026-10-29T18:16Z).
- **The department's response.** US Attorney Bill Essayli on X: "The district court denied our emergency request to sequester ballots from 20,000 suspected noncitizens"; "This ongoing emergency disenfranchises American citizens through dilution" — Just the News; 2paragraphs (above).
- **The lawsuit.** It was filed 15 Oct against California and Los Angeles County — Democracy Docket https://www.democracydocket.com/news-alerts/judge-rejects-doj-demand-that-california-set-aside-20000-ballots/ (dated 2026-10-28, modified 2026-10-29 00:42Z, read 2026-10-29T18:09Z). The same page links a separate item: the Trump prosecutor leading the case was "disqualified from post, but order stayed" (headline only).
- **Jurisdiction this time.** In the first round the court dismissed because the district court had not yet ruled. There is now an appealable denial of a TRO/preliminary-injunction-type request, so the jurisdictional ground of 26 Oct no longer applies by the same logic. This is our inference; whether Carter's order counts as a TRO or a preliminary injunction for appeal purposes was not checked.

## Risks
- **Before the window.** A ruling on Thu 29 Oct makes the claim moot or NO. The morning desk must check the docket.
- **No emergency motion.** If none was filed, there is nothing to rule on quickly, which means NO.
- **Holding back.** The panel may set argument for Sun 1 Nov or Mon 2 Nov and rule after the window.
- **Order type.** An administrative injunction ("temporarily, pending consideration") counts under the criterion. A mere briefing order does not.
- **Same panel.** The same panel (Bennett, Sanchez, Bumatay) may be assigned as a related case. Two of its members have already signalled denial on the merits. This affects the content of a ruling, not its timing.
