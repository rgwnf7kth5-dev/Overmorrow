# ecb-candidate-31
- **Claim:** By 23:59 CET on Saturday 31 October 2026, a euro-area government officially nominates a named candidate for President of the European Central Bank, or the Eurogroup President first announces a deadline for candidacies for the post.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 CET on 30 Oct and 23:59:59 CET on 31 Oct 2026, any of the following happens.
  - (a) The government of a euro-area member state officially and publicly states that it has nominated, put forward, submitted or formally proposed a named person as its candidate for President of the ECB to succeed Christine Lagarde. This can come from the head of government, the finance minister, a government spokesperson or an official government or ministry channel; a decision of a cabinet meeting held in the window counts.
  - (b) The Eurogroup President or the Council of the EU publishes a statement naming at least one candidate for ECB President put forward by a member state.
  - (c) The Eurogroup President announces, for the first time, a deadline for candidacies for the post of ECB President.

  NO otherwise. The following do not count: statements of mere support or backing without nominating language; reports citing unnamed sources; candidates' own declarations; nominations for other ECB Executive Board seats (Schnabel, Lane); and any announcement made before 30 Oct, including the 29 Oct statements.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-30T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-10-31T23:59:59+01:00 (Europe/Brussels)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-29T18:30:00Z

## Schedule
- Unscheduled. The procedure opened on 29 Oct. ECB press release, 29 Oct 11:00 CET: Lagarde "today informed the Governing Council and the President of the European Council António Costa that she will step down from her position on 7 February 2027. President Lagarde's successor will be appointed by the European Council following the nomination process outlined in Article 283 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union." — https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2026/html/ecb.pr261029~abbb6dc64b.en.html (published 2026-10-29 11:00 CET, read 2026-10-29T18:03Z).
- Costa on X (via ČTK): "Aby bylo zajištěno hladké předání vedení ECB, bude dnes zahájen proces jmenování jejího nástupce. Ten bude probíhat bez průtahů s cílem, aby Evropská rada dospěla k rozhodnutí včas." — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/prezidentka-ecb-lagardeova-7-unora-predcasne-odstoupi-z-funkce/2894542 (published 2026-10-29 11:41, read 2026-10-29T18:04Z).
- The Council published two statements on 29 Oct. One is a "Press statement by Eurogroup President Kyriakos Pierrakakis following the announcement…" (11:10 CET); the other is a "Statement by President António Costa on the forthcoming departure…" (11:00 CET). Both are in the Council press RSS, https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/rss/pressreleases.ashx (read 2026-10-29T18:06Z). The RSS summary of Pierrakakis's statement says only that he "commented on the announcement". **The statement page itself could not be opened** (consilium.europa.eu browser check). Whether it already sets a deadline for candidacies is unknown, and the morning desk must check it. If it does, route (c) is closed and only (a) and (b) remain.
- Next Eurogroup: 9 November 2026 (MF/Milano Finanza 9 Oct: "I ministri dell'area euro torneranno a discutere sul dossier nella riunione del 9 novembre"), outside the window.

## Facts
- The ECB confirmed the step-down date of 7 Feb 2027; Lagarde's term was to run to 31 Oct 2027 (ECB release above; ČTK above: "Osmiletý mandát její šéfky měl vypršet v říjnu 2027").
- Second outlet: To Vima (Athens), 29 Oct 12:56: "Christine Lagarde will leave the European Central Bank on February 7, 2027 … Two former national central bank chiefs are viewed as the main contenders for the position: Klaas Knot of the Netherlands and Pablo Hernández de Cos of Spain." — https://www.tovima.com/world/lagarde-to-exit-ecb-as-europe-faces-economic-headwinds/ (published 2026-10-29 10:56 EET, read 2026-10-29T18:10Z). Newsquawk, 29 Oct 10:20 UTC: "ECB President Lagarde to step down on 7 February 2027, the ECB confirms." — https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/ecb-president-lagarde-to-step-down-on-7-february-2027 (read 2026-10-29T18:11Z).
- Voting rule (ČTK above): the decision is taken by the 21 euro-area states; the head of the bank needs at least 16 states representing at least 65% of the euro-area population. Agence Europe describes the same reinforced qualified majority (below).
- Netherlands: the government "oficializa su apoyo a Knot" (5 Aug 2026, via Bloomberg). A Dutch Finance Ministry spokesperson stressed his "alta cualificación" without ruling out another post — Moncloa.com https://www.moncloa.com/2026/08/06/sucesion-del-bce-knot-paises-bajos-3411504/ (published 2026-08-06 12:13 CEST, read 2026-10-29T18:16Z). Whether this was a formal nomination is unclear; no call for candidates existed then.
- Spain: Hernández de Cos has the backing of Sánchez and of opposition leader Feijóo. Pierrakakis reportedly sees de Cos and Knot as the only two candidates. Merca2 expects "la decisión llega en diciembre", at the December European Council — https://www.merca2.es/2026/10/19/hernandez-de-cos-bce-presidencia-2476398/ (published 2026-10-19 08:37 CEST, read 2026-10-29T18:16Z). The prime ministers of both countries "used the sidelines of the United Nations General Assembly in New York to endorse their candidates" — Bloomberg via The Edge Malaysia https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/820155 (published 2026-10-01 16:20 MYT, read 2026-10-29T18:20Z).
- Spain holds a general election on 29 Nov (desk dossier data/dossiers/2026-11-01/es-puente-out-31.signals.md). Milano Finanza notes de Cos could be "penalizzato dalla decisione del governo Sanchez di andare a elezioni a novembre".
- Germany: per MF, Merz was to meet Knot and de Cos. The chances that Germany puts forward Nagel are "in calo", and Berlin is focused on keeping Schnabel's board seat — https://www.milanofinanza.it/news/parte-il-riassetto-dei-vertici-bce-2720133 (published 2026-10-09 00:14 CEST, read 2026-10-29T18:16Z).
- Precedent, same Eurogroup President, Schnabel seat:
  - Lagarde's letter asking to launch the procedure reached Costa about 1 Oct (The Edge, above).
  - On 8 Oct the Eurogroup "sets October 28th deadline for ECB nominations" — https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/eurogroup-sets-october-28th-deadline-for-ecb-nominations-and-expects-to-decide-on-a-successor-to-ecbs-schnabel-as-soon-as-next-month (published 2026-10-08 14:53 UTC, read 2026-10-29T18:20Z).
  - Pierrakakis: "Wir eröffnen ein Auswahlverfahren für eine einzelne Position mit einem bestimmten Zeitrahmen" — cash.ch https://www.cash.ch/news/top-news/ezb-nominierungsfrist-fur-schnabel-nachfolge-steht-lagardes-plane-weiter-offen-975350 (published 2026-10-09 08:07, read 2026-10-29T18:16Z).
  - So the deadline was set at a scheduled Eurogroup meeting about a week after the request, not by written procedure.
- Precedent, Vice-President seat: on 9 Jan 2026 Pierrakakis "announced the names of the six candidates" (Centeno, Kazāks, Müller, Rehn, Šadžius, Vujčić) on the deadline; the Eurogroup discussed them on 19 Jan — Agence Europe https://agenceurope.eu/en/bulletin/article/13783/15/six-candidates-vie-for-vice-presidency-of-frankfurt-monetary-institute (published 2026-01-10, read 2026-10-29T18:15Z).
- 2019 precedent for a board seat: at the 9 Oct 2019 Eurogroup the President "asked euro area members to put forward candidates", and by the deadline Germany alone had named Schnabel (statement of 25 Oct 2019) — https://www.pubaffairsbruxelles.eu/?p=12568 (read 2026-10-29T18:20Z). German media had reported the nomination a day early: ING, 22 Oct 2019, https://think.ing.com/snaps/ecb-the-good-german/.
- No government nomination for the presidency, and no Eurogroup deadline for the presidency, was found in news up to 29 Oct ~19:00 CET (ČTK, To Vima, Newsquawk; the Council RSS shows only the two 29 Oct statements).

## Base rate
- Class `unscheduled-announcement-within-one-day` (pipeline/reference-classes.json): own record 1 yes / 17 no. Loose fit: this is a two-day window with three routes, right after the procedure opened.

## Schedule slip
- `none`: no scheduled event. Signs: the next Eurogroup is 9 Nov (outside the window). The Dutch cabinet normally meets on Fridays (30 Oct). Spain is in an election campaign.

## Risks
- If Pierrakakis's 29 Oct statement already set a deadline, route (c) is closed.
- Leaders may prefer a package deal (president, Schnabel seat, Lane) at the December European Council. Governments may then wait for the formal call before nominating, as happened for the Schnabel seat.
- Ambiguity: a minister may say "we support Knot/de Cos" without nominating language. By the criterion that is NO, so the resolver must read the wording.
- An over-the-weekend gap: Saturday announcements are rare.
