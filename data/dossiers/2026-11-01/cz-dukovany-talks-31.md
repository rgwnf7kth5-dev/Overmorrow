# cz-dukovany-talks-31
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Prague time on Saturday 31 October 2026, KHNP and the Daewoo E&C-led construction consortium (with Doosan Enerbility) resume talks on the Dukovany II construction contract, or KHNP withdraws or suspends its 29 October decision to end those talks.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 CET on 30 Oct 2026 and 23:59:59 CET on 31 Oct 2026, KHNP, the consortium, Daewoo E&C, Doosan Enerbility, EDU II, ČEZ, the Czech Ministry of Industry and Trade or the Korean Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy publicly states (or a major Czech or Korean outlet reports, citing one of them) that (a) KHNP and the consortium have resumed or agreed to resume formal negotiations on the construction-works contract, or (b) KHNP has withdrawn, suspended or put on hold its decision to end the talks, or has halted the termination of its cooperation agreement with the consortium.
