# fr-budget-1
- **Claim:** The French National Assembly rejects Part 1 (revenues) of the 2027 finance bill in a vote held by 23:59 Paris time on 5 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 00:00 Paris time on 4 Nov and 23:59 Paris time on 5 Nov 2026 the Assemblée nationale votes on the whole of the first part (revenues) of the PLF 2027 and rejects it, in a solemn vote or any other vote on the whole part, as recorded by the Assemblée's records or LCP, AFP, Le Monde or Public Sénat. A vote that ends after 23:59 on 5 Nov does not count. NO if no such vote is held in that window (including a vote set for 6 Nov or later, or use of Article 49.3 instead), or if the part is adopted.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-05T23:59:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-03T18:15Z. Read times are approximate, within the same quarter of an hour.
