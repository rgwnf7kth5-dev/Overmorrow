# fr-strike-2
- **Claim:** The French Interior Ministry's nationwide count of demonstrators for the 5 November 2026 joint-union day of strikes and marches reaches at least 350,000, in a figure given by 23:59 Paris time on 5 November.
- **Criterion:** YES if the nationwide figure for demonstrators on 5 Nov 2026 given by the French Interior Ministry (or the Interior Minister) by 23:59 Paris time on 5 Nov is 350,000 or more, as reported by AFP, Le Monde, franceinfo, BFMTV, Le Parisien or Libération. Union and CGT counts do not count. If the ministry gives more than one nationwide figure by 23:59, the last one counts. NO if that figure is below 350,000, including if the marches do not take place.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-05T23:59:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-03T18:15Z. Read times are approximate, within the same quarter of an hour.
