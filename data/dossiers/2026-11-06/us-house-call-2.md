# us-house-call-2
- **Claim:** AP declares between 00:00 ET on 4 November and 23:59 ET on 5 November 2026 that one party has won control of the US House of Representatives (218 seats).
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 ET on 4 Nov and 23:59 ET on 5 Nov 2026, the Associated Press declares that the Democratic or the Republican Party has won a majority in the House in the elections of 3 Nov 2026, or, if AP makes no explicit declaration, if AP race calls bring one party to at least 218 of the 435 seats in that time. AP's dated report decides; a call made in the window counts even if it is read later. Calls by other outlets do not count. NO if neither happens in the window, including if the election is not held. A declaration before 00:00 ET on 4 Nov falls outside the window; the morning run rewrites the claim in that case.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-05T23:59:00-05:00 (05:59 CET on 6 Nov).
- **Written at:** 2026-11-03T18:15Z, before the first polls closed. No result is known. The morning run updates this.

## Schedule
- First polls close 18:00 ET on 3 Nov (parts of IN, KY); last at 01:00 ET on 4 Nov (Aleutians), so the window runs about 47 hours past the last closing — https://political.org/2026/10/27/2026-midterms-when-polls-close-on-election-night-and-when-results-are-expected/ (published 2026-10-27, read by sweep 2026-11-01 ~17:50Z, carried from us-house-call-1) "a final Aleutian Islands site at 1 a.m."
- AP has no set time. It declares the House at 218 winners and counts seat by seat, with no national model — https://www.wbur.org/news/2024/10/16/how-ap-calls-election-winners-races (published 2024-10-16, read 2026-11-03T18:06Z). A seat where both finalists are of one party joins the tally early (same page).

## Facts
- **AP's rule for a race:** it declares only when "there is no scenario that would allow the trailing candidates to close the gap" — AP via https://www.clickorlando.com/news/politics/2022/11/13/control-of-congress-why-the-ap-hasnt-called-the-house/ (published 2022-11-13, read by sweep 2026-11-01). It may decline a call under 0.5 points — WBUR, above.
- **Margin:** cross-aggregator generic-ballot average on 30 Oct D+8.6 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections (read 2026-11-01T18:05Z) "Democrats +8.6%". RCP's final 2018 lead was D+7.2 — https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/article/the-2026-midterms-are-next-week-which-party-has-a-better-chance-of-winning-heres-what-the-latest-polls-say-155319308.html (published 2026-10-31 21:35Z, read by sweep 2026-11-01).
- **Seats needed:** chamber before the vote R 218, D 214, 1 independent; Democrats need net +3 on their 2024 result; redistricting moved about 6 net seats toward Republicans on paper (own arithmetic in us-house-dem-1) — Wikipedia, above.
- **Slow counts:** California accepts ballots postmarked by 3 Nov through 10 Nov (work/agenda-world.json, sweep 2 Nov, page not re-opened). "6–8 California House races may not be called for 10–14 days" — https://uspollingdata.com/news/2026-election-night-guide/ (read 2026-11-02T18:24Z; weak source, dated April 2026). AP did not call Alaska's House race in 2024 until 20 Nov (NPR 19 Oct, via work/agenda-world.json, not re-read).
- **California ballots dispute:** the Supreme Court on 2 Nov rejected the Justice Department's request about 20,000 ballots, according to one analysis column — https://www.atalayar.com/en/articulo/gustavo-aristegui/gustavo-aristegui-geopolitical-analysis-of-3-november/20261103180000230247.html (published 2026-11-03 18:00 +01:00, read 2026-11-03T18:07Z). Not confirmed from a second source here.
- **Early vote:** 53,688,976 by 1 Nov against 45,920,446 in 2022 — https://election.lab.ufl.edu/data-downloads/earlyvote/2026/US.csv (downloaded by sweep 2026-11-01 17:43Z).

## Risks
- The call may come before 00:00 ET on 4 Nov, outside this window; the morning rewrites it.
- A narrow chamber makes the 218th seat a slow-count seat in California or Washington, as in 2022.
- AP needs 218 individual calls; a 23:59 ET cut-off applies to its time stamp.
- Redistricting makes the seat count less predictable from the national lead.
