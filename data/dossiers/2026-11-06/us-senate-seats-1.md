# us-senate-seats-1
- **Claim:** By 23:59 ET on 5 November 2026, AP race calls give Democrats, with independents who have said they will caucus with them, at least 54 of the 100 Senate seats.
- **Criterion:** YES if, counting the 34 Democratic and caucusing independent seats not up in 2026 plus the 2026 races AP has called for a Democrat, the total is at least 54 at 23:59 ET on 5 Nov 2026. An independent winner counts only with a public statement that they will caucus with the Democrats. AP's dated race calls decide (AP data as carried by NPR's results feed, field winnerDateTime); calls by other outlets do not count. NO otherwise.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-05T23:59:00-05:00.
- **Written at:** 2026-11-04T04:15Z (morning run; replaces us-senate-call-1 and us-house-call-2, whose calls are likely before the window).

A very close race may go to recount range; AP may decline to call under 0.5 pts. https://www.wbur.org/news/2024/10/16/how-ap-calls-election-winners-races

- AP rule: no race call unless the trailing candidate has no path.
