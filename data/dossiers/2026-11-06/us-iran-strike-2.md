# us-iran-strike-2
- **Claim:** US forces strike a target on Iranian territory on 5 November 2026 (Tehran time).
- **Criterion:** YES if the White House (including President Trump), the Department of Defense / Department of War or US Central Command confirms a US military strike (air, missile or drone) on a target on Iranian territory, including Iranian territorial waters and islands, carried out between 00:00 and 23:59 Tehran time on 5 Nov 2026. A confirmation published after the window counts. Iranian claims alone, unnamed officials, strikes only on Iranian proxies outside Iran, on vessels in international waters, or by Israel alone do not count. NO if no such strike took place in the window.
- **Deadline:** strike on 5 Nov, 23:59 Tehran time
- **Written at:** 2026-11-03T18:15Z. Read times as stated.
