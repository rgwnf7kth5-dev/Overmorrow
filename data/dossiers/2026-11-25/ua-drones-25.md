# ua-drones-25
- **Claim (binary):** According to the Ukrainian Air Force's first morning summary on Tuesday 24 November 2026, Russia launched at least 130 drones at Ukraine in the night of 23–24 November (from 18:00 Kyiv time on 23 November).
- **Criterion:** YES if the Ukrainian Air Force's first summary of the overnight attack published on Tue 24 Nov 2026 (Kyiv time), covering the attack counted from 18:00 Kyiv time on 23 Nov, gives a UAV total of 130 or more. Source: the Air Force Telegram channel (t.me/kpszsu) or Facebook page, or Ukrinform, Ukrainska Pravda, Kyiv Independent or Interfax-Ukraine repeating it. "Drones" means strike UAVs as the summary counts them: Shahed-type and jet-powered Shaheds, Gerbera, Parodiya and other decoys, and any jet munitions such as Banderol/Dan-T that the summary includes in its UAV total. Missiles listed separately are not counted. A summary issued late in the morning or around noon still counts as the first summary. Separate daytime reports and later corrections do not count. NO if the total is 129 or fewer, or if the Air Force reports that no drones were launched that night. If no summary for that night has been published, the claim stays pending. (Same counting rules as ua-drones-23/-24, shifted by one day, as a threshold claim.)
- **Event window:** attack 2026-11-23T18:00:00+02:00 to 2026-11-24T12:00:00+02:00 (Europe/Kyiv, EET). The summary normally comes 08:00–10:00 Kyiv; after very large attacks up to about 12:00.
- **Evidence expected:** Air Force Telegram summary "У ніч на 24 листопада (з 18:00 23 листопада) противник атакував …", repeated by Ukrinform (rubric-ato; headline form "Air defenses down X of Y Russian drones") and the Kyiv Independent. evidence_at 2026-11-24T14:00:00+02:00.
- **Deadline (resolves by):** 2026-11-26T05:00:00+01:00
