## Facts — the case
- NSS judgment 5 Afs 151/2024-56 of 31 Jul 2025 (senate chaired by Jakub Camrda): "Kasační stížnost se zamítá." Plaintiff Český rozhlas, defendant the Appeals Finance Directorate — https://vyhledavac.nssoud.cz/DokumentOriginal/Index/743926 (read 2026-11-22T17:58Z).
- Subject: VAT for the monthly periods of 2014. ČRo's supplementary returns of 29 Jul 2015 excluded licence-fee income from exempt supplies, claiming a difference of −CZK 93,492,385; the CJEU (C-11/15, 22 Jun 2016, on the NSS's reference) held fee-funded public broadcasting is not a supply "for consideration" — same judgment, paras [1]–[2].
- After two rounds in the courts (Municipal Court 9 Af 12/2017; NSS 4 Afs 130/2021 of 26 Nov 2021), the Directorate on 16 Dec 2022 set monthly differences (from −CZK 758,924 to +CZK 1,682,232) and penalties of CZK 103,089 (Mar), 31,074 (Jun), 598 (Nov) and 336,446 (Dec 2014); it applied a revenue-based pro-rata coefficient (a broadcast-time key would have given ČRo only 1.5% of full deduction) — paras [5]–[6].
- The Municipal Court dismissed ČRo's action (17 Af 3/2023-117); the NSS rejected the cassation complaint, held no grounds to refer a new preliminary question to the CJEU ("Nejsou zároveň dány ani důvody pro položení předběžné otázky Soudnímu dvoru", para [49]) and upheld the penalties (paras [58]–[59]).
- ČRo's complaint is characterised by the Constitutional Court as "právo na soudní ochranu" (right to judicial protection) — programme page (above). The complaint text was not found.
- Context: the coalition is pushing to abolish licence fees for ČT and ČRo; Okamura says fees should be abolished "nejpozději do léta" (ČTK headline in related list, 22 Nov, https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/poslanci-se-budou-zabyvat-rozpoctem-a-schvalovat-dan-na-rafinerie/2904989).
