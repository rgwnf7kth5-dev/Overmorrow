# gulf-ukmto-25
- **Claim:** UKMTO or JMIC reports at least one attack, explosion, mine strike, projectile or drone hit, armed hostile approach, boarding or seizure involving a merchant vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, the Persian Gulf, the Gulf of Oman or the Arabian Sea that took place on Tuesday 24 November 2026 (00:00–23:59 Gulf time).
- **Criterion:** YES if the UK Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) or the Joint Maritime Information Center (JMIC) reports a qualifying incident involving a merchant vessel that occurred between 00:00 and 23:59 Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4) on 24 Nov 2026.
  - Qualifying incidents: an attack, explosion, mine strike, projectile or drone hit, hostile approach (armed craft firing on or attempting to board the vessel), boarding, hijacking or seizure.
  - Area: the Strait of Hormuz, the Persian (Arabian) Gulf, the Gulf of Oman or the Arabian Sea (including the waters around Socotra).
  - Accepted evidence: ukmto.org, UKMTO's X account, or a wire, gCaptain, Lloyd's List, Khaleej Times, Gulf News, Arab News, Iran International, Ship & Bunker, Al Jazeera, Newsquawk or another established news outlet quoting UKMTO or JMIC.
  - Timing: when UKMTO/JMIC give only a date and no time, an incident dated 24 Nov counts. A report published after the window (including a "time-late" report) counts if the incident occurred in the window.
  - Military action: a merchant vessel disabled, boarded or seized by US or other military forces counts only if UKMTO or JMIC itself reports that incident.
  - Not counted: suspicious approaches, hailings or sightings without weapons fire or a boarding attempt; GPS or navigation-aid interference alone; notices of an "incident" or "suspicious activity" (including "an incident involving a tanker and military forces") that state no attack, explosion, hit, boarding or seizure; incidents in the Gulf of Aden, the Bab el-Mandeb or the Red Sea; claims by Iran (including IRGC/navy statements), the Houthis or other parties that neither UKMTO nor JMIC reports.
  - NO if no such incident occurred in the window.
