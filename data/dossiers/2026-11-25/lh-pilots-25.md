# lh-pilots-25
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Frankfurt time on Tuesday 24 November 2026 the pilots' union Vereinigung Cockpit calls a strike at Lufthansa.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 20:00 on Sun 22 Nov and 23:59 on Tue 24 Nov 2026 (Europe/Berlin), Vereinigung Cockpit (VC) publicly calls its pilots out on strike at Lufthansa (Lufthansa Classic, Lufthansa Cargo or another Lufthansa group flight operation covered by the current dispute), naming the date(s) or starting time of the walkout, as shown by a VC press release (vcockpit.de) or reporting by dpa, Reuters, Handelsblatt, hessenschau or aeroTELEGRAPH. A strike that actually begins in the window also counts. A report published after 23:59 about a call made in the window counts.

## Facts
- **The ultimatum:** VC demands a "Eckpunktepapier… das sich an der vom Mediator vorgeschlagenen Systematik orientiert. Andernfalls betrachtet sich die Gewerkschaft nicht länger an die Friedenspflicht gebunden." — cash-online https://www.cash-online.de/a/lufthansa-piloten-streik-rueckt-nach-gescheiterter-mediation-naeher-730791/ (published 2026-11-18T06:35Z, read 2026-11-22T17:53:57Z).

'Offenbar will der Arbeitgeber keine Einigung mit uns', sagt VC-Präsident Andreas Pinheiro." — cash-online (above); dpa via ad-hoc-news https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/wirtschaft/lufthansa-piloten-drohen-ab-23-november-mit-neuen-streiks/70527391 (published 2026-11-17T15:30+01:00, read 2026-11-22T17:54:06Z) "Danach wären Arbeitskampfmaßnahmen möglich."

## Risks
- Lufthansa may reply by Monday with a paper VC can call a basis for talks → no strike call.
- VC may announce only that it is "prepared" for industrial action, or call a strike later in the week or in December (pre-Christmas leverage) → NO.
- Legal question: pay agreement open only from 1 Jan 2027; a strike now would rest on the pension/working-conditions dispute where the peace obligation has lapsed (VC's own statement) — Lufthansa could seek an injunction, which does not affect whether a call is made.
