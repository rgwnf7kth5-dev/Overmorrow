# ep-eia-25
- **Claim:** On Tuesday 24 November 2026 the European Parliament adopts its first-reading position on the regulation speeding up environmental assessments (300-day cap) with at least 400 votes in favour.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 20:00 on Sun 22 Nov and 23:59 on Tue 24 Nov 2026 (Europe/Paris, Strasbourg), the plenary adopts its position on procedure 2025/0391(COD) (report A10-0290/2026, rapporteur Emma Ytterell) and the decisive vote — the vote on the Commission proposal as amended (or, if that is the only final vote held, the legislative resolution) — records 400 or more votes in favour, as shown by Parliament's press release, the minutes / roll-call results on europarl.europa.eu (doceo PV, RCV) or the Legislative Observatory. NO if fewer than 400 vote in favour, if the text is rejected, or if the vote is postponed beyond 24 Nov or referred back to committee before a final vote. Amendment votes and the decision to open negotiations do not count. A report published after 23:59 about a vote held in the window counts.

## Facts
- **Committee vote, 4 Nov: 36 in favour, 35 against, 11 abstentions** — "On Wednesday, the Environment, Climate and Food Safety Committee adopted its position on the proposed regulation on speeding up environmental assessments by 36 votes in favour and 35 against, with 11 abstentions." — Insight EU Monitoring (reproducing the EP press release) https://ieu-monitoring.com/editorial/eu-parliament-committee-backs-300-day-cap-on-environmental-assessments/1253979 (published 2026-11-05, read 2026-11-22T17:56:56Z); same figures Agence Europe https://agenceurope.eu/en/bulletin/article/13975/13/meps-call-for-300-day-cap-to-speed-up-project-permits (published 2026-11-04, read 2026-11-22T17:56:57Z) "narrowly approved… by 36 votes to 35, with 11 abstentions".
- **Content of the committee text:** 300-day overall maximum for screening plus full assessment (excluding the developer's report time), extendable by 60 days in exceptional cases; screening in 45 days; at least 30 days of public comment; strategic-project toolbox kept but tacit approval removed — same two sources.
- **The split is over protected species, not the 300-day cap:** S&D "voted against the report… cannot accept changes that undermine the protection of wildlife"; "the rapporteur ultimately chose to support an agreement with the right wing"; "the report adopted by a margin of just one vote" — S&D https://www.socialistsanddemocrats.eu/newsroom/environmental-assessments-simplification-must-never-mean-deregulation (published 2026-11-04T12:00Z, read 2026-11-22T17:56:59Z). An S&D–Greens–Left alternative compromise was rejected in committee 48 to 34; The Left called the text an "extremely dangerous environmental setback" — Agence Europe https://agenceurope.eu/en/bulletin/article/13976/12/european-parliament-groups-sd-and-the-left-deplore-environmental-assessments-vote-outcome (published 2026-11-05, read 2026-11-22T17:56:58Z).
- **Plenary compromise tabled:** "The EPP, Renew Europe and S&D groups have tabled a set of joint amendments that will be voted on… in order to facilitate a compromise"; harm to nests/eggs tolerated only if it causes no decline in bird populations, with cumulative-impact assessment and monitoring; Christophe Clergeau (S&D) said on 18 Nov "a compromise was on the table" — Agence Europe 19 Nov (above).
- **Comparable plenary vote:** Omnibus I negotiating position (sustainability reporting/due diligence), 13 Nov 2025, adopted with the right, S&D against: "With 382 votes in favour, 249 against, and with 13 abstentions" — EP press release https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20251106IPR31296/sustainability-reporting-and-due-diligence-meps-back-simplification-changes (published 2025-11-13T12:22, read 2026-11-22T17:56:41Z). A text backed by EPP, S&D and Renew together usually draws well above 400 (no sourced count gathered tonight).
- Arithmetic: 720 MEPs; recent plenary turnout typically 640–670 (Omnibus I: 644 voting). 400 needs roughly 61% of those voting.
