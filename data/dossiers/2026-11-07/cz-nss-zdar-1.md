# cz-nss-zdar-1
- **Claim:** By 23:59 on 6 November 2026, the Supreme Administrative Court rejects or dismisses Radek Černý's petition against the Senate election in the Žďár nad Sázavou district.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 00:00 on 5 Nov and 23:59 Prague time on 6 Nov 2026 the Supreme Administrative Court (NSS) issues a decision that rejects, dismisses or otherwise leaves unsuccessful the petition of Radek Černý (ODS) on the Senate election in the Žďár nad Sázavou district, as shown on the NSS official board or website or reported by ČTK. NO if the court upholds the petition or issues no decision on it in the window.
- **Deadline / event window:** 2026-11-05T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-06T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Prague)
- **Written at:** 2026-11-04T18:20:00Z

## Schedule
- No hearing or decision date found. The electoral panel, chaired by Vojtěch Šimíček, must decide by 19 Nov — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/2895136 (published 2026-10-30 16:54, read 2026-11-04T17:54Z) "Volební senát musí o stížnostech rozhodnout do 19. listopadu"
- So the decision can fall on 5-6 Nov or in the following two weeks; the date of the decision is the open point.

## Facts
- As of 4 Nov the court had decided nine of 16 filings, upholding none; Černý's filing undecided — ČT24 https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/domaci/soudy-resi-volebni-stiznosti-v-karvine-se-dostalo-spd-na-moldave-se-bude-volit-znovu-379283 (published 2026-11-04 17:20, read 2026-11-04T17:51Z) "NSS zatím nerozhodl o podání neúspěšného kandidáta Radka Černého (ODS)"
- Klement (KDU-ČSL) took 11,373 first-round votes, Černý 11,372; Klement won the runoff of 16-17 Oct; Černý says he does not aim to overturn the result but to remove doubts — Seznam Zprávy/ČTK https://www.seznamzpravy.cz/clanek/318225 (published 2026-10-19 13:54, read 2026-11-04T17:54Z) "Do druhého kola ho dostalo 11 373 hlasů, třetí Černý měl 11 372 hlasů."
- After the 2020 elections in the same districts, none of seven filings succeeded — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/2895136 "soud žádnému ze sedmi podnětů nevyhověl"
- NSS annulled Senate results in the past: Most (read in search listing only, https://www.e15.cz/domaci/spravni-soud-senatni-volby-v-moste-jsou-neplatne-1325210) and Praha 11 (https://www.novinky.cz/clanek/domaci-v-praze-11-se-budou-opakovat-senatni-volby-313685); neither page opened.
- The new Senate meets on 19 Nov, the same day as the court's limit (board check, ČTK 18 Oct).

## Base rate
- ČTK: 0 of 7 upheld after 2020; 2026 so far 0 of 9. A close margin may need a recount of ballots, which is unusual and not in these counts.

## Schedule slip
- cz-court-ruling: 0.12 (scripts/refdata/slip.mjs), but that is for a set date; here no date is set, so timing risk is larger than the class suggests.

## Risks
- Main NO path: the court decides after 6 Nov; it has until 19 Nov and a one-vote case may be taken last, perhaps with a hearing or evidence from ballots.
- Moot risk: a decision on the evening of 4 Nov or published that day; recheck on 5 Nov.
- NO path: court orders a fresh vote or finds a defect (a one-vote margin makes any counting error matter).
- Trap: the date of the decision counts, not the date of publication; ČTK normally reports both.
