# us-iran-strike-3
- **Claim:** US forces strike a target on Iranian territory on 5 or 6 November 2026 (Tehran time).
- **Criterion:** YES if the White House (including President Trump), the Department of Defense / Department of War or US Central Command confirms a US military strike (air, missile or drone) on a target on Iranian territory, including Iranian territorial waters and islands, carried out between 00:00 on 5 Nov and 23:59 on 6 Nov 2026 Tehran time. A confirmation published after the window counts. Iranian claims alone, unnamed officials, strikes only on Iranian proxies outside Iran, on vessels in international waters, or by Israel alone do not count. NO if no such strike took place in the window.
- **Deadline:** strike on 5 or 6 Nov, 23:59 Tehran time
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-04T18:05Z. Read times as stated.

## Schedule
- No scheduled event. Military plans are reported from unnamed officials only; no date is confirmed.

## Risks
- A strike could come late on 4 Nov and so fall before the window; a further one would still be needed.
- Anonymous sourcing alone gives NO even if a strike occurred.
- A "defensive" hit on a launcher on Iranian soil counts; one on a boat in international waters does not, and location may be disputed.
- 20:30 UTC on 4 Nov is 00:00 Tehran on 5 Nov; 20:29 UTC on 6 Nov closes the window.
