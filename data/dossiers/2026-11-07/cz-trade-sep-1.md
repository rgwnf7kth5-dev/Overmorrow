## Consensus
- None found. Calendars were unreadable; KB's readable text gives no trade figure. Sweep flagged the same gap.
- Proxy CZK +24.1 bn, own arithmetic, not a forecast by anyone: September 2025 first figure 29.7 less August's y/y deterioration 5.6. Jan–Aug average deterioration is 5.35 bn a month (42.8/8), which gives about the same.

## Facts
- August 2026: deficit CZK 5.3 bn, CZK 5.6 bn worse y/y; exports +8.5% to 371.9 bn, imports +10.2% to 377.2 bn — https://csu.gov.cz/rychle-informace/zahranicni-obchod-se-zbozim-srpen-2026 (read 2026-11-04T17:49Z) "deficitem 5,3 mld. Kč".
- Oil and gas deficit deepened by 8.6 bn, coke and refined products by 2.3 bn, motor-vehicle surplus shrank by 3.7 bn — https://csu.gov.cz/rychle-informace/zahranicni-obchod-se-zbozim-srpen-2026.
- Jan–Aug 2026 surplus CZK 83.7 bn, down 42.8 bn y/y — https://csu.gov.cz/rychle-informace/zahranicni-obchod-se-zbozim-srpen-2026.
- July also in deficit — https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/stavebni-vyroba-v-cesku-v-srpnu-mezirocne-klesla-oznamil-csu/2883884 (published 2026-10-07, read 2026-11-04T17:51Z) "byl v srpnu stejně jako v červenci ve schodku".
