- **Claim:** By 23:59 Kyiv time on 6 November 2026, the White House, Lubinets or Lantratova announces a new specific humanitarian measure agreed between Russia and Ukraine.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 00:00 Kyiv time on 5 Nov and 23:59 Kyiv time on 6 Nov 2026 the White House (including Melania Trump or Donald Trump), Ukrainian ombudsman Dmytro Lubinets or Russian commissioner Yana Lantratova states on the record that Russia and Ukraine have agreed a new specific measure: a new local ceasefire, a new evacuation or aid mission for a named place, or the return of a stated number of children, civilians or prisoners not announced before the window.
