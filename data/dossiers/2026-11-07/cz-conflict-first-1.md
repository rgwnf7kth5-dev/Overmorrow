## Facts
Coalition prevailed to add the bill to the sitting's programme. It would let firms of cabinet members draw subsidies if the member does not directly decide on them, open public contracts to such firms, and cut the time for recovering wrongly paid SZIF subsidies from ten years to four, also for money already paid; that would touch the dispute over billions of crowns paid to Agrofert in 2017-2021. — https://www.novinky.cz/clanek/domaci-kritizovana-novela-o-stretu-zajmu-jde-do-prvniho-cteni-40606816 (published 2026-11-04T18:59Z, read 2026-11-05T04:10Z)
Transparency International urged the proposers to withdraw it, calling it weakened control and a de facto legislative amnesty. The bill was tabled in early June by a group led by ANO vice-chair Radek Vondráček; the government took a neutral stand. Babiš denies any conflict of interest; Schillerová defends the bill. — same source
ANO, SPD and Motoristé hold a Chamber majority (108 of 200). The opposition has used long debates this sitting: a four-hour block of agenda proposals on 3 Nov. A first reading can be cut only by a procedural vote; an opposition club can ask for a pause; the stated programme also holds third readings on Friday morning (see cz-chamber-any-1.md).
