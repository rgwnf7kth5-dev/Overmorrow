# us-iran-ship-2
- **Claim:** UKMTO reports at least one new attack or incident involving a merchant vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, the Gulf of Oman or the Persian Gulf that occurs on Friday 6 November 2026 (Gulf time).
- **Criterion:** YES if the UK Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) reports an attack, explosion, mine strike, projectile hit, boarding or seizure involving a merchant vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, the Gulf of Oman or the Persian Gulf that occurred between 00:00 and 23:59 Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4) on 6 Nov 2026, as shown on ukmto.org or by a wire, gCaptain or Lloyd's List quoting UKMTO. A UKMTO report published after the window counts if the incident occurred in it. UKMTO reports of suspicious approaches, hailings, sightings or GPS interference alone do not count, nor do claims by Iran or other parties that UKMTO does not report. NO if no such incident occurred on that day.
- **Deadline:** incident on 6 Nov, 23:59 Gulf time (UTC+4)
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-04T18:05Z. Read times as stated.
