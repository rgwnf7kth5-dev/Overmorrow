# us-ms-senate-1
- **Claim:** AP declares Democrat Scott Colom the winner of the US Senate race in Mississippi between 00:00 ET on 5 November and 23:59 ET on 6 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 ET on 5 Nov and 23:59 ET on 6 Nov 2026, the Associated Press declares Scott Colom the winner of the 3 Nov 2026 US Senate election in Mississippi. AP's dated report decides; a call made in the window counts even if it is read later. Calls by other outlets, concessions and claims of victory do not count. NO if AP makes no such declaration in the window, including if AP declares Cindy Hyde-Smith the winner. A declaration before 00:00 ET on 5 Nov falls outside the window; the morning run rewrites the claim in that case.

- AP rule: "will declare a winner only when it's determined there is no scenario that would allow a trailing candidate to close the gap"; no automatic recounts and none on candidate request, only by court order; AP may call a race if the lead is too large for a recount or legal challenge to change the result — https://www.lanacion.com.ar/usa/ap-decision-notes-what-to-expect-in-mississippi-on-election-day-nid30102026/ (AP copy, published 2026-10-30T18:22Z, read 2026-11-04T17:53Z).
- Post-certification, a candidate can file a challenge in court (WAPT 74335127).
- Plurality wins, no runoff — https://www.kbtx.com/2026/11/04/colom-hyde-smith-separated-by-narrow-margin-mississippi-senate-race/ (modified 2026-11-04T16:18Z, read 2026-11-04T17:49Z).
- Both sides confident. Colom: "We have an army of lawyers" (WLBT). Hyde-Smith's manager: "thousands of outstanding ballots, including affidavit and absentee ballots" (WAPT 74333074).
- Late Election Day returns favoured Colom: Hinds County 61,793 to 14,702 — https://msindy.org/p/colom-pulls-ahead-of-hyde-smith-by (published 2026-11-04T06:22Z, read 2026-11-04T17:52Z).
