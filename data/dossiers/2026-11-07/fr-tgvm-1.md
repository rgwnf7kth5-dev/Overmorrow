# fr-tgvm-1
Claim: The SNCF's next-generation TGV M makes its inaugural journey from Paris Gare de Lyon to Marseille Saint-Charles on 6 November 2026.
Criterion: YES if a TGV M (TGV Inoui of the new generation, Alstom Avelia Horizon) carries its announced inaugural journey from Paris Gare de Lyon and arrives at Marseille Saint-Charles between 00:00 and 23:59 Paris time on 6 Nov 2026, according to SNCF or to AFP, Le Monde, Le Figaro, franceinfo or La Provence. A journey with press and guests counts; regular ticket sale is not required. NO if the journey does not reach Marseille in that time, including if it is cancelled or postponed.
Deadline: 2026-11-06T23:59:00+01:00
Written at (UTC): 2026-11-04T18:05:00Z

## Schedule
Announced by SNCF on 14 Oct (agency report); SNCF newsroom page not opened.
