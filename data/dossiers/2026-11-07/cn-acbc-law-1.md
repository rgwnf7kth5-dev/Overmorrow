# cn-acbc-law-1
Claim: By 23:59 Beijing time on 6 Nov 2026, the NPC Standing Committee adopts the Anti-Cross-Border Corruption Law.
Criterion: YES if Xinhua or the NPC website (npc.gov.cn) reports that the 14th NPC Standing Committee, at its 25th session, voted to adopt the Anti-Cross-Border Corruption Law [反跨境腐败法] between 5 Nov 00:00 and 6 Nov 23:59 Beijing time. A change to the law's name does not matter. NO if the bill is held over for further review or no vote takes place in that window.
