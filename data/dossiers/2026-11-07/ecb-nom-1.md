# ecb-nom-1

Criterion: YES if, between 00:00 on 5 Nov and 23:59 Brussels time on 6 Nov 2026, the Spanish government (prime minister, economy minister, a ministry or an on-record spokesperson) or the Eurogroup (its president or an on-record spokesperson) states that Spain has submitted or is submitting Pablo Hernández de Cos as its formal nominee, as shown on an official site or reported by EFE, El País, Reuters, Bloomberg or AFP. Earlier endorsements, such as Sánchez's of 23 September, and unattributed sourcing do not count. NO if no such statement is made in that time, including if Spain nominates someone else.
