# za-joburg-largest-1
Claim: By 23:59 SAST on 6 Nov 2026, the DA is ahead of every other party in the IEC's results for the City of Johannesburg in the 4 Nov municipal election.
Criterion: YES if the IEC's headline percentages for the City of Johannesburg (results.elections.org.za), as shown at 23:59 SAST on 6 Nov 2026 with at least 90% of the city's voting districts captured, put the DA above every other party. If the IEC has declared the final result by then, that result decides. If 90% is not reached by then or the deadline reading cannot be retrieved, the IEC's later declared result for the city decides, since the votes were cast on 4 Nov. NO if another party is first or the DA is level with it. Many parties stood; only the top position counts.

## Risks
- Turnout: MarkData modelled 48%; DA lead depends on it, and Ipsos (registered voters) shows the opposite order.
- Fragmentation: MK, EFF and ActionSA above 10 in Johannesburg; the ANC's share is hard to poll in townships.
- Polls are mid-August to mid-September for the two with figures; the October SRF figures were not read.
- Ballot basis: metros have ward and PR ballots, and media may quote different totals. Criterion fixes the IEC's headline figure, but a very close race could differ by basis.
- Count pace: late capture or objections could leave part of the count open at the deadline; the declared result then decides.
