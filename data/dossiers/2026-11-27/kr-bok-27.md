# kr-bok-27
- **Claim:** At its meeting on Thursday 26 November 2026 the Bank of Korea Monetary Policy Board raises the base rate by 25 basis points to 3.25%, with at least one member dissenting in favour of keeping it at 3.00%.
- **Criterion:** YES if the Bank of Korea announces on 26 Nov 2026 (Asia/Seoul) that the Monetary Policy Board has set the Base Rate at 3.25% (a 25bp increase from 3.00%) AND at least one board member dissented in favour of holding the rate at 3.00%, as stated by the BoK (decision statement or the governor at the post-meeting press conference, which normally names dissenters) or reported that day by Yonhap, Reuters or Bloomberg.
