# kr-bok-27
- **Claim:** At its meeting on Thursday 26 November 2026 the Bank of Korea Monetary Policy Board raises the base rate by 25 basis points to 3.25%, with at least one member dissenting in favour of keeping it at 3.00%.
- **Criterion:** YES if the Bank of Korea announces on 26 Nov 2026 (Asia/Seoul) that the Monetary Policy Board has set the Base Rate at 3.25% (a 25bp increase from 3.00%) AND at least one board member dissented in favour of holding the rate at 3.00%, as stated by the BoK (decision statement or the governor at the post-meeting press conference, which normally names dissenters) or reported that day by Yonhap, Reuters or Bloomberg. NO if the rate is set at 3.25% unanimously, if the board leaves the rate at 3.00% or sets any other rate, or if no rate decision is taken on 26 Nov 2026. If the dissent is first disclosed later (e.g. in the minutes on about 15 Dec), that disclosure decides; a member dissenting for a larger hike does not count.
