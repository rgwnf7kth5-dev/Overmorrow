# es-debate-27
- **Claim (interval):** Average audience of Spain's four-way TV election debate (Sánchez, Feijóo, Abascal, Colau) on Thursday 26 November 2026, all channels carrying it combined, in millions of viewers.
- **Criterion:** The figure is the combined average audience (audiencia media, sum of all channels that carried the debate) for the debate of 26 November 2026 as first published by Barlovento Comunicación with Kantar Media data, normally the next morning (27 Nov). If Barlovento gives no combined figure, the sum of the per-channel Kantar average audiences of the debate itself (not pre- or post-debate programmes) reported by FormulaTV or elDiario.es/Vertele the same day decides, national and regional channels included. If the debate is not held on 26 November 2026, the claim is void (no figure). A debate held with an empty lectern (a candidate absent) still counts.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-26T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-26T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Madrid); Antena 3 special from 21:00 CET, debate start not yet published (19 Nov face-to-face began 22:00).
- **Evidence expected:** Barlovento Comunicación (X/@blvcom, report) and FormulaTV/Vertele audience pages on Friday 27 Nov from about 09:00 CET.
- **Deadline (resolves by):** 2026-11-28T05:00:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-24T19:30:00+01:00

## Reference value and scale (for the number)
- **There is no analyst consensus for TV audiences.** Reference value used: **8.5 million**, our own: the 19 Nov 2026 face-to-face reached 8.30m on six national channels (plus an unsummed amount on La 2, Canal 24 Horas and about nine regional channels), and multi-channel four- and five-way debates of 2016–2019 drew 8.6–10.5m. The four-way is the last debate before the vote and the first with four leaders; it may be carried on fewer channels (not yet announced). 8.5m is close to the 19 Nov six-channel sum, below its probable all-channel total.
- **Scale:** no series in pipeline/reference-data/series.json (`node scripts/refdata/series.mjs scale es-debate-audience` → basis "published or pooled", pooled factor 1.274). Change between two consecutive debates of one campaign with similar carriage: 1993 9.6 → 10.5 (+0.93m), 2008 13.04 → 11.95 (−1.09m); RMS 1.01m (n = 2). × pooled factor 1.274 = 1.29 → **scale 1.3m** (basis `pooled`).
- `node scripts/refdata/models.mjs quantiles 8.5 1.3 1.6` → q10 5.83, q50 8.50, q90 11.17 (million).

## Risks
- Fewer channels carrying the signal (e.g. Antena 3/laSexta only, as in 2023) → figure could fall to 5–6m.
- Barlovento reporting only national channels, or a combined figure including or excluding the 24h channel; the criterion uses Barlovento's first published combined figure.
- A candidate's absence (empty lectern), scheduling against football or other events, late start.
- Campaign fatigue after the 19 Nov and 23 Nov debates (the 23 Nov spokespeople's debate drew 3.1m on La 1).
