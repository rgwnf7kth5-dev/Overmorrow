# uk-tube-27
- **Claim:** The RMT union's 24-hour strike by London Underground drivers goes ahead from noon on Thursday 26 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if RMT drivers on the London Underground begin the announced strike (walkout) at any time on Thursday 26 November 2026 (UK time), as reported by BBC News, TfL or the RMT, even if the strike is cut short later. NO if the RMT suspends, calls off or postpones the 26 November walkout so that no RMT drivers' strike on the Underground starts on 26 November 2026. A strike that goes ahead with fewer lines closed than TfL expects still counts as YES.

- **Evidence expected:** BBC London live/news reports on Thursday afternoon; TfL status updates; Evening Standard.

## Facts
- Acas talks on Tue 24 Nov failed; strike confirmed for noon Thursday — BBC https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cv1j6539l6d7o (published 2026-11-24 16:39, read 2026-11-24T17:54Z) "It follows last-ditch talks between Transport for London (TfL) and the union".
- TfL's expected service: normal until about 10:00 GMT Thursday; from midday disruption on all lines; no service on Circle or Piccadilly lines all day, none on Waterloo & City after midday; Friday late start, normal by about 18:00 — BBC (same page) "No service is expected on the Circle or Piccadilly lines throughout the day or on the Waterloo & City line after midday."; same wording from TfL in Evening Standard https://www.standard.co.uk/news/london/tube-strikes-rmt-union-london-underground-drivers-b1306024.html (published 2026-11-24 17:02, read 2026-11-24T17:55Z) "From midday, most Tube services are expected to run but there will be disruption on all lines."
- Further strike dates: 17 and 19 Dec, 4 and 6 Jan, 17, 23 and 25 Feb — BBC (same page).
- Dispute: TfL's voluntary compressed four-day week for drivers (36 → 35 h); Aslef accepted it, RMT opposes; RMT wants 32 h over four days — BBC explainer https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c0ljyd03n88o (published 2026-03-10, updated 2026-11-24 11:39, read 2026-11-24T17:54Z) "The union instead wants a 32-hour week over four days".
- TfL (Nick Dent) says it made a "new, workable option" and urged RMT to put it to members — Evening Standard (same page) "TfL has urged the RMT to put the new offer to its members."
- RMT: "RMT has already suspended strike action three times for talks, yet no real improvements have been forthcoming." — RMT release (above).
- Earlier waves in this dispute: March strikes called off on 18 Mar (6 days before) — https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c77e7k1x5x4o (published 2026-03-18 16:41, read 2026-11-24T17:54Z) "Tube strikes due later this month have been called off following talks"; April strikes went ahead (BBC explainer: "previously staged four days of strike action across April and June"); May strikes (19 and 21 May) suspended on Mon 18 May, the day before — https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cze2wrk08kko (published 2026-05-18 05:00, read 2026-11-24T17:54Z) "at the 11th hour the employer has shifted its position"; 16 and 18 June dates cancelled, 2 and 4 June went ahead (same pages).
- Impact of June strike: no service on Circle and Waterloo & City; major disruption on Piccadilly, Metropolitan, Central; 90% of Jubilee services ran; Tube journeys −40% on the Thursday — BBC explainer (above) "On the Thursday of June's strike, Tube journeys fell by 40%".
- RMT drivers are roughly half of Tube drivers; more than half of drivers worked during the June strike — BBC explainer "Only drivers belonging to the RMT union would walk out - that is roughly half the total number of drivers".
- RMT referendum April 2025: 1,219 against, 126 for the proposals; Aslef members backed them by 70% — BBC https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckz6qw2y3pypo (published 2026-11-16 06:17, read 2026-11-24T17:54Z) "1,219 of its drivers voted against the proposals and 126 in favour".
- Political pressure: MPs urge the mayor to intervene; mayor's office urged both sides to keep talking — same BBC 16 Nov page.
