- **Criterion:** as gulf-ukmto-26, shifted to Thursday 26 Nov. YES if UKMTO or JMIC reports a qualifying incident involving a merchant vessel that occurred between 00:00 and 23:59 Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4) on 26 Nov 2026.
  - Qualifying: attack, explosion, mine strike, projectile or drone hit, hostile approach (armed craft firing on or attempting to board), boarding, hijacking or seizure.
  - Area: Strait of Hormuz, Persian (Arabian) Gulf, Gulf of Oman, Arabian Sea (incl. waters around Socotra).
  - Evidence: ukmto.org, UKMTO's X account, or a wire or established outlet (gCaptain, Lloyd's List, Khaleej Times, Gulf News, Arab News, Iran International, Ship & Bunker, Al Jazeera, Newsquawk…) quoting UKMTO or JMIC.
  - Date only, no time: an incident dated 26 Nov counts. A delayed or time-late report published after the window counts if the incident occurred in it.
  - Military action against a merchant vessel counts only if UKMTO or JMIC itself reports it.
  - Not counted: suspicious approaches without weapons fire or boarding attempt; GPS/navigation interference alone; bare "incident"/"suspicious activity" notices (incl. "tanker and military forces") stating no attack, explosion, hit, boarding or seizure; Gulf of Aden, Bab el-Mandeb, Red Sea; Iranian (IRGC/navy), Houthi or other claims not reported by UKMTO/JMIC.
  - NO if no such incident occurred in the window.
