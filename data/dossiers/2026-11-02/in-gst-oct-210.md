This is the only 2026 month found at or above ₹2.10 lakh crore apart from April. Apr–Jun 2026 together come to ₹6.32 lakh crore (₹8.43 − ₹2.11; own arithmetic). The individual months were not collected.
- **Feb 2026:** ₹1.83 lakh crore, +8.1%. Net cess revenue "dropped significantly" to Rs 5,063 crore from Rs 13,481 crore — Business Today 1 Mar 2026 (above).
- **Oct 2025 (the base):** gross ₹1,95,936 crore, +4.6% from ₹1,87,346 crore. Domestic ₹1.45 lakh crore (+2%); imports ₹50,884 crore (+13%). Net about ₹1.69 lakh crore. Refunds ₹26,934 crore (+~40%) — Business Today 1 Nov 2025 (above) and Tribune/ANI (above), "the recent GST rate rationalisation introduced in late September 2025".
- **Definition change (important):** the y/y bases now quoted for 2025 months are lower than the figures first published in 2025. Bases now quoted: Jul 2025 ₹1,83,065 crore; Aug 2025 ₹1.74 lakh crore; Sep 2025 ₹1.77 lakh crore. From memory, not re-verified tonight, the first releases in 2025 were about ₹1.96 lakh crore (Jul), ₹1.86 lakh crore (Aug) and ₹1.89 lakh crore (Sep). The gap of about ₹0.12–0.13 lakh crore matches the compensation cess, which was largely abolished with GST 2.0 on 22 Sep 2025. Inference: the current headline "gross GST" excludes cess, and the 2025 bases have been restated the same way. It is not known whether the ₹1,95,936 crore first published for Oct 2025 included cess. If it did, the comparable Oct 2025 base excluding cess is lower, roughly ₹1.83–1.89 lakh crore (own estimate). In that case ₹2.10 lakh crore would need about +11–15% y/y rather than +7.2%.
- **Growth needed (own arithmetic):** ₹2.10 lakh crore vs Sep 2026's ₹2.04 lakh crore is +2.9% month on month. Against the published Oct 2025 ₹1.96 lakh crore it is +7.2% y/y. Recent y/y growth on the restated bases: Jul +15.4%, Aug +14.8%, Sep +14.7%. Applying +14.7% to a cess-excluded base of ₹1.83–1.89 lakh crore gives ₹2.10–2.17 lakh crore (own arithmetic).
- **Seasonality:** October collections reflect September activity, plus quarter-end filings. From memory, not re-verified: Oct was above Sep in 2023 (₹1.72 vs ₹1.63 lakh crore), 2024 (₹1.87 vs ₹1.73) and 2025 (₹1.96 vs ₹1.89, figures as first published).
- **Festive timing:** in 2026 Navratri runs 11–20 Oct and Diwali falls on 8 Nov. Most festive spending therefore lands in October activity, which is collected in November. In 2025, Navratri began on 22 Sep together with the rate cuts.

## Risks
- **Definition/base:** the threshold of ₹2.10 lakh crore is an absolute level. Sep 2026 came in at ₹2.04 lakh crore. October needs about +3% month on month. If the headline excludes cess, the October level depends on cess-free growth momentum only.
- **Refunds and imports:** import GST has driven growth (+26–29% y/y). A drop in imports, for example from oil-price swings or tariffs, would hit the headline.
- **Rounding:** reports in lakh crore to two decimals ("2.10") could hide ₹2,09,6xx crore. The criterion needs ₹2,10,000 crore or more, so the exact crore figure should be checked.
- **Publication:** a quiet GSTN posting with press coverage only on 2 Nov would make the claim NO under a strict reading. The criterion allows the GSTN data "as reported by PIB or two major Indian outlets" on 1 Nov.
