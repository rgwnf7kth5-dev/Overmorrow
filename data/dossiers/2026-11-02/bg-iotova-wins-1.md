# bg-iotova-wins-1

- **Claim:** Iliana Iotova wins the Bulgarian presidential runoff held on Sunday 1 November 2026, receiving more valid votes than Andrey Gyurov. (Spec problem: the candidate is **Andrey** Gyurov. Georgi Kandev is his running mate. See Risks.)
- **Criterion:** YES if the runoff is held on 1 Nov 2026 and the Central Election Commission (CIK) results show Iotova with more valid votes than Gyurov. These may be preliminary results at or near 100% of protocols, or final results. NO if Gyurov has more votes, or if the runoff is not held on 1 Nov 2026.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-01T07:00:00+02:00 to 2026-11-01T20:00:00+02:00 (Europe/Sofia). Results come overnight 1–2 Nov.
- **Written at:** 2026-10-30T18:20Z

## Schedule
- **Runoff date and campaign timetable.** The runoff is on Sun 1 Nov. Campaigning ends at 24:00 on Fri 30 Oct, and Sat 31 Oct is the day of reflection. The runoff uses the same polling sections and machines as round 1. Applications submitted by 10 Oct (current-address voting, mobile boxes, voting abroad) remain valid, and no new deadline was set. Ballots for sections abroad have already been sent by diplomatic cargo. ## Risks
- **Turnout differential.** Turnout was 22.98% in round 1. A small electorate makes the result sensitive to mobilisation. Gyurov's PP–DB base mobilised strongly in round 1 (BNT), and the endorsements from Borissov and others may add GERB voters.
- **Polls are of little use.** Round-1 polls missed by 15–28 points on the lead, and no post-round-1 runoff poll was found.
- **Revival voters.** Kostadinov's voters may abstain or choose "I support no one" rather than vote Iotova.
- **Spec text error.** The claim text names "Georgi Gyurov". The candidate is Andrey (Andrei) Gyurov, and Georgi Kandev is his vice-presidential running mate. The criterion says only "Gyurov", so resolution is unaffected, but the text should be corrected.
- **Close result.** A very close result could mean the preliminary count near 100% differs from the final result. The criterion allows either.
