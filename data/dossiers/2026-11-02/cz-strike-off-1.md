# cz-strike-off-1
- **Claim:** By 23:59 on Sunday 1 November 2026, the organisers of the 3 November strike at Česká televize and Český rozhlas announce that it is called off or postponed.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 00:00 on 31 Oct and 23:59 CET on 1 Nov 2026 the Veřejnoprávně initiative or the trade unions organising the 3 Nov strike at ČT and ČRo publicly announce that the strike is cancelled, postponed or suspended at both broadcasters. Changes to its form, or one broadcaster dropping out while the other strikes, do not count. NO otherwise.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-31T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-01T23:59:59+01:00 (Europe/Prague)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-30T18:15:00Z

## Schedule

ČRo will publish the effect on its stations in a press release on Mon 2 Nov, and ČT will publish strike details on Monday — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/do-uterni-stavky-se-zapoji-1340-zamestnancu-a-externistu-cro/2895159 (published 2026-10-30 17:41, read 2026-10-30T18:05Z) "budeme informovat v tiskové zprávě v pondělí 2. listopadu". - 31 Oct is a Saturday and 1 Nov a Sunday.

## Base rate
- No class fits closely. Loose class: `unscheduled-announcement-within-one-day` (pipeline/reference-classes.json), base_rate null, own record 1 YES / 18 NO. Calling off a mass strike two days before it, with lists already handed in and no concession on the table, is rarer than a generic announcement.

## Schedule slip
- Slip class: none (skipped). For context, the 3 Nov strike itself would fall under `strike-or-protest`, 0.250 [estimate] (run 2026-10-29T18:05Z, via cz-strike-joins-31).

## Risks
- YES paths: a concrete government concession over the weekend, such as Babiš publicly withdrawing the bill or guaranteeing current funding, which organisers accept as grounds to suspend. A security or emergency event (a broadcasting-law emergency) forcing a postponement. A legal challenge to the strike's legality that leads organisers to postpone.
- Why NO is the default: the decisive political event (the coalition council) is on Mon 2 Nov, outside the window. The committee pause to December changes nothing before Tuesday. SPD has hardened. Lists of strikers have been filed. The June precedent shows organisers held to a strike after Babiš's softer words.
- Resolution traps: a change of form (for example "strike only on ČT24"), or ČRo or ČT alone dropping out, does not count. A "suspension" announced on Mon 2 Nov is outside the window.
