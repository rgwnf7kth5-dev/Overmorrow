# il-recommend-bibi-1
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Israel time on Sunday 1 November 2026, a party outside Netanyahu's 53-seat bloc publicly announces that it will recommend Benjamin Netanyahu to President Herzog or will join a government led by him.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 00:00 on 31 Oct and 23:59 Israel time on 1 Nov 2026 the leader or official spokesperson of a party elected to the Knesset on 27 Oct that is not part of Netanyahu's 53-seat bloc (Likud and its pre-election allies) publicly announces that the party will recommend Netanyahu for prime minister or will join a government he leads. Statements of openness, conditions, talks or unnamed sources do not count; an individual MK's defection without the party does not count. NO otherwise.
- **Event window:** 2026-10-31T00:00:00+02:00 to 2026-11-01T23:59:59+02:00 (Asia/Jerusalem). Shabbat runs until Saturday evening (about 17:30).
- **Written at:** 2026-10-30T18:30Z

## Base rate
- No class fits a party crossing blocs before the president's consultations. Loose: `coalition-deal-announced-short-window` 0.30 (own record 1 YES / 6 NO) — a different, easier event. `unscheduled-announcement-within-one-day`: own record about 1 YES / 17 NO.
- Precedent (news record, not re-checked tonight): in 2019–2021 no party elected in the anti-Netanyahu camp announced a recommendation of Netanyahu before consultations; Gantz's 2020 move to join a Netanyahu government came weeks after the vote and after Gantz had himself received the mandate.

## Schedule slip
- Slip class: none. Not run. Recommendations are formally made in the consultations from about 4 Nov, outside the window.

## Risks
- **Toward YES:** pressure for a unity government amid the Iran war (Trump-linked pressure reported earlier); Liberman reportedly cut contact with the opposition and wants an Eisenkot–Netanyahu unity government; a party could announce early to shape Herzog's choice.
- **Toward NO:** every party outside the bloc has publicly pledged not to sit with Netanyahu (Liberman 29 Oct, Bennett 11 Oct, Eisenkot repeatedly, Lapid 30 Oct, Democrats, Arab parties); Shabbat covers most of Saturday; recommendations are normally made in front of the president from 4 Nov; any move would come first through unnamed-source reports and "conditions", which do not count.
- **Spec problem:** the criterion defines the bloc as "Likud and its pre-election allies", but the 53-seat bloc includes UTJ (7), which left the coalition in 2025 and sat in opposition at dissolution (Wikipedia dissolution table, 17 July 2026). If UTJ were treated as outside the bloc, a UTJ statement could be argued to count. Proposed fix: name the bloc explicitly — Likud, Shas, UTJ, Otzma Yehudit, Religious Zionism (53) — and list the qualifying parties: Yashar, Together (B'Yachad), The Democrats, Yisrael Beytenu, Joint List, Ra'am.
- **Ambiguity:** "we will join a unity government with Likud" without Netanyahu as PM (e.g. rotation with Eisenkot first) — counts only if Netanyahu leads the government. A rotation where Netanyahu serves first would count.
