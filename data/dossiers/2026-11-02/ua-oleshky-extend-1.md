Unnamed sources, proposals or calls for extension do not count. NO otherwise.

## Facts
- **When and by whom agreed.** The White House announced the truce on 23 Oct as mediated by the Trump administration; Ukraine and Russia then confirmed it — Kyiv Independent https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-war-latest-oleshky-ceasefire-holds-as-zelensky-secures-new-aid-pledges/ (published 2026-10-28 20:20Z, read 2026-10-30T17:55Z) "announced by the White House on Oct. 23". The formal agreement was signed on 23 Oct in Belarus by ombudsmen Lubinets (UA) and Lantratova (RU); brokered by Melania Trump and her team — VOA (above).
- **Original terms.** "The agreement on establishing a six-day ceasefire and evacuation operation in Oleshky" (Melania Trump, per VOA). Three stages per Lubinets: ceasefire; delivery of food, water, medicine; evacuation of residents who wish to leave — VOA (above).
- **Area widened.** Russia proposed moving the start forward two days and widening the truce beyond Oleshky to other parts of Kherson Oblast; Ukraine agreed (adviser Marc Beckman) — Kyiv Independent (above). Trump: the ceasefire "has expanded beyond Oleshky, within the Kherson Region, to provide safe passage for the humanitarian convoys" — VOA (above).
- **Aid convoy.** An aid convoy entered Oleshky on Friday 30 Oct with food and medical supplies (Izvestia) — Al Jazeera https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/30/aid-convoy-enters-besieged-ukrainian-town-oleshky (published 2026-10-30 10:45, read 2026-10-30T18:01Z) "an aid convoy entered Russian-held Oleshky".
- **Evacuation.** Lantratova: 25 buses with at least 750 seats; evacuation until 14:00 local on 30 Oct; about 2,000 people remain in the town — Al Jazeera (above). Route: Oleshky → Anapa → flight to Gomel (Belarus) → Chernihiv region — VOA (above); newscord comparison page (BBC, CNN, Independent, VOA) https://newscord.org/article/evacuation-corridor-opens-from-russian-occupied-oleshky-under-melania-trump-brok--Story_20261030_Oleshkyevacuationbega57e6cc0 (published 2026-10-30, read 2026-10-30T17:58Z).
- **Truce holding.** "no violations of the ceasefire have been recorded" (Lubinets, 54 hours in) — VOA (above). Lantratova: "two people were killed in the demining effort in the city" — Al Jazeera (above).
- **Extension talk: none found.** None of Kyiv Independent (28 Oct), VOA (29 Oct), RFE/RL (29 Oct, updated 13:07 CET) https://www.rferl.org/a/oleshky-ukraine-russia-melania-trump-ceasefire/33886379.html, Meduza (29 Oct 21:10), Al Jazeera (30 Oct 10:45) or the newscord comparison of BBC/CNN/VOA/Independent (30 Oct) mentions any proposal to extend the truce past 1 Nov. Kyiv Independent 28 Oct: the truce is "expected to last into early November". The 30 Oct sweep (world.json) also records "No extension discussed so far."
- **Fear of what follows.** BBC (via newscord, above): Kherson residents fear the truce lets Russia build up forces on the Dnipro banks, with a renewed assault after it expires.
- **Earlier dossier (29 Oct) background still valid:** Lubinets' 29 Oct remark that the mission would take about a week (Fox News, via interfax-Ukraine https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/general/1227758.html); leaked documents mention further monthly "repatriation operations" (Meduza 28 Oct). These could be run as new truces rather than an extension.
- **Wider diplomacy.** Zelensky said technical-level talks with Russia and the US would take place in Abu Dhabi in the first half of November; Kremlin aide Ushakov said not before 4 Nov — Kyiv Independent (above) and sweep world.json (Ushakov line from a weak substack source).
- **Spec note:** the previous claim ua-oleshky-extend-31 (window 30–31 Oct) covered the same event; its outcome is not known at writing. Nothing found suggests an extension was announced on 30 Oct (before this window).

## Risks
- **Toward YES:** the evacuees' transfer on 31 Oct or the aid distribution runs late; residents who stayed need further deliveries; the White House (Melania Trump) wants to show a durable success before the 3 Nov midterms; Russia uses an extension as goodwill before Abu Dhabi talks.
- **Toward NO:** the stated tasks (convoy 30 Oct, evacuation 30–31 Oct) were done on schedule, so the truce lapses as planned; the parties prefer a new truce per monthly operation; any extension is quiet or announced by ombudsmen only (Lubinets and Lantratova are not listed as qualifying announcers in this spec — a statement only from them would not count unless the President's Office/Kremlin/MFA/MoD or a US government body repeats it); the truce breaks down.
- **Resolution ambiguity:** an announcement by Melania Trump personally — she is not explicitly named; the criterion says "US government (White House…)". If posted via the White House it counts; her personal account is a judgment call. Spec fix suggested: name the First Lady's office / Lubinets / Lantratova as qualifying sources, since they are the actual operators of this truce.
