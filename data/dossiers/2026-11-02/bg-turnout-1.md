# bg-turnout-1

- **Claim:** Turnout in the Bulgarian presidential runoff on 1 November 2026, as reported by the CIK, is … % (interval).
- **Criterion:** The turnout percentage of the 1 Nov 2026 runoff in CIK's results, including voters abroad as CIK reports it in the headline figure. Preliminary results at or near 100% of protocols count, as do final results.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-01T07:00:00+02:00 to 2026-11-01T20:00:00+02:00 (Europe/Sofia)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-30T18:25Z

## Schedule
- **Runoff on Sun 1 Nov.** Same sections and machines as round 1. There is no new application deadline: round-1 applications for abroad, current-address and mobile voting stay valid. Ballots for abroad have been sent. Source: CIK spokesperson via pbnovini — https://www.pbnovini.com/balgariya/2026/okonchatelno-ot-tsik-22-98-izbiratelna-aktivnost-samo-31-spreli-mashini-i-bez-novi-zayavleniya-za-balotazha/ (published 2026-10-28 11:42Z, read ~2026-10-30T18:08Z).
- **CIK reporting.** CIK publishes intraday turnout (11:00, 13:00/16:00, 18:00 style bulletins) and a final figure once protocols are processed.

## Facts
- **Round-1 turnout (25 Oct): 22.98%.**
  - CIK spokesperson Balova-Tsvetkova: "Избирателната активност на 25 октомври е била 22,98%" — pbnovini (above).
  - The same figure appears in Sofia Globe https://sofiaglobe.com/2026/10/26/yotova-gyurov-to-face-off-in-bulgarian-presidential-election-run-off-partial-results/ (published 2026-10-26 11:04Z, read 2026-10-30T18:12Z) "Turnout in first round … set a new record low, at 22.98 per cent, according to CEC figures."
  - podtepeto.com https://podtepeto.com/izbori/pod-23-e-bila-aktivnostta-na-prezidentskite-izbori/ (published 2026-10-28 14:00Z, read ~2026-10-30T18:10Z) also gives "22.98%".
- **Our cross-check.** Wikipedia's CIK-sourced table: all valid votes incl. "none" 1,521,710 + invalid 3,278 = 1,524,988 of 6,637,238 registered = 22.976% (our computation; raw wikitext read 2026-10-30T18:13Z).
- **Discrepancy: the claim's 22.93% is not CIK's figure.** The spec figure comes from riotimesonline https://www.riotimesonline.com/bulgaria-politics-explained-who-holds-power-2026/ (published 2026-10-27 05:39Z, read 2026-10-30T18:00Z) "only 22.93% of 6.64 million registered voters took part". CIK's figure is 22.98%.
- **Round-1 intraday turnout** (Mediapool headlines, search results only, not opened):
  - "Исторически ниска активност - 16.04% към 16 часа"
  - "Рекордно ниска активност - 20.8% към 18 часа"
  - Final 22.98%.
- **Turnout record.** Lowest since 1989 (burgasmedia headline "при най-ниска активност от 1989 г.", search title). 2021 round 1 was 40.47%: Wikipedia infobox shows −17.49 pp for 2026.
- **Turnout history of presidential runoffs**, from Wikipedia infoboxes and tables (raw wikitext read 2026-10-30T18:14–18:17Z):

  | Year | Round 1 | Runoff | Change | Runoff ÷ round 1 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 2006 | 44.11% | 42.62% | −1.49 pp | 0.966 |
  | 2011 | 52.29% | 48.25% | −4.04 pp | 0.923 |
  | 2016 | 56.28% | 50.44% | −5.84 pp | 0.896 |
  | 2021 | 40.47% | 34.85% | −5.62 pp | 0.861 |

  - 2011: round 1 was held together with local elections ("held in conjunction with local elections").
  - 2016 votes: 3,947,575 (round 1) and 3,540,964 (runoff), from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Bulgarian_presidential_election (read 2026-10-30T18:01Z).
  - 2021: round 1 was combined with the parliamentary election. The 2021 runoff was 34.84% per CIK (news.bg headline cited in Wikipedia: "ЦИК изчисли окончателно: 34.84% избирателна активност на балотажа за президент"). Sofia Globe 2021-11-22: "about 2.31 million votes cast … turnout of about 34.8 per cent" (lowest presidential runoff to that date). BNR cites "30.3%" for the 2021 runoff; the base is unclear and conflicts with CIK.
  - In every case the runoff turnout was below round 1. Rounds 1 that coincided with another vote (2011 local, 2021 parliamentary) inflate the comparison. In 2026, round 1 was a stand-alone presidential vote in the sources read; no concurrent election is mentioned.
- **2021 runoff intraday** (BNT headlines, search titles): 15.5% at 13:00, 24.12% at 16:00.
- **Mobilisation signals for the runoff.**
  - The race is close: 2.81-point gap. Source: Sofia Globe https://sofiaglobe.com/2026/10/30/bulgarias-2026-presidential-elections-three-former-pms-two-former-presidents-endorse-gyurov/ (published 2026-10-30 13:53Z, read 18:12Z).
  - New endorsements for Gyurov: Borissov, Kostov, Denkov, Plevneliev, Terziev; Stoyanov earlier (same source). GERB had no candidate in round 1, and its voters partly stayed home (BNT, Simeonov: April GERB voters in round 1 "no more than 100,000").
  - Kostadinov expects some Revival voters to abstain or vote "I support no one" — Mediapool https://www.mediapool.bg/prezidentski-izbori-2026/kostadinov-obyavi-che-gyurov-nyama-da-poluchi-nito-edin-glas-ot-izbiratelite-na-vazrazhdane/ (published 2026-10-27 11:48 EET, read ~18:09Z).
  - One televised debate was held (BNT, 29 Oct) — Sofia Globe (published 2026-10-29 22:10Z).
- **Abroad.** Roughly 81,000 voters abroad in round 1. This is our derivation from 2,342 "none" votes = 2.89% (Novinite 241263, published 2026-10-26 11:41, read ~17:59Z). That is about 1.2% of the 6.64m electorate. No new abroad registrations are allowed for the runoff (pbnovini).
- **No published turnout forecast** from a sociologist was found for the runoff.

## Base rate
- **Bulgarian presidential runoffs since 2006, runoff turnout ÷ round-1 turnout:** 0.966, 0.923, 0.896, 0.861. Mean 0.91; none above 1 (n=4).
  - Applied mechanically to 22.98%, this gives 19.8–22.2%.
  - The 2026 round 1 had no concurrent election boosting it, unlike 2011 and 2021, so the historic drop may overstate the 2026 drop.
- **Pipeline class `turnout-above-previous-election`** (base 0.5, own record 1/0) refers to the previous comparable election. Here that is the round 1 just held (see bg-turnout-above-r1-1).
- **Not checked:** foreign two-round runoffs after a record-low first round with a close gap.

## Schedule slip
- Slip class `election-held`: `node scripts/refdata/slip.mjs election-held` gives prior 0.01, own record 0/19, rate **0.005** [estimate]. No sign of postponement.

## Risks
- **Downside:** Bulgaria's runoff record is uniformly lower; apathy is at a record; it is a weekend in early November; some Revival voters deliberately abstain.
- **Upside:** A close two-way race with polarising stakes (Russia/Black Sea security vs "peace"), new endorsements from Borissov and other ex-leaders, and fewer candidates. The low 22.98% base means a small absolute mobilisation moves the percentage.
- **Measurement:**
  - CIK's election-night headline (section reports at 20:00) can differ by tenths from the final figure computed from protocols.
  - The voter-list denominator may change slightly between rounds (2016: 7,014,723 vs 7,020,119).
  - Abroad voters are included in CIK's figure.
