- **Q2 fare guidance (given 20 Jul):** "Q2 pricing is trending modestly down (y-o-y)"; "the booking window remains closer-in than last year which further reduces visibility"; H1 fare outcome depends on August/September close-in bookings; "it remains far too early to provide any meaningful FY27 PAT guidance" — Q1 FY27 release (above). O'Leary: "I am bearish on pricing"; discounted fares expected through the summer and probably the rest of the year — Irish Times https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/07/20/ryanair-profits-fall-by-a-third-to-538m/ (published 2026-07-20, read 18:07Z).
- **Earlier guidance (18 May):** fares to fall mid-single digits in Apr–Jun "then stay roughly level in July-September"; cost per passenger could rise mid-single digits if fuel stays high — LSE.co.uk https://www.lse.co.uk/news/ryanair-trims-prices-and-seeks-to-dispel-jet-fuel-fears-5bomkgk9j0gtvmh.html (published 2026-05-18, read 18:08Z). The July update was weaker (Q2 "modestly down").
- **Fuel:** 80% of FY27 jet fuel hedged at c.$67/bbl; spot jet fuel "more than doubled" y/y in Q1; remaining 20% bought at spot prices while the US–Iran war continues — Q1 release and Irish Times 20 Jul (above).
- **Traffic:** H1 traffic growth 6%, H2 2%; FY27 216m (+4%) — Q1 release (above).
- **Arithmetic for the threshold (own):** H1 FY27 ≥ €2.54bn needs Q2 FY27 PAT ≥ €2.00bn, i.e. +16% on Q2 FY26 (≈ €1.72bn), with fares guided modestly down and costs up. H1 FY27 = Q1 €0.538bn + Q2; equal Q2 to last year gives ≈ €2.26bn.
- **Consensus:** not found in a primary or news source. Digrin lists for RYA.IR on 2 Nov: EPS estimate 1.50, revenue estimate 6.4bn, period and currency not stated; for RYAAY (ADR = 2 shares): EPS 3.46, revenue 6.5bn — Digrin (above). Own arithmetic: Q1 FY27 EPS €0.5121 on €537.7m PAT implies ~1.05bn shares, so EPS €1.50 ≈ €1.58bn PAT. If that is a Q2 figure, H1 ≈ €2.11bn; if it is H1, ≈ €1.58bn. Both below €2.54bn. The revenue figure (6.4bn) fits neither Q2 nor H1 cleanly (Q1 alone was €4.38bn), so treat Digrin as weak.
