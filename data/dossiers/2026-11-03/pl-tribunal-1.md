# pl-tribunal-1
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Warsaw time on 2 November 2026, Poland's Prosecutor General Waldemar Żurek formally files a motion to lift the immunity of Constitutional Tribunal president Bogdan Święczkowski.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 00:00 on 1 Nov and 23:59 Warsaw time on 2 Nov 2026 Żurek, or the National Prosecutor's Office on his behalf, formally files (sends to the competent body) a motion to lift Święczkowski's immunity, as stated by the ministry or prosecution or reported by PAP, TVN24, Rzeczpospolita or a wire agency. Announcements that a motion is being prepared do not count. NO otherwise.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-01T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-02T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Warsaw)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-31T18:20:00Z. Read times below are approximate, all between 17:15Z and 17:50Z on 31 Oct.

**Main finding: the claim as posed is moot.** Żurek already sent the motions on Fri 30 Oct, before the window opens. They went to the Tribunal and were delivered to Anna Korwin-Piotrowska, and on 31 Oct the Tribunal confirmed it had received them. A new filing in the window would happen only if Żurek sent yet another motion. See "Proposed replacement" at the end.

## Schedule
- There is no scheduled filing. The motions were sent on 30 Oct (Rzeczpospolita, Notes from Poland, below).
- The competent body is the General Assembly of Tribunal judges. Consent to prosecute a Tribunal judge requires a General Assembly resolution "bezwzględną większością głosów sędziów Trybunału uczestniczących w Zgromadzeniu" (art. 23(1), act of 30 Nov 2016 on the status of Tribunal judges), and resolutions need at least 2/3 of the 15 judges present (art. 8(1) of the organisation act). Source: Prokuratura Krajowa communiqué of 3 Dec 2025 (below).
- **Next dated step:** Korwin-Piotrowska has convened a General Assembly for **Tue 3 Nov 2026**. Its stated agenda is the draft 2027 Tribunal budget. No source lists the immunity motions as an agenda item (prawo.pl, rp.pl, Interia, below). The Assembly falls one day after the claim window.

## Facts
- 30 Oct, about 18:00: a measure on the Tribunal's website suspended president Święczkowski and vice-president Bartłomiej Sochański for one month. Korwin-Piotrowska cited art. 130 of the law on common courts — Seznam Zprávy https://www.seznamzpravy.cz/clanek/zahranicni-nocni-strkanice-soudcu-s-prezidentem-polsko-resi-nebyvalou-ustavni-krizi-319216 (published 2026-10-31 ~09:16, read 2026-10-31T17:20Z) "požádal o zbavení imunity obou odstavených funkcionářů".
- 30 Oct: Żurek "announced he had submitted motions to the TK to waive the immunity from prosecution" of Święczkowski and Sochański. He said the documents were "delivered to the longest-serving judge of the Constitutional Tribunal" — Notes from Poland https://notesfrompoland.com/2026/10/30/chaos-at-polands-constitutional-court-as-government-backed-judges-seek-to-oust-chief-justice/ (published 2026-10-30 21:55 CET, read 2026-10-31T17:35Z).
- 30 Oct: five motions were sent: four on Święczkowski and one on Sochański. Żurek also sent 50 volumes of case files to Korwin-Piotrowska and said "dalsze informacje w sprawie będą przekazywane przez Trybunał Konstytucyjny" — Rzeczpospolita https://www.rp.pl/sady-i-trybunaly/art45423951-premier-komentuje-sytuacje-w-tk-zgromadzenie-ogolne-sedziow-3-listopada (published 2026-10-30 19:08, upd. 20:35, read 2026-10-31T17:40Z).
- 30 Oct: Korwin-Piotrowska called a General Assembly for 3 Nov to adopt the 2027 budget, calling it the "ostatni moment, kiedy jeszcze można zwołać zgromadzenie ogólne". The immunity "może też zostać uchylony" (article's wording, not an agenda item). Quorum is 10 of 15 judges — prawo.pl https://www.prawo.pl/prawnicy-sady/zgromadzenie-ogolne-sedziow-tk-anna-korwin-piotrowska-immunitet-bogdana-swieczkowskiego,1556209.html (published 2026-10-30 21:03, read 2026-10-31T17:42Z).
- 31 Oct: a Tribunal communiqué signed by Korwin-Piotrowska confirms the immunity motions were received and "zostaną rozpatrzone zgodnie z obowiązującymi zasadami". It gives no date. It also restricts the Chancellery and Legal Service Office and says entry to the building is not possible — Interia https://wydarzenia.interia.pl/kraj/news-nowy-komunikat-trybunalu-konstytucyjnego-nawiaze-bezposredni,nId,23566878 (updated 2026-10-31, cached 14:21, read 2026-10-31T17:45Z).
- 31 Oct, Korwin-Piotrowska's order: most staff are suspended with pay, judges are advised to work outside the building, the registry is suspended, and the General Assembly is convened for 3 Nov. Święczkowski's counter-order no. 16/2026 makes 3–6 Nov days off for staff and disputes her order's legal effect — Rzeczpospolita https://www.rp.pl/sady-i-trybunaly/art45425341-wojna-na-zarzadzenia-swieczkowski-podwaza-decyzje-korwin-piotrowskiej (published 2026-10-31 11:28, upd. 14:53, read 2026-10-31T17:48Z) "obecnie wstęp do siedziby Trybunału nie jest możliwy".
- 30–31 Oct, Nawrocki: he cut short a state visit to Senegal and entered the court building shortly after midnight, telling the judges "Nejste soudci Ústavního soudu". He met Supreme Court head Kapiński and Święczkowski, and called a meeting of parliamentary group leaders for Sat 31 Oct, which the Left boycotts — ČT24 https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/svet/nawrocki-vstoupil-do-budovy-polskeho-ustavniho-soudu-doslo-ke-slovnim-potyckam-379167 (published 2026-10-31, read 2026-10-31T17:15Z); Seznam Zprávy (above).
- Police kept Święczkowski out of the building on 30 Oct. Seven judges were escorted in by the State Protection Service, and Sochański was not admitted — Notes from Poland (above).
- **Precedent:** on 30 Sep 2025 the prosecution (Zespół nr 3, Pegasus case) filed a motion for consent to prosecute Święczkowski for alleged abuse of power. On 15 Oct 2025 the General Assembly (11 judges present, 10 voting) refused unanimously. The prosecution says that resolution "nie wywołuje skutków prawnych" because two contested judges took part, and that the motion can be referred again — Prokuratura Krajowa https://www.gov.pl/web/prokuratura-krajowa/komunikat-o-nierozpoznaniu-przez-zgromadzenie-ogolne-sedziow-trybunalu-konstytucyjnego-wniosku-o-uchylenie-immunitetu-bogdanowi-swieczkowskiemu (published 2025-12-03, read 2026-10-31T17:30Z); Notes from Poland https://notesfrompoland.com/2025/10/17/polish-constitutional-court-rejects-justice-ministers-request-to-lift-chief-justices-immunity/ (published 2025-10-17, read 2026-10-31T17:32Z).
