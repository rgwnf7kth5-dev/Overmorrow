## Facts
- **Talks ended:** KHNP ended talks without agreement with the consortium of Daewoo Engineering & Construction and Doosan Enerbility on the civil-works contract for the two new units, citing "rozdílné postoje k zásadním podmínkám" (different positions on key terms) — ČT24/ČTK 29 Oct (above).
- **KHNP's next steps:**
  - weighing ending its cooperation agreement with the consortium;
  - planning to prepare a new tender for a construction contractor;
  - securing continuity of key work during the transition, partly doing it directly.

  Source: ČT24/ČTK 29 Oct (above).
- **KHNP statement to ČT, 30 Oct:** the top priority is keeping the schedule. "Nyní chce vybrat nového dodavatele stavebních prací a zajistit pokračování klíčových projektových činností." "Rozhodnutí o ukončení jednání bylo přijato po pečlivém zvážení dopadů na celkový harmonogram projektu." — ČT24 https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/ekonomika/korejci-hledaji-nove-stavebniky-pro-dukovany-cesi-jim-pripominaji-cenu-a-sankce-379156 (published about 2026-10-30, the page shows only a relative time; read 2026-10-31T17:47Z).
- **Havlíček (Minister of Industry and Trade):**
  - the schedule is not endangered, according to his Korean counterpart;
  - KHNP is ready to talk to other contractors, and enough large firms are interested;
  - he leaves the matter to the Korean side, which guarantees delivery on time.

  Source: ČT24/ČTK 29 Oct (above). This is a paraphrase; no direct quote with a date for a new contractor was found.
- **EDU II CEO Petr Závodský:** "Kontrakt má pevnou cenu a za neplnění podmínek obsahuje případné sankce." Existing contracts, including the Doosan Škoda Power turbine supply, remain valid — ČT24 30 Oct (above).
- **ČEZ CEO (not named in the summary read):** the news is significant and may be "a pricing negotiation played out in the media" (paraphrase) — ČT24 30 Oct (above).
- **Czech firms:** a ČVUT researcher thinks price was the core dispute and that the first works must start soon. The Czech construction industry association says Czech firms have the necessary experience but need timely information — ČT24 30 Oct (above).
- **Project timetable:** construction of the first unit is to start in 2029, with completion planned for 2036. The total cost is CZK 407bn for two units at last year's prices. No change to the plan has been announced — ČT24/ČTK 29 Oct (above); Havlíček headline "Stavba by měla začít v roce 2029" https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/domaci/ministr-havlicek-predstavi-dalsi-kroky-ke-stavbe-novych-bloku-dukovan-370374 (not opened).
- **No timetable for a new contractor:** none was given in any source read. The process KHNP describes (prepare and run a new tender) cannot plausibly finish within four days.
