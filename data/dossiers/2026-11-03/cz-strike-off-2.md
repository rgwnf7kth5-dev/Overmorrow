# cz-strike-off-2
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Prague time on 2 November 2026, the organisers call off or postpone the strike of Česká televize and Český rozhlas employees planned for 3 November.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 00:00 on 1 Nov and 23:59 Prague time on 2 Nov 2026 the organisers of the 3 Nov strike at ČT and ČRo publicly announce that the strike is called off, postponed or suspended, as reported by ČTK, ČT24, iROZHLAS or Mediář. The organisers are the Veřejnoprávně initiative or the trade unions at ČT and ČRo. A change in the form of the strike that keeps a work stoppage on 3 Nov does not count. NO otherwise.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-01T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-02T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Prague)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-31T18:15Z. All read times are approximate, between 17:40Z and 18:05Z on 31 Oct.

## Schedule
- **Declared:** the Veřejnoprávně initiative declared a full-day strike for Tue 3 Nov; the ČT and ČRo unions criticised the bill in a joint statement. "Pracovníky České televize a Českého rozhlasu tímto vláda nutí k důrazné celodenní stávce." — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/pracovnici-ct-a-cro-vyhlasili-na-utery-3-listopadu-celodenni-stavku/2892145 (published 2026-10-23 15:07, updated 16:21, read 2026-10-31T17:50Z).
- **Run with union support:** "Veřejnoprávně s podporou odborů stávku vyhlásilo"; 1,340 ČRo employees and freelancers have signed up. ČRo will publish the broadcast changes on Monday: "O tom, jak se stávka projeví ve vysílání jednotlivých stanic, budeme informovat v tiskové zprávě v pondělí 2. listopadu." ČT plans to publish details on Monday too — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/do-uterni-stavky-se-zapoji-1340-zamestnancu-a-externistu-cro/2895159 (published 2026-10-30 17:41, read 2026-10-31T17:48Z).

## Facts
- **Organisers:** Veřejnoprávně, an initiative of employees of both broadcasters. Spokespeople include:
  - Daniel Stach (ČT presenter);
  - Lucie Pávová (ČRo, strike committee);
  - Eliška Záleská (ČT).

  OS Media (chair Jan Křemen) backs the strike — ČTK 23 Oct (above); Seznam Zprávy https://www.seznamzpravy.cz/clanek/domaci-zivot-v-cesku-ve-vysilani-to-bude-znat-co-prinese-stavka-televize-a-rozhlasu-319119 (undated on page, about 30–31 Oct; read 2026-10-31T17:52Z).
- **Plan for 3 Nov:**
  - The strike runs from midnight to midnight: "Stávka je od půlnoci do půlnoci".
  - Most ČRo staff take part, and ČT keeps a minimum crew.
  - There are marches in the morning and a rally at 16:36 on Václavské náměstí, with rallies in other cities.
  - ČT24 news runs hourly: "Zprávy pak poběží jednou za hodinu místo běžného půlhodinového intervalu."

  Source: Seznam Zprávy (above).
- **Only stated reason to interrupt is an emergency:** "Rozhlas i televize ale plánují stávku omezit nebo přerušit v případě, kdy dojde k mimořádné události" — Seznam Zprávy (above). Stach: if a crisis such as floods or a tornado occurs, "protest bude přerušen" — Novinky https://www.novinky.cz/clanek/podcasty-politalk-daniel-stach-ke-stavce-ct-a-cro-40604645 (published 2026-10-23 16:24, read 2026-10-31T18:02Z).
- **No conditions for calling it off:** in none of the five articles read did the organisers name a political condition, such as withdrawal of the bill, under which they would call the strike off.
- **Legal date:** Stach says the date follows the legal procedure, so the strike could not start sooner — Novinky (above).
- **Demands are about the bill and the process** (no state-budget funding, a proper expert debate), not a single concession — ČTK 23 Oct (above).
- **Coalition is not withdrawing the bill:** "vládní koalice návrh zákona o změně financování nestáhne" — Ekonomický deník https://ekonomickydenik.cz/babis-vyhruzky-zruseni-poplatku-nestahne/embed/ (October 2026, read 2026-10-31T18:03Z). The bill passed first reading. Babiš's new voluntary-fee idea (31 Oct) would come as a change in second reading in January, and the coalition council meets on Mon 2 Nov — ČT24/ČTK https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/domaci/babis-vlada-chysta-navrh-podle-nehoz-by-ct-a-cro-nefinancoval-primo-statni-rozpocet-379172 (read 2026-10-31T17:45Z).
- **No reaction yet:** no reaction by Veřejnoprávně to Babiš's 31 Oct voluntary-fee proposal was found (search 2026-10-31T17:58Z).
- **Precedent:** in June 2026, Babiš said after a government meeting that there was "no longer any reason to protest" because only the financing part would change. The staff protest went ahead — Ground News summary of Novinky/Aktuality.sk https://ground.news/article/andrej-babis-there-is-no-longer-any-reason-to-protest-for-ct-and-cr (published 2026-06-01, read 2026-10-31T18:00Z). The June warning strike also took place — WTOP/AP https://wtop.com/world/2026/06/czech-public-broadcasters-stage-warning-strike-over-government-plan-to-change-funding/ (search listing only).
- **Others joining:** student strike committees and universities join on 3 Nov — ČT24 https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/domaci/studenti-vyhlasili-stavku-kvuli-ruseni-poplatku-pro-ct-a-cro-379086 (from running.json, not re-opened).

  - The coalition council withdraws the bill or freezes it, and the organisers suspend the strike.
  - A natural disaster or security emergency, the condition they have named.
  - A legal injunction.
