# cz-coalition-media-1
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Prague time on 2 November 2026, Andrej Babiš, Tomio Okamura or Petr Macinka publicly states that the coalition council of ANO, SPD and Motoristé has agreed on a new model of funding Česká televize and Český rozhlas.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 00:00 on 1 Nov and 23:59 Prague time on 2 Nov 2026 Babiš, Okamura or Macinka publicly states that the coalition council or the leaders of ANO, SPD and Motoristé sobě agreed on or approved a new model of funding ČT and ČRo, as reported by ČTK, ČT24, iROZHLAS or Seznam Zprávy. Statements that talks continue, that only principles were discussed, or that the decision was postponed resolve NO.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-01T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-02T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Prague)
- **Written at:** 2026-10-31T18:15Z. All read times below are approximate, between 17:40Z and 18:05Z on 31 Oct.

- No time of day for the council and no planned press conference were found. No official coalition or government page announces it; the date rests on Babiš's statement as reported.

## Facts
- **Babiš's model:** fees become voluntary, with a state-guaranteed amount, and the money does not come "directly" from the budget. "Budeme navrhovat dobrovolnost poplatků, kdo chce, ať platí, kdo nechce, nebude platit." "A chci přijít s nějakým návrhem, že peníze nejsou přímo z rozpočtu" — ČT24/ČTK (above).
- **Who drafts it:** prepared with Finance Minister Alena Schillerová and ministry staff. Firms would stop paying. The guaranteed amount would be a mandatory expenditure. Babiš has already presented the idea to ČT director general Hynek Chudárek — Mediář (above), "Chci přijít s návrhem, že peníze nejdou přímo z rozpočtu."
- **Timing of the law:** it will not take effect in January. Second reading falls around Christmas because of the 60-day period, so January: "Druhé čtení podle něj s ohledem na lhůtu 60 dnů vychází na Vánoce, takže přijde na řadu až v lednu." — ČT24/ČTK (above).
- **Bill status:** ANO, SPD and Motoristé unexpectedly put the government bill (abolition of fees, funding from the state budget) on priority reading on Friday, and it passed first reading — ČT24/ČTK (above): "Koalice ANO, SPD a Motoristů předlohu nečekaně zařadila k přednostnímu projednání v pátek."
- **SPD (Okamura, 30 Oct):** "To, co se určitě nezmění, je, že ty poplatky zrušíme." On tax-based funding: "Pakliže by to mělo být pojato tak, že se zvýší daně českým občanům, tak s tím máme problém." Okamura expects the new financing form to be agreed **in the first half of next year** — ČTK (above).
- **SPD position:** "Co se týče SPD, my máme jednu neměnnou pozici." — ČTK (above). Inference, not a statement: "voluntary fees" means keeping fees in some form, which conflicts with SPD's "we will abolish the fees" wording. A guarantee paid from public money may fall under SPD's tax objection.
- **Motoristé:** no statement on the voluntary-fee model was found. In April, Culture Minister Oto Klempíř (Motoristé) said abolishing fees was not in the Motoristé programme and that withdrawing the bill "není na pořadu dne" — Lupa.cz https://www.lupa.cz/clanky/babis-pracovni-skupina-financovani-verejnopravnich-medii-poplatky-zruseni/ (published 2026-04-23, read 2026-10-31T18:00Z).
- **Babiš–Chudárek meeting 29 Oct:** the model outside the state budget was mentioned, but nothing was agreed — cz_candidates research note (Seznam Zprávy https://www.seznamzpravy.cz/clanek/318853, not re-opened tonight).
- **Bill not withdrawn:** "vládní koalice návrh zákona o změně financování nestáhne", "O jeho konečné podobě se však bude ještě jednat." — Ekonomický deník https://ekonomickydenik.cz/babis-vyhruzky-zruseni-poplatku-nestahne/embed/ (October 2026, exact date not shown; read 2026-10-31T18:03Z).
- **Earlier announcements were processes, not agreements:**
  - 22 Apr 2026: after Babiš met Chudárek, the only announcement was a working group, with no funding agreement — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/kvuli-zakonu-o-ct-a-cro-vznikne-pracovni-skupina/2815795 (published 2026-04-22 17:28, read 2026-10-31T18:00Z).
  - In April, Babiš already floated voluntary payment and a guaranteed amount in law, yet the coalition kept the abolition bill — Lupa.cz (above).
  - Late May 2026: a coalition debate dropped the comprehensive media law — Mediář (above). No details were read.
- **Babiš's reversals:** the Mediář headline reads "Babiš to zase mění" (above). The model has changed several times: Nacher's bill (fees kept, exemptions), then abolition with budget funding, now voluntary fees with a guarantee.

## Risks
- **For YES:** Babiš announces after the council that "the coalition agreed to propose voluntary fees in second reading". Even a principle-level agreement worded as "dohodli jsme se" may count, so the wording matters.
- **For NO:**
  - SPD refuses anything that keeps fees.
  - The outcome is "Finance Ministry will prepare variants".
  - The council is moved, or holds no press conference.
  - Statements come only from ministers or MPs not named in the claim. Schillerová, for example, would not count, because only Babiš, Okamura and Macinka do.
- **Ambiguity:** the line between "agreed on the model" and "agreed to continue on Babiš's proposal" will be a judgement call. Resolve on the exact wording reported by ČTK.
