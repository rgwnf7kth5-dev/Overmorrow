# cz-refinery-banks-12
- **Claim:** On 11 November 2026 the Chamber's budget committee adopts a resolution on the refinery-tax bill that includes an amendment extending the extraordinary tax (or a new levy) to banks or to another sector besides refineries.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 10 Nov 06:00 and 11 Nov 23:59 (Prague), the budget committee adopts a resolution on the refinery-tax bill whose recommended amendments extend the tax, or add a levy, to banks or any other sector besides refining (e.g. defence/arms makers, energy), per the resolution on psp.cz or ČTK/ČT24 reports. NO otherwise (including if such an amendment is only filed for the plenary second reading, or the committee does not adopt a resolution).
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-11T23:59+01:00
