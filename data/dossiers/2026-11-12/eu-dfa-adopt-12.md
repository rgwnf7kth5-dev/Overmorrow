# eu-dfa-adopt-12
- **Claim:** The European Commission adopts and publishes its legislative proposal for a Digital Fairness Act on Wednesday 11 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, on 11 Nov 2026 (Brussels time), the Commission adopts a legislative proposal for the Digital Fairness Act (a proposed regulation or directive on digital fairness / unfair commercial practices, whatever its final official title) and makes it public, shown by a Commission press release, the Commission's website or press corner, a COM document, or reports by major outlets that the proposal was adopted or presented that day. NO if the presentation is postponed beyond 11 Nov or only a communication or non-legislative document is presented.

## Facts
- Draft (71 pages, per Contexte leak) is a Regulation repealing and replacing the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive — DFA Newsletter above.
- Draft content per Euronews: a blacklist of ten dark patterns, ban on drip pricing, loot boxes and pay-to-win banned for minors, "terminate the contract here" button, right to disable autoplay and streaks, rules for AI shopping agents, Commission fines up to 6% of global turnover; applies 12 months after entry into force; impact assessment: €88.4bn consumer losses avoided 2025–2035 — Newswav/Euronews above. Commission spokesperson: "does not comment on leaks".
- AFP: influencer ads must be labelled "advertisement" or "commercial collaboration"; drafts "still subject to revisions" — AFP above.
- Germany's non-paper of 14 Oct argues for amending existing directives instead of a new horizontal law (reported by Agence Europe, via Euronews above).
- Bloomberg, 9 Nov: "EU Targets Addictive Social Media, AI Chatbot Features in Draft Law" (headline only, from search results) — https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/eu-plans-crackdown-on-addictive-social-media-and-ai-bot-features.
- Delays: due mid-2026, then Q3, then end of year; business associations asked for a pause in March citing reservations of the Regulatory Scrutiny Board — Eunews https://www.eunews.it/en/2026/10/26/a-fiery-november-ahead-college-eu/ (published 2026-10-26, read 2026-11-09T17:53Z).
