# cz-prague-partner-12
- **Claim:** Between 06:00 on 10 November and 23:59 on 11 November 2026, STAN and Spolu announce a Prague city coalition that includes Praha sobě.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 2026-11-10T06:00 and 2026-11-11T23:59 Prague time, leaders of STAN and Spolu publicly announce the final composition of the Prague city coalition and Praha sobě is named as a member (with or without other parties), as reported by ČTK (ceskenoviny.cz), ČT24, praha.eu or the parties' own channels. NO if the coalition announced in the window is STAN and Spolu with the Pirates, or STAN and Spolu alone, or if no final composition is announced in the window. A preferred option still subject to further party approval, or a statement that talks continue, does not count as an announcement.

- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-09T18:15Z. Read times as stated.
