# nvidia-huang-brief-12
- **Claim:** Between 00:00 ET on 10 November and 23:59 ET on 11 November 2026 Nvidia, Jensen Huang or one of the three House committees that asked for his briefing (Foreign Affairs, Intelligence, Select Committee on China) publicly confirms that Huang will appear for, or has agreed to, the requested closed-door congressional briefing on his contacts with Alice Huang.
- **Criterion:** YES if in that window Nvidia, Huang, or the chair or an official spokesperson of the House Foreign Affairs Committee, the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence or the House Select Committee on the CCP states on the record that Huang has agreed to or will attend the briefing (on 18 Nov or another date), as published by the speaker or reported by at least one of AP, Reuters, Bloomberg, CNBC, NYT, WSJ, Washington Post, Politico, The Hill, NBC, CNN. Anonymous sourcing alone does not count; an offer to send other Nvidia officials instead of Huang does not count. NO if no such on-the-record confirmation is made in the window (including if Huang declines or no answer is made public).
