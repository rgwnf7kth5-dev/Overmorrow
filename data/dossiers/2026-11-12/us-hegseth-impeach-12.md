## Facts
- Adoption needs a simple majority of those voting; the House GOP majority is narrow and Democrats are expected to back it: Jeffries "hard yes" (ABC "This Week", 8 Nov, via https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/hegseth-could-face-impeachment-vote-161823113.html, published 2026-11-09T17:28Z, read 2026-11-09T17:52Z) "with a narrow GOP majority, Republican votes will be needed."
- Republicans undecided or leaning: French Hill "still considering"; Don Bacon "wrestling" with it — "I don't think he's been a good secretary, but do you impeach a guy for that? It has to be a high crime or misdemeanor" (same article).
- Massie told the paper the chances of passage are better after the election, but he hasn't spoken "to Republicans directly who are committed to supporting it" — The Washington Sun, Joe Gould, "Massie says chances of Hegseth impeachment are up after GOP midterm losses" (full URL in the us-hegseth story file sources) (published 2026-11-09T17:22Z, read 2026-11-09T17:53Z).
- CNN 4 Nov: "multiple GOP sources said they expect it to pass" — https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/04/politics/pete-hegseth-impeachment-trump (read 2026-11-08T17:53Z, per us-hegseth-vote-11 dossier). Axios via MLQ (8 Nov): GOP leaders "are bracing for enough GOP defections to pass" it.

- A motion to table (not debatable) or to refer comes first if leaders or any member offer one; adoption only happens if such motions fail (Washington Sun above). In 2023 the Mayorkas resolution was referred 209-201 with eight Republicans joining Democrats (https://rollcall.com/2023/11/13/house-sidesteps-effort-to-impeach-homeland-security-chief-over-border-policy/).
- History: Hegseth would be the third Cabinet secretary impeached (Belknap 1876, Mayorkas 2024) — Yahoo/CBS article above.
