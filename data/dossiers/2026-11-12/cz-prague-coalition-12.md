# cz-prague-coalition-12
- **Claim:** Between 06:00 on 10 November and 23:59 on 11 November 2026, STAN and Spolu publicly announce the final composition of the Prague city coalition, naming every party in it.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 2026-11-10T06:00 and 2026-11-11T23:59 Prague time, leaders of STAN and Spolu (e.g. Petr Hlaváček, Tomáš Portlík) publicly announce the final composition of the coalition they will form in the Prague city assembly, naming every party in it (STAN and Spolu with Praha sobě, with the Pirates, or STAN and Spolu alone declared as the final coalition), for example at a press conference, in a joint statement or by signing a coalition agreement, as reported by ČTK (ceskenoviny.cz), ČT24, praha.eu or the parties' own channels. A statement that talks continue, a preferred option still subject to further party approval, or a "core" of STAN and Spolu with the third partner still open does not count. NO if no such announcement is made in the window.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-11T23:59+01:00; evidence by 2026-11-12T05:00+01:00
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-09T18:10Z. Read times as stated.

## Facts
- Seats in the 65-seat assembly: STAN 17, Spolu 17, Praha sobě 13, ANO 11, Pirates 7; STAN and Spolu together have 34 — Pražský deník/ČTK 9 Nov (link above) "Komunální volby v Praze vyhrálo hnutí STAN, získalo 17 mandátů ... Piráti získali sedm mandátů."
- All parties have ruled out ANO; the coalition will be STAN and Spolu, possibly with a third partner, Praha sobě or the Pirates; "rozhodnutí bude především na Starostech" — Pražský deník/ČTK 9 Nov (link above).
- Council seats offered: Praha sobě two (transport, property), Pirates one (property, housing); STAN would have five seats and the mayor, Spolu four with Praha sobě; STAN and Spolu five each with the Pirates — same article.
- 22 Oct: Hlaváček and Portlík said they would offer the third place to Praha sobě and, failing agreement, govern as two — https://prazska.drbna.cz/politika/903728-stan-a-spolu-jsou-v-praze-pripraveni-na-koalici-post-tretiho-nabidnou-praze-sobe.html (published 2026-10-22T13:35+02:00, read 2026-11-09T17:56Z) "Místo třetího v koalici nabídnou Praze sobě, pokud nebude souhlasit, jsou připraveni vytvořit ji ve dvou."
- 3 Nov: Hlaváček said talks with the Pirates were "o něco dál" than with Praha sobě; Scheinherr confirmed his club agrees to two council seats — ČTK https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/2895938 (read 2026-11-09T17:56Z).
- 6 Nov, after STAN–Spolu–Praha sobě talks: Scheinherr: "Myslím, že jsme dospěli k nějakému řešení finálního uspořádání, a to bude teď probráno na všech klubech"; Portlík: Spolu "respektuje rozhodující slovo hnutí STAN" — Blesk/ČTK (link above).
- Hlaváček (Blesk, 9 Nov): "Já o širší koalici stojím" — he wants a third partner; talks continue with both Pirates and Praha sobě (link above).
- Precedent: in 2022–23 the Prague talks lasted until early 2023 (watchlist background, cz-prague-mayor-stan).
