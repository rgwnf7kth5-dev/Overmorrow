- **Criterion:** YES if, between 09:00 Gulf time (UTC+4) on 10 Nov 2026 (= 06:00 Prague) and 23:59 Gulf time on 11 Nov 2026, at least one of the following, speaking on the record for one of the three governments (US: President, White House, State Department, Witkoff or Kushner; Russia: Kremlin incl. Statements by anonymous sources, media reports without an attributable official, mere "agreement to continue talks" or "to report to capitals", and announcements first made after 23:59 Gulf time on 11 Nov do not count. ## Risks
- Outcomes announced on 12 Nov or later (as with the 5 Mar announcement after Geneva) → NO here.
- Ambiguous wording ("agreed in principle to continue humanitarian exchanges", "next meeting before the end of the month") — the criterion needs one of the four listed items, with a calendar date for (b).
- Sources: only anonymous officials quoted (as in January) → does not count.
- A prisoner exchange happening in the window but not linked by any official to the talks → does not count under (a).
- European side meetings (E3, EU, NATO, Italy) are not parties to this criterion; agreements between Ukraine and Europeans do not count.
