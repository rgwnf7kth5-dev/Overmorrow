# uk-easyjet-vote-12
- **Claim:** easyJet shareholders approve Apollo's £7.15-a-share takeover on 11 November 2026: the scheme passes the court meeting and the special resolution passes the general meeting.
- **Criterion:** YES if easyJet's announcement of the results of the Court Meeting and General Meeting (RNS, published on 11 Nov 2026 by 23:59 London time; or the meeting results as reported by Reuters/Bloomberg/the Guardian citing that announcement) states that (a) the Scheme was approved at the Court Meeting by a majority in number of Scheme Shareholders voting, representing at least 75% in value of the Scheme Shares voted, and (b) the special resolution was passed at the General Meeting with at least 75% of votes cast. NO if either vote fails, or if either meeting is adjourned or not held on 11 Nov 2026. A late announcement of results of meetings held on 11 Nov leaves the claim pending, not NO.
- **Deadline:** Court Meeting 10:00 GMT, General Meeting 10:15 GMT (11:00/11:15 CET) on 2026-11-11; window 2026-11-10 06:00 CET to 2026-11-11 23:59 GMT
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-09T18:15Z

## Facts
- Required majorities: Court Meeting "a majority in number of the Scheme Shareholders ... who represent 75 per cent. or more in value of the Scheme Shares voted"; General Meeting "at least 75 per cent. of the votes cast" — scheme announcement above.
- Board recommendation: "the easyJet Directors unanimously recommend that easyJet Shareholders vote ... in favour of the Scheme at the Court Meeting and the Resolution at the General Meeting"; directors' irrevocables 429,985 shares (~0.06%); Evercore advises the Cash Offer is fair and reasonable; no recommendation on the unlisted Alternative Offer — scheme announcement above.
- Haji-Ioannou family concert party (incl. founder Sir Stelios) gave an irrevocable undertaking to support the deal for about 15.31% of issued share capital; offer £7.15 cash, ~£5.7bn, 25p above Castlelake's final £6.90 proposal, 81% premium to 394p on 28 May — https://www.aviationbusinessnews.com/industry-news/the-easyjet-private-equity-takeover-full-deal-details (published 2026-08-07, read 2026-11-09T17:54:41Z) "irrevocable undertaking to support the transaction in respect of approximately 15.31%"
- Opposition from employees, not shareholders: European Works Council opposes the deal; employee bodies in France, Portugal and Spain did not endorse it; UK and German bodies neutral on financial terms — Reuters via Euronext https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/apollos-easyjet-buyout-faces-union-pushback-ahead-first-quarter-2027-close (published 2026-10-15T16:40Z, read 2026-11-09T17:59:00Z); https://pipelineroad.com/news/20261015-apollo-s-5-7bn-easyjet-takeover-faces-opposition-from-europe (read 2026-11-09T17:54:19Z). Employee bodies have no vote at the meetings.
- Rollover shareholders' rights limited under the unlisted alternative (City AM, 13 Aug) — https://www.cityam.com/exclusive-easyjet-shareholder-rights-to-be-eroded-under-apollo-deal/ (read 2026-11-09T17:54:42Z). Concerns the alternative, not the cash vote.
- Turnout risk: EU ownership-and-control rules suspend voting rights of non-EU shares; AGM 2026 turnout "under 50%"; derivative positions (AQR, Citadel 2.7%+) reduce turnout; with low turnout a ~12.5% holder could block a 75% resolution; author does not expect these risks to blow up the deal — https://tompowdrill.substack.com/p/easyjet-takeover-and-votes (published 2026-07-23, read 2026-11-09T17:54:37Z). Written before Stelios gave his irrevocable.
- No public statement by an institutional shareholder or proxy adviser (ISS, Glass Lewis) opposing the scheme was found (WebSearch 9 Nov; Reuters/Euronext, PipelineRoad, Airways Magazine 19 Oct https://www.airwaysmag.com/new-post/easyjet-apollo-takeover-employee-opposition read 2026-11-09T17:59:01Z). Proxy adviser recommendations not found either way.
- Regulatory clearances (EU ownership and control, several states) outstanding; they are conditions for completion, not for the 11 Nov votes — Reuters/Euronext above.

## Risks
- Headcount test at the court meeting (majority in number) — historical share-splitting attempts (Dee Valley) were disregarded by the court (Powdrill piece).
- Low turnout magnifies a blocking minority; no blocking holder identified, and the largest holder is committed in favour.
- Whether the concert party votes at the same court meeting or is treated as a separate class (it elected/intends the unlisted alternative per sweep notes): the announcement mentions one Court Meeting only; if excluded, approval depends on the remaining holders.
