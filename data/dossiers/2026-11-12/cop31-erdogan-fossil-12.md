## Facts
- Erdoğan, 9 Nov (transcript from his office, via Reuters/Al-Monitor): "Our goal is to make this summit a platform where concrete results are produced, where countries turn their pledges into action" — https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/cop31-summit-leaders-urge-crisis-wracked-world-move-faster-climate (published 2026-11-09T16:13Z, read 2026-11-09T17:53Z). The quoted passage does not mention fossil fuels.

- COP31 President Kurum (Thursday 5 Nov): "everyone now understands that energy that you import is energy that you do not control. Electrification is the answer" — Al Jazeera/AFP/Reuters https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/9/turkiye-pushes-electrification-top-of-climate-agenda-as-cop31-set-to-open (published 2026-11-09T05:02Z, read 2026-11-09T17:53Z). Kurum has said electrification will "automatically" reduce fossil-fuel use — Climate Home briefing https://www.climatechangenews.com/2026/11/05/cop31-briefing-what-to-expect-from-the-antalya-climate-summit/.
