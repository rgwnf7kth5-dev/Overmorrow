# cop31-guterres-fossil-12
- **Claim:** In his address at the opening of the COP31 Leaders' Summit in Antalya on Wednesday 11 November 2026, UN Secretary-General António Guterres explicitly calls for moving away from fossil fuels.
- **Criterion:** YES if Guterres delivers an address (in person or by video) to the Antalya Leaders' Summit on 11 Nov 2026 (Antalya time, UTC+3) and that address, as published by the UN (un.org/sg statements, UN News, UN press release) or as shown on the UNFCCC webcast or quoted by a major news agency or outlet, contains an explicit call to transition away from, phase out, phase down, move away from or end fossil fuels or their expansion. NO if he speaks that day and the address contains no such call, or if he does not address the summit on 11 Nov. A mention of fossil fuels only in another sense (for example as a statistic) without such a call does not count.
