# cz-letna-next-16
- **Claim:** At its Letná rally on Sunday 15 November 2026, Milion chvilek announces a further protest action with a specific date.
- **Criterion:** YES if by 23:59 Prague time on 15 Nov 2026 Milion chvilek pro demokracii (from the Letná stage, or on its website, official social accounts or in a statement reported by ČTK or ČT24 that day) announces at least one further collective protest action that it calls or co-calls (a demonstration or rally in Prague or elsewhere, coordinated protests in several towns, a march, a strike or warning strike, or a similar public protest) together with a specific calendar day after 15 Nov 2026 (a stated date, or a day that can be identified, e.g. "next Sunday", "on 10 December, the day the committee meets"). Not enough: a month, season or period only ("in December", "before the second reading"); an ultimatum or deadline without a dated action; petitions, letters, online or individual actions (contacting MPs, joining parties); invitations to attend 17 November commemorations organised by others; actions announced only by other groups (students, unions, Veřejnoprávně). A protest gathering or march on 17 Nov that Milion chvilek itself calls counts. NO if no such announcement is made by 23:59 on 15 Nov or the rally does not take place on 15 Nov.

## Risks
- "Next steps" may be organisational (local groups, petitions, a "network" of towns, campaign before elections) without a date; regional protests may be announced as "in the coming weeks".
- A date may be given only after the rally (on Monday or later), as in Dec 2019 — would be NO.
- Ambiguity over 17 Nov: a call to "come to Národní třída on 17 Nov" may be an invitation to commemorations (not counted) rather than a Milion chvilek protest (counted); resolver must read the wording.
- An action called jointly with students/unions counts only if Milion chvilek calls or co-calls it.
