# nl-vvd-exit-16
- **Claim:** Between 00:00 on Saturday 14 and 23:59 on Sunday 15 November 2026 (Amsterdam time), the VVD officially announces that it is leaving the Jetten cabinet, or Prime Minister Rob Jetten offers the resignation of the cabinet (or of the VVD's ministers) to the King.
- **Criterion:** YES if, in the window 2026-11-14T00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-15T23:59+01:00, either (a) VVD leader Dilan Yeşilgöz, the VVD parliamentary group or the VVD party officially and on the record announces that the VVD withdraws from the coalition / that its ministers and state secretaries leave the cabinet, or (b) the government (rijksoverheid.nl, the Government Information Service, or the Prime Minister on the record) announces that the resignation of the whole cabinet or of the VVD members of the cabinet has been offered to the King. NOS or ANP reporting of such an on-the-record announcement suffices; anonymous "sources say" reports do not. NO if neither happens in the window — including if the three parties announce a continuation/"doorstart", if talks are still going on at 23:59 Sunday, or if the VVD only sets conditions. An announcement made before 14 Nov 00:00 falls outside the window (the morning editor must then replace the claim).
- **Event window:** 2026-11-14T00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-15T23:59+01:00 (Europe/Amsterdam)
- **Deadline (resolves_by):** 2026-11-17T05:00+01:00

## Base rate
- No reference-data class. Own count of recent Dutch coalition crises that came to leader-level crisis talks: the cabinets that fell did so within about 0–2 days of the peak — Rutte IV (fell 7 Jul 2023 after talks over several days; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Rutte_cabinet, read 2026-11-13T17:58Z), Schoof (PVV left 3 Jun 2025; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schoof_cabinet, read 2026-11-13T17:58Z); earlier falls (Balkenende I 2002, II 2006, IV 2010; Rutte I 2012) from general knowledge, not re-opened. Crises that ended in a continuation exist too (e.g., Rutte II 2015, ministers resigned but cabinet stayed) — not counted systematically, so no rate is given. The related class "coalition-deal-announced-short-window" (own record 3/15 yes, base 0.30, pipeline/reference-classes.json) concerns deals, not exits.
- Key structural point for the window: if the VVD decides to leave, the decision may come on Friday night (before the window) — then the claim is moot; if the talks carry over, a weekend announcement is plausible.

- Middle outcomes: VVD stays but demands renegotiation; a single VVD minister (e.g., Heinen) resigns without the party leaving — NOT a YES unless all VVD members leave or a cabinet/VVD resignation is offered to the King.
- Wording: "the VVD pulls its support" in a sources-based report is not enough; needs on-the-record statement.
