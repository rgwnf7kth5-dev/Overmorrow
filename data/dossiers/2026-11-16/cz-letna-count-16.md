# cz-letna-count-16
- **Claim:** Milion chvilek announces that at least 300,000 people attended its "Letná proti 4:36" rally in Prague on Sunday 15 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if the first attendance figure for the rally that Milion chvilek pro demokracii announces by 23:59 Prague time on 15 Nov 2026 (from the stage, by Mikuláš Minář or another organiser, or on the association's website, official social accounts or in a statement to the media) is 300,000 or more. A range counts at its lower bound; "over / at least / about 300,000" counts as 300,000. An interim figure explicitly given as partial while people are still arriving ("už teď je nás X") is disregarded if the organisers give a further figure by 23:59; otherwise it counts. Comparisons without a number ("more than in 2019", "the largest since 1989") are not a figure. Police, mobile-operator (T-Mobile, O2, Vodafone), media or expert estimates do not count. NO if that figure is below 300,000, if the organisers announce no figure by 23:59 on 15 Nov, or if the rally does not take place on 15 Nov.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-15T13:00+01:00 to 2026-11-15T23:59+01:00 (Europe/Prague)
- **Evidence expected:** figure announced from the stage during the rally (14:00 to about 16:30–17:00); reported live by ČTK, ČT24, Seznam Zprávy, Novinky. evidence_at 2026-11-15T18:00+01:00
