## Risks
- Tit-for-tat after the Sirik/Bahrain exchange could bring more Iranian drone launches (US interceptions count) or US "defensive" strikes; Belaili says retaliation without limits will come "at the appropriate time".
- Trump calls strikes "quite unlikely" and says Gulf states want the status quo; APEC summit with Xi and Putin next week may restrain both sides.
- CENTCOM often does not announce small strikes ("limited, unannounced strikes", CTP-ISW 3 Nov); Trump confirms loosely and late, sometimes without a date. The resolver must place the action in the Tehran day.
- CENTCOM denials (as with the Bahrain radar) can be mistaken for confirmations; only a US statement that US forces acted counts.
- Blockade enforcement: only weapons fire on a blockade-runner counts; CENTCOM has disabled five ships since July (per us-iran-ship dossiers), so this path is infrequent.
