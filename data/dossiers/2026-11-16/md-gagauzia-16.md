# md-gagauzia-16
- **Claim:** In the first round of the election of Gagauzia's Bashkan (governor) on Sunday 15 November 2026, Alexandr Stoianoglo finishes first, more than 10 percentage points ahead of the runner-up.
- **Criterion:** YES if the preliminary results of the 15 Nov 2026 Bashkan first round published by Moldova's Central Electoral Commission (CEC, pv.cec.md / cec.md) or the Central Electoral Council of Gagauzia, with all or nearly all (at least 95%) polling-station protocols processed, show Alexandr Stoianoglo with the highest share of valid votes and his share minus the second-placed candidate's share greater than 10.0 percentage points. Final CEC results take precedence if published before the resolver reads. NO if he is not first, if his lead is 10.0 points or less, if the first round is not held on 15 Nov 2026, or if it is declared invalid (turnout below the legal threshold).
- **Event window:** polling 2026-11-15T07:00+02:00 to 21:00+02:00 (Europe/Chisinau; hours standard for Moldovan elections, not confirmed for this vote); window closes 2026-11-15T23:59+02:00; preliminary results overnight; evidence by 2026-11-16T09:00+02:00; resolver reads 2026-11-17T05:00+01:00.

## Facts
- Eight registered candidates: Fiodor Gagauz (PSRM; MP 2014–2025), Alexandr Stoianoglo (independent; MP, ex-Prosecutor General 2019–2021, 2024 national presidential runoff candidate), Cornel Dudnic (independent; ex-MP), Ivan Cîlcic (Gagauz People's Party, PPG), Petru Vlah (New Historical Option, NOI), Ivanna Koksal (independent; MP 2019–2025), Serghei Anastasov (independent; mayor of Comrat since 2015), Oleg Garizan (independent; mayor of Copceac, ex-MP) — Wikipedia (as above), citing CEC registration decisions on cece.cec.md.
- PSRM backs Fiodor Gagauz after Grigorii Uzun (2023 runoff candidate) withdrew on 21 Sep because the new rule requires Bashkan candidates to speak Romanian and Gagauz — Wikipedia (as above) "on 24 September, the PSRM announced it would back Fiodor Gagauz".
- PAS and other pro-European parties field no candidate — Wikipedia (as above). PAS leader Igor Grosu denied that Stoianoglo is PAS's hidden candidate — Radio Moldova https://radiomoldova.md/p/84770 (published 2026-09-10 07:54Z, read 2026-11-13T17:55Z) "Clearly, that is false."
- Evghenia Guțul (Bashkan elected 2023, Șor bloc) was finally convicted (7 years, illegal financing related to the Șor party); vacancy confirmed 17 Aug 2026 — Radio Moldova p/84770 "Bashkan's position became vacant after Evghenia Guțul's final conviction this summer"; Wikipedia (as above).
- In the 2024 national presidential runoff Stoianoglo took 97.04% in Gagauzia — BFM https://www.bfm.ru/news/561302 (published 2024-11-04, read 2026-11-13T17:55Z) "получил в Гагаузии 97,04% голосов"; Atlantic Council https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/blogs/ukrainealert/moldovas-fight-against-kremlin-interference-faces-gagauzia-election-test/ (published 2026-10-21, read 2026-11-13T17:54Z) "took 97 percent of the region's vote".
- **Polls (IMAS, three waves; the only pollster found):**
  - Wave 1, 11–19 Sep 2026, n=818, share of respondents with a preference (decided base, as tabulated by Wikipedia): Stoianoglo 47.4, Grigorii Uzun 16.4 (later withdrew), Koksal 8.3, Anastasov 6.6, Gagauz 4.7 — Wikipedia (as above).
  - Wave 2, 17–25 Oct 2026, n=825, decided base: Stoianoglo 45.5, Gagauz 18.1, Koksal 15.0, Anastasov 9.5, Garizan 5.1, Vlah 3.3, Dudnic 1.9, Cîlcic 1.7 (lead 27.4) — Wikipedia (as above), citing Nokta 27 Oct "Стояногло лидирует в опросе … но 30% избирателей не определились".
  - Wave 3, published 12 Nov 2026 (fieldwork dates and sample not given in the report read), **all respondents** base: Stoianoglo 26.5, Gagauz 17.5, Koksal 9.6; "more than 27%" undecided; 71% say they will vote; series for all respondents: Stoianoglo 32.4 → 29.2 → 26.5, Gagauz 3.2 → 11.6 → 17.5 — Logos Press https://logos-pres.md/ru/novosti/gagauziya-nakanune-vyborov-stoyanoglo-i-gagauz-lidiruyut-v-oprosah/ (published 2026-11-13 17:22+02:00, read 2026-11-13T17:55Z) "26,5% респондентов намерены поддержать Александра Стояногло, на втором месте Федор Гагауз – 17,5%".
  - Conversion: waves 1–2 all-respondent figures are 0.68 and 0.64 of the decided-base figures (29.2/45.5, 11.6/18.1 = 0.64). If 66–73% of wave-3 respondents are decided, the decided-base shares are Stoianoglo ≈36–40, Gagauz ≈24–27, Koksal ≈13–15; lead ≈12.3–13.6 points (all-respondent lead 9.0). Lead on decided base by wave: 42.7 (vs Uzun 31.0) → 27.4 → ≈13.
  - Same wave: 44% think the Bashkan election will not be fair and free, 40% that it will; respondents named Gagauz's campaign the most active (29.5% vs Stoianoglo 18.7%) — Logos Press (as above).
- TikTok campaign: Radio Europa Liberă and WatchDog.MD reported dozens of new, near-identical TikTok accounts promoting Fiodor Gagauz (≈150,000 views by 23 Oct); Gagauz and PSRM deny involvement — Wikipedia (as above), citing REL 23 Oct and NewsMaker 27 Oct.
- 2023 Bashkan election: round 1 (16 Apr 2023) Guțul 26.47%, Uzun 26.40%, Petrov 16.24%, Croitor 10.35%, Dudoglo 8.99% (turnout 61.70% of 92,563); runoff (30 Apr) Guțul 52.34%, Uzun 47.66% (turnout 57.99%) — Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Gagauz_gubernatorial_election (modified 2026-10-23, read 2026-11-13T17:54Z). ECFR estimated €4m spent to buy 27,000 votes for Guțul — Wikipedia 2026 page (as above).
- Validity threshold: under the Gagauz rules used in 2023, the election was valid only if at least 50% of registered voters took part — g4media (as above) "Alegerile sunt considerate valabile dacă la ele participă nu mai puțin de 50% din alegătorii înscriși pe liste." The Electoral Code amendments of Aug 2026 (Law 192/2026) moved the Gagauz elections under the national code; the threshold now in force was not confirmed tonight (searched; not found).
- Constitutional Court ruled national legislation takes precedence and the CEC may organise the vote under it; 9 July 2026 ruling curtailed Gagauz powers (Stoianoglo called it a "political mistake") — Wikipedia (as above); sweep note citing Logos Press 7 Oct.

## Base rate
- No reference class for a first-round margin in a regional election. Comparable own count: Gagauz Bashkan first rounds since 2015 with a clear frontrunner — 2015 and 2019 decided in round one, 2023 a near-tie (0.07 pt) — not re-read tonight; weak.
- Poll error (pipeline/reference-data/poll-error.json): pooled lead-error rms 2.73 pp across 6 national elections (final-week polls); Czech parliamentary rms 3.63. Here: one pollster, a single wave whose fieldwork ended an unknown number of days before the vote, 27%+ undecided, and a lead that fell about 15 points per wave — error should be well above those national figures.

## Risks
- Momentum: Gagauz gained ≈6 points (all-respondent) per wave and Stoianoglo lost ≈3; on the trend the lead on election day may be below the last measured ≈13.
- Undecided voters (27%+) and organised mobilisation/vote buying (2023 precedent) can favour the candidate backed by a party machine (PSRM).
- Possible late withdrawals in favour of one candidate (none found as of 13 Nov).
- Disputes over the Romanian-language rule and the CEC's role may feed a contestation narrative after the vote; preliminary vs final count differences are normally small.
- Turnout threshold under the new rules not confirmed; if the round is declared invalid the claim resolves NO.
