## Risks
- A date could be published on Friday evening 13 Nov (after writing, before the window): the desk should check at freeze; if a date is already public, the claim is moot and should be dropped.
- Venue-only or "next week" statements do not count; the resolver needs a specific day.
- Anonymous-source reports of a date (Axios, Reuters) do not count; on-record confirmation often lags.
- A date for a bilateral US–Russia or Europe–Russia contact is not the next round; a technical/working-group meeting counts only if officials describe it as the next round of the US–Russia–Ukraine talks.
