# cz-ccyb-26
- **Claim:** At its financial-stability meeting on Wednesday 25 November 2026 the ČNB Bank Board leaves the countercyclical capital buffer (CCyB) rate for the Czech Republic at 1.5%.
- **Criterion:** YES if the CCyB decision taken by the ČNB Bank Board at its financial-stability meeting held on 25 Nov 2026 (Europe/Prague) leaves the announced CCyB rate unchanged at 1.5% (no new rate set, neither higher nor lower), as stated in the ČNB's decision/press release on cnb.cz (normally 15:00 on the meeting day; publication on 26 Nov also counts) or reported by ČTK. NO if the board sets a different CCyB rate at that meeting, or if no financial-stability meeting/decision takes place on 25 Nov 2026. Decisions on other instruments (LTV/DTI/DSTI, systemic risk buffer) do not affect the result.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-25T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-25T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Prague)
- **Evidence expected:** ČNB decision on cnb.cz at 15:00 on 25 Nov 2026 (press conference with the autumn Financial Stability Report: page says 26 Nov from 15:00)
- **Resolves by:** 2026-11-27T05:00:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-23T18:00:00Z

## Facts
- Current state: rate applied now 1.25%; 1.5% applies from 1 Jul 2027 — ČNB CCyB page https://www.cnb.cz/cs/financni-stabilita/makroobezretnostni-politika/proticyklicka-kapitalova-rezerva/ (read 2026-11-23T17:51:01Z) "aktuální sazba: 1,25 % / sazba od 1. 7. 2027: 1,5 %". So "keeps at 1.5%" means no new provision changing the announced 1.5%.
- Oddity on the same Czech page: a third line reads "sazba uplatňovaná od D. měsíc RRRR: 1,75 %" (template placeholder date with a value of 1.75%); the English page shows the same line as "Rate valid from D MONTH YYYY: X%" — https://www.cnb.cz/en/financial-stability/macroprudential-policy/the-countercyclical-capital-buffer/ (read 2026-11-23T17:51Z). It may be a leftover template line or a pre-staged page; it is not an announcement.
- 4 Jun 2026 decision: CCyB +25bp to 1.5% from 1 Jul 2027; mortgage rules and SyRB unchanged — https://www.cnb.cz/cs/financni-stabilita/rozhodnuti-bankovni-rady-cnb/Rozhodnuti-bankovni-rady-CNB-v-oblasti-financni-stability-1780578000000 (decision of 4 Jun 2026, read 2026-11-23T17:51Z) "Důvodem je zejména silná a plošná úvěrová aktivita, růst zadluženosti domácností i podniků a pokračující růst cen bytů".
- June 2026 minutes: unanimous 6–0 for the hike (Michl, Zamrazilová, Frait, Kubelková, Procházka, Seidler); uncertainty "komplikuje časování a kalibraci tohoto kroku"; LTV 80% (90% under 36) kept 6–0, DTI/DSTI stay deactivated; Frait and Seidler: activating DTI/DSTI could become "nevyhnutelnou reakcí" if standards loosen for longer — https://www.cnb.cz/cs/financni-stabilita/rozhodnuti-bankovni-rady-cnb/Rozhodnuti-bankovni-rady-CNB-v-oblasti-financni-stability-1780578000000/?tab=record (minutes of 4 Jun 2026, read 2026-11-23T17:53Z) "Pro toto rozhodnutí hlasovalo všech šest přítomných členů bankovní rady".

- September 2026 (Q3) decision: CCyB kept at 1.5% — press release URL above (read 2026-11-23T17:51Z) "je bankovní rada připravena sazbu proticyklické kapitálové rezervy dále zvýšit, přičemž pravděpodobnost tohoto kroku vzrostla"; reasons to hold: "další vývoj finančního cyklu je obestřen značnou mírou nejistoty", ČNB "nedávno zpřísnila nastavení domácí makroobezřetnostní a měnové politiky", debt "zůstává z historického pohledu umírněná", banks not loosening standards.

## Base rate
- Class: ČNB quarterly CCyB decisions since 2023, hold vs change. From ČNB provisions (OOP) and press releases: changes II/2023 (2.25% from 1 Jul 2023), III/2023 (2% from 1 Oct 2023), I/2024 (1.75% from 1 Apr 2024), II/2024 (1.25% from 1 Jul 2024), I/2026 (1.5% from 1 Jul 2027, decided 4 Jun 2026) — OOP pages under https://www.cnb.cz/cs/financni-stabilita/makroobezretnostni-politika/proticyklicka-kapitalova-rezerva/opatreni-obecne-povahy-ke-stanoveni-sazby-proticyklicke-kapitalove-rezervy/ (read 2026-11-23T17:54Z). Confirmed holds: Nov 2025, Mar 2026, Sep 2026 (press releases above); the rate stayed 1.25% from Jul 2024 until the June 2026 decision (quarterly meetings in between, e.g. 27 Nov 2024 and 11 Sep 2025 per minutes titles in search results, were holds by implication).
- Approximate count Q1 2023–Q3 2026: about 15 quarterly decisions, 5 changes, about 10 holds → hold rate ≈ 0.67 (n ≈ 15; dates of the 2023 decisions not individually confirmed). Since the cut to 1.25% (mid-2024): 1 change in about 9 decisions.
- After a "probability rose" signal at a hold (Mar 2026), the next meeting hiked (Jun 2026): n = 1. Same signal given in Sep 2026.
- In the current upswing, ČNB raised CCyB at a big (FSR) meeting, not a quarterly per rollam one.

## Risks
- For a hike (NO): explicit forward signal in Sep ("readiness to raise further"); strong credit growth, house prices, debt growth; the same signal in Mar 2026 was followed by a hike; template line "1,75 %" on the ČNB page.
- For a hold (YES): 25bp CCyB hike from June only applies from Jul 2027; base rates raised in June and November 2026; long rates up, mortgage activity cooling (5 Nov minutes); energy shock and geopolitical uncertainty; board split on monetary policy (3 cautious members).
- A cut is very unlikely given the communication; a different decision on mortgage rules (e.g. DTI/DSTI activation) does not count for this claim.
