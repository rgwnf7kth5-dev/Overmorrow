# ua-drones-26
- **Claim (binary):** According to the Ukrainian Air Force's first morning summary on Wednesday 25 November 2026, Russia launched at least 150 drones at Ukraine in the night of 24–25 November (from 18:00 Kyiv time on 24 November).
- **Criterion:** YES if the Ukrainian Air Force's first summary of the overnight attack published on Wed 25 Nov 2026 (Kyiv time), covering the attack counted from 18:00 Kyiv time on 24 Nov, gives a UAV total of 150 or more. Source: the Air Force Telegram channel (t.me/kpszsu) or Facebook page, or Ukrinform, Ukrainska Pravda, Kyiv Independent or Interfax-Ukraine repeating it. "Drones" means strike UAVs as the summary counts them: Shahed-type and jet-powered Shaheds, Gerbera, Parodiya and other decoys, and any jet munitions such as Banderol/Dan-T that the summary includes in its UAV total. Missiles listed separately are not counted. A summary issued late in the morning or around noon still counts as the first summary. Separate daytime reports and later corrections do not count. NO if the total is 149 or fewer, or if the Air Force reports that no drones were launched that night. If no summary for that night has been published, the claim stays pending. (Same counting rules as ua-drones-25, shifted by one day; threshold moved from 130 to 150, the median of the last 14 nights.) The claim counts weapons launched, not casualties or damage.
- **Event window:** attack 2026-11-24T18:00:00+02:00 to 2026-11-25T12:00:00+02:00 (Europe/Kyiv, EET). Summary normally 08:00–10:00 Kyiv; after very large attacks up to about 12:00.
