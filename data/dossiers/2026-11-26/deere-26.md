- Comparison base: fiscal 2026 actual includes ~$382m+ tariff recoveries; a strong Q4 raises the bar the 2027 outlook must clear.
- Deere habitually guides conservatively and raises during the year (FY2026: $4.0–4.75bn → $4.75–5.0bn), which argues for a cautious first figure.
- Tariff costs rising to ~$1bn in 2027; South America and Europe weak.
- Format: Deere has given a net income range in every recent Q4 release; if it gives only EPS or qualitative guidance the criterion resolves NO.
