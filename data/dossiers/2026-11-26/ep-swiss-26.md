# ep-swiss-26
- **Claim:** On Wednesday 25 November 2026 the European Parliament gives its consent to the EU–Switzerland package of agreements with at least 550 votes in favour.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 and 23:59 on Wed 25 Nov 2026 (Europe/Paris, Strasbourg), the plenary votes on the recommendation for consent to the EU–Switzerland broad package of agreements (procedure 2025/0162(NLE), report A10-0236/2026, rapporteur Christophe Grudler), consent is given, and that vote records 550 or more votes in favour, as announced in plenary and shown in Parliament's press release, the minutes / roll-call results (doceo PV, RCV) or HowTheyVote.eu. If consent is put to several separate votes (e.g. one per agreement or Council decision), YES only if every one of them records 550 or more in favour. NO if any consent vote records fewer than 550 in favour, if consent is refused, or if the consent vote is not held on 25 Nov. The vote on the accompanying interim-report resolution (2025/0162R(NLE), A10-0235/2026) does not count; later "corrections to votes" do not change the result. A report published after 23:59 about a vote held in the window counts.

## Facts
- **Majority required:** EP newsletter (above) says consent needs an "absolute majority of its members" — 361 of 720. The legal basis for that requirement was not confirmed on a treaty or Rules-of-Procedure page tonight (general consent under Art. 218(6) TFEU is normally by a majority of votes cast; the EP's own statement is used here). The claim threshold (550) is above 361, so the distinction does not affect YES/NO.

## Risks
- Opposition sources: PfE (incl. FPÖ, whose Vilimsky is shadow), ESN (AfD) and some NI may oppose free movement / dynamic alignment; group positions for the plenary were not found tonight (possible, not reported). In the Swiss PNR vote 75 voted against and 30 abstained → 549.
- Several separate consent votes (one per agreement) would require all to clear 550; the electricity or free-movement agreement could draw more opposition than the others.
- Attendance at midday Wednesday votes is usually high; a low-turnout vote (<640) makes 550 hard even with ~85% support.
