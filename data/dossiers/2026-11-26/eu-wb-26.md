# eu-wb-26

## Facts
- **A draft declaration exists and is meant to be adopted at the end:** "Dokument deklaracije, u koju je RSE imao uvid, bi trebalo da bude usvojen na kraju samita." … "Deklaraciju usvajaju lideri EU, a učesnici iz regiona se s njom usklađuju." — RFE/RL Balkan Service https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/eu-samit-zapadni-balkan-deklaracija/33900296.html (published 2026-11-17T11:15Z, read 2026-11-23T17:53:14Z). Draft content: Belgrade–Pristina normalisation ("All agreements reached in the EU-facilitated Belgrade-Pristina Dialogue must be implemented"), Growth Plan support "conditioned on the partners' constructive engagement … in the normalization of their relations", condemnation of glorification of war criminals and genocide denial, CFSP/sanctions alignment paragraph, "Enlargement is a realistic possibility, which should be seized".

The current draft has sharpened language on both issues" — RFE/RL, Rikard Jozwiak, https://www.rferl.org/a/wider-europe-briefing-jozwiak-western-balkans-enlargement/33899782.html (published 2026-11-17T11:34Z, read 2026-11-23T17:53:14Z). Acting President and Speaker Aleksandar Čupić declined Costa's invitation (received Friday 20 Nov) because the parliament session continues on 24 Nov and he took office only on Thursday; he says "he found understanding for his position among EU representatives"; government is in a technical mandate — EWB https://europeanwesternbalkans.com/2026/11/23/cupic-explains-why-he-will-not-attend-upcoming-eu-western-balkans-summit/ (published 2026-11-23T10:42Z, read 2026-11-23T17:53:05Z). RFE/RL (17 Nov) said caretaker PM Đuro Macut might represent Serbia if no new cabinet is sworn in; who attends is unconfirmed.

Montenegro reached 25 of 33 closed chapters (~19 Nov; scout, EWB related list); expected to be its last summit as a candidate (RFE/RL Balkan Service above).

## Risks
- The text is EU leaders' declaration "in consultation with" partners; in Dec 2025 it was adopted without Serbia present. But in June 2026 a single partner's refusal (Serbia) stopped it — so a Serbian representative at the table (e.g. caretaker PM) objecting to the sharper sanctions/dialogue language could again block it.
- With a caretaker Serbian government and an acting president absent, Belgrade may simply not attend at head-of-government level, which in December 2025 let the declaration pass.
- Other partners: North Macedonia (Bulgaria/Greece agreements language), Kosovo (normalisation conditionality) could object; no report of objections found.
- Within the EU, Hungary's position on enlargement language: no report found.
- Resolution risk: a "chair's statement" or "Costa's conclusions" instead of a joint declaration counts NO under the criterion.
