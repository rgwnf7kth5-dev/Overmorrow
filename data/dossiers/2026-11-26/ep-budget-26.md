# ep-budget-26
- **Claim:** On Wednesday 25 November 2026 the European Parliament approves the joint text of the 2027 EU budget with at least 420 votes in favour.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 and 23:59 on Wed 25 Nov 2026 (Europe/Paris, Strasbourg), the plenary approves the conciliation joint text of the 2027 general budget (procedure 2026/0196(BUD), report A10-0305/2026, Hadjipantela/Ušakovs) and the vote on the joint text records 420 or more votes in favour, as announced in plenary and shown in Parliament's press release, the minutes / roll-call results (doceo PV, RCV) or HowTheyVote.eu. NO if fewer than 420 vote in favour, if the joint text is rejected, or if no vote on it is held on 25 Nov. Later "corrections to votes" filed by MEPs do not change the result; votes on draft amending budget 3/2026 or other items do not count. A report published after 23:59 about a vote held in the window counts.
