## Risks
- Base-month effect: the October 2025 PCE price level was estimated by BEA without regular October 2025 CPI collection (2025 shutdown); how BEA treated that month (and whether it is revised in this release) changes the y/y figure. Not confirmed tonight how BEA's October 2025 core index was constructed.
- Rounding: consensus 3.2 sits between 3.15 and 3.25; a hot unrounded m/m (0.3x) or revisions to August–September can push to 3.3.
- Non-CPI components (portfolio-management fees tied to equity prices; healthcare and airfares from PPI) can move core PCE away from what CPI implies.
- Media may lead with m/m or headline PCE; criterion is core y/y only.
