## Facts
- Slip history (three dates so far): Fri 20 Nov noon CT (Army advisory) → Mon 23 Nov (acting Army Secretary Adam Telle, 19 Nov: "I have directed Army Materiel Command to postpone tomorrow's controlled detonation … until Monday, November 23"; Monday is the "latest possible moment prior to a forecasted temperature drop") — ABC News https://abcnews.com/US/iowa-army-plant-lead-azide-detonation/story?id=138261613 (published 2026-11-20T15:14Z, read 2026-11-23T17:50:58Z) → Tue 24 Nov (DOJ court filing Sat 21 Nov) → Wed 25 Nov (Army post Sun 22 Nov) — BBC URL above "In its court filing on Saturday, the justice department said the army would carry out the detonation on Tuesday."
- Reasons for slips: first, public communication and pressure from officials (Grassley: delay "to further educate the public" — ABC); then the court stay and the move to an emergency exemption; latest, "optimal weather conditions and all mitigation measures" (BBC).
- Litigation: Earthjustice for the Iowa Environmental Council and Sierra Club Iowa Chapter filed on 19 Nov in the **US Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit**, challenging the EPA emergency RCRA permit (granted 13 Nov) — Earthjustice https://earthjustice.org/press/2026/groups-sue-to-halt-planned-detonation-at-ammunition-plant-in-iowa (published 2026-11-19T20:42Z, read 2026-11-23T17:50:59Z) "The complaint filed in the Eighth Circuit Court of Appeals highlights EPA's failure to require the Army to consider safer treatment alternatives".
- The Eighth Circuit granted an administrative stay (pause) on Fri 20 Nov — Al Jazeera https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/23/extremely-concerning-inside-a-us-army-plan-to-detonate-iowa-lead-stores (published 2026-11-23T02:09Z, read 2026-11-23T17:50:33Z) "The US Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit granted a pause on Friday."
- Government position: DOJ filing (Sat 21 Nov) says the Army may proceed without the permit under the RCRA emergency exception: "The emergency exception does not depend on the Emergency Permit and is therefore not affected by, nor contradictory to, the terms of this Court's administrative stay" (Al Jazeera); "There is an immediate risk that the lead azide … will explode as temperatures are expected to drop below 30 degrees Fahrenheit (-1C) in the next several days" (BBC).
- Plaintiffs: filed a new motion on Sun 22 Nov asking the court "to pause proceedings so that it may submit additional information before the scheduled detonation" (BBC). No ruling on it, no hearing date and no temporary restraining order proceeding found as of 2026-11-23T17:55Z. The case is a petition in the appellate court, not a district-court TRO.
- Local officials: Middletown Mayor Rodney Johnson (23 Nov) says the detonation "must take place … if we don't take this opportunity to Safely dispose of the problem, then actual lives may be at stake" (BBC). Iowa's newly elected Democratic Senator Josh Turek calls for a full probe (BBC). Republican Senator Chuck Grassley raised concerns, pressed for more communication (ABC). Axios Des Moines headline 19 Nov (search snippet only; site refused scripted reading): "Iowa leaders ask Army to delay planned lead detonation" (Rob Sand, Josh Turek) — https://axios.com/local/des-moines/2026/11/19/iowa-middletown-lead-azide-rob-sand-josh-turek. Rep. Eric Sorensen (meteorologist) said NWS forecasts did not offer favourable conditions (inversion, light winds) over the coming week (Al Jazeera).
- Material: 68 barrels, about 9,700 lb (4.4 t) of lead azide, stored ~20 years; recrystallised after the water/ethanol mixture evaporated; found in March 2026 — Al Jazeera; ABC (Army Sustainment Command spokesperson Justine Barati). Two rapid detonations planned inside earth-covered concrete magazines with sand barriers (ABC).
- Weather: NWS forecast for Middletown area (gridpoint DVN 45,34, updated 2026-11-23T17:26Z, read 17:53Z) — Tuesday 38°F, NE 5 mph, mostly cloudy; **Wednesday 41°F, NE 5–10 mph, mostly cloudy**; Wednesday night 28°F, light snow likely; Thanksgiving (Thu) chance of rain and snow — https://api.weather.gov/gridpoints/DVN/45,34/forecast.
