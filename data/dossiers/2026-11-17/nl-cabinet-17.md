# nl-cabinet-17
- **Claim:** Between 06:00 on Sunday 15 and 23:59 on Monday 16 November 2026 (Amsterdam time), D66, VVD and CDA announce on the record that they have reached agreement to continue the Jetten cabinet together.
- **Criterion:** YES if, in the window 2026-11-15T06:00+01:00 to 2026-11-16T23:59+01:00, Prime Minister Rob Jetten (or the government via rijksoverheid.nl / the Government Information Service) states on the record that D66, VVD and CDA have reached agreement / that the three parties continue the cabinet together, AND VVD leader Dilan Yeşilgöz (or the VVD officially) confirms on the record that the VVD stays in the cabinet (a joint press appearance or joint statement counts for both). An agreement that leaves details to be worked out later, or that includes inviting a fourth party into the cabinet or a support arrangement, counts, provided the three parties say they carry on together. NOS or ANP reporting of such on-the-record statements suffices; anonymous "sources say" reports do not. NO if no such announcement is made in the window — including if the talks are adjourned or merely "continue", if the VVD announces it leaves, or if the resignation of the cabinet or of VVD members is offered to the King. An agreement announced before 06:00 on 15 Nov falls outside the window (the morning editor must then replace the claim).
- **Event window:** 2026-11-15T06:00+01:00 to 2026-11-16T23:59+01:00 (Europe/Amsterdam)
- **Evidence expected:** press statements after the talks at De Zwaluwenberg (Sunday) or in The Hague (Monday); NOS/ANP live. evidence_at 2026-11-16T22:00+01:00
- **Deadline (resolves_by):** 2026-11-18T05:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-14T18:05Z (19:05 Amsterdam). Talks adjourned Saturday evening and resume Sunday morning; the morning editor must re-read NOS before the freeze.

## Facts
- Background: Tax plan 2027 passed the Tweede Kamer Thu 12 Nov 76–74 after D66 and CDA backed a PRO/Volt amendment (box-3 top rate 38.5% above €60,000 return; ~€1bn shift from income tax to wealth tax); VVD voted against — NRC liveblog (above) "Mensen met grote vermogens zullen iets zwaarder worden belast"; NOS https://nos.nl/artikel/2639225-belastingplan-aangenomen-en-nu-is-de-vraag-wat-de-vvd-gaat-doen (published 2026-11-12 22:50, read 2026-11-13).
- Fri 13 Nov: eight hours of talks at the Ministry of General Affairs ended without a solution; cabinet stays "for now" — NOS https://nos.nl/artikel/2639364-kabinet-zit-er-voorlopig-nog-maar-crisissfeer-blijft (published 2026-11-13 19:30, read 2026-11-14T17:54Z) "Het kabinet blijft voor nu bij elkaar, maar de crisissfeer is niet voorbij."
- Jetten (Fri): things must improve "inhoudelijk, politiek en persoonlijk"; "Als je dat vandaag per se had willen afronden dan had dat ook mis kunnen gaan." — same NOS article. Earlier (Thu) he said a cabinet without VVD ministers is "not an option" — NL Times 2026-11-12 (read 2026-11-13).
- Yeşilgöz (Fri): "Op de huidige manier kunnen wij niet doorgaan. Wat de VVD betreft is een minderheidskabinet een mislukt experiment"; wants a "substantive correction" and a "political correction", possibly an extra party in the cabinet — NOS liveblog (above, entry 13 Nov). She added the outcome may be "dat we met z'n drieën besluiten om naar de koning te gaan" — NOS https://nos.nl/artikel/2639395-het-kan-nog-steeds-fout-gaan-is-de-conclusie-na-urenlang-crisisoverleg (published 2026-11-13 22:15, read 2026-11-14T17:53Z).
- D66 and CDA were "geschrokken" by her tone and doubt whether the VVD wants a deal; "twee kampen tegenover elkaar" — NOS https://nos.nl/artikel/2639424-d66-vvd-en-cda-zetten-crisisoverleg-voort-in-hilversum-uitkomst-ongewis (published 2026-11-14 05:29, read 2026-11-14T17:53Z) "Deze partijen kunnen moeilijk inschatten of de VVD er wel uit wil komen."
