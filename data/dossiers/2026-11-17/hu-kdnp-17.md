# hu-kdnp-17
- **Claim:** Hungary's Christian Democratic People's Party (KDNP) suspends Miklós Seszták's party membership or expels him from the party, announced by Monday 16 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 05:00 CET on 15 Nov 2026 and 23:59 Budapest time on 16 Nov 2026, it is announced that a KDNP body (the Fegyelmi és Etikai Bizottság / Országos Fegyelmi és Etikai Bizottság, the Országos Elnökség, or another competent party body) has suspended Miklós Seszták's party membership (tagsági jogviszony felfüggesztése, including as an interim measure for the duration of the disciplinary procedure) or expelled him from the party (first-instance decision suffices). The announcement may come from KDNP or one of its officials (Semjén, Latorcai, Rétvári, party spokesperson) or be reported by MTI, HVG, 444, Index, Magyar Nemzet or Mandiner. Requests, initiatives or statements that a procedure has been started or that suspension "is being initiated" do not count (Latorcai's initiative of 14 Nov does not count); Seszták leaving the party himself, or being excluded only from the KDNP parliamentary group, does not count. NO otherwise, including if the decision was announced before 05:00 CET on 15 Nov.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-15T05:00+01:00 to 2026-11-16T23:59+01:00 (Europe/Budapest)
- **Evidence expected:** no scheduled moment; KDNP statement via MTI or Facebook post of a KDNP official; reports by HVG/444/Index the same day. evidence_at 2026-11-17T09:00+01:00
- **Deadline (resolves by):** 2026-11-18T05:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-14T18:25Z

- Procedure (KDNP Fegyelmi és Etikai Szabályzat, 6 Dec 2025) — https://new.kdnp.hu/sites/default/files/2026-01/kdnp-fegyelmi-es-etikai-szabalyzat-202512%2006.pdf (19 pages, read 2026-11-14T18:12Z):
  - § 31: "A Fegyelmi Bizottság az eljárás megindításakor vagy annak bármely további szakaszában ideiglenes intézkedésként elrendelheti a vizsgálat alá vont tag tagsági jogviszonyának felfüggesztését" — the disciplinary committee can order suspension as an interim measure when opening the procedure or at any later stage.
  - § 44(1): the chair of the first-instance committee decides on admitting a complaint within 8 days of receipt; § 43: if the procedure is not started within 30 days, another committee can be designated.
  - § 27: penalties include "kizárás a párttagságból"; § 32(3): a first-instance expulsion suspends membership rights immediately, regardless of appeal.
  - § 23(3)–(5): the national committee (Országos Fegyelmi és Etikai Bizottság, 3-member panel) acts at first instance in cases of members of the Országos Választmány and may take over any county-level case; appeals from its first-instance decisions go to the Országos Elnökség.
  - Which body is competent for Seszták (national or Szabolcs-Szatmár-Bereg county committee) is not stated in the reports.
- So the 8-day admission deadline runs to about 22 Nov; nothing obliges a decision on Sunday or Monday.

## Facts
- NVVH found HUF 5,071,632,000, EUR 2,658,350 and USD 1,257,100 in cash (over HUF 6bn) in searches on 12–13 Nov in the Seszták case — 444, 14 Nov, above (from hu-probe-17).
- Seszták (development minister 2014–18) is suspected of taking HUF 12.6bn in bribes in the Volánbusz case; in custody since 28 Sep (arrest ordered 30 Sep), detention extended to 30 Jan; bail of HUF 300m rejected — 444, 14 Nov above; https://444.hu/2026/10/28/meghosszabbitotta-a-birosag-sesztak-miklos-letartoztatasat (read 2026-11-14T17:54Z) (from hu-probe-17).
- He remains a KDNP member of the National Assembly (444, 14 Nov). KDNP's caucus has eight members (444, 28 Sep — https://444.hu/2026/09/28/sesztak-miklos-mellett-a-kdnp-s-frakciotarsai-sem-alltak-ki, read 2026-11-14T18:16Z).
- KDNP caucus leader Bence Rétvári (14 Nov) asked Seszták to give back his mandate — https://444.hu/2026/11/14/retvari-arra-keri-sesztakot-hogy-adja-vissza-a-kdnp-s-mandatumat (read 2026-11-14T17:54Z); Fidesz caucus leader János Bóka called on him to resign "haladéktalanul" — https://hvg.hu/itthon/20261114_boka-janos-frakciovezeto-sesztak-miklos-lemondas-nvvh-elasott-milliardok (read 2026-11-14T17:53Z) (from hu-probe-17).
- Latorcai: "a mai napon a bizalma megszűnt" (his trust ended today) — 444 and Index above.
- Fidesz statement: "aki nem tartja magát a letett esküjéhez, azzal a Fidesz–KDNP nem tud közösséget vállalni"; yet on Saturday morning the Fidesz website still listed Seszták as a political victim — Index above; HVG Latorcai article above.
- Lawyer Gábor Papp: the money found "is not my client's money" — https://hvg.hu/itthon/20261114_sesztak-miklos-elasott-milliardok-nvvh-papp-gabor-ugyved (read 2026-11-14T17:53Z) (from hu-probe-17).
- In September KDNP colleagues did not speak up for Seszták (unlike for Balázs Hankó); Rétvári then said Seszták got involved only because he supervised the area concerned — 444, 28 Sep above.
- Context: KDNP is now in opposition (444 calls Latorcai "az ellenzéki párt ügyvezető alelnöke"); PM Péter Magyar called on Fidesz-KDNP MPs to resign their seats.
