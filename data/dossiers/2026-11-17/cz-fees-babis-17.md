# cz-fees-babis-17
- **Claim:** On Monday 16 November 2026 PM Andrej Babiš publicly states that the government will not withdraw its bill abolishing the ČT and ČRo licence fees, or that the compulsory licence fees will be abolished.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 and 23:59 Prague time on 16 Nov 2026, Andrej Babiš says publicly (at the 07:30 briefing before departure to Israel, to journalists in Israel, in an interview, or in his own social-media post or video) either (a) that the government/coalition will not withdraw ("nestáhne") the bill abolishing the ČT/ČRo licence fees, or (b) that the compulsory licence fees will be abolished / that the government is sticking to abolishing them ("poplatky zrušíme", "na zrušení poplatků trváme", including "poplatky budou dobrovolné"), as reported by ČTK, ČT24, iROZHLAS, Seznam Zprávy, Novinky or Aktuálně, or visible in his own recording. A statement that the bill will be changed or that a new financing model will come in January counts as YES if it is accompanied by (a) or (b). Statements by other ministers or ANO officials do not count. NO if he makes no such statement on 16 Nov (including if he does not speak about the issue or only says "we will discuss it" / "we respect the protesters"), or if he announces that the bill will be withdrawn, frozen or suspended, that the fees will be kept, or that abolition depends on a referendum or other condition not yet met.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-16T00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-16T23:59+01:00 (Europe/Prague)
- **Evidence expected:** ČTK live stream of the 07:30 briefing and ČTK/ČT24 reports by about 09:00; possibly further remarks to Czech journalists in Israel in the evening. evidence_at 2026-11-16T10:00+01:00
