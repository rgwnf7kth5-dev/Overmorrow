## Procedure (what counts)
- Persian Wikipedia "فوریت (مجلس)" (read 2026-11-14T18:00Z) https://fa.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D9%81%D9%88%D8%B1%DB%8C%D8%AA_(%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%B3): the urgency is voted on after one proposer and speakers for/against. Double-urgency bills, once urgency is approved, are printed and distributed and taken up in plenary 24 hours after distribution, without committee referral ("بیست و چهار ساعت پس ازتوزیع درمجلس مطرح می‌گردد. این گونه طرح‌ها و لوایح به کمسیون ارجاع نمی‌گردد"). Triple urgency: debated in the same session. Guardian Council must be informed and attend, giving its view within 24 hours (Art. 86).
- Consequence: if the double urgency passes on Sunday, the bill itself can come up at the earliest ~24 hours after distribution. Majlis open sessions are normally held Sunday, Tuesday and Wednesday (general knowledge, not verified for this week), so a vote on the bill itself on Monday 16 Nov is unlikely. The claim therefore counts the urgency vote. The vote threshold for double urgency was not verified.
- The Wikipedia summary may reflect an older version of the internal rules; recent amendments not checked.

## Facts
- Bill content: removes the ijtihad requirement (1983 law) for the intelligence minister; creates within the ministry an "Intelligence Protection Organization" and an "Ideological(-Political) Organization" whose heads are appointed directly by Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei; the minister is still nominated by the president and confirmed by parliament — Iran International https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611100687 (published 2026-11-10 22:22Z, updated 2026-11-11 15:03Z, read 2026-11-14T17:58Z) "Their directors would be appointed by Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei".
- Goudarzi described the two organisations as a substitute for the religious qualification and said the change would speed up naming a new minister — same article; Persian version "اقدامی برای «جبران حذف شرط اجتهاد»".
- The ministry has had no permanent minister since Esmail Khatib was killed in an Israeli strike in March 2026 (27 Esfand 1404); an unnamed acting minister runs it; Pezeshkian has nominated nobody; Art. 135 of the constitution allows a caretaker for max. three months; a 45-day extension granted with the Leader's permission expired in early Mehr — Iran International Persian (above).
- National Security Committee chair Ebrahim Azizi (1 Nov) called eight months without a minister in wartime unlawful — Iran International Persian (above).
- Mixed committee signals: deputy chair Amir Hayat Moghadam claimed committee approval; committee spokesman Hassan Qashqavi said members only discussed it — Iran International English 10 Nov (above).
- Possible candidate: cleric Mostafa Pourmohammadi reportedly considered by Pezeshkian; no nomination — The Defense News (above).
- Related debate: proposals to merge the ministry with the IRGC Intelligence Organisation discussed in Etemad (close to Pezeshkian) — Iran International https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611121433 (published 2026-11-13 03:30Z, read 2026-11-14T17:58Z).
- Hamshahri headline (search result only) mentions opponents ("مخالفان چه می‌گویند؟"); their arguments not read.
- Since its establishment in 1984, every permanent intelligence minister has been a cleric — Iran International (above).
