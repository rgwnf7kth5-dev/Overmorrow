# cop31-draft-17
- **Claim:** The COP31 presidency (Türkiye and Australia) publishes a first draft of the Antalya cover decision by the end of Monday 16 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 on Sun 15 Nov and 23:59 on Mon 16 Nov 2026 Antalya time (Europe/Istanbul, UTC+3), the COP31 presidency publishes on unfccc.int or on the official COP31 presidency website a first draft of the cover decision, i.e. a document in decision form (draft decision text with preambular and/or operative paragraphs, options or brackets allowed) presented as the draft cover decision / overarching political decision of COP31, as shown by the document's posting time or by the first time-stamped report of its release by Carbon Brief, Climate Home News, ENB (IISD) or Reuters. A compilation or summary of Parties' views (such as the 13 Nov "Summary of reflections" informal note or an update of it) does not count. NO if no such draft is published in the window, including if it appears after 23:59 on 16 Nov or if the presidency drops the cover decision.
- **Deadline (event window end):** 2026-11-16T23:59+03:00 (Europe/Istanbul); resolves by 2026-11-18T05:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-14T18:10Z

## Facts
- 13 Nov: presidency published a "Summary of reflections" informal note compiling Parties' views (electrification 35 by 35, welcome of COP30 presidency roadmaps on transitioning away from fossil fuels and on deforestation, adaptation-finance baseline); text says it does not reflect consensus — Lusa above "não reflete consenso"; Media India above.
- EU (Commission's Diana Acconcia): the summary is a basis for further consultations, not for negotiating an agreement — Lusa above "serve de base para novas consultas".
- ENB 13 Nov (published 14 Nov): co-facilitators Ayşin Turpancı (COP31 Presidency) and Sally Box (Presidency of Negotiations) invited views on the informal note; Cuba objected to duplication with agenda items; Arab Group against "top-down sectoral targets and energy pathways"; Grupo Sur: cover decision should complement, not replace, the negotiating process; Brazil frustrated with the note — https://enb.iisd.org/sites/default/files/2026-11/enb12906e.pdf (ENB Vol. 12 No. 906, dated 14 Nov 2026, read 2026-11-14T17:58Z) "invited views on an informal note."
- ENB corridors: statements on the cover decision "went on (and on)"; a Microsoft outage on Thu/Fri disrupted circulation of draft texts — ENB above "making it more difficult for Parties to send emails and circulate draft text."
- Climate Home 14 Nov: midway stocktaking plenary under Türkiye and Australia on Saturday night 14 Nov; "Countries will have to decide whether to move forward with a cover decision as the main outcome"; some large developing countries pushed back all week on a cover decision and on sectoral targets — Climate Home Day 6 above.
- Simon Stiell (14 Nov) urged governments "to move swiftly to the hardest issues and to avoid tactical delays" — Climate Home Day 6 above.
- Pre-COP (7 Oct): Bowen announced the presidency would prepare a cover decision; does not want to negotiate a long political declaration — https://www.setav.org/en/after-the-pre-cop-the-cover-decision-the-access-demand-and-turkiyes-two-proposals (read 2026-11-14T17:59Z) "COP31 will conclude with a cover decision prepared by the presidency."
- Conference ends Fri 20 Nov — Lusa above "A COP31 termina no dia 20 de novembro."
- Outcome of the Saturday-night stocktaking plenary (14 Nov, Antalya evening) not yet known at time of writing.

## Risks
- "Early next week" may mean Tuesday (as at COP30) — the main path to NO.
- The presidency could first issue a revised compilation/"second iteration" of the informal note rather than draft decision text; criterion excludes compilations, so labelling matters. Edge case: a document titled "draft text" but structured as a list of elements.
- Release could slip just past midnight Antalya time (COP30 draft came overnight).
- The cover decision could be dropped or turned into a "presidency summary" after pushback.
- Detection: unfccc.int pages render without content in the reader; resolution may rely on Carbon Brief tracker, Climate Home, ENB or Reuters time stamps.
