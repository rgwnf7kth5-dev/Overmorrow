## Risks
- Japanese preliminary GDP surprises are large relative to the q/q figure because the rate is annualised (×~4); a 0.1 pt q/q miss is ~0.4 pt annualised.
- Inventory and net-export swings (higher crude imports) and the consumption fall after energy-subsidy expiry are the main uncertain components.
- Rounding: the annualised figure is published to one decimal.
- The Reuters poll is from 6 Nov; later data (September industrial output, trade) may have shifted forecasts.
