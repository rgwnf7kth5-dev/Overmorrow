# cn-mfa-trilateral-17
- **Claim:** China's Foreign Ministry explicitly confirms that Xi Jinping will hold a three-way meeting with Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin, by the end of Monday 16 November 2026 (Beijing time).
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 on 15 Nov and 23:59 on 16 Nov 2026 Beijing time, the Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs (a spokesperson at the regular press conference, a spokesperson announcement or statement, or an item on mfa.gov.cn; or Xinhua/CCTV carrying an announcement attributed to the ministry or its spokesperson) states that President Xi Jinping will hold, attend or host a meeting together with US President Trump and Russian President Putin (the three heads of state together) at or on the sidelines of the APEC meeting in Shenzhen. A date is not required. Formulas such as "China is in communication with relevant parties", "we will release information in a timely way", announcements of bilateral meetings only, or confirmations by the US or Russia alone do not count. NO otherwise, including if the meeting is called off.

- **Evidence expected:** Monday regular press conference (normally 15:00 Beijing); English transcript on mfa.gov.cn the same evening; Reuters/AFP/AP reports within an hour. evidence_at 2026-11-16T20:00+08:00

## Risks
- Beijing's practice is to announce leaders' meetings late, often the day before or the day itself; a confirmation on Tue 17 or Wed 18 Nov would be NO here.
- Spokesperson may confirm in hedged form ("the three heads of state will meet" vs "will have contact"/"interaction"); criterion requires a statement that Xi will meet with both together — a vague "will have exchanges with leaders attending APEC" does not count.
- Chinese-language-only announcement (e.g. Xinhua Chinese) attributed to the MFA spokesperson counts; resolver must check Chinese sources too (fmprc.gov.cn/web, xinhuanet.com).
- Saturday/Sunday announcements are possible (MFA issues weekend spokesperson announcements); covered by the window from 00:00 Sun 15 Nov.
