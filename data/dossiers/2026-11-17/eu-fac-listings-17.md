# eu-fac-listings-17
- **Claim:** The Council of the EU adopts on Monday 16 November 2026 new sanctions listings of at least 145 individuals and entities under its Ukraine-territorial-integrity, Russia-hybrid-threats and cyber-attacks sanctions regimes combined.
- **Criterion:** YES if, on 16 Nov 2026 (Brussels time), the Council of the EU adopts (at the Foreign Affairs Council or by written procedure concluded that day) new listings of individuals and entities under (a) the regime for actions undermining Ukraine's territorial integrity (Decision 2014/145/CFSP / Regulation (EU) No 269/2014), (b) the regime for Russia's destabilising activities / hybrid threats (Decision (CFSP) 2024/2643 / Regulation (EU) 2024/2642) and (c) the cyber-attacks regime (Decision (CFSP) 2019/797 / Regulation (EU) 2019/796), and the number of new listings under (a)+(b)+(c) together is 145 or more, as stated in the Council's press releases of 16 Nov or, if they give no figure, as counted from the implementing acts published in the Official Journal (L series) dated 16 Nov. Extensions, amendments of existing entries and delistings do not count. NO if fewer than 145 such listings are adopted on 16 Nov, or none (e.g. adoption postponed or blocked).

## Facts
- EU ambassadors (Coreper) agreed the listings on Wed 11 Nov; ministers to formally approve on 16 Nov, effect on OJ publication; per Reuters-cited diplomats: 113 under the Ukraine territorial-integrity framework (incl. 50 Russian banks, shadow-fleet supporters, energy firms, business leaders), 31 under the hybrid-activities regime, 1 under the cyber regime — https://shafaq.com/en/World/EU-envoys-back-145-new-Russia-sanctions-listings (published 2026-11-11T22:26Z, read 2026-11-14T17:53Z) "European Union ambassadors agreed on Wednesday to add 145 individuals and entities".
- Breakdown per European Pravda via Interfax-Ukraine: territorial integrity 12 individuals + 101 entities (50 banks among the entities); hybrid 30 individuals + 1 entity; cyber 1 entity — https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/general/1235072.html (published 2026-11-11T18:33Z, read 2026-11-14T17:53Z) "will include 12 individuals and 101 legal entities. Among these 101 are 50 Russian banks". "The final sanctions lists are to be approved at a meeting of the EU Foreign Affairs Council in Brussels on Monday, November 16."
- Hybrid listings reportedly linked to an attempted drone attack on Leipzig/Halle airport, which Berlin blamed on Moscow — Shafaq/Reuters above.
- Commissioner Albuquerque (EP, 12 Nov): ministers should adopt further individual sanctions on Monday aimed at refineries and the shadow fleet; 22nd sectoral package still being finalised; ~4,600 listings under the territorial-integrity regime so far; 690+ shadow-fleet vessels; transactions banned with 100+ Russian banks. French minister Haddad: 150 further entities and 50 banks, mostly proposed by France — https://www.ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/eu-v-pondeli-prijme-dalsi-sankce-proti-rusku-zameri-se-na-rafinerie-a-flotilu/2901659 (ČTK, published 2026-11-13 12:46, read 2026-11-14T17:53Z) "se tyto sankce budou týkat 150 dalších subjektů a 50 bank".
- Same FAC agenda: Ukrainian FM Sybiha attends in person; air defence, winter energy support, Commission briefing on 2027 Ukraine funding (€45bn of a €70bn gap), Abu Dhabi talks — European Pravda/Yahoo above.
- Precedent: an earlier 2026 FAC (Brussels Times, undated in the readable text, "on Monday") adopted "almost 1600" listings in the military-industrial complex plus 77 listings over the Duma "elections" in occupied territories, separate from the 22nd package — https://www.brusselstimes.com/world/2360181/eus-foreign-affairs-council-steps-up-pressure-on-russia-and-involvement-in-the-middle-east (read 2026-11-14T17:53Z) "The council adopted the largest-ever package of listings targeting almost 1600 individuals and entities". Albuquerque: since last autumn the EU adds individual listings at every FAC (ČTK above).
- Listings require unanimity of the 27 (CFSP decisions); Coreper agreement on 11 Nov means no member state objected at ambassador level per the reports above.

## Risks
- Exact count: threshold equals the reported total (145). One name dropped (legal-service review, last-minute objection, a regime counted differently) gives NO. Reports differ slightly on hybrid split (30+1 individuals/entity vs "31") but agree on 145 total.
- Haddad's figure was "150 entities"; the final number could also be higher.
- Press releases may be split per regime; the cyber listing may be announced separately or with a different date; counting must sum all three.
- A member state (unanimity) could hold up the territorial-integrity listings at the FAC itself.
- Adoption by written procedure on another day (e.g. 17 Nov) would mean NO.
