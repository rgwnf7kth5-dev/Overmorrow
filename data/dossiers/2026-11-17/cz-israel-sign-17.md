# cz-israel-sign-17
- **Claim:** During PM Andrej Babiš's official visit to Israel, Czech and Israeli government representatives sign at least one written intergovernmental or interministerial document (agreement, memorandum, joint declaration or letter of intent) on Monday 16 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, on 16 Nov 2026 (Israel time, 00:00–23:59), at least one written bilateral document between the Czech Republic and the State of Israel or between their governments, ministries or armed forces general staffs (agreement, treaty, memorandum of understanding, joint/common declaration, letter of intent, protocol, programme or action plan of cooperation) is signed by a Czech government member or official acting for the Czech state (PM, a minister, a deputy minister, the Chief of the General Staff, an ambassador) and an Israeli counterpart, as reported by the Czech Government Office (vlada.gov.cz), a Czech or Israeli ministry, the Israeli PMO (gov.il), ČTK, ČT24, Times of Israel or Jerusalem Post. Letters of intent count. A joint statement published without a signature by both sides counts only if a source says it was signed. Contracts or MoUs between companies (e.g. IAI, Elbit, Rafael and Czech firms) or between hospitals, universities or agencies without a government signatory do not count; a purchase contract signed by the Czech Ministry of Defence with an Israeli state-owned firm does not count (not a government-to-government document). NO if no such document is signed on 16 Nov, including if the signing happens on 17 or 18 Nov, or if the visit or the Netanyahu meeting does not take place on 16 Nov.

## Risks
- Defence procurement: an Arrow/Barak MX decision is a big-ticket procurement; Czech budget limits (2 % GDP) and Israeli government transition may keep talks at "general" level with no paper. A letter of intent on air defence is the most plausible defence document.
- Timing: ministers' counterpart meetings and any signings could fall on Tue 17 or Wed 18 Nov (health ministry visit is Wed) → NO for this claim.
- Caretaker status of Netanyahu's government could discourage new binding agreements but not MoUs/declarations.
- Reporting: a signed document by ministers (e.g. health MoU) might be reported only by the ministry on its site/social media; check MZd, MO, MZV, gov.il.
- Classification: company-level MoUs (common in defence visits) do not count; a source may blur "memorandum" between firms and ministries.
