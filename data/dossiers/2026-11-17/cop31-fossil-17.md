# cop31-fossil-17
- **Claim:** The COP31 presidency publishes a first draft of the Antalya cover decision by the end of Monday 16 November 2026, and that draft contains the phrase "transition away from fossil fuels" or "transitioning away from fossil fuels".
- **Criterion:** YES if a first draft of the cover decision that counts under cop31-draft-17 (published on unfccc.int or the official COP31 presidency site between 06:00 on 15 Nov and 23:59 on 16 Nov 2026, Europe/Istanbul) contains, anywhere in its text (including preamble, options or square brackets, and including quotation of the COP28 Global Stocktake paragraph), the words "transition away from fossil fuels", "transitioning away from fossil fuels" or "transition(ing) away from fossil fuels in energy systems", checked against the document itself or a verbatim quote by Carbon Brief, Climate Home News, ENB or Reuters. References only to "fossil fuel subsidies", "phase-out/phase-down of fossil fuels", an acronym such as "TAFF" without the words do not count unless the words above also appear verbatim somewhere in the draft (a roadmap title containing the words counts). NO if no such draft is published in the window, or if the draft published in the window lacks the phrase.

## Facts
- The 13 Nov presidency "Summary of reflections" welcomes the COP30 presidency's roadmaps on transitioning away from fossil fuels (TAFF) and on deforestation and records calls for a pathway for them toward GST 2 — https://mediaindia.eu/environment/cop31-presidencies-outline-key-climate-priorities-ahead-of-ministerial-negotiations/ (published 2026-11-14 06:35Z, read 2026-11-14T17:58Z) "welcomes the COP30 Presidency’s roadmaps on transitioning away from fossil fuels (TAFF)".
- Earth.org lists among the compiled proposals "Transition away from fossil fuels in energy systems, phase out of inefficient fossil fuel subsidies." — https://earth.org/cop31-week-1-recap/ (published 2026-11-14 02:48Z, read 2026-11-14T17:58Z).
- Lusa/EFE: the summary includes Brazil's proposed roadmap "para o abandono dos combustíveis fósseis" — https://expressodasilhas.cv/mundo/2026/11/13/cop31-presidencia-divulga-primeiro-resumo-das-propostas-dos-paises/105529 (read 2026-11-14T17:58Z).
- EU asked for the text to reflect challenges in implementing GST 1 "including transitioning away from fossil fuels"; Arab Group rejected "top-down sectoral targets and energy pathways" — ENB Vol. 12 No. 906 https://enb.iisd.org/sites/default/files/2026-11/enb12906e.pdf (read 2026-11-14T17:58Z) "including transitioning away from fossil fuels".
- Presidency's flagship 35-by-35 electrification proposal does not link to phasing out fossil fuels — Media India above "does not explicitly link the target to ... phasing out fossil fuels".
- COP30 precedent: the first Mutirão draft (18 Nov 2025) had options on a transition roadmap; 350.org said the roadmap "explicitly excludes fossil fuels"; Reuters: one option "called for a process to help countries overcome their dependency on fossil fuels" — https://350.org/press-release/350-org-response-and-analysis-cop30-mutirao-draft-presidency-text-on-climate-finance-and-fossil-fuels/ ; https://www.thestar.com.my/news/world/2025/11/18/cop30-presidency-draws-up-early-draft-of-deal-text (read 2026-11-14T17:59Z). Whether the exact phrase appeared in that first draft was not verified.
- COP28 (Dubai, 2023) GST decision contains "transitioning away from fossil fuels in energy systems" (para 28) — standard language; Climate Home Day 6 notes civil society demands on "the just and equitable transition away from fossil fuels" — https://www.climatechangenews.com/2026/11/14/cop31-bulletin-day-6-limited-progress-as-ministers-arrive-in-antalya/.
