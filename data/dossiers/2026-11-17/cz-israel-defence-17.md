## Facts — systems and procurement background
- SPYDER (Rafael): intergovernmental contract signed 2021 (Koudelka – Eshel), CZK 13.7bn incl. VAT, four batteries replacing 2K12 KUB at the 25th Air Defence Missile Regiment Strakonice; I-Derby ER missiles ordered 2023; two batteries delivered by May 2026 per Babiš, all four during 2026, full operational capability expected 2028; the MoD prepares acquisition of "SPYDER All-in-One" (preliminary consultations) — norskluftvern.com above (citing MoD, Czech Radio, parliamentary record of 16 Apr 2026).
- Concept for the Development of State Air Defence approved Feb 2026; ambition to cover all layers up to the lower layer of missile defence; Zůna: cost "hundreds of billions of crowns to 2035 and beyond" (norskluftvern.com above). New army concept approved first week of Nov 2026; government plans 2 % of GDP for defence (echo24 8 Nov above).
- Slovakia bought Barak MX (IAI) for about EUR 560m; first battery delivered April 2026 — https://www.ynetnews.com/business/article/h1kgbvdtwg (published 2026-04-24, read 2026-11-14T18:13Z): "multi-layered air-defense system designed to intercept ... drones, fighter aircraft, cruise missiles and ballistic missiles".
- Germany bought Arrow 3 (Babiš 8 Nov above).
- Israeli politics: Netanyahu caretaker PM; Eisenkot tasked with forming a government (from cz-israel-sign-17).
