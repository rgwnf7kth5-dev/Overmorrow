# hu-probe-17
- **Claim:** Former Hungarian development minister Miklós Seszták, held in custody in a bribery probe, gives up his seat in the National Assembly by Monday 16 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 19:00 CET on 14 Nov 2026 and 23:59 Budapest time on 16 Nov 2026, Miklós Seszták resigns his parliamentary mandate: he publicly announces (personally, in writing, or through his lawyer) that he is giving up his seat, or the Speaker of the National Assembly, the KDNP caucus or MTI reports that he has submitted his resignation; reported by MTI, Telex, HVG, 444 or the National Assembly. The date on which the resignation takes legal effect does not matter. Statements that he is considering it, conditional statements, or leaving/being suspended from the KDNP party or caucus while keeping the seat do not count. NO otherwise.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-14T19:00+01:00 to 2026-11-16T23:59+01:00 (Europe/Budapest)
- **Evidence expected:** no scheduled moment; statement via lawyer Gábor Papp, KDNP caucus or Speaker's office, reported by MTI/Telex/HVG/444. evidence_at 2026-11-17T09:00+01:00
- **Deadline (resolves by):** 2026-11-18T05:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-14T18:05Z

## Facts
- NVVH (National Asset Recovery and Protection Office) found HUF 5,071,632,000, EUR 2,658,350 and USD 1,257,100 in cash (over HUF 6bn, ~USD 20m) buried in forests, hidden in farm and warehouse buildings, a home and a safe, in searches on 12–13 Nov in Budapest and Pest, Békés and Nógrád counties, in the Seszták criminal case — https://444.hu/2026/11/14/felfuggeszthetik-sesztak-miklos-parttagsagat (published 2026-11-14 16:50, read 2026-11-14T17:54Z) "5 071 632 000 forintot, 2 658 350 eurót és 1 257 100 amerikai dollárt találtak".
- Seszták (development minister 2014–18) is suspected of taking bribes linked to Volánbusz bus purchases and state construction; in custody since late September; denies wrongdoing — https://www.dw.com/en/hungary-millions-in-hidden-cash-found-in-orban-graft-probe/a-79946923 (published 2026-11-14 09:19Z, read 2026-11-14T17:53Z) "The former minister was taken into police custody in late September. He has denied wrongdoing."
- Timeline: immunity suspended 28 Sep, taken into custody the same day, court ordered arrest on 30 Sep (to 30 Oct); suspicion of HUF 11bn+ in bribes (12.6bn in all) — https://hvg.hu/itthon/20261114_sesztak-miklos-elasott-milliardok-nvvh-papp-gabor-ugyved (published 2026-11-14 10:20Z, read 2026-11-14T17:53Z) "A KDNP parlamenti képviselőjének mentelmi jogát szeptember 28-án függesztették fel"; detention extended to 30 Jan, bail of HUF 300m rejected — 444, 28 Oct, above; 444, 14 Nov, above.
- He is still a sitting KDNP member of the National Assembly — 444, 14 Nov, above "Seszták Miklós KDNP-s országgyűlési képviselőt".
- Lawyer Gábor Papp (to Telex): "this is not my client's money"; the case has several other suspects — hvg.hu, above "ez nem a védence pénze".
- KDNP caucus leader Bence Rétvári (Sat 14 Nov, Facebook) asked Seszták to give back his mandate and clear himself before the authorities — https://444.hu/2026/11/14/retvari-arra-keri-sesztakot-hogy-adja-vissza-a-kdnp-s-mandatumat (published 2026-11-14 13:16, read 2026-11-14T17:54Z) "azt kérem Seszták Miklóstól, hogy adja vissza a mandátumát".
- Fidesz caucus leader János Bóka (Sat afternoon, Facebook live) called on him to resign his mandate "immediately" — https://hvg.hu/itthon/20261114_boka-janos-frakciovezeto-sesztak-miklos-lemondas-nvvh-elasott-milliardok (published 2026-11-14 14:49Z, read 2026-11-14T17:53Z) "Felszólítom Seszták Miklóst, hogy haladéktalanul mondjon le mandátumáról"; "ezt már nem teheti országgyűlési képviselőként".
- KDNP executive vice-chair Csaba Latorcai initiated a party disciplinary and ethics procedure and the suspension of Seszták's party membership for its duration — 444, 14 Nov, above "párttagságának felfüggesztését kezdeményezte".
- PM Péter Magyar called on "honest" Fidesz-KDNP MPs to resign their seats; called Fidesz a criminal organisation — 444, Rétvári article, above; DW above.
- As of 18:00Z on 14 Nov, no report of a resignation or of any response from Seszták himself to the calls (hvg.hu/itthon and 444.hu front pages read 2026-11-14T17:54Z).
- Context: Orbán's brother Áron Orbán is in custody (444, 13 Nov); former minister Balázs Hankó also detained since September (search results, euronews 29 Sep).

## Risks
- Seszták is in custody: any written resignation goes through his lawyer or prison and may become public with a delay; a resignation signed on Monday but only reported on Tuesday still counts if the report shows it was submitted by 23:59 16 Nov (evidence may come late — the claim then waits).
- Ambiguity: "I will resign" vs. a formal letter to the Speaker — the criterion counts a public announcement or a reported submission.
- Lawyer's line ("not his money") and his earlier claim of being a political prisoner (Fidesz framing per HVG headline) point toward contesting the case rather than stepping down; the mandate gives no immunity now but keeps salary/status.
- Possible exit route other than resignation: KDNP suspends or expels him (does not count).
