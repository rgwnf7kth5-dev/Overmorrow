# anthropic-range-17
- **Claim:** Anthropic raises the price range of its Nasdaq IPO (ANT) above the filed $70–78 per share, or prices the offering above $78, by 23:59 ET on Monday 16 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 ET on Sun 15 Nov and 23:59 ET on Mon 16 Nov 2026, (a) Anthropic, PBC (CIK 0002130357) files on SEC EDGAR an S-1/A, free writing prospectus or other offering document (EDGAR acceptance time) stating an estimated price range whose upper bound exceeds $78.00 per share; or (b) Anthropic or a lead underwriter publicly announces, or Reuters, Bloomberg, CNBC, the WSJ or the FT reports as done (not as considered or expected), that the range has been raised above $70–78; or (c) the offering is priced above $78.00 per share within that window. NO if none of this happens in the window (including if the range is kept, narrowed within $70–78, lowered, or raised only from 17 Nov onwards; reports that bankers "weigh" or "may" raise the range do not count).

## Risks
- The issuer may keep the range and simply price at or above $78 on 18 Nov (as Arm, Reddit, Klarna did), which is NO.
- A raise could come on Tue 17 Nov (1 business day before pricing, as in 4/8 raises), outside the window.
- At $1.4tn and ~$64bn, the deal size and the AI-stock sell-off may make bankers prefer an unchanged range; Reuters reported the valuation "slightly below some investor expectations", which cuts both ways.
- Detection: an S-1/A filed late on Monday may only be reported on Tuesday; the criterion uses EDGAR acceptance time. A press report of a raise without a filing counts only if it reports the raise as done.
- Pricing could be accelerated to Monday (counts under (c) only if above $78).
