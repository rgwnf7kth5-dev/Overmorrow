## Base rate
- Class: scheduled Moody's sovereign review of the UK leads to a rating or outlook change. Compiled from the countryeconomy history (above): since the calendar regime (2014), changes on or near scheduled dates in Sep 2014, Sep 2017, Nov 2019, Oct 2020, Oct 2022 and Oct 2023 (Jun 2016 was an unscheduled post-referendum action) — about 6 changes in roughly 25 scheduled dates (two per year assumed, as in the 2026 calendar), rate about 0.24. Since the Oct 2023 outlook change: no change in about 5 scheduled dates (including 21 Nov 2025 affirmation and 22 May 2026).
- Moody's moves on the UK came with fiscal or political shocks (2016 referendum, 2022 mini-budget turmoil) or after sustained trends; outlook changes preceded the last two downgrades.

## Risks
- Outlook change to negative: higher debt interest (OBR +£14bn a year vs March), headroom cut to £15bn, gilt yields at multi-decade highs in October, inflation 3.5% and Bank Rate rising, debt near 100% of GDP.
- Political risk: Burnham government and pressure for defence and nationalisation spending; Moody's said policy shifts, not statements, drive the rating.
- In favour of no change: Budget well received, fiscal rules kept, stable outlook only one year old; Moody's affirmed in Nov 2025 and did nothing in May 2026.
