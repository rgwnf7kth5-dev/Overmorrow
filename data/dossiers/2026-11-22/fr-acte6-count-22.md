## Consensus and scale
- Consensus: no analysts' or official forecast exists. Used **106 thousand = the ministry's Act V count (14 Nov)**, the last comparable Saturday, the same rule as for Act IV (which used Act III's 320 and came in at 146). The documented trend (median -26% per Saturday) gives about 78; the anniversary week and the seven-country call are the only stated reasons for a rebound. Forecasters should judge between those; the consensus is a stand-in, not a forecast.
- Scale: series `fr-acte-interior-ministry-count-2026` has n=1 recorded surprise (`node scripts/refdata/series.mjs scale fr-acte-interior-ministry-count-2026` → basis "published or pooled", pooled_factor 1.385). Used scale_basis **pooled**: root mean square of the four Saturday-to-Saturday relative changes (-25.5%, -22.0%, -54.4%, -27.4%) = 34.8%, applied to the 106 level = 36.9k, times 1.385 = **51 thousand**. (In absolute terms the RMS of changes was 122k, but levels have fallen five-fold, so the relative form fits better.)
- `node scripts/refdata/models.mjs quantiles 106 51 1.6` → {"q10":1.4254,"q50":106,"q90":210.5746}. The normal q10 near 1 is not realistic for a nationwide count (a floor of about 20–30k seems likely); forecasters should skew.

## Risks
- Rebound: anniversary week, the European call, the DAL housing march and roundabouts could lift the count above 106; a permitted Bastille–Concorde march would draw more in Paris than a banned one.
- Fall: the trend (-26% a week), the union day only four days earlier (Tue 17 Nov), no youth/union Saturday call found, and a roundabout format that the ministry may count as "actions" rather than marchers.
- Definitional: the ministry may give separate figures (marches vs roundabout/toll actions) or only an interim 14:00 figure; it may also publish only a Fri–Sun total. The criterion takes the last national demonstrators figure for Saturday by 23:59 Sat.
- Violence or a Paris ban could change the policing message and the figure's timing.
