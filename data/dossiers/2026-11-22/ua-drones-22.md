# ua-drones-22
- **Claim:** The Ukrainian Air Force reports that Russia launched at least 150 drones at Ukraine in the night of 20–21 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if the Ukrainian Air Force's first summary of the overnight attack published on 21 Nov 2026, covering the attack that normally counts from 18:00 Kyiv time on 20 Nov, states that Russia launched at least 150 drones (strike UAVs as the summary counts them: Shahed-type and jet-powered Shaheds, Gerbera, Parodiya and other decoys, and any jet munitions such as Banderol/Dan-T that the summary includes in the UAV total; missiles listed separately not counted), as published on the Air Force's Telegram channel (t.me/kpszsu) or Facebook page or repeated by Ukrinform, Ukrainska Pravda, Kyiv Independent or Interfax-Ukraine. A summary issued late in the morning or around noon (as after the large attack on 17 Nov) still counts as the first summary. Separate daytime reports and later corrections do not count. NO if that summary gives fewer than 150 drones.
- **Event window:** attack 2026-11-20T18:00+02:00 to 2026-11-21T12:00+02:00 (Europe/Kyiv); summary normally 08:00–10:00 Kyiv on 21 Nov, after very large attacks up to about 12:00.
- **Evidence expected:** Air Force Telegram summary "У ніч на 21 листопада (з 18:00 20 листопада) противник атакував …", relayed by Ukrinform ("rubric-ato") and Kyiv Independent's daily "Russian attacks kill …" article. evidence_at 2026-11-21T14:00+02:00.
