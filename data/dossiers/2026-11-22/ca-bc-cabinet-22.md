# ca-bc-cabinet-22
- **Claim:** The cabinet sworn in with Premier Lorne Doerkson by 23:59 Pacific time on Saturday 21 November 2026 has fewer than 27 members besides the premier (ministers and ministers of state).
- **Criterion:** YES if a new British Columbia cabinet (Executive Council) is sworn in between 06:00 on Fri 20 Nov 2026 and 23:59 on Sat 21 Nov 2026 (America/Vancouver) and the number of ministers plus ministers of state sworn in, not counting the premier and not counting parliamentary secretaries, is 26 or fewer, per the BC government's news release / cabinet list (news.gov.bc.ca) or, failing that, The Canadian Press or CBC. NO if that number is 27 or more, or if no new cabinet is sworn in within the window.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-20T06:00-08:00 – 2026-11-21T23:59-08:00 (America/Vancouver)
- **Evidence expected:** BC government news release with the cabinet list on 20 Nov (Office of the Premier, news.gov.bc.ca), CP/CBC lists the same afternoon; evidence_at 2026-11-20T15:00-08:00.
- **Deadline (resolves by):** 2026-11-23T05:00:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-19T18:00Z

## Facts
- Benchmark: Eby's Nov 2024 cabinet "includes 23 ministers, four ministers of state and 14 parliamentary secretaries"; Conservative leader Rustad called it "bloated, expensive" and "a taxpayer-funded loyalty program" (42 of 47 NDP MLAs given posts) — CP via CFJC https://cfjctoday.com/2024/11/20/ebys-ndp-cabinet-bloated-expensive-says-b-c-conservative-leader-john-rustad/ (published 2024-11-20T23:19Z, read 2026-11-19T17:50Z). CBC headline counted "27 cabinet ministers" (ministers + ministers of state) — https://www.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.7386565 (published 2024-11-18, read 2026-11-19T17:50Z).
- Ministries currently listed on the BC government news site (pre-transition structure): Office of the Premier plus ~20+ ministries (Agriculture and Food; Attorney General; Children and Family Development; Citizens' Services; Education and Child Care; Emergency Management and Climate Readiness; Energy and Climate Solutions; Environment and Parks; Housing and Municipal Affairs; Indigenous Relations and Reconciliation; Infrastructure; Jobs and Economic Growth; Mining and Critical Minerals; Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills; Public Safety and Solicitor General; Social Development and Poverty Reduction; Tourism, Arts, Culture and Sport; Transportation and Transit; Water, Land and Resource Stewardship; Finance/Health/Forests/Labour not shown in the truncated read) — https://news.gov.bc.ca/ (read 2026-11-19T17:49Z).
- Precedent of a new premier shrinking cabinet: Christy Clark 2011, 17 ministers vs 23 under Campbell — Global News https://globalnews.ca/news/111136/christy-clark-cuts-b-c-cabinet-by-one-quarter/ (published 2011-03-15, read 2026-11-19T17:50Z).
- Caucus size: 64 Conservative MLAs (30 rookies among the 63 sworn on 19 Nov) — My Nelson Now (above). A larger caucus than Eby's 47 gives room for a larger cabinet; CBC (4 Nov) quotes Christy Clark expecting "strong regional representation" — https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/what-s-next-as-the-b-c-conservatives-transition-from-opposition-to-government-9.7407834 (read 2026-11-19T17:49Z).
- No report on the planned size of Doerkson's cabinet was found.

## Risks
- Large caucus and regional balancing may push the count to 27+, especially if many ministers of state are created.
- Counting ambiguity: "ministers of state" vs "parliamentary secretaries" vs "associate ministers"; the criterion counts every member of the Executive Council sworn in other than the premier.
- Swearing-in postponed beyond 21 Nov → NO.
