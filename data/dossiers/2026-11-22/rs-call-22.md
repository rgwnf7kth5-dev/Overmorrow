# rs-call-22
- **Claim:** Speaker Aleksandar Čupić signs the decision calling Serbia's presidential election by 23:59 CET on Saturday 21 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 06:00 on Fri 20 Nov 2026 and 23:59 on Sat 21 Nov 2026 (Europe/Belgrade), Aleksandar Čupić, as Speaker of the National Assembly performing the duties of President of the Republic, signs (adopts) the decision calling the election for President of the Republic (Odluka o raspisivanju izbora za predsednika Republike), as published in the Službeni glasnik or announced by the National Assembly / Presidency, or reported by RTS, Tanjug, N1, Danas or Insajder. NO if no such decision is signed in that window. A report published after 23:59 about a decision signed within the window counts.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-20T06:00+01:00 – 2026-11-21T23:59+01:00 (Europe/Belgrade)
- **Evidence expected:** announcement by Čupić / the Assembly and RTS/Tanjug/N1 reports the same day; decision in the Službeni glasnik within days. evidence_at 2026-11-21T20:00+01:00.
- **Deadline (resolves by):** 2026-11-23T05:00:00+01:00
- **Written at:** 2026-11-19T18:02Z

## Schedule
- No date announced for the call. Srpske novine (19 Nov): "Datum raspisivanja još nije objavljen." — https://srpske.rs/vesti/politika/2026/11/19/aleksandar-cupic-izabran-za-predsednika-skupstine-sa-140-glasova-radenovic-dobio (published 2026-11-19T13:00Z, read 2026-11-19T17:50Z).
- AP (via SNM): "Čupić će uskoro raspisati vanredne predsedničke izbore, koji moraju da budu održani do kraja godine" — https://snm.rs/agencija-ap-o-izboru-cupica-za-predsednika-skupstine-srbije/ (published 2026-11-19T17:40Z, read 17:50Z). Kurir: "Javnost očekuje da Čupić u najkraćem roku raspiše predsedničke izbore." — https://www.kurir.rs/vesti/politika/10166930/kako-je-cupic-postao-v-d-predsednika-republike (published 2026-11-19T14:40+01:00, read 17:50Z). Neither quotes Čupić on timing.
- Čupić's only statement on duties: "trudiću se da obavljam dužnost u skladu sa Ustavom, zakonom i Poslovnikom skupštine" — Kurir (above).
- Legal deadline: vote within three months of the end of the mandate (27 Sep → by 27 Dec); "od dana raspisivanja izbora do dana glasanja ne može proteći manje od 30 ni više od 60 dana" — Nedeljnik https://www.nedeljnik.rs/cupic-postaje-i-v-d-predsednika-srbije-evo-koja-su-njegova-ovlascenja/ (published 2026-11-19T14:22+01:00, read 17:50Z). Hence the latest date to call is 27 Nov (B92, 30 Oct: https://www.b92.net/english/politics/280780/brnabic-presidential-elections-to-be-called-by-november-27/vest, read 2026-11-18). Note: Nedeljnik and Glas javnosti wrongly write that "the latest date to call" is 27 Dec — https://www.glas-javnosti.rs/vesti/politika/cupic-je-sada-i-vd-predsednika-srbije-evo-sta-ce-raditi (published 2026-11-19T15:45+01:00, read 17:50Z); Mondo, Kurir and B92 give 27 Dec as the date by which the vote must be held.
- Brnabić on 30 Oct: elections to be called by 27 Nov at the latest, and if new bodies are formed by then, the new acting president takes over that duty — Mondo https://mondo.rs/Info/Politika/a2313951/da-li-aleksandar-cupic-postaje-v-d-predsednika-srbije.html (published 2026-11-19T15:21+01:00, read 17:51Z) "da će predsednički izbori biti raspisani najkasnije do 27. novembra, odnosno da će tu obavezu, ukoliko se do tada formiraju novi organi vlasti, preuzeti novi vršilac dužnosti predsednika Republike."
- Arithmetic: a call on Fri 20 Nov allows a vote from Sun 20 Dec; a call on Sat 21 Nov–Fri 27 Nov allows only a vote between 21 and 27 Dec (Sunday 27 Dec being the only Sunday). So calling on 20 Nov is the last date that keeps 20 Dec available; any later call effectively fixes the vote around 27 Dec.

## Facts
- Čupić elected speaker Thu 19 Nov by public roll-call vote, 140 for, Radenović (SNS) 97, of 239 voting; result ~13:27 — Danas https://www.danas.rs/vesti/politika/aleksandar-cupic-izabran-za-predsednika-narodne-skupstine/ (published 2026-11-19T13:29+01:00, read 17:50Z) "Od 239 poslanika koji su pristupili glasanju za Čupića je glasalo 140 ... Aleksandar Čupić je sada i vršilac dužnosti predsednika Republike, u njegovoj nadležnosti je i raspisivanje predsedničkih izbora."
- 134 present Student List MPs + 6 minority MPs voted for him (FoNet via Insajder, as reported by Srpske novine, above).
- The constituent sitting continued on 19 Nov with the election of deputy speakers, secretary-general and working bodies; Čupić issued seven warnings to SNS MPs including Vučić — N1 blog https://n1info.rs/vesti/blog-konstitutivna-sednica-skupstine-treci-dan-izbori-2026/ (published 2026-11-19T09:33+01:00, modified 17:42Z, read 17:51Z). Whether the sitting runs into Friday is not known (SNS obstruction of the agenda).
- Student List priority after the speaker: Law on Government by urgent procedure "da bismo mogli što pre da izaberemo Vladu" — Đajić, N1 18 Nov (see rs-speaker-21 dossier). The 90-day government-formation clock is running (Kurir related headline "Srbija najkasnije do Sretenja mora da dobije novu Vladu").
- Campaign signs: Glas javnosti related headline "Vučić najavio veliki predizborni skup u subotu u Beogradskoj areni: Predstavljamo program, ideju i viziju Ujedinjene Srbije" (19 Nov; article itself not opened) — on the Glas javnosti page above.
- Candidates: none formally named by the Student List found in today's coverage.

## Base rate
- No reference class in pipeline/reference-data/. Serbian precedents (desk knowledge, not re-verified online this session): 2012 — after Tadić resigned on 4 Apr 2012, Speaker Slavica Đukić Dejanović called the presidential election the next day (5 Apr) for 6 May; 2017 — Speaker Maja Gojković called it on 2 Mar for 2 Apr; 2022 — Speaker Ivica Dačić on 15 Feb for 3 Apr. In 2017 and 2022 the call came close to the earliest date useful for the chosen vote date. A newly elected speaker calling within 1–2 days of election: one case (2012, n=1 comparable).
