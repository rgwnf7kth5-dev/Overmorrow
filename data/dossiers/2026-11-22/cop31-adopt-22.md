## Schedule slip
- slip_class: other-scheduled. `node scripts/refdata/slip.mjs other-scheduled` → prior 0.07, own 7/98, rate 0.071 [estimate] (run 2026-11-19T17:51Z). This generic class covers whether a scheduled event happens; for a COP closing the overrun record above is the relevant measure.
- Signs of slippage: second draft only on Thu afternoon and "not expected to be the final version"; EU response deferred to Friday; Panama's written veto threat; finance (adaptation tripling baseline) deferred but unresolved; Arab Group/LMDC/Russia hostile to the 35x35 goal; ambitious countries criticise the fossil-fuel deletion. A third draft would likely come Friday or Friday night, putting the plenary on Saturday at the earliest.
- Signs it may not slip far: the presidency has already cut the most contested items (fossil language, $120bn), the pattern of COP30 (deleted fossil language, adopted Saturday); Bowen negotiating through Thursday night.

## Risks
- Overrun into Sunday (COP25, COP27, COP29 pattern), e.g. if the EU, AOSIS/LDCs or Latin American ambition countries hold out for restoring fossil-fuel language and the presidency issues another round of drafts.
- Panama (or another party) objects at the gavel; under consensus practice the presidency may still gavel over a lone objection (COP28/COP29 precedents of gavelling with objections recorded afterwards), but a formal block would delay or prevent adoption.
- No cover decision at all (the presidency drops it and adopts only agenda-item decisions) → NO. Copenhagen 2009 is the last case of no agreed outcome.
- Edge: plenary convened Saturday evening, gavel after 00:00 Sunday → NO. Antalya is UTC+3 all year (23:59 = 21:59 Paris).
- Edge: an early adoption before 07:00 Fri 20 Nov would fall outside the window (practically excluded: no consensus text exists yet on 19 Nov evening).
