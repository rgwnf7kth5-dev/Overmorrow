# gulf-ukmto-22
- **Claim:** UKMTO or JMIC reports at least one new attack or security incident involving a merchant vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, the Persian Gulf, the Gulf of Oman, the Arabian Sea or the Red Sea/Gulf of Aden that took place on Saturday 21 November 2026 (Gulf time).
- **Criterion:** YES if the UK Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) or the Joint Maritime Information Center (JMIC) reports an attack, explosion, mine strike, projectile or drone hit, hostile approach (armed craft firing on or attempting to board the vessel), boarding, hijacking or seizure involving a merchant vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, the Persian (Arabian) Gulf, the Gulf of Oman, the Arabian Sea (including the waters around Socotra), the Gulf of Aden, the Bab el-Mandeb or the Red Sea that occurred between 00:00 and 23:59 Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4) on 21 Nov 2026, as shown on ukmto.org or by a wire, gCaptain, Lloyd's List, Khaleej Times, Gulf News, Arab News, Iran International, Ship & Bunker, Al Jazeera or another established news outlet quoting UKMTO or JMIC. When UKMTO/JMIC give only a date and no time, that date decides. A report published after the window (including a "time-late" report) counts if the incident occurred in it. A merchant vessel disabled, boarded or seized by US or other military forces counts only if UKMTO or JMIC itself reports that incident. Suspicious approaches, hailings or sightings without weapons fire or a boarding attempt, GPS or navigation-aid interference alone, notices of an "incident" or "suspicious activity" that state no attack, explosion, hit, boarding or seizure, and claims by Iran (including IRGC statements), the Houthis or other parties that neither UKMTO nor JMIC reports do not count. NO if no such incident occurred on that day.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-21T00:00:00+04:00 to 2026-11-21T23:59:00+04:00 (Asia/Dubai) = 20 Nov 21:00 CET to 21 Nov 20:59 CET.

## Risks
- Late reporting: UKMTO dated a strike to 13 Nov only on 17 Nov; the ADNOC 17 Nov strike came as a time-late report on 18 Nov. A 21 Nov incident reported after 23 Nov 05:00 CET would be missed by the resolver (the claim would then resolve on incomplete evidence).
- Under-reporting: UKMTO said last month that many companies do not report incidents (gCaptain 19 Nov).
- Vague notices ("incident", "suspicious activity") and IRGC/Houthi claims alone do not count.
- Quiet runs happen (12–14 Nov, 18–19 Nov so far). Diplomacy: none active that pauses attacks; IRGC vows not to allow the blockade to continue; US struck Qeshm (17 Nov).
- Ambiguous geography for far-offshore piracy (e.g. 430 nm from Socotra) — criterion includes the Arabian Sea including the waters around Socotra.
