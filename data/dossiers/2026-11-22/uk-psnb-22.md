## Risks
- No real consensus: the £18.0bn centre is a desk proxy; analysts may centre lower if they expect self-assessment/receipts timing effects or higher if they track the OBR's upward revision (~£18bn for the year, about £1.5bn a month).
- Debt interest: index-linked gilt accruals follow RPI with a lag; higher inflation (CPI 3.5%) raises October interest costs.
- October receipts include corporation-tax and VAT timing; local-government and public-corporation estimates are highly provisional and often revised.
- September's figure may be revised in the same release, but only October's first estimate counts.
