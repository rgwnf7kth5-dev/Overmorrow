# fr-acte6-route-22
- **Claim:** The Paris police prefecture allows the Yellow Vest march on Saturday 21 November 2026 to proceed from Bastille to Concorde.
- **Criterion:** YES if, at 14:00 Paris time on Sat 21 Nov 2026, the Act VI march declared by the gilets jaunes coordination may lawfully proceed on a route that starts at Place de la Bastille and ends at Place de la Concorde: either the Préfecture de police has issued no order banning or modifying it, or it has authorised that route (street changes that keep the Bastille start and the Concorde end count as YES), or the administrative court has suspended a ban or modification. NO if, at 14:00 on 21 Nov, a prefectoral order in force bans the march or moves its start or end point away from Bastille or Concorde (including a march allowed only on another route or ending short of Concorde), or if the declared march is withdrawn or replaced by another route by agreement with the prefecture. Sources: the Préfecture de police website (orders), AFP, Le Monde, Le Figaro, Le Parisien, franceinfo or BFMTV. Whether marchers actually reach Concorde, and police action during the march, do not count.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-20T06:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-21T14:00:00+01:00 (Europe/Paris)
- **Evidence expected:** Prefecture order or press report on Fri 20 or Sat 21 Nov; on the day, reports of the march setting off with or without a ban.

- Timing: if the order lands on Thursday evening (before Fri 06:00), the outcome is known before the freeze; the criterion still reads the state at 14:00 Sat, but the desk should swap the claim (see notes).
