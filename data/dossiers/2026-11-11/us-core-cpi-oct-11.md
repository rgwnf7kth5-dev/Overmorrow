## Risks
- Rounding: the claim is the published one-decimal figure; a 0.25-0.26 unrounded value publishes as 0.3, 0.24 as 0.2 (BLS rounds from the index levels).
- The October 2025 gap may distort seasonal factors for October 2026; BLS has said it handles this with imputation (not read here), which could add noise to the seasonally adjusted core change.
- Consensus is split between 0.2 and 0.3; the last two prints came in above the 0.2 consensus.
- Unlike usual months, there is no y/y headline; resolver must use m/m s.a. in Table A or the text ("rose 0.x percent").
- Shutdown or BLS technical delay: no sign.
