# g7-statement-11
- **Claim:** The G7 foreign ministers (Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, the United Kingdom and the United States, with the EU High Representative) publish a joint statement adopted at their Paris meeting of 9-10 November 2026, on at least one topic or on the meeting as a whole, by 23:59 Paris time on 10 Nov 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 09:00 Paris time on 9 Nov and 23:59 Paris time on 10 Nov 2026 a text titled or described as a joint statement, joint declaration or communiqué of the G7 foreign ministers (in the name of all seven G7 foreign ministers, with or without the EU High Representative), issued from the Paris meeting, is published on the website of France's foreign ministry (diplomatie.gouv.fr), the US State Department, gov.uk, Global Affairs Canada, the Auswärtiges Amt, the Farnesina or Japan's MOFA. A statement on one topic (for example Iran, Hormuz, Ukraine or AI) counts. A chair's statement or chair's summary by France alone, a statement by the presidency, or a text signed by fewer than all seven does not count. NO if no such joint text is published by that time.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-10T23:59+01:00 (Europe/Paris)
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-08T18:05Z. Read times as stated.
- Sharpened from "a joint statement or chair's statement": the French side has signalled there will be no joint communiqué (AP, below), and all of the last formal meetings produced at least a joint text, so the test that carries information is a joint text signed by all seven, not a chair's text.
