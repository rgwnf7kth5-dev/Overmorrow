# cz-sk-session-11
- **Claim:** The Czech and Slovak governments hold their joint session in Bratislava on 10 November 2026, and the two prime ministers or their ministers sign at least one memorandum, agreement or protocol at it (a joint declaration or joint statement alone does not count).
- **Criterion:** YES if, on 10 Nov 2026, the joint session of the governments of the Czech Republic and the Slovak Republic takes place in Bratislava and at least one memorandum, agreement or protocol (a bilateral document between the two governments, or between ministries or ministers of the two countries) is signed on that day at the session, as reported by ČTK/ceskenoviny.cz, TASR/teraz.sk, vlada.gov.cz or vlada.gov.sk. A joint declaration or joint statement of the prime ministers, a communique, or an announcement of future talks does not count. NO if the session does not take place in Bratislava on 10 Nov, or if only joint declarations or statements are signed or no document is signed.
- **Deadline:** event 2026-11-10 (session in Bratislava, Slovak National Gallery); evidence by 2026-11-11T12:00+01:00
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-08T18:10Z. Read times as stated.
- Why this test: signing some joint document is near certain (every joint session with a retrievable record produced one, below), so the claim asks the more open question, whether a memorandum, agreement or protocol is signed, not merely a declaration. No specific agreement to be signed has been named in advance in any source found.

## Facts
- Format of the March session: 31 Mar 2026, Nová Horka castle, Studénka. The PMs signed the Memorandum of Enhanced Cooperation and a memorandum of understanding and cooperation on peaceful use of nuclear energy; Havlíček and Saková signed an agreement between the ministries on shared responsibility for gas storage in Dolní Bojanovice — https://uv.gov.cz/en/media-centrum/aktualne/czech-slovak-government-meeting-reaffirmed-the-strengthening-of-the-partnership-between-the-two-countries-226355/ (Czech government release, read 2026-11-08T17:51Z) "signed the Memorandum of Enhanced Cooperation ... Both Prime Ministers also signed a Memorandum of Understanding and Cooperation in the field of the peaceful use of nuclear energy". Same in TASR/STVR: https://enrsi.stvr.sk/articles/topical-issue/439502/slovak-and-czech-premiers-sign-memorandum-on-enhanced-cooperation (published 2026-04-01).
- The March memorandum had been approved by the Czech cabinet a week earlier (23 Mar), i.e. documents were announced about a week ahead — https://www.noviny.sk/zahranicie/1185956-vlada-schvalila-memorandum-o-prehlbenej-spolupraci-medzi-ceskom-a-slovenskom (published 2026-03-23T15:55+01:00). For 10 Nov no such cabinet approval of a document for signing was found as of 8 Nov (searches of ČTK, TASR and vlada pages).
- Trenčín, 3 Apr 2023 (Fiala and Heger): a written joint statement; no agreement found in the Czech government release or ČT24 — https://uv.gov.cz/en/media-centrum/aktualne/members-of-the-government-met-with-their-slovak-counterparts--they-discussed-regional-cooperation-and-energy-security-204438/ "The prime ministers also signed a joint statement (in Czech language)"; https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/domaci/slovensko-pomuze-cesku-s-pripravou-obranne-dohody-s-usa-uvedl-heger-po-spolecnem-jednani-vlad-8327 (published 2023-04-03T16:07+02:00) "Ve společném písemném prohlášení po jednání v Trenčíně".
- Valtice, 11 Nov 2019: joint declaration of the prime ministers — https://vlada.gov.cz/cz/media-centrum/aktualne/spolecne-prohlaseni-predsedu-vlad-177607/ (published 2019-11-11, read 2026-11-08T17:52Z).
- Skalica, 24 Apr 2014: joint declaration of the session — https://vlada.gov.cz/cz/media-centrum/tiskove-zpravy/spolecne-prohlaseni-zasedani-vlad-ceske-republiky-a-slovenske-republiky--117953/ (published 2014-04-24, read 2026-11-08T17:52Z).
- Number of sessions: the March 2026 session was the ninth since 2012; "most recently in 2023 in Trenčín"; none in 2024-2025 (the Fiala cabinet suspended the consultations) — same Czech government release as above. The 10 Nov session would be the tenth, held only about seven months after the ninth.
- Agenda signalled for 10 Nov: evaluation of how the March memorandum is being implemented and next steps — Blanár per ČTK 23 Oct "se budou na listopadovém jednání některá z témat z memoranda vyhodnocovat, cílem je pak posunout spolupráci dále" (Macinka article above).
- Related meetings: Babiš and Fico unveiled a friendship statue in Prague on 27 Oct (ČTK article above); foreign ministers Macinka and Blanár signed a consultation plan for 2027-2028 on 23 Oct (Macinka article above), a ministry-level document signed outside the session.

## Risks
- Session may produce only a joint declaration or statement (as in 2014, 2019, 2023), with the agenda an evaluation of the March memorandum.
- Ministry-level documents may be signed without press reports naming them as signed "at the session", or be signed on another day.
- Czech government releases list signings late the same day; ČTK and TASR normally report them at the press conference (13:45 per the schedule listing).
- Session could be moved or cut short (not suggested by any source).
