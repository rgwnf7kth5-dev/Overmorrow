## Risks
- Pulses: big nights come a few days apart; mass strikes often fall just before or around diplomatic moments, but a lull on the eve of talks is also possible.
- Daytime waves are counted separately and remove drones from the night count.
- Summary start time can differ (e.g. 19:00); it still counts as the first morning summary.
- The Air Force may combine missiles and drones in one headline; only the drone figure counts.
