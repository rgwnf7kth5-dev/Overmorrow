# cz-students-protest-11
- **Claim:** The Prague student protest in support of Czech Television and Czech Radio goes ahead at náměstí Republiky on 10 November 2026, and ČTK or ČT24 reports at least 2,000 participants.
- **Criterion:** YES if the student protest on náměstí Republiky in Prague (announced for 16:36 on 10 Nov 2026) takes place that day and ČTK (ceskenoviny.cz) or ČT24 reports, by 12:00 on 11 Nov 2026, that at least 2,000 people took part: a numerical estimate of 2,000 or more (police, organisers or the agency's own, as quoted by ČTK or ČT24), or a description of the crowd as "tisíce lidí" / "několik tisíc" / "desítky tisíc" (thousands), which counts as at least 2,000. Descriptions such as "stovky" or "stovky studentů" are below the threshold. Crowds at other Prague venues (Dejvice campus, faculties) do not count unless ČTK or ČT24 report them as part of the náměstí Republiky protest. NO if the protest is cancelled or not held on 10 Nov, or the reported size is below 2,000, or no size above "stovky" is reported.
- **Deadline:** event 2026-11-10 from 16:36 (Prague time); evidence by 2026-11-11T12:00+01:00
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-08T18:25Z. Read times as stated.
- Threshold: 2,000, set below the "several thousand" that ČTK reported for the combined march on Václavské náměstí on 3 Nov and well above the "hundreds" of the separate student columns, because the 10 Nov protest is student-led only, on a working day, at 16:36.
