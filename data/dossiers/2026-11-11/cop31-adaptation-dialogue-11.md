## Facts
- Opening plenaries Monday 9 Nov; negotiations all day 10 Nov; lunchtime Youth-led Climate Forum part I; afternoon FMCP4 part II and an LCIPP round table — overview schedule above.
- Agenda risk at the opening: African countries asked for extra agenda items (adaptation finance, trade) and Nepal for a mountains work programme; Australia, running the negotiations, is "doing its best to try to avoid an agenda fight at the start" — Climate Home News https://www.climatechangenews.com/2026/11/05/cop31-briefing-what-to-expect-from-the-antalya-climate-summit/ (published 2026-11-05, read 2026-11-08T17:51Z). The same briefing: Lula, Burnham (UK) and Sánchez are early names on the leaders' list; "the EU saying it's coming empty-handed" on finance; developing countries push a number for the adaptation-finance tripling goal.
