# infineon-outlook-11
- **Claim:** In its results release for fiscal 2026 on 10 November 2026, Infineon forecasts revenue growth of at least 15 percent for fiscal 2027.
- **Criterion:** YES if, on 10 Nov 2026, Infineon's press release on the fourth-quarter and fiscal 2026 results, or the same-day analyst-call presentation or intro statement published on infineon.com, gives an outlook for fiscal 2027 revenue of at least 15 percent growth over fiscal 2026 revenue as reported in the same release: a stated growth rate (or range whose midpoint is at least 15 percent), a euro revenue figure (or range whose midpoint is) at least 1.15 times reported FY2026 revenue, or a wording whose lower bound is 15 percent or more ("mid-teens or higher", "high-teens", "around 20 percent"). Purely qualitative wording ("significant", "strong") or a "low-teens" figure does not count. NO if the outlook implies less than 15 percent growth, is qualitative only, or gives no FY2027 revenue outlook. NO if no results are published on 10 Nov 2026.

## Risks
- Wording risk is the main one: Infineon may guide with qualitative terms ("significant growth") or with a euro revenue-growth band; a qualitative-only outlook resolves NO under this criterion, though call comments may add numbers (these count if published on infineon.com the same day).
- New three-division reporting and restated history are due with this release; FY2026 reported revenue may differ slightly from €16.3 billion (Q4 guide €4.7 billion; if Q4 comes in different, the base changes within the same release).
- Currency: the outlook assumes a USD/EUR rate (US$1.15 in the Q4 guide); an adverse currency assumption reduces euro growth.
- Management said the update would be "material" for the AI target; this does not fix total revenue growth, with automotive growing slower than the group.
- Possible event after the window (analyst call transcript) not counted unless on infineon.com on 10 Nov.
