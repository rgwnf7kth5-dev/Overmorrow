# us-iran-ship-11
- **Claim:** UKMTO or JMIC reports at least one new attack or incident involving a merchant vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, the Gulf of Oman or the Persian Gulf that occurs on Tuesday 10 November 2026 (Gulf time).
- **Criterion:** YES if the UK Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) or the Joint Maritime Information Center (JMIC) reports an attack, explosion, mine strike, projectile hit, hostile approach (armed craft firing on or attempting to board the vessel), boarding or seizure involving a merchant vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, the Gulf of Oman or the Persian Gulf that occurred between 00:00 and 23:59 Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4) on 10 Nov 2026, as shown on ukmto.org or by a wire, gCaptain, Lloyd's List, Khaleej Times, Gulf News, Arab News or Al Jazeera quoting UKMTO or JMIC. A merchant vessel disabled, boarded or seized by US or other military forces under the US blockade of Iranian ports (for example under Task Force Iron Gate) counts only if UKMTO or JMIC itself reports that incident; a CENTCOM or Iranian statement alone does not count. A report published after the window counts if the incident occurred in it. Reports of suspicious approaches, hailings or sightings without weapons fire or a boarding attempt, and GPS interference alone, do not count, nor do claims by Iran or other parties that neither UKMTO nor JMIC reports. NO if no such incident occurred on that day.

## Risks
- Attacks cluster, with quiet days (25, 27, 31 Oct, 4 Nov); 8-9 Nov unknown at writing.
- A Qatar-brokered pause or deal could cut attacks; a resumption of US-Israeli strikes would likely bring Iranian action at sea (IRGC threat above).
- IRGC mine and fire claims count only if UKMTO or JMIC reports an incident dated 10 Nov.
- UKMTO often reports late or with the previous day's date (Huda notice dated 7 Nov, posted 8 Nov); an incident late on 9 Nov GMT may fall on 10 Nov Gulf time and vice versa.
- Whether a "military forces" notice shows weapons fire or boarding can be unclear (the 7 Nov bulk-carrier notice said only "incident").
- ukmto.org is unreadable by script; a minor notice not relayed by any listed outlet may be missed.
