# py-blocked-11
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Asunción time on 10 November 2026, Paraguay's central bank (BCP) pays out the Deposit Guarantee Fund (FGD) advance to ueno bank depositors with no debts to ueno whose accounts were held back from the first two payment groups (blocked accounts, reserved balances or several accounts to be consolidated).
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 Asunción time on 9 Nov 2026 and 23:59 Asunción time on 10 Nov 2026, the Banco Central del Paraguay (or its ueno intervention team) credits, or orders the crediting of, FGD advance payments to ueno depositors who have no overdue debts with ueno and who were left out of the first two payment groups (deposits up to G. 10 million, and above that up to 40 minimum wages) because of account blocks, reserved balances (for example automatic debits or card operations) or the need to consolidate several accounts of one person, as stated by the BCP in a communiqué, press conference or official channels, or reported as done by ABC Color, Última Hora, La Nación (Paraguay), Hoy or EFE. Payments to depositors with debts netted off (the subject of py-ueno-debtors-10), payments for certificates of deposit (CDA), announcements of a future date and the preparation of lists do not count. NO if no such payment has been credited or ordered by 23:59 on 10 Nov.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-10 23:59 America/Asuncion (UTC-3)
- **Event window:** 2026-11-09T00:00-03:00 to 2026-11-10T23:59-03:00
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-08T18:15Z. Read times as stated.

## Reconciliation: resignation or impeachment
- Both happened in sequence. On 5 Nov Peña announced a resignation, then the same evening said he would face the impeachment trial — La Nación (Argentina) https://www.lanacion.com.ar/el-mundo/renuncio-el-presidente-de-paraguay-santiago-pena-nid05112026/ (published 2026-11-05T16:20Z, modified 2026-11-06, read 2026-11-08T17:53Z) "ante la continuidad del juicio político, me presentaré y ejerceré mi derecho a la defensa", "después de haber presentado su renuncia horas antes".
- On 6 Nov the Senate removed him ("destituido") for "mal desempeño de funciones" by 44 votes; his resignation was stuck ("renuncia trabada"); Pedro Alliana was sworn in the same evening, Antonio Barrios as vice president — ABC Color https://www.abc.com.py/politica/2026/11/06/en-vivo-juicio-politico-a-pena-senado-retoma-proceso-en-medio-de-renuncia-trabada-y-pedido-de-prorroga/ (published 2026-11-06T23:18Z, read 2026-11-08T17:52Z) "El Senado destituyó por 44 votos a Santiago Peña por mal desempeño de funciones"; Hoy https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2026/11/06/el-senado-destituye-al-presidente-santiago-pena (published 2026-11-06T21:59Z) "con 44 votos de todos los presentes, fue destituido como presidente del Paraguay". So the correct term is removal by impeachment, not resignation.

## Facts
- Paid so far: first lot Thursday 722,000 depositors, second Friday 475,000; about 1,197,000 of 1,867,000 accounts with a balance, about 670,000 remaining; about G. 996,000 million disbursed — La Nación print edition above. The earlier groups were "cuentas sin dinero bloqueado o reservado para pago de otros servicios" (Telefuturo quoted by Última Hora, same ÚH article above).
- 8 Nov, BCP on X: CDA are not in the first two groups; "La información sobre las siguientes etapas será comunicada próximamente"; payments may be delayed for clients with automatic debits or card operations "debido a las verificaciones necesarias antes de liberar los fondos"; "los casos se irán revisando posteriormente" — La Nación https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/11/08/bcp-aclara-que-los-cda-no-estan-incluidos-en-las-dos-primeras-etapas-de-pago/ (published 2026-11-08T16:59Z, read 2026-11-08T17:52Z). Also: lower-than-expected sums may be a first advance "que posteriormente será completado".
- BCP transfers funds to the paying banks, which credit depositors — same La Nación article.
- Custody: Peña charged on Saturday 7 Nov (lesión de confianza, administración en provecho propio); judge Humberto Otazú ordered preventive custody at the Regimiento Escolta Presidencial and froze assets of Peña and ex-BCP head Carlos Carvallo (estimated damage over USD 815 million); Carvallo in preventive custody for 15 days at the police Agrupación Especializada — Última Hora https://www.ultimahora.com/juez-congela-bienes-de-santiago-pena-y-carlos-carvallo-por-desfalco-millonario-tras-caida-de-ueno (published 2026-11-08T16:06, read 2026-11-08T17:52Z) and https://www.ultimahora.com/ex-titular-del-bcp-cumplira-reclusion-por-15-dias-en-la-agrupacion-especializada (published 2026-11-07T23:43). No hearing, appeal date or Congress vote dated 10 Nov was found.
