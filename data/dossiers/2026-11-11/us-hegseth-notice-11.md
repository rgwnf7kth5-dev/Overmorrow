## Schedule slip
- slip_class none: the vote is not on a published schedule; the date is set by when Massie gives notice and when the Speaker designates the time. Republican leaders can choose the day inside the window, and can postpone by the early-adjournment route used in September.

## Risks
- Timing: a notice Monday or Tuesday most likely puts the vote on Wed 11 or Thu 12 Nov, which is NO. A same-day or next-day motion to table is possible (leaders sometimes move to table or refer on the day it is called up).
- Hegseth resigns or is fired first (Massie says he then would not file).
- Parliamentarian or Speaker rules it not privileged; or the House adjourns early again.
- Only a voice vote or an unrecorded disposition is not enough; a yea-nay vote would normally be demanded.
