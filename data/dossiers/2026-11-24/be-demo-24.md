## Consensus and surprise size
- **Consensus: 50,000** — no turnout expectation published by the unions or police for 23 Nov (none found). Value = median of the six available police counts (30,000; 60,000; 80,000; 80,000; 40,000; 30,000) → sorted 30k, 30k, 40k, 60k, 80k, 80k → median (40,000+60,000)/2 = 50,000. Restricting to the five full national demonstrations (excluding 13 Jan 2025) gives a median of 60,000; the two most recent counts (12 May and 9 Oct 2026) average 35,000.
- **Scale: 23,400 ("published")** — sample standard deviation of the same six sourced police counts: mean 53,333; deviations −23,333, +6,667, +26,667, +26,667, −13,333, −23,333; sum of squares 2,733.3 million; ÷(n−1=5) = 546.7 million; √ = 23,381 → 23,400. (Population SD 21,300; SD of log counts 0.46, i.e. a factor of about 1.6.) No series exists: `series.json` has no demonstration key, so `series.mjs scale` was not applicable.

## Risks
- Police may publish no count, or only an estimate via a spokesperson to one outlet; on 25 Jun 2025 no police count was found. Count could be reported as a range.
- Turnout drivers upward: three unions (Synova back, unlike 9 Oct), broader civil-society call, the day of the PM's report to the King, strike notice (FGTB), budget measures in the news (index saving, public holidays). Downward: sixth-plus action in two years, the last two counts were 40,000 and 30,000; flights grounded and 40% of De Lijn not running hamper travel; November weather.
- Violence on the margins (as on 14 Oct 2025, 9 Oct 2026) does not change the count but may delay its publication.
