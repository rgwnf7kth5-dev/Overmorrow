# be-king-24
- **Claim:** The Royal Palace announces by 23:59 Brussels time on Monday 23 November 2026 that King Philippe has accepted the resignation of Bart De Wever's government.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 05:00 on Sun 22 Nov 2026 and 23:59 on Mon 23 Nov 2026 (Europe/Brussels), the Royal Palace announces (statement on monarchie.be or press communiqué, as reported by Belga, VRT NWS, RTBF, Le Soir or De Standaard) that the King has accepted the resignation of the De Wever government, including if it stays on as a caretaker government. A resignation offered and held in abeyance ("tient sa décision en suspens"), refused, or followed by the appointment of a mediator/informateur without acceptance does not count. NO otherwise. A report published after 23:59 about an announcement made within the window counts.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-22T05:00:00+01:00 – 2026-11-23T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Brussels)
- **Evidence expected:** Palace communiqué after the PM's audience, expected Monday morning (time not announced); evidence_at 2026-11-23T14:00:00+01:00.
- **Deadline (resolves by):** 2026-11-25T05:00:00+01:00
- **Written at (UTC):** 2026-11-21T18:15Z

## Facts
- **2 Nov:** De Wever tendered the government's resignation after another night without agreement; the King put it on hold and began consulting coalition leaders — Eunews https://www.eunews.it/en/2026/11/02/belgian-government-in-turmoil-over-budget-king-puts-prime-minister-de-wevers-resignation-on-hold/ (published 2026-11-02T12:20+01:00, read 17:57Z) "The Palace has announced that the King 'is keeping his decision on hold' and that, from Tuesday, he will begin 'consultations with the leaders of the various parties in the majority coalition.' De Wever will be received again on Friday 6 November".
- **6 Nov:** the King refused the resignation and sent the coalition back to talks, next report 16 Nov — Brussels Signal https://brusselssignal.eu/2026/11/belgian-king-refuses-de-wevers-resignation-and-orders-coalition-back-to-budget-talks/ (published 2026-11-06T12:26Z, read 17:57Z) headline "Belgian King refuses De Wever's resignation and orders coalition back to budget talks"; "He has now asked De Wever to resume negotiations immediately and report back to the Palace on November 16." (Also Euronews 6 Nov, per data/watchlist.json; not readable, HTTP 406.)
- **9 Nov:** Palace said all five coalition leaders formally committed to concessions (per data/watchlist.json history note citing VRT, 9 Nov; not re-opened).
- **15–16 Nov:** talks collapsed on Sunday night, four parties accepted De Wever's framework, Vooruit refused; on 16 Nov the King gave another week (statement above). VRT 16 Nov: "The king had asked him to return after the Belgian leader tendered his resignation two weeks ago." Whether the 2 Nov resignation is formally still pending is not stated in the 16 Nov statement.
- **17–20 Nov:** De Wever met CD&V, Les Engagés and MR, not Vooruit (VRT EN 19 Nov https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/en/2026/11/19/pm-bart-de-wever-talking-with-all-coalition-partners-bar-vooruit/). Rousseau (Vooruit, 20 Nov): "Iedereen weet dat we verder willen onderhandelen. Wij hopen dat er een akkoord komt." (VRT liveblog above).
- **Fri 20 Nov evening:** De Wever and Rousseau met for about three hours; content not disclosed — VRT liveblog (above) "De 2 zouden zo'n 3 uur hebben samengezeten ... Ook is niet duidelijk of het vertrouwen tussen de 2 hersteld is. Tot nu geven alle partijen aan dat ze nog altijd verder willen onderhandelen, en niet aansturen op het vallen van de regering en mogelijk nieuwe verkiezingen. Een planning voor de komende dagen is er wel nog niet." RTBF/Belga (above): "Aucun élément de la discussion n'a filtré à ce stade"; "Les négociations portent toujours sur un assainissement budgétaire de 10 milliards d'euros. À ce stade, aucun accord n'a été annoncé."
- **Weekend so far (to Sat 21 Nov ~18:00 CET): no five-party budget agreement reported**, no meeting scheduled (VRT 17:04, RTBF 11:44 above). The government approved the first tranche of provisional twelfths (Jan–Mar 2027) for submission to the Chamber (VRT liveblog: "De federale regering heeft de weg vrijgemaakt voor de eerste schijf voorlopige twaalfden").
- Sticking point: a contribution from millionaires; De Wever's possible offer is lowering the securities-account tax threshold from €1m to €750,000, so far rejected by Vooruit (VRT liveblog, 20 Nov entry).
- Fitch kept Belgium at A+ and expects the impasse to be resolved: "We verwachten ook deze keer een vergelijkbare uitkomst." (VRT liveblog).
- Plan B: replacing Vooruit with Anders would leave "une majorité réduite à 76 sièges sur 150" — RTBF 20 Nov https://www.rtbf.be/article/budget-federal-remplacer-vooruit-par-anders-une-piste-politiquement-et-mathematiquement-fragile-11818602 (via be-budget-23 dossier).
- Analyst Carl Devos (UGent, before the Friday meeting): "als er geen telefoon komt, gaan we op deze manier naar grote miserie, maandag bij de koning" (VRT liveblog).
- Palace options on Monday (as the sequence shows): (a) acknowledge progress and ask the PM to continue, setting a new report date (as on 6 and 16 Nov); (b) keep a (re)offered resignation in abeyance and consult party leaders (as on 2 Nov); (c) appoint a mediator/informateur; (d) accept the resignation, the government continuing in current affairs. Only (d) is YES. No source found that De Wever intends to re-tender the resignation on Monday.

- A government collapse via a Vooruit exit without a Palace acceptance on Monday is NO.
