# ua-drones-24
- **Claim (interval):** The number of drones that the Ukrainian Air Force's first morning summary on Monday 23 November 2026 says Russia launched at Ukraine in the night of 22–23 November (from 18:00 Kyiv time on 22 November).
- **Criterion:** The UAV total in the Air Force's first summary of the overnight attack published on Mon 23 Nov 2026 (Kyiv time), covering the attack counted from 18:00 Kyiv time on 22 Nov. Source: the Air Force Telegram channel (t.me/kpszsu) or Facebook page, or Ukrinform, Ukrainska Pravda, Kyiv Independent or Interfax-Ukraine repeating it. "Drones" means strike UAVs as the summary counts them: Shahed-type and jet-powered Shaheds, Gerbera, Parodiya and other decoys, and any jet munitions such as Banderol/Dan-T that the summary includes in its UAV total. Missiles listed separately are not counted. A summary issued late in the morning or around noon still counts as the first summary. Separate daytime reports and later corrections do not count. If the Air Force reports that no drones were launched that night, the value is 0. If no summary for that night has been published, the claim stays pending. (Same criterion as ua-drones-23, shifted by one day.)
- **Event window:** attack 2026-11-22T18:00:00+02:00 to 2026-11-23T12:00:00+02:00 (Europe/Kyiv, EET). The summary normally comes 08:00–10:00 Kyiv; after very large attacks up to about 12:00.

## Risks
- **Skew:** a mass strike (≥280) on the claim's night lands far above the median; such nights came every 1–8 nights in the last month. The 20 Nov strike was 3 nights before.
- **Retaliation cycle:** Ukraine's Crimean Bridge strikes (Zelensky 21 Nov) and Putin's earlier vow of "far more painful" retaliation (ua-drones-23) keep the upside tail fat.
- **Daytime waves** (Ihnat: "more than 100 drones during the day") can move drones out of the night count.
- **Summary format:** a first summary "as of" late morning while the attack is ongoing takes the partial count as published.
- **Diplomacy:** no pause announced; a surprise de-escalation would push the count down.
