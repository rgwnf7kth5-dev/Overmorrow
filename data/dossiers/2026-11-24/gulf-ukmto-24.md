# gulf-ukmto-24
- **Claim:** UKMTO or JMIC reports at least one new attack or security incident involving a merchant vessel in the Strait of Hormuz, the Persian Gulf, the Gulf of Oman or the Arabian Sea that took place on Monday 23 November 2026 (00:00–23:59 Gulf time).
- **Criterion:** YES if the UK Maritime Trade Operations (UKMTO) or the Joint Maritime Information Center (JMIC) reports a qualifying incident involving a merchant vessel that occurred between 00:00 and 23:59 Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4) on 23 Nov 2026.
  - Qualifying incidents: an attack, explosion, mine strike, projectile or drone hit, hostile approach (armed craft firing on or attempting to board the vessel), boarding, hijacking or seizure.
  - Area: the Strait of Hormuz, the Persian (Arabian) Gulf, the Gulf of Oman or the Arabian Sea (including the waters around Socotra).
  - Accepted evidence: ukmto.org, UKMTO's X account, or a wire, gCaptain, Lloyd's List, Khaleej Times, Gulf News, Arab News, Iran International, Ship & Bunker, Al Jazeera, Newsquawk or another established news outlet quoting UKMTO or JMIC.
  - Timing: when UKMTO/JMIC give only a date and no time, an incident dated 23 Nov counts. A report published after the window (including a "time-late" report) counts if the incident occurred in the window.
  - Military action: a merchant vessel disabled, boarded or seized by US or other military forces counts only if UKMTO or JMIC itself reports that incident.
  - Not counted: suspicious approaches, hailings or sightings without weapons fire or a boarding attempt; GPS or navigation-aid interference alone; notices of an "incident" or "suspicious activity" that state no attack, explosion, hit, boarding or seizure; incidents in the Gulf of Aden, the Bab el-Mandeb or the Red Sea; claims by Iran (including IRGC/navy statements), the Houthis or other parties that neither UKMTO nor JMIC reports.
  - NO if no such incident occurred in the window.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-23T00:00:00+04:00 to 2026-11-23T23:59:00+04:00 (Asia/Dubai) = 22 Nov 21:00 CET to 23 Nov 20:59 CET (after the 22 Nov 05:00 CET freeze).
- **Evidence expected:** UKMTO Warning/advisory (ukmto.org, X @UK_MTO), relayed by gCaptain, Arab News, Gulf News, Al Jazeera, Newsquawk, Iran International; JMIC advisory notes. evidence_at 2026-11-24T12:00:00+04:00.
