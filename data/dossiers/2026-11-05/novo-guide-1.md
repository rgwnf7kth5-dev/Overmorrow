# novo-guide-1
- **Claim:** In its third-quarter results, published by 4 November 2026, Novo Nordisk raises the midpoint of its 2026 adjusted sales growth outlook at constant exchange rates above −3%.

## Risks
- The range may be narrowed from one side only: "−1% to −5%" keeps the midpoint at −3.0% and is NO; "0% to −4%" gives −2.0% and is YES.
- Adjusted sales vs reported sales: the criterion reads the adjusted CER line. Reported and DKK figures differ.
- If the company changes the definition of the outlook, the resolver must read the adjusted CER line as given.
- Operating profit may move while sales does not. That does not count.
