# ua-pow-1
- **Claim:** Russia and Ukraine carry out a prisoner-of-war exchange on 3 or 4 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if Zelensky, Ukraine's Coordination Headquarters for the Treatment of Prisoners of War or the Russian Defence Ministry states that an exchange of prisoners of war between Russia and Ukraine took place on 3 or 4 November 2026 (Kyiv time), with prisoners returned to both sides. Returns of bodies, and one-way releases or repatriations of civilians only, do not count. NO if no such exchange takes place on those two days.
- **Deadline:** 23:59 Kyiv on 4 Nov 2026. Evidence expected the same day.
- **Written at:** 2026-11-02T18:30Z. Read times 18:15–18:25Z on 2 Nov.
- **Change to the desk's text:** the "announced by 23:59" condition is dropped, because the brief says a late source must not give NO. Exchanges have been announced the same day.

## Risks
- Exchanges come without notice, so the absence of a date says little.
- Body repatriations and civilian-only returns could be confused with an exchange; they do not count.
- A return of "missing in action" soldiers like 24 Aug counts only if described as an exchange with prisoners going both ways.
- Russia's holiday of 4 Nov and strikes around it (Ushakov, in ua-talks-date-2) may affect timing; no source links them to exchanges.
