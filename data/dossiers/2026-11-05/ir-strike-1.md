# ir-strike-1
- **Claim:** US forces strike targets on Iranian territory between 08:30 Tehran time on 3 November and 23:59 Tehran time on 4 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if the White House, President Trump, the Department of Defense / Department of War or US Central Command confirms a US military strike (air, missile or drone) on a target on Iranian territory, including Iranian territorial waters and islands, carried out between 08:30 Tehran time on 3 Nov and 23:59 Tehran time on 4 Nov 2026. A confirmation published after the window counts. Iranian claims alone, anonymous officials, strikes only on Iranian allies outside Iran, on vessels in international waters, or by Israel alone do not count. NO if no such strike took place in the window.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-04T23:59:00+03:30 (21:29 CET). Window opens at 06:00 CET, the morning freeze.
- **Board question:** us-iran-strikes-november uses the same definition from 4 Nov 00:00 CET to 30 Nov; this claim takes the first days of it plus 3 Nov.
- **Written at:** 2026-11-02T18:40Z. Pages opened this evening were read between 18:15Z and 18:30Z on 2 Nov through the fetch tool, which returns excerpts; facts reused from data/dossiers/2026-11-04/ keep their original read time.

## Risks
- Trump can strike on short notice after the polls close; he can also wait on the Qatar talks or the result.
- A "self-defence" strike on launchers or boats in Iranian waters would count; a clash on the high seas would not.
- Israel may move alone, which does not count.
- Confirmation can lag or come only from anonymous officials; a strike in the window with later on-record confirmation still counts.
