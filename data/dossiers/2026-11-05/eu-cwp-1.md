# eu-cwp-1
- **Claim:** The European Commission adopts its 2027 Work Programme by 23:59 Brussels time on 4 November 2026.
- **Criterion:** YES if the Commission adopts its Commission Work Programme 2027 by 23:59 Brussels time on 4 Nov 2026, as shown by the date on the COM document or by the Commission's work programme page or press release. NO if it is not adopted by that time. A later publication of a text dated 4 Nov or earlier counts as YES.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-03T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-04T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Brussels).
- **Written at:** 2026-11-02T18:25Z. All read times 2026-11-02 between 18:12Z and 18:19Z.

## Risks
- A slip to a later College meeting (the list runs to 24 Nov) while the enlargement package keeps 4 Nov.
- Written procedure or a date mismatch between adoption and publication: the criterion goes by the document's date.
