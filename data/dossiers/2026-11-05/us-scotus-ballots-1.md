# us-scotus-ballots-1
- **Claim:** The US Supreme Court grants, in whole or in part, by 23:59 ET on 4 November 2026 the Trump administration's request concerning about 20,000 California mail ballots.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 00:00 ET on 3 Nov and 23:59 ET on 4 Nov 2026, the Supreme Court or a single justice issues an order granting in whole or in part the administration's application of 2 Nov 2026 about the roughly 20,000 California mail ballots of voters it calls likely noncitizens. A temporary administrative order counts only if it actually requires California to set aside, segregate or not count those ballots. An order that merely asks for a response does not count. NO if the application is denied, withdrawn or still undecided at the end of the window.
- **Deadline:** 2026-11-04T23:59:00-05:00.
- **Status check:** as of the pages read at about 18:25Z on 2 Nov the application is filed and undecided, so the claim stands. The morning overnight check must confirm that no order came before the window opens.

## Facts
- **The request:** a "limited injunction" directing California to "stop commingling suspected illegal ballots with legal ones" while the case continues; the government wants about 20,000 ballots kept from the count until the voters' status is verified — AP, above.
- **California's reply so far:** the federal list is "highly unreliable" and likely includes many US citizens — AP, above. "All of the state's roughly 23 million registered voters were mailed a ballot, and hundreds of thousands have already been processed" (AP, above, as rendered by the fetch tool).
- **District court:** US District Judge David O. Carter denied the restraining order on about 28 Oct — NewsOne https://newsone.com/6875092/judge-denies-trump-withhold-california-ballots/ (published 2026-10-29, read 2026-11-02T18:25Z) "would wreak havoc and disrupt the election that is already underway"; "This Court declines to be a party to such process". AP attributes the "wreak havoc" warning to another district judge; the two reports differ and the order itself was not read. Carter, per AP: "our democracy is in danger" if the executive can discount votes on "unproven allegations".
- **Ninth Circuit, first round (26 Oct):** 2–1 dismissal for lack of jurisdiction, Bennett and Sanchez against, Bumatay dissenting — Fox LA https://www.foxla.com/news/ninth-circuit-dismisses-trump-administrations-emergency-appeal-over-20000-california-mail-ballots (published 2026-10-26 17:20 PDT, read 2026-11-02T18:20Z) "mere 15 hours". Sanchez: "a matter squarely within the state's traditional domain"; "inviting disorder and confusion for voters and election officials alike" — Al Jazeera https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/27/us-court-rules-against-trump-administration-in-california-voting-case (published 2026-10-27, read 2026-11-02T18:25Z).
- **Ninth Circuit, second round (30 Oct):** emergency request denied — Reuters via GV Wire https://gvwire.com/?p=275447 (published 2026-10-30, read 2026-11-02T18:25Z) "second time that week"; 2–1, Bumatay: "The district court, and now our court, denies this reasonable request." — RedState https://redstate.com/jenvanlaar/2026/10/30/it-looks-like-the-dispute-over-likely-noncitizens-on-cas-voter-rolls-is-going-to-scotus-n2208832 (published 2026-10-30 18:06, read 2026-11-02T18:25Z; partisan outlet, used for the dissent quote only).
- **Lawsuit:** filed 15 Oct — Fox LA, above. Los Angeles County expects about 2.75 million mail ballots and called the federal list unreliable — AP via https://www.nbcbayarea.com/news/california/9th-circuit-doj-california-mail-ballots/4164386/ (published 2026-10-26, read 2026-11-02T18:25Z).
- **The Court this year on elections:** *Watson v. Republican National Committee* (June 2026, 5–4, Barrett): federal law does not bar counting timely postmarked mail ballots that arrive after Election Day; Alito, Thomas and Gorsuch dissented, Kavanaugh in part. On 24 Aug the Court lifted an injunction against the president's executive order on mail voting, Sotomayor and Jackson dissenting; Jackson: "needlessly injects chaos and uncertainty into the midterms" — Newsweek, above. Kavanaugh "has consistently emphasized" avoiding late election disruption and has invoked the Purcell principle — Newsweek, above. What the Court finally did with the 3 Sep application was not found.

## Risks
- The Court may simply not rule before the vote, or ask for a response with a date after 4 Nov; undecided is NO.
- An administrative order that holds ballots briefly would count under the criterion if it really requires setting them aside.
- A partial grant of any scope counts, even one that stops short of halting the count.
- Mail ballots keep arriving until 10 Nov, so the request does not become moot on election day.
