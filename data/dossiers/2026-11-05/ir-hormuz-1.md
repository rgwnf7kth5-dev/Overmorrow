# ir-hormuz-1
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Tehran time on 4 November 2026 Iran, Qatar or the United States publicly announces that an arrangement reopening the Strait of Hormuz has been reached.
- **Criterion:** YES if, between 08:30 Tehran time on 3 Nov and 23:59 Tehran time on 4 Nov 2026, the government of Iran (president, foreign minister, foreign ministry spokesman or Supreme National Security Council), of Qatar (prime minister, foreign ministry) or of the United States (President Trump, the White House or the State Department) states on the record that an agreement or arrangement has been reached under which the Strait of Hormuz will reopen to commercial shipping. One government's statement is enough, even if another disputes it. Statements that a deal is close or possible, proposals, offers, conditional promises, announcements of talks and passage arrangements for single ships or single countries do not count. NO if no such statement is made in the window.

## Risks
- Trump tends to declare agreements early; under the criterion his statement alone counts if he says it is agreed, not merely close. Iran could deny it the same day.
- Iran may announce a "reopening" on its own terms (a toll or permit scheme); it counts only if described as an arrangement reached, and only for commercial shipping generally.
- US strikes after the vote would end the talks.
- A deal may be held back by the US until the result is known, or announced after the window.
