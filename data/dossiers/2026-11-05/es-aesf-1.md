# es-aesf-1
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Madrid time on 4 November 2026, Spain's rail safety agency AESF orders a limit of 40 km/h on high-speed lines for trains that lose LZB or ERTMS transmission.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 00:00 on 3 Nov and 23:59 Madrid time on 4 Nov 2026 the Agencia Estatal de Seguridad Ferroviaria issues an order, resolution, instruction or safety recommendation that sets 40 km/h or less as the maximum speed under the backup systems (BSL, ASFA) on high-speed lines when LZB or ERTMS transmission is lost, as shown by AESF, the Transport Ministry, the BOE or a report by EFE, El País, elDiario.es or RTVE. A measure by Renfe, Adif or Iryo alone does not count, nor does a different limit. NO otherwise.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-03T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-04T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Madrid)
- **Written at:** 2026-11-03T04:15Z. Replaces es-aid-1, which became moot.
- **Why replaced:** Sánchez said on 2 Nov the cabinet approves the Urda aid decree on Tue 3 Nov (elDiario, Atlántico, Infobae), so the old claim was certain and before the event day.
- **Facts:** 69 dead, more than 200 injured in the Urda (Toledo) high-speed crash. Semaf (drivers' union) and Iryo asked AESF for 40 km/h under BSL and ASFA when LZB or ERTMS transmission is lost. Renfe is preparing instructions that would also bar normal running (up to 200 km/h) on LZB failure (Infobae agency copy, 2 Nov). Puente appears before the Diputación Permanente on Tue afternoon and presents his department's report at the post-cabinet press conference. Regulators usually act after technical analysis; the CIAF investigation is under way. AESF's route beyond Infobae has not been read.
