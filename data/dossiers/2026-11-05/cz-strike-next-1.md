# cz-strike-next-1
- **Claim:** By 23:59 Prague time on 4 November 2026, the organisers of the ČT and ČRo strike or the broadcasters' unions announce a further strike with a stated date.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 00:00 on 3 Nov and 23:59 Prague time on 4 Nov 2026 the Veřejnoprávně initiative, a strike committee of ČT or ČRo staff, or a trade union at ČT or ČRo (including OS Média) publicly announces a further strike of ČT or ČRo staff with a stated date, as reported by ČTK, ČT24 or iROZHLAS.
