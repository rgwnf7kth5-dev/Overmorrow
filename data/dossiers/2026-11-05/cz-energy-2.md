# cz-energy-2
- **Claim:** Between 3 November and 23:59 Prague time on 4 November 2026, E.ON Energie, innogy Energie or Pražská energetika (PRE) publicly announces its 2027 electricity or gas prices for households on open-ended tariffs.
- **Criterion:** YES if between 00:00 on 3 Nov and 23:59 Prague time on 4 Nov 2026 at least one of E.ON Energie, innogy Energie or Pražská energetika publicly announces (press release, new price list on its website, or statement to media reported by ČTK or another major Czech outlet) the household price of electricity or gas on open-ended (non-fixed) products that will apply from 1 January 2027, either as new prices, as a stated percentage or CZK/MWh change, or as an explicit statement that these prices stay unchanged from 1 January 2027. Changes to fixed-price products only, and statements without a figure or a clear "unchanged", do not count. NO otherwise.
- **Rewrite:** the suggested claim included ČEZ Prodej. ČEZ announced on 30 Oct, so it is moot for ČEZ and is left out. The window starts on 3 Nov and overlaps cz-energy-1 (edition 2026-11-04) on that day; the wording follows cz-energy-1.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-03T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-04T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Prague)
- **Written at:** 2026-11-02T18:30Z. Facts marked "per dossier" come from data/dossiers/2026-11-04/cz-energy-1.md and were not re-opened today.

## Risks
- YES paths: an E.ON release in the first week of November as in 2025; a spokesperson's figure or "beze změny" in a media round-up; customer letters reported in the media; a PRE notice.
- NO paths: the 30-day rule leaves until about 1 Dec; suppliers may wait for ERÚ's final prices at the end of November; answers such as "sledujeme trh".
- Moot risk: an announcement dated 2 Nov or the morning of 3 Nov could reach the desk after writing. The morning should recheck. An announcement on 2 Nov does not count, and it would leave only the other two actors.
- Resolution traps: fixed products; a change effective in December 2026; business customers only; parent-company statements abroad.
