# eu-enl-1
- **Claim:** The enlargement package the European Commission publishes by 4 November 2026 names a calendar year for closing accession negotiations with Montenegro.
- **Criterion:** YES if any document of the Commission's 2026 enlargement package (the Communication, the Montenegro report or the Montenegro roadmap), published by 23:59 Brussels time on 4 Nov 2026, gives a specific calendar year, or a date within one, for closing or concluding accession negotiations with Montenegro as the Commission's own target, expectation, indicative timeline or assessment (such as "on track to close by end-2027"). Merely reporting Montenegro's own goal, without the Commission adopting it, does not count; nor do dates for single chapters or for accession itself. NO if no such statement appears, or if the package is not published by that time.
- **Event window:** 2026-11-03T00:00:00+01:00 to 2026-11-04T23:59:00+01:00 (Europe/Brussels). Documents stay readable on the Commission's enlargement pages.
- **Written at:** 2026-11-02T18:25Z. All read times 2026-11-02 between 18:10Z and 18:19Z. Rewrite note: the opening of clusters with Ukraine and Moldova was rejected as the target, see Facts (only clusters 4 and 5 remain and the Council, not the Commission, opens them). A single country and a Commission-owned statement keep the claim checkable.

## Facts
- The roadmaps are expected to carry timelines, none quoted — https://newunionpost.eu/2026/10/14/enlargement-package-2026-postponed/ (published 2026-10-14, read 18:12Z) "indicative timelines for closing the negotiating chapters". The source gives no date for any country, and speaks of chapters, not of negotiations as a whole.
- Roadmaps "would aim to establish commitments not only for the candidate countries but also for EU member states" (same URL).
- Conflicting signal: Ukrainska Pravda headline (27 Oct) reports no deadlines for concluding talks — https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/10/27/8059955/ (headline only, page not retrievable, from work/agenda-europe.json; not re-read). It is not known whether this concerns Ukraine only.
- EPC frames concreteness as open — https://epc.eu/events/enlargement-package-2026-roadmaps-reforms-and-gradual-integration/ (read 18:13Z): the discussion will address how concrete the roadmaps will be.
- Precedent, 2025 package (4 Nov 2025): the Commission itself named years — https://www.brusselstimes.com/1827680/eu-enlargement-frontrunners-are-leaving-laggers-behind-in-the-accession-process (published 2025-11-06, read 18:18Z) Montenegro "is on track to close the negotiations by the end of 2026, subject to maintaining the pace of reforms"; Albania "is on track to conclude the negotiations by 2027."
- Montenegro now: three more chapters closed in October, 12 left — https://brusselssignal.eu/tag/eu-enlargement/ (tag page, item dated 2026-10-13, read 18:18Z) "Montenegro closed three more chapters and now has 12 left." So the end-2026 date of the 2025 package would need all 12 to close in about two months; the Commission must restate, move or drop it.
- Croatian objections to Montenegro's timetable are noted in an item dated 2026-09-24 (same tag page; article not read).
- Albania: only four of 33 chapters closed; "Tirana wants talks 'wrapped up by the end of 2027'" (same tag page, 2026-10-14); student-led protests and a political crisis (2026-10-19).
- EU leaders, October summit: conclusions set no deadlines on enlargement and asked ministers for further analysis (same tag page, 2026-10-16).
- Ukraine and Moldova: clusters 1 (15 June), 6 (July), 2 and 3 (13 Oct) open; 24 of 33 chapters; clusters 4 and 5 still to open, "as soon as possible" (Kos); next is a positive Interim Benchmark Assessment Report — https://newunionpost.eu/2026/10/13/ukraine-moldova-clusters-eu-accession/ (published 2026-10-13, read 18:13Z). The cluster decision lies with the Council, and the two countries are linked by the "package approach".

## Risks
- Wording: "by the end of 2027" counts; "in the coming period", "as soon as conditions are met" or a chapter-by-chapter timeline without an end date does not. Text may attribute the year to Podgorica only.
- A year may appear only in the speech of Kos or von der Leyen: that is not a package document and does not count.
- The roadmap may be annexed or published later than the Communication.
- Member-state resistance to dates (October summit set none).
